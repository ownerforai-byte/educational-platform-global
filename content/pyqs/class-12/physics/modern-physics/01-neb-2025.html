1.

In the photoelectric effect, increasing the intensity of the incident light (frequency unchanged) increases: (A) the maximum kinetic energy of photoelectrons (B) the stopping potential (C) the number of photoelectrons per second (D) the threshold frequency

ID

phy-mod-2025-1

Type

mcq

Marks

1

Difficulty

easy

Solution

Each photon ejects one electron, so doubling the intensity doubles the number of photons and hence the saturation photocurrent.

The energy of each photon, hν, is unchanged, so the maximum kinetic energy hν−W and the stopping potential stay the same; the threshold frequency is a property of the metal. Answer (C).
