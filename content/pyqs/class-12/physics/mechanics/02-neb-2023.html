Solution

On a flat curve friction alone supplies the centripetal force: fs​=mv2/r≤μs​N=μs​mg. The limiting speed is vmax​=μs​gr​=0.4×10×50​=200​≈14.1m/s (about 51km/h). Any faster and the required centripetal force exceeds maximum static friction, so the car skids.

4.

State Newton's law of gravitation. Derive the expression for acceleration due to gravity g at a height h above Earth's surface (radius R, mass M), and show that g decreases with altitude. Evaluate the ratio gh​/g at h=R.

Solution

Newton's law: every mass attracts every other with F=GMm/r2 along the line joining them. At height h, distance from Earth's centre is r=R+h, so mgh​=GMm/(R+h)2, giving gh​=GM/(R+h)2. At the surface g=GM/R2, hence gh​/g=R2/(R+h)2=1/(1+h/R)2, which falls as h grows — gravity weakens with altitude. At h=R: gh​/g=1/(1+1)2=1/4, so gravity is one-quarter of its surface value.
