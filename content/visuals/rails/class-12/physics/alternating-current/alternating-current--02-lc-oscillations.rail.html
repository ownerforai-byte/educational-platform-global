Concept

Text

LC oscillations involve the continuous exchange of energy between a capacitor's electric field and an inductor's magnetic field. This process is analogous to mechanical harmonic motion and produces oscillations at a natural frequency determined by L and C.

Conditions

Text

❌ Thinking LC circuits lose energy immediately without resistance ✅ An ideal LC circuit (zero resistance) oscillates indefinitely; only practical circuits lose energy due to resistance (damped oscillations). · ❌ Current is maximum when charge on capacitor is maximum ✅ When charge on the capacitor is maximum, the potential energy is maximum and the current is zero. When the current is maximum, the magnetic energy is maximum and charge is zero (energy transfer).
