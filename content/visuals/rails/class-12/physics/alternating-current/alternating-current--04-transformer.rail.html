Text

A transformer changes AC voltage based on mutual induction. It uses the turns ratio to step voltage up or down, while ideally conserving power. Energy losses are mitigated through core design and winding techniques.

Transformer: An electrical device based on mutual induction that changes the voltage level of an AC supply. · It works on the principle of electromagnetic induction: a varying current in the primary coil induces an EMF in the secondary coil via a common magnetic core. · Transformers can be step-up (increase voltage) or step-down (decrease voltage).
