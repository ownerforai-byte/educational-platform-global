A series LCR circuit combines resistance, inductance, and capacitance. It demonstrates resonance when inductive and capacitive reactances cancel, leading to minimum impedance and maximum current.

Conditions

Text

❌ Believing resonance occurs at all frequencies ✅ Resonance occurs only at a specific frequency (f_r = 1 / (2π√LC)) where inductive reactance equals capacitive reactance (X_L = X_C). · ❌ Misinterpreting phase angle as constant ✅ The phase angle φ (where tan φ = (X_L - X_C) / R) depends on the frequency of the AC source; it is zero at resonance.
