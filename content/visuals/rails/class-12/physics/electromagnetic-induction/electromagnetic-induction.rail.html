Title

Lenz's law — the minus sign that guards energy

Href

Rows

1.
Label

Concept

Text

Changing magnetic flux induces EMF (Faraday: ε = −dΦ/dt) — motion, changing current or rotating coils all generate electricity. Lenz's minus sign is energy conservation in disguise: induced effects always OPPOSE the change (push a magnet in, the coil repels it — you do the work that becomes electrical energy). Self-induction (coils fight their own current changes, L) and mutual induction (transformers, M) industrialise the effect; LR circuits grow/decay exponentially toward V/R.

2.
Label

Formula

Kind

formula

Text

ε = −N·dΦ/dt (Φ = BA·cosθ) · motional: ε = Blv · self: ε = −L·dI/dt, solenoid L = μ₀N²A/l · mutual: ε₂ = −M·dI₁/dt · LR growth: I = (V/R)(1−e^(−Rt/L)) · decay: I = I₀e^(−Rt/L), τ = L/R

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Flux must CHANGE — static fields induce nothing. Lenz opposition assumes passive circuits (no batteries driving the other way). LR exponentials need constant R, L and step voltage. Motional ε = Blv needs perpendicular v, B, l (resolve components otherwise).

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Dropping a magnet through a copper tube: eddy currents brake the fall (Lenz demo). AC in the primary drives transformer secondaries via mutual M. LR decay: at t = τ current falls to 37%; growth reaches 63% — mirror of RC charging with L↔C swapped.

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Rod of length l sliding at v on U-rails in field B: area grows at lv per second → dΦ/dt = Blv → ε = Blv, current ε/R opposing the motion (Lenz) — the mechanical work F·v you supply equals the electrical power ε²/R. Energy ledger closed, minus sign honoured.

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Faraday gives EMF, not current direction alone — Lenz + right-hand rules complete it. Ideal-inductor formulas ignore winding resistance and core saturation. Lenz predicts opposition, never magnitude; quantitative work still needs the flux rate.

7.
Label

Derivation

Text

Lenz from energy: suppose induced current AIDED the change — growing flux makes more current makes more flux, free energy forever. The minus sign forbids it; pushing the magnet against repulsion supplies exactly the electrical output. Self-inductance: flux linkage NΦ ∝ I defines L = NΦ/I; solenoid gives L = μ₀N²A/l via B = μ₀nI.

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

'Direction of induced current?' → ask what change, oppose it (repel approaching N, attract receding N). LR circuits: write τ = L/R, then 63%/37% benchmarks. Transformer next unit leans entirely on mutual M — learn it here once.
