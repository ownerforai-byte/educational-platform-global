Dihybrid cross — the 9:3:3:1 that proves independence

1.

Concept

Text

Mendel's three laws (dominance, segregation, independent assortment) emerge from one experiment: cross round-yellow × wrinkled-green peas and the F2 ratio 9:3:3:1 factorises into two independent 3:1 ratios — genes on different chromosomes assort independently. Incomplete dominance blends (snapdragon pink), codominance shows both (AB blood), linkage binds neighbours until crossing over swaps them. DNA's double helix (A=T, G≡C), semiconservative replication, transcription→translation (central dogma) and lac-operon regulation mechanise it all; Hardy–Weinberg (p²+2pq+q²) freezes allele frequencies without evolution.

2.
Label

Formula

Kind

formula

Text

monohybrid F2: 3:1 (genotypic 1:2:1) · dihybrid F2: 9:3:3:1 = (3:1)×(3:1) · test cross: Aa × aa → 1:1 if heterozygous · Hardy–Weinberg: p² + 2pq + q² = 1 (no selection/mutation/migration/drift) · sex-linked: X-linked recessive hits males (one X, no mask)

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Independent assortment needs genes on DIFFERENT chromosomes (linked genes break 9:3:3:1 toward parental combos). Mendelian ratios need large progeny, complete penetrance and no lethality. Hardy–Weinberg is a null model — any violation signals evolution in action. Central-dogma direction holds with retroviral exceptions noted.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

ABO blood: IA/IB codominant, i recessive — six genotypes, four phenotypes. Haemophilia/colour-blindness ride the X into males. Linkage maps from crossover % (1% = 1 map unit). Human genome project: ~3 billion bases, ~20,000 genes — objectives were sequence, map and functional meaning.

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Dihybrid RrYy × RrYy: each trait segregates 3:1 independently; multiply — (3 round:1 wrinkled)×(3 yellow:1 green) = 9 round-yellow : 3 round-green : 3 wrinkled-yellow : 1 wrinkled-green. The Punnett square's 16 boxes just tabulate this product — write the two 3:1s and multiply.

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Mendel's laws fail linked genes, polygenic traits (height, skin — continuous, not ratios) and cytoplasmic inheritance. Hardy–Weinberg describes populations that by definition are NOT evolving. Molecular details (splicing, epigenetics) complicate the clean dogma.

7.
Label

Derivation

Text

Segregation from meiosis: homologous chromosomes part in anaphase I, so alleles part — the 1:2:1 genotypic ratio is combinatorics of two heterozygote gamete sets. Independent assortment from random metaphase orientation of DIFFERENT pairs — multiply the single-trait probabilities. Chromosome theory (Sutton–Boveriment) maps genes onto this mechanics; Morgan's white-eyed flies proved sex linkage.

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

F2 ratio read: 9:3:3:1 = dihybrid; 3:1 = monohybrid; 1:1 = test cross; 1:2:1 = incomplete/codominance or genotypic. Linked genes? Expect parental excess over 9:3:3:1. Sex-linked recessive pedigree: affected fathers → carrier daughters → grandsons.
