Concept

Text

Biodiversity stacks three levels — genetic (within species), species (counts), ecosystem (habitat variety) — peaking in the tropics (latitudinal gradient) with Nepal packing disproportionate variety into its altitude span. Loss drivers chant HIM: Habitat loss, Invasive species, overexploitation/climate (plus pollution). Conservation splits: in-situ (parks, reserves, sacred groves — evolution continues) vs ex-situ (zoos, seed banks, cryopreservation — backup copies). IUCN Red List ranks risk: extinct → critically endangered → … → least concern.

2.
Label

Formula

formula

Text

levels: genetic < species < ecosystem · loss drivers: habitat + invasives + overuse + pollution + climate · in-situ: national parks / wildlife reserves / conservation areas (Nepal: Chitwan, Sagarmatha, Annapurna) · ex-situ: zoos / botanical gardens / seed banks / cryo · IUCN ladder: EX · EW · CR · EN · VU · NT · LC

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

In-situ needs viable habitat size + corridors (fragments bleed species). Ex-situ needs genetic diversity sampled (few founders = inbreeding) plus reintroduction habitat waiting. Red-List categories need population data — data-deficient species wait in DD limbo. Nepal patterns need altitude-zone reading (tropical→alpine compression).

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

One-horned rhino (Chitwan recovery via anti-poaching + habitat) vs tiger (territory-huge, corridor-dependent). Endemics (spiny babbler — Nepal-only bird) carry maximum conservation weight per individual. Sacred groves and community forests show culture doing in-situ work.

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Classify a case: dwindling vulture populations (diclofenac poisoning) → identify driver (chemical/pollution + overexploitation-adjacent) → prescribe in-situ (ban drug, safe zones) + ex-situ (captive breeding) → monitor via Red-List criteria. Driver→strategy mapping is the answer template.

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Counting species undercounts microbes/fungi (the unknown majority). Ex-situ freezes evolution (no selection pressure). Parks displace people without buffer-zone justice. Red Lists lag behind field reality (assessment backlog).

7.
Label

Derivation

Text

From island biogeography: species richness balances immigration vs extinction — shrink/fragment habitat and extinction wins (species–area curve S = cA^z predicts the loss). Conservation follows: bigger + connected + representative reserves minimise the bleed; corridors restore the immigration term.

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

Levels chant: genetic–species–ecosystem. Loss: HIM-plus (habitat, invasives, misuse, pollution, climate). In-situ = in place (parks); ex-situ = out of place (zoos/banks). IUCN: CR–EN–VU in descending danger. Nepal examples: rhino, tiger, spiny babbler.
