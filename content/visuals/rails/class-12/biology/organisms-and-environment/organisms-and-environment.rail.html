Title

Energy flow and pyramids — why food chains stay short

Href

/class-12-notes/biology

Icon

Sigma

Stat Key

pyq:biology

Rows

1.
Label

Concept

Text

Ecosystems run on captured light: GPP fixes it, respiration spends it, NPP stores the surplus; decomposers close the loop. Only ~10% transfers per trophic level (Lindeman) — hence upright energy pyramids, short food chains and tiny top-predator populations. Populations grow exponentially until K caps them (logistic dN/dt = rN(1−N/K)); succession marches bare ground → pioneers → climax; C/N/P cycles shuttle the same atoms through living and non-living pools.

2.
Label

Formula

Kind

formula

Text

NPP = GPP − respiration · 10% law: ~10% per level (5 levels ⇒ 0.001% reaches top) · logistic: dN/dt = rN(1 − N/K) · pyramids: energy always upright; biomass/numbers can invert (pond/forest quirks) · succession: nudation → invasion → competition → climax

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

10% is an average (1–20% real range) for transfer EFFICIENCY between levels. Logistic growth needs density-dependent regulation (competition, predation, disease). Pyramid shape depends on currency (energy/mass/counts) — state which. Nutrient cycles need microbial transformers (nitrifiers, decomposers).

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Pond biomass pyramid inverts (tiny fast-dividing producers support heavier fish). Parasitic food chains invert numbers pyramids. Nitrogen cycle's exclusive microbial steps (fixation → ammonification → nitrification → denitrification) make fertilisers leaky. Lichens pioneer bare rock (weathering + organic build-up).

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Why can't food chains exceed 4–5 levels? Start 10,000 kJ at producers: 1,000 → 100 → 10 → 1 kJ by level five — insufficient to sustain a viable top population. The 10% compounding is the proof; inverted biomass pyramids survive only because turnover (rate), not standing crop, feeds the next level.

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

10% is descriptive, not lawlike — detritus chains and omnivory blur levels. Logistic K shifts with environment (not a constant). Succession-to-climax assumes stable climate (disturbance regimes rewrite endings). Cycles leak (N₂O emissions, P runoff) — human forcing breaks closure.

7.
Label

Derivation

Text

From thermodynamics: respiration + egestion dissipate ~90% as heat/waste per transfer (second law — energy quality degrades), leaving ~10% as production; iterate over levels for the pyramid shape. Logistic from exponential dN/dt = rN throttled by remaining capacity (1−N/K) — density dependence derived from competition.

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

Energy questions: 10% per level, count the transfers. Pyramid shape: ask the currency first (energy = always upright). Succession order chant: bare→pioneer→intermediate→climax. Cycles: name the microbial step (fixers, nitrifiers, decomposers).
