Concept

Text

Agriculture: Bt cotton carries bacterial cry genes (bollworm guts perforate on feeding); nematode-resistant tobacco silences parasite genes via RNAi. Medicine: recombinant insulin (1983 — first therapeutic, E. coli-assembled A+B chains), gene therapy (ADA deficiency: patient lymphocytes corrected ex vivo), therapeutic proteins (tPA, interferons, growth hormone). Transgenic animals model disease and manufacture drugs (Rosie cow's enriched milk). Biopiracy (patenting others' bioresources — neem, turmeric, basmati fights) closes with ethics and patents.

2.
Label

Formula

Kind

formula

Text

Bt: cryIAc/cryIIAb → gut-alkaline-activated toxin → pest death (plant-safe, human-safe) · RNAi: dsRNA → Dicer → siRNA → RISC silences nematode mRNA · insulin: proinsulin minus C-peptide, A+B chains + S–S bridges (E. coli) · ADA therapy: lymphocyte harvest → corrected gene → reinfuse (not permanent — stem-cell fix needed)

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Bt works on chewing pests ingesting toxin (sucking pests need other genes). RNAi needs sequence match to the target mRNA. Gene therapy needs ex-vivo cell survival + engraftment (lymphocytes die out — repeat doses). Patents need novelty + utility (traditional knowledge defeats biopiracy claims).

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Golden rice (β-carotene pathway) fights vitamin-A blindness. Flavr Savr tomato delayed softening (antisense polygalacturonase). Dolly (cloned sheep) proved nuclear transfer; ANDi (fluorescent monkey) proved germline transgenesis. Bt resistance management mandates refuge (non-Bt) acres.

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Why is recombinant insulin safer than pig insulin? Sequence-identical human protein (no allergy), unlimited fermenter supply (no slaughterhouse limits), no animal-pathogen risk. Production: synthesise A and B chain genes → express in E. coli → extract, join disulphides → purify — Eli Lilly's 1983 Humulin pipeline.

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Gene therapy is mostly somatic and transient (germline edits raise heritable-ethics walls). Bt selects resistant pests (refuge strategy slows, not stops). Transgene escape via pollen worries ecologists. Biopiracy battles show patents lag behind traditional knowledge documentation.

7.
Label

Derivation

Text

From tools to products: identify the gene (cry, insulin) → clone into a delivery vector (Ti plasmid for plants, retrovirus for ADA lymphocytes) → transform target → select expressers → scale (fields/fermenters/clinics). Each application reuses the principles-unit pipeline with a new cargo — the exam's favourite 'apply the pipeline' move.

Shortcut

Text

Agri examples: Bt cotton (insect), RNAi tobacco (nematode), Golden rice (vitamin). Medicine: insulin (diabetes), ADA (gene therapy), tPA (clots). Ethics: biopiracy = patenting others' biodiversity (neem/turmeric/basmati defences).
