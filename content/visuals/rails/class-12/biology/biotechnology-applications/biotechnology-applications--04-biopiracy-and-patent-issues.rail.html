Study notes

Text

A biopatent is a legal right granted over an invention: a new process, a modified organism, or a product. It lasts for a set time and stops others from using that invention commercially without permission. The right is given by a patent office after a claim is examined. · Biopiracy is the use of a community's biological resource or traditional knowledge for profit without permission, without naming the source, and without sharing the benefit. It is a taking. A patent is a grant. The two words are not synonyms. · Turmeric has been used in India for wound healing for a very long time. A US patent was nonetheless issued on that use. India's Council of Scientific and Industrial Research showed the use was already documented traditional knowledge, and the patent was revoked.
