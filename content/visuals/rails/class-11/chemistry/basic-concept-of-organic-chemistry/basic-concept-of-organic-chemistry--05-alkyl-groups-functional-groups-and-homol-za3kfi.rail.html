Text

Three words organise the families. An alkyl group (R–, CₙH₂ₙ₊₁) is an alkane minus one hydrogen — methyl, ethyl, propyl — the monovalent skeleton piece. A functional group (–OH, –CHO, >C=O, –COOH, –COOR, –X, –NH₂) is the reactive patch that decides the family's chemistry, since the C–C/C–H skeleton is inert.…

Solved

Text

The alkanol ladder — methanol, ethanol, propan-1-ol differ only by CH2 steps: boiling points climb 65 -> 78 -> 97 C while every member burns, oxidises and esterifies like an alcohol — gradation in physics, uniformity in chemistry.
