Catenation and functional groups — why carbon gets its own chemistry

Rows

1.
Label

Concept

Text

Carbon's tetra-covalency plus unmatched catenation (C–C chains/rings of any length) explodes into millions of compounds — hence a separate organic chemistry. Functional groups (−OH, −CHO, −COOH, −NH₂…) are the reactive addresses: same group, same chemistry, whatever the chain. Homologous series (CH₂ steps) make properties march in trends. Cracking splits heavy fractions, reforming reshapes them; octane number grades petrol knock-resistance (iso-octane = 100), cetane grades diesel ignition.

2.
Label

Formula

Kind

formula

Text

catenation: C–C ≈ 348 kJ/mol (strong enough to chain indefinitely) · homologues differ by –CH₂– · IUPAC reads: CH₃CH₂CH₂COOH = butanoic acid · CH₃CHO = ethanal · HOCH₂CH₂OH = ethane-1,2-diol · CH₃CH(CH₃)COOH = 2-methylpropanoic acid · octane number: iso-octane 100, n-heptane 0

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Functional-group behaviour transfers across homologues only when the group dominates reactivity (long chains add van-der-Waals noise). IUPAC naming here caps at short chains — longest carbon backbone carrying the principal group wins. Octane/cetane scales compare against fixed reference fuels under standard test engines.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Branched isomers share formulas but split properties (branching lowers boiling point). Bond-line formulas hide C/H (vertices = carbon). Tetraethyl lead raised octane historically — phased out for lead toxicity; oxygenates (MTBE/ethanol) replaced it. Cetane is diesel's mirror scale (ignition quality, not knock).

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Name CH₃CH₂CH₂COOH: 4-carbon chain with terminal −COOH → butanoic acid; CH₃CHO: 2 carbons, aldehyde → ethanal; HOCH₂CH₂OH: diol → ethane-1,2-diol; CH₃CH(CH₃)COOH: 3-carbon acid backbone + methyl at C2 → 2-methylpropanoic acid (NEB 2024, 3 marks — suffix picks the principal group).

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Catenation is carbon's superlative, not monopoly (Si–Si chains exist but hydrolyse). Functional-group transferability breaks with conjugation/neighbouring groups. Octane number predicts knock, not energy content or emissions.

7.
Label

Derivation

Text

Why separate study? Count: tetravalent carbon × chain/branch/ring/finite functional permutations → combinatorial explosion (millions known) vs thousands of inorganics. Classification tames it: hydrocarbons vs derivatives; open/acyclic vs cyclic; then functional-group families, each with one characteristic reaction set.

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

IUPAC: find principal group → longest chain holding it → number to give it lowest locant → name substituents alphabetically. Suffix decides family (−oic acid beats −al beats −ol). Octane = petrol knock scale; cetane = diesel ignition scale — never swap them.

9.
Label

Board question

Text

NEB 2024 (3): IUPAC names — butanoic acid, ethanal, ethane-1,2-diol, 2-methylpropanoic acid. NEB 2023 (2): long branched alcohol IUPAC. Catenation reasons + octane/cetane meanings rotate yearly.
