1. Topic Introduction — A Family Tree for Millions With tens of millions of organic compounds, chemistry would be impossible without classification. The tree has two great branches — open-chain (acyclic/aliphatic) and closed-chain (cyclic) — and the cyclic branch splits into homocyclic (alicyclic + aromatic) and heterocyclic. Learn the tree once and every new compound has an address. · ## 2. Fundamental Concepts ### 2.1 Open-chain (acyclic / aliphatic) compounds - Chains of carbon atoms, straight or branched, no ring. - Saturated (alkanes): only single bonds — CH₄, C₂H₆, C₃H₈. - Unsaturated: at least one multiple bond — alkenes (C=C, ethene CH₂=CH₂), alkynes (C≡C, ethyne HC≡CH). ### 2.2 Closed-chain (cyclic) compounds - Homocyclic: ring made only of carbon atoms.…
