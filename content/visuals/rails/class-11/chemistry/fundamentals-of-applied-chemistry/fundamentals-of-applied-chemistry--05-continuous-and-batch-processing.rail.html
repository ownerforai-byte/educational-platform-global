Batch and continuous are the two architectures of chemical production: a recipe followed in time versus an equilibrium held in space. Batch wins flexibility, low capital, and validation-friendly lots (pharma, food, dyes); continuous wins scale economics, consistency and low labour (ammonia, acids, petrochemicals) — because high fixed costs demand unbroken tonnage.…
