Label

Conditions

Text

❌ Equal volumes of any two gases have equal masses. ✅ They have equal numbers of molecules; masses differ because molecular masses differ. · ❌ Vapour density is the same as density. ✅ Vapour density is a relative quantity measured with respect to hydrogen (or air); density is an absolute mass per unit volume.

Solved

Text

Problem: VD of a gas is 22; find its molar mass and the mass of 5.6 L at STP. Solution: M = 2 × 22 = 44 g mol⁻¹; n = 5.6/22.4 = 0.25 mol → mass = 0.25 × 44 = 11 g. · Problem: Calculate the number of molecules in 11.2 L of CH₄ at STP and the number of hydrogen atoms present. Solution: n = 11.2/22.4 = 0.5 mol → molecules = 0.5 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 3.011 × 10²³; H atoms = 4 × that = 1.204 × 10²⁴.
