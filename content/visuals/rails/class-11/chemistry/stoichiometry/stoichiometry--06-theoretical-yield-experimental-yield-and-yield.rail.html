Label

Solved

Text

Problem: 100 g CaCO₃ gives 47.6 g CaO. Find the % yield. Solution: n(CaCO₃) = 1 mol → theoretical CaO = 56 g; % yield = 47.6/56 × 100 = 85%. · Problem: 7.0 g of ethene (0.25 mol) is hydrated to give 9.2 g ethanol. Find the % yield. Solution: theoretical ethanol = 0.25 × 46 = 11.5 g; % yield = 9.2/11.5 × 100 = 80%.

2. Why the actual yield is lower than the theoretical yield · • The reaction is incomplete — the limiting reactant is not fully consumed in the time allowed. · • The reaction is reversible, so it stops at an equilibrium position (this is exactly the case in the Haber process).

Label

More ideas

Text

❌ The theoretical yield is what you actually get. ✅ It is the maximum possible amount; the actual yield is always lower for practical reasons. · ❌ Yield and atom economy measure the same thing. ✅ Yield is about recovery of product; atom economy is about how much of the reactant mass ends up in the desired product. · ❌ Yield can be predicted by stoichiometry alone. ✅ Stoichiometry gives only the theoretical maximum; the actual yield depends on kinetics, equilibrium and technique.
