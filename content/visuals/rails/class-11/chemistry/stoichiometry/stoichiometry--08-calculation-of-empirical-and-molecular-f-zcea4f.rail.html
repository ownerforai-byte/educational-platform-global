Percentage composition = element mass ÷ molar mass × 100, always summing to 100: H₂SO₄ (2.04/32.65/65.31), urea N 46.67% (the bag proved), NH₄NO₃ N 35%, CaCO₃ Ca 40%. Backward: % → grams → moles → ratio → empirical (CH₂O); with M_r (often 2×VD): n = M_r/E_r → molecular (C₆H₁₂O₆; M_r 90 → C₃H₆O₃). Combustion analysis gives %C (×12/44) and %H (×2/18), O by difference.…
