Text

The empirical formula gives the simplest whole-number atom ratio; the molecular formula gives the true atom count and equals the empirical formula multiplied by n = molecular mass ÷ empirical mass. The four-step ritual — assume 100 g, convert to moles, divide by the smallest, clear fractions (0.5→×2, 0.33→×3, 0.25→×4) — turns percentage composition into the empirical formula: 40% C, 6.67% H, 53.33% O → CH₂O…

Label

Conditions

Text

Empirical mass vs molecular mass: empirical mass = mass of the simplest unit (CH₂O = 30); molecular mass = mass of the true molecule (C₆H₁₂O₆ = 180). n = 180/30 = 6. · Ratio vs substance: CH₂O is the empirical formula of glucose, formaldehyde AND acetic acid — the empirical formula alone cannot identify a compound.

Board question

Text

NEB 2022/2020 (3m): A compound contains 40% C, 6.67% H and 53.33% O by mass. Calculate its empirical formula. — Per 100 g: C = 40/12 = 3.33 mol; H = 6.67/1 = 6.67 mol; O = 53.33/16 = 3.33 mol. Divide by 3.33 → C : H : O = 1 : 2 : 1 → CH₂O. · NEB 2024 (2m): A compound has empirical formula CH₂O and molar mass 180 g/mol. Find its molecular formula. — Empirical mass = 12 + 2 + 16 = 30; n = 180/30 = 6; molecular formula = C₆H₁₂O₆ (glucose).
