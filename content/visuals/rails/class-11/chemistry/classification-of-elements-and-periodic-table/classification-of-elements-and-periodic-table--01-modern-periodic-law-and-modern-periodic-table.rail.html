Text

• Dobereiner's Law of Triads (1829): Grouped elements into triads where atomic mass of middle element was roughly arithmetic mean of first and third (e.g., Li 7, Na 23, K 39; Ca 40, Sr 88, Ba 137; Cl 35.5, Br 80, I 127). Failed because only a few triads could be identified. · • Newlands' Law of Octaves (1865): When elements are arranged in order of increasing atomic mass, every eighth element shows similar physical and chemical properties like musical notes (Sa, Re, Ga, Ma...). Failed after Calcium (valid only up to Ca, Z=20) and could not accommodate noble gases discovered later.

Moseley's Experiment and Modern Periodic Law (1913) · • English physicist Henry Moseley bombarded various metal anticathodes with high-speed cathode rays (electrons) and studied characteristic X-ray emission spectra.

Label

Text

• Moseley's Law: The square root of the frequency (√ν) of the characteristic X-ray spectral line is directly proportional to the atomic number (Z) of the target element: · ν​=a(Z−b) · where ν = frequency of K_α X-ray line, Z = atomic number (nuclear charge), a and b are constants characteristic of the spectral line series (screening constant b ≈ 1 for K-series).

• Fundamental Conclusion: Plot of √ν vs Z yields a straight line, whereas plot of √ν vs atomic mass (A) does not. This conclusively proved that atomic number (Z), representing the net positive nuclear charge (number of protons), is the fundamental property governing the chemical identity of elements, NOT… · • Statement of Modern Periodic Law: 'The physical and chemical properties of elements are periodic functions of their atomic numbers.' · ### 3. Resolution of Mendeleev's Anomalies by Modern Periodic Table

• Anomalous Pairs Resolved: Argon (Z=18) naturally precedes Potassium (Z=19); Cobalt (Z=27) naturally precedes Nickel (Z=28); Tellurium (Z=52) naturally precedes Iodine (Z=53) purely by increasing atomic numbers, regardless of atomic weights. · • Position of Isotopes: Since isotopes share identical atomic numbers (same Z, different mass number A), they occupy one single position in the periodic table without conflict. · • Scientific Division into Blocks: Reflects filling of electron shells according to quantum mechanics (Aufbau principle, Pauli exclusion, Hund's rule).
