Solved

Text

Problem: Arrange Mg²⁺, Na⁺, F⁻, O²⁻ in decreasing size. Solution: they are isoelectronic (10 electrons), so the smallest nuclear charge gives the largest radius → O²⁻ (Z = 8) > F⁻ (9) > Na⁺ (11) > Mg²⁺ (12). · Problem: Which has the higher first ionisation energy, Be or B? Solution: Be (899 kJ mol⁻¹) > B (801 kJ mol⁻¹), since Be's 2s² is a filled subshell and its electron penetrates closer to the nucleus than B's 2p electron.

1. Meaning of periodicity · Periodicity is the recurrence of similar physical and chemical properties at regular intervals when elements are arranged in order of atomic number. Its cause is the periodic repetition of the valence-shell electronic configuration: trends across a period follow from increasing Z_eff, trends down a group from increasing n and increasing shielding.

More ideas

Text

• A cation is smaller than its parent atom (Na 186 pm → Na⁺ 95 pm); an anion is larger (Cl 99 pm → Cl⁻ 181 pm) because added electrons increase repulsion while the nuclear charge is unchanged. · • Down a group the ionic radius increases for the same charge (Li⁺ < Na⁺ < K⁺ < Rb⁺ < Cs⁺); across a period ions of the same charge get smaller. · • Isoelectronic series (same number of electrons): radius decreases as nuclear charge increases, N³⁻ > O²⁻ > F⁻ > Ne > Na⁺ > Mg²⁺ > Al³⁺; for one element Fe³⁺ < Fe²⁺.
