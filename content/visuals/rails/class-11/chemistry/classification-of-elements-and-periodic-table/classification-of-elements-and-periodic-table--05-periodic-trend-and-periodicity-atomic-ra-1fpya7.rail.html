6. Common Confusions Radius dip exceptions: noble-gas radii are van der Waals radii — larger than the neighbouring halogen's covalent radius; do not read them as a trend break in bonding radius. · IE dips are about subshell stability: Be→B is s² vs p¹ (energy level); N→O is half-filled vs paired (repulsion). Two different causes — quote the right one for the right pair. · EA vs EN: EA is measurable energy for an isolated atom; EN is a relative tendency of a bonded atom. Cl has the most negative EA but F has the highest EN.
