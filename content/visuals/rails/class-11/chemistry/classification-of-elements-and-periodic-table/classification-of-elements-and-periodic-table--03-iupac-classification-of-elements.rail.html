1. Meaning — what IUPAC is and what it decides · IUPAC (International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry) is the world authority that standardises chemical nomenclature, atomic weights and the format of the periodic table. Its classification of elements covers group numbering 1–18, the s/p/d/f block division, the metal–nonmetal–metalloid grouping, collective group names, and the naming of newly discovered elements.
