3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Z vs Z_eff: Z is the total proton count and never changes with position; Z_eff is what a particular electron actually feels — quoting one where the other is asked loses the mark. · Same-shell shielding is weak (0.35): that is the entire reason Z_eff rises across a period; treating all shielding as equal makes the trend disappear.

6. Common Confusions Z_eff is electron-specific: the inner 1s electrons of Na feel Z_eff ≈ +10.9, not +1 — always say which electron you are computing for. · Down a group Z grows but Z_eff does not: the new shell's shielding cancels the extra protons; students who quote "Z increases so pull increases down a group" get the trend backwards. · Shielding vs penetration: shielding is what other electrons do to you; penetration is how close you get despite them. s penetrates best and is least shielded.
