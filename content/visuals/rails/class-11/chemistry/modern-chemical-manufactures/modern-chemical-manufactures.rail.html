Title

Haber process — why high pressure makes ammonia

Href

Stat Key

pyq:chemistry

Rows

1.
Label

Concept

Text

N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ is exothermic with 4 gas moles collapsing to 2 — so high pressure (fewer moles) and low temperature (exothermic) favour yield, but low T starves the rate: industry compromises at ~450 °C, ~200 atm, iron catalyst. The same Le Chatelier logic designs Ostwald (Pt-catalysed NH₃ oxidation to HNO₃), Contact (V₂O₅, SO₂→SO₃→oleum→H₂SO₄), Solvay (NaCl + NH₃ + CO₂ → NaHCO₃) and urea (NH₃ + CO₂) plants — flow sheets included.

2.
Label

Formula

Kind

formula

Text

Haber: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃, ΔH = −92 kJ/mol · ~450 °C, ~200 atm, Fe/Mo catalyst · Ostwald: 4NH₃ + 5O₂ →(Pt) 4NO → NO₂ → HNO₃ · Contact: 2SO₂ + O₂ →(V₂O₅) 2SO₃; oleum H₂S₂O₇ + H₂O → 2H₂SO₄ · Solvay: NaCl + NH₃ + CO₂ + H₂O → NaHCO₃↓ + NH₄Cl

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Haber pressure must pay for itself (compression cost vs yield gain — the economic optimum, not the chemical maximum). Iron catalyst needs pure feed (S/CO poison it). Ostwald's Pt gauze needs ~900 °C; Contact's V₂O₅ window is ~450 °C; Solvay recycles its NH₃ (ammonia-soda loop) or the economics collapse.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Hard-water softening with washing soda: Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺ precipitate as carbonates — temporary AND permanent hardness both yield (NEB 2023, 4 marks with reactions). Urea (NH₂CONH₂, 46% N) is the top-N fertiliser, made from NH₃ + CO₂. Diaphragm cells keep Cl₂ and NaOH apart to stop back-reaction.

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Why high pressure for Haber? 4 gaseous moles → 2: Le Chatelier pushes right as P rises — yield climbs steeply to ~200 atm, then compression costs outrun gains (NEB 2024, 5 marks: principle + flow-sheet description + the pressure economics, plus why ~450 °C compromises rate against yield).

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Single-pass conversion is only ~15% — unreacted N₂/H₂ must be recycled (flow sheet's loop). Catalysts age (poisoning, sintering). Every process pays an environmental bill: NOₓ/N₂O emissions, acidic effluent, diaphragm-cell mercury legacy (membrane cells replace it).

7.
Label

Derivation

Text

From equilibrium: Kp for ammonia synthesis falls with T (exothermic), so low T favours product — but Arrhenius starves the rate; the 450 °C compromise maximises rate×yield economics. Pressure: Qp vs Kp analysis shows conversion rising with P^(−2) dependence — the quantitative spine behind 'high pressure'.

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

Haber numbers: 450 °C, 200 atm, Fe — chant them. Every manufacture answer: principle (equilibrium/redox) + conditions + flow sheet + catalyst + uses. Hardness removal: washing soda precipitates Ca/Mg carbonates — write both reactions.

9.
Label

Board question

Text

NEB 2024 (5): Haber process + why high pressure. NEB 2023 (4): hard water — temporary/permanent removal with washing soda + reactions. Ostwald/Contact/Solvay principles with flow sheets rotate yearly.
