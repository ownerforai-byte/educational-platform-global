Unit Title

Hydrocarbons

Syllabus Topics

  • Saturated hydrocarbons (Alkanes): preparation from haloalkanes (reduction and Wurtz reaction), decarboxylation, catalytic hydrogenation of alkene and alkyne
  • Chemical properties of alkanes: substitution reactions (halogenation, nitration and sulphonation only), oxidation of ethane
  • Unsaturated hydrocarbons (Alkenes): preparation by dehydration of alcohol, dehydrohalogenation, catalytic hydrogenation of alkyne
  • Chemical properties of alkenes: addition reaction with HX (Markovnikov's addition and peroxide effect), H2O, O3, H2SO4 only
  • Alkynes: preparation from carbon and hydrogen, 1,2-dibromoethane, chloroform/iodoform only
  • Chemical properties of alkynes: addition reaction with H2, HX, H2O; acidic nature (action with sodium, ammoniacal AgNO3 and ammoniacal Cu2Cl2)
  • Test of unsaturation (ethene and ethyne): bromine water test and Baeyer's test
  • Comparative studies of physical properties of alkane, alkene and alkyne
  • Kolbe's electrolysis methods for the preparation of alkanes, alkenes and alkynes

Rows

1.
Label

Concept

Text

Alkanes substitute (free-radical halogenation), alkenes/alkynes ADD across the π bond. Markovnikov: the H of HX lands on the already H-rich carbon, via the more stable carbocation (3° > 2° > 1°) — peroxides flip it for HBr only (Kharasch/anti-Markovnikov). Terminal alkynes are weakly acidic (≡C–H + Na/ammoniacal Ag⁺/Cu⁺ salts). Brown bromine water and purple Baeyer's reagent both bleach at unsaturation.

2.
Label

Formula

Kind

formula

Text

Markovnikov: H→H-rich C, X→H-poor C (stable carbocation path) · peroxide effect: HBr ONLY, reversed · ethene + H₂/Ni → ethane · but-2-ene + O₃/Zn,H₂O → 2 CH₃CHO (double bond location proof) · acetylene + 2[Ag(NH₃)₂]⁺ → Ag–C≡C–Ag↓ (white) + …

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Markovnikov needs an unsymmetrical alkene + ionic (non-peroxide) HX addition. Peroxide reversal is HBr-specific (HCl/HI stay Markovnikov — bond-strength energetics). Catalytic hydrogenation needs Ni/Pt/Pd surface; Baeyer's stays alkaline and cold or it over-oxidises.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Ethene → ethane by H₂/Ni (NEB 2023, mechanism = surface adsorption, syn-addition). But-2-ene ozonolysis gives 2 ethanal — cleavage products map the old double bond (NEB 2022). Wurtz couples 2RX + 2Na → R–R (symmetrical alkanes best); Kolbe electrolyses RCOONa → R–R at the anode.

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Ethane from ethene: CH₂=CH₂ + H₂ →(Ni, heat) CH₃–CH₃ — H₂ adsorbs dissociatively on nickel, both H add syn to the π bond (NEB 2023, 3 marks). Ozonolysis proof: but-2-ene CH₃CH=CHCH₃ cleaves to 2×CH₃CHO; counting carbonyl fragments reconstructs the alkene skeleton (NEB 2022, 3 marks).

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Markovnikov predicts the MAJOR product — rearrangements (hydride/methyl shifts to stabler carbocations) rewrite outcomes. Free-radical halogenation of alkanes gives mixtures (selectivity 3° > 2° > 1° only biases). Wurtz fails for mixed R–R′ (statistical trio) and bulky halides (elimination wins).

7.
Label

Derivation

Text

HX adds via protonation: H⁺ attacks the π bond forming the more substituted carbocation (hyperconjugation + inductive stabilisation scale with alkyl count), X⁻ then captures it — product distribution follows carbocation stability, hence Markovnikov. Peroxide flips the FIRST step to Br· radical addition, whose stability order reverses the outcome for HBr.

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

'Addition of HX' → check symmetry first (symmetrical = one product, rule moot). Ozonolysis fragments: join carbonyl carbons back with C=C. Unsaturation tests: Br₂ water (brown→colourless) or Baeyer's (purple→brown MnO₂) — either proves π bonds.

9.
Label

Board question

Text

NEB 2023 (3): ethane from ethene + mechanism. NEB 2022 (3): but-2-ene ozonolysis — equations + products. Markovnikov/peroxide statements and alkyne acidity tests rotate yearly.
