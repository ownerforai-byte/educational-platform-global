6. Common Confusions The molecule does not oscillate between forms: resonance is a static averaging — the hybrid exists all the time; the canonical forms are bookkeeping, never real intermediates. · Atoms never move in resonance: only π electrons and lone pairs shift; any "structure" with a relocated H or O is a different molecule (a tautomer), not a resonance form. · More forms ≠ more stability automatically: quality (octets, charge placement) matters; equivalent forms contribute equally regardless of count.
