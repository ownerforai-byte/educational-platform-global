Common Confusions Electron geometry ≠ molecular shape: NH₃'s electron geometry is tetrahedral but its shape is pyramidal — the shape name counts atoms only. · Multiple bonds count once: CO₂ has two domains (not four) — each double bond is one region of electron density. · H₂S is not "mini-water": same domain count, but 92° vs 104.5° — larger, more diffuse lone pairs on sulphur and longer S–H bonds change the balance; never copy water's angle.
