6. Common Confusions Polar bonds ≠ polar molecule: CO₂ and CCl₄ have polar bonds but zero net moment — symmetry cancels. Polarity of a molecule is a vector question, not a bond question. · Dipole direction convention: the chemists' arrow points from positive to negative (crossed tail at δ⁺); physics defines the vector the opposite way. Quote μ magnitudes, not directions, unless the convention is stated. · Ionic character is not all-or-nothing: HF is 43% ionic and 57% covalent simultaneously — real bonds are mixtures, which is why Fajan's rules (topic 28) modulate ionic compounds toward covalency.
