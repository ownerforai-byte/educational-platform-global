6. Common Confusions The cations do not move during conduction: only the electron sea flows; the lattice stays fixed. (In electrolysis the ions move — different process.) · Metallic bonds are not covalent: no localized shared pairs between specific atoms — the electrons are collective property of the crystal. · Low mp metals exist: Na (98 °C), Hg (liquid) — "metals have high mp" is a trend, not a law; the trend tracks valence electrons and cation charge.
