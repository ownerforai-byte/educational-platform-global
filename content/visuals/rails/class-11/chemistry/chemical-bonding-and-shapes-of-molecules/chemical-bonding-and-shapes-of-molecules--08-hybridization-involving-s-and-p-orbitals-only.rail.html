6. Common Confusions Hybridisation is per-bonding-event, not a free-atom property: an isolated carbon does not "contain" sp³ orbitals; they exist in the molecule, formed as bonds form. · Lone pairs occupy hybrids too: NH₃ and H₂O are sp³ — the lone pairs sit in sp³ hybrids; that is why their angles are near-tetrahedral rather than 90°. · More p-character ≠ stronger bond: s-character strengthens (closer to nucleus) — sp bonds beat sp³ bonds in strength and shortness.
