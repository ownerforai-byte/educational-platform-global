1. Meaning of an orbital · An orbital is the three-dimensional region around the nucleus in which the probability of finding the electron is maximum (the ~90–95% boundary-surface region) for a given energy state. It comes from the wave function ψ of the Schrödinger equation, and |ψ|² is the probability density. An orbital has no sharp boundary and does not describe a path.

2. Orbital vs orbit · • Orbit (Bohr): fixed two-dimensional circular path with definite radius and energy. · • Orbital (quantum mechanics): three-dimensional probability distribution — spherical (s) or directional (p, d) — with no definite path and no sharp edge.
