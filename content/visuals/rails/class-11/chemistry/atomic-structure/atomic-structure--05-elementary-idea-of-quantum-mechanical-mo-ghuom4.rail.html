Derivation

Text

1. Meaning — dual nature of matter · Light behaves both as a wave and as a stream of particles (photons). In 1924 Louis de Broglie proposed the converse for matter: every moving material particle has a wave associated with it. These waves are called matter waves or de Broglie waves, and the hypothesis is the bridge from Bohr's orbits to the quantum-mechanical model.
