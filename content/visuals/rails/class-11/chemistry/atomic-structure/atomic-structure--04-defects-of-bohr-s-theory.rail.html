Label

Solved

Text

Problem: Why is the ground-state radius of He⁺ half that of hydrogen? Solution: r₁ = 0.529 n²/Z Å = 0.529/2 = 0.2645 Å — the formula works because He⁺ has one electron, and fails for neutral helium. · Problem: Show a Bohr orbit violates the uncertainty principle for H (n = 1). Solution: a fixed r gives Δx ≈ 0 and a definite speed gives Δp ≈ 0, so Δx·Δp → 0 < h/4π — impossible; hence the orbit is not a real trajectory.
