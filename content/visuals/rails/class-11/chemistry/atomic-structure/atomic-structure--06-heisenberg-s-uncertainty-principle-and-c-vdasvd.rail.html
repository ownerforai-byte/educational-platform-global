Solved

Text

Problem: Minimum velocity uncertainty for an electron localised within 10⁻¹⁰ m. Solution: Δv ≥ h/(4πmΔx) = 6.626×10⁻³⁴/(4π × 9.11×10⁻³¹ × 10⁻¹⁰) ≈ 5.8 × 10⁵ m s⁻¹ — comparable to the electron's own speed, so the orbit picture collapses. · Problem: Can an electron be confined in a nucleus of radius 10⁻¹⁵ m? Solution: Δp ≈ h/(4πΔx) ≈ 5.3 × 10⁻²⁰ kg m s⁻¹ → KE ≈ (Δp)²/2m ≈ 10¹⁰ eV, far beyond nuclear binding energies; β-particles are therefore created during decay, not stored in the nucleus.

1. Meaning and statement · Werner Heisenberg (1927) showed that for a microscopic particle it is impossible to determine simultaneously and exactly both its position and its momentum: Δx⋅Δp≥4πh​, or Δx⋅Δv≥4πmh​. Other conjugate pairs obey the same pattern: ΔE⋅Δt≥4πh​ and ΔL⋅Δθ≥4πh​.
