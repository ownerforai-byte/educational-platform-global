Text

Heisenberg's uncertainty principle: Δx·Δp_x ≥ h/4π = ħ/2 (position-momentum); ΔE·Δt ≥ ħ/2 (energy-time); ΔL_x·ΔL_y ≥ ħ|⟨L_z⟩|/2 (angular momentum). Fundamental from [x,p]=iħ, not instrumental. Bohr orbit: exact r,v → Δx·Δp=0 forbidden; n=1 gives Δp/p≈50%. Energy-time: ΔE·Δt≥ħ/2 → finite τ gives ΔE=ħ/τ (natural line width, Lorentzian). Electron in nucleus: Δx=1 fm → K≥9.5 MeV → impossible (atomic eV vs nuclear MeV).…
