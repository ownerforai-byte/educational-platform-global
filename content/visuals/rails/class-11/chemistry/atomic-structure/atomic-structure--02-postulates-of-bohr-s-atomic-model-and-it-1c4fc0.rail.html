1. Topic Introduction — Quantisation Saves the Atom In 1913 Niels Bohr repaired Rutherford's model by adding one idea: the electron's angular momentum is quantised. Only certain orbits exist, no radiation is emitted inside them, and light appears only when the electron jumps between orbits. The model works exactly for hydrogen and one-electron ions (He⁺, Li²⁺). · ## 2. Fundamental Concepts ### 2.1 The postulates (state in order — NEB 3m) 1.
