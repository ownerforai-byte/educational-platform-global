Text

Orbit vs jump: energy is possessed in an orbit but radiated only during a jump between orbits — the orbit itself emits nothing. · n₁ vs n₂: in the Rydberg equation n₁ is the lower (final) level and n₂ the upper (initial) level; reversing them gives a negative result.

More ideas

Text

Balmer vs Lyman direction: Lyman needs jumps down to n = 1 (from any higher level); Balmer needs jumps down to n = 2. A 3 → 2 jump is Balmer even though it starts at n = 3. · Rydberg sign convention: 1/λ = R(1/n₁² − 1/n₂²) with n₁ the lower level; writing the terms the other way gives a negative 1/λ — physically meaningless. · ## 7. Exam Application NEB-style (2m): Why does hydrogen produce a line spectrum rather than a continuous spectrum? Because Bohr orbits are quantised and stationary: radiation is emitted only in jumps between fixed levels, so each photon has one definite energy — discrete lines, not a continuum.
