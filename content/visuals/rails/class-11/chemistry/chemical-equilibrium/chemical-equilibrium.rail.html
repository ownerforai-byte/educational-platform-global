Le Chatelier — the stressed equilibrium fights back

Stat Key

pyq:chemistry

Rows

1.
Label

Concept

Text

Equilibrium is DYNAMIC — forward and reverse reactions race on at equal speed, so concentrations freeze while molecules never rest. The mass-action ratio at that freeze is K (Kc in concentrations, Kp in partial pressures). Le Chatelier predicts the pushback: disturb the system (concentration, pressure, temperature) and it shifts to undo the disturbance.

2.
Label

Formula

Kind

formula

Text

Kc = [products]^p/[reactants]^r (equilibrium only!) · Kp = Kc·(RT)^Δn · N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃: Kc = [NH₃]²/([N₂][H₂]³); Kc = 0.5 with [N₂] = 1.0, [H₂] = 0.5 → [NH₃] = √(0.5×1×0.125) = 0.25 M

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

K is constant at CONSTANT temperature only — a new T means a new K. Pure solids/liquids vanish from K expressions (activity 1). Le Chatelier predicts DIRECTION, never speed (catalysts: no shift, faster arrival). Q vs K decides the shift: Q < K moves right.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ (4 gas moles → 2): raising pressure shifts RIGHT toward fewer moles (NEB 2024). N₂O₄ ⇌ 2NO₂ (ΔH = +57 kJ): heating shifts right (endothermic direction), cooling left (NEB 2022). Catalyst: reaches the same K faster — position untouched.

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Kc = 0.5 for ammonia synthesis, [N₂] = 1.0 M, [H₂] = 0.5 M at equilibrium: 0.5 = [NH₃]²/(1.0 × 0.125) → [NH₃]² = 0.0625 → [NH₃] = 0.25 M (NEB 2024, 3-mark pattern). Exponents mirror coefficients — the commonest mark-trap.

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Le Chatelier is qualitative and occasionally fooled (complex multi-equilibrium systems, inert-gas addition at constant volume does nothing). K says nothing about HOW FAST (diamond is 'unstable' to graphite forever). Pressure shifts need Δn(gas) ≠ 0 — equal-mole reactions ignore pressure.

7.
Label

Derivation

Text

At equilibrium forward rate = reverse rate: kf[A]^a[B]^b = kr[C]^c[D]^d → K = kf/kr = products/reactants — the mass-action expression falls out of kinetics. Gas form: substitute Pᵢ = [i]RT per species → Kp = Kc(RT)^Δn with Δn = (product − reactant) gas moles.

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

Shift checklist: concentration (add → consume it), pressure (toward fewer gas moles), temperature (toward the heat-absorbing side), catalyst (no effect). Δn first, arrows second. Kc expressions: coefficients become exponents, solids/liquids deleted.

9.
Label

Board question

Text

NEB 2024 (3): Le Chatelier + pressure effect on N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃. NEB 2024 (3): Kc = 0.5 numerical above. NEB 2022 (3): N₂O₄ ⇌ 2NO₂ (ΔH = +57 kJ) — T/P/concentration effects.
