Gas equilibria speak two dialects — concentrations and partial pressures — and Kp = Kc(RT)^Δn is the dictionary. The derivation is one ideal-gas line: p = [i]RT turns every pressure into a concentration, and collecting the leftover RT factors leaves (RT) raised to Δn, the change in gaseous moles between products and reactants.…
