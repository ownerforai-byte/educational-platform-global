Quick concepts

Text

Two poisoning strategies: soft metals (Hg, Pb, Cd, As(III)) bind the soft sulphur of cysteine and disable enzymes wholesale, while mimicry (Pb2+ as Ca2+, Cd2+ as Zn2+) lets them sneak into the wrong seats — and chelation therapy wins by out-gripping both.
