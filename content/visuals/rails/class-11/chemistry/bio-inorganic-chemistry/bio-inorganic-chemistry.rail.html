Title

Metal ions in life — Mg in leaves, Fe in blood

Href

Rows

1.
Label

Concept

Text

Life runs on metal ions: bulk Na⁺/K⁺/Mg²⁺/Ca²⁺ move fluids, fire nerves and build bones, while trace Fe/Cu/Zn/Ni/Co/Cr catalyse enzymes in milligrams. Chlorophyll is a magnesium porphyrin — no Mg, no photosynthesis. Haemoglobin is an iron porphyrin — no Fe, no oxygen transport. The Na⁺/K⁺ pump burns ATP to hold the gradients nerves fire on; the same metals turn toxic in excess (Pb, Hg, Cd, As) or even iron overload.

2.
Label

Formula

Kind

formula

Text

chlorophyll: Mg-centred chlorin (C₅₅H₇₂MgN₄O₅) — Mg deficiency → chlorosis (yellow leaves) · haem: Fe²⁺ porphyrin ×4 in haemoglobin; Fe deficiency → anaemia · Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase: 3 Na⁺ out, 2 K⁺ in per ATP · trace MCQ answer: iron builds haemoglobin (NEB 2022)

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Essentiality is dose-shaped: deficiency cripples, excess poisons (same element, U-shaped curve). Chlorophyll needs Mg specifically — other M²⁺ will not substitute in the ring. Pump stoichiometry (3:2) holds per ATP hydrolysed under physiological conditions.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Mg-deficient soil → yellow (chlorotic) leaves, stunted photosynthesis (NEB 2023). Cobalt centres vitamin B₁₂; zinc fingers grip DNA; copper rides in cytochrome oxidase and haemocyanin. Arsenic groundwater and Minamata mercury show the toxic end of the same periodic table.

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Chlorophyll formula centres on Mg²⁺ in a chlorin ring — magnesium harvests light energy into the photosystems; deficiency yellows leaves and stalls growth, corrected by Mg fertilisers like Epsom salt (NEB 2023, 3 marks). The haemoglobin MCQ resolves the same way: iron is the O₂-binding trace metal (NEB 2022).

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

One ion, many jobs — assigning single functions oversimplifies (Ca²⁺ signals, structures AND clots). Soil/plant/animal transfer chains complicate deficiency diagnosis. Toxicity thresholds vary by species, age and chemical form (methylmercury ≫ elemental Hg).

7.
Label

Derivation

Text

Why Mg for chlorophyll? Its size and +2 charge fit the chlorin cavity and tune the ring's absorption into the red/blue peaks — photochemistry follows from the metal–ligand electronics. Why Fe for blood? Fe²⁺ binds O₂ reversibly (oxy/deoxy flip) without oxidising — the porphyrin cage protects the +2 state that free Fe²⁺ would lose.

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

Green plant pigment → Mg; red blood pigment → Fe; nerve gradients → Na⁺/K⁺ pump (3-out-2-in); bones/teeth → Ca/P. Deficiency questions: match the metal to its flagship molecule and the symptom writes itself.

9.
Label

Board question

Text

NEB 2023 (3): chlorophyll formula + Mg's role + deficiency effect. NEB 2022 (1): trace element for haemoglobin — iron (MCQ). Ion-pump stoichiometry and metal-toxicity pairs rotate yearly.
