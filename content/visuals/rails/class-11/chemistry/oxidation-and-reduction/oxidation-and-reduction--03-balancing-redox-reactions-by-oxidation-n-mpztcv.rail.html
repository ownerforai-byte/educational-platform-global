Conditions

Text

Balance O with H₂O and H with H⁺ — in that order: reversing the sequence scrambles ionic equations; charge (electrons) always comes last. · Equivalent mass is reaction-specific: K₂Cr₂O₇ is 49 as an oxidant but different in other roles — always derive n from the actual half-reaction quoted.

11.
Label

Quick concepts

Text

Both methods are the same conservation law in two costumes: electrons (or oxidation-number units) lost must equal gained. The ion-electron half-reactions reappear verbatim as electrode reactions in electrolysis and cells — learn them once, use them thrice.
