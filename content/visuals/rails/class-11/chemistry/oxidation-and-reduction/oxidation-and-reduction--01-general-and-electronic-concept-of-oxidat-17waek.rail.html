Text

Redox is electron bookkeeping: oxidation is loss, reduction is gain (OIL RIG), and because electrons are conserved the two always run together — one species climbs in oxidation number while another falls. The oxidising agent accepts electrons and is itself reduced (KMnO₄, K₂Cr₂O₇, O₂, F₂); the reducing agent donates them and is itself oxidised (Zn, H₂, CO, C) — role and fate are always opposite.…

6. Common Confusions The agent does the opposite of its name: an oxidising agent does not get oxidised — it causes oxidation and is itself reduced. Role and fate are opposite; exams punish swapping them. · Oxidation number is not charge: Mn is +7 in KMnO₄ but no Mn⁷⁺ ion floats in the solution — oxidation numbers are bookkeeping, not ionic charges. · Disproportionation needs an intermediate state: the element must sit between two accessible states (Cl at 0 between −1 and +1; O in H₂O₂ at −1 between −2 and 0) — F₂ cannot disproportionate because −1 is its floor.
