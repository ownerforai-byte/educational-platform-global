Text

The atom is the smallest reacting particle; the molecule the smallest independently existing one; ions carry charge; radicals act as units (SO₄²⁻) or carry unpaired electrons (Cl·). The amu = 1/12 of carbon-12 = 1.66×10⁻²⁷ kg, and relative atomic mass compares atoms to that standard. SI's base unit of amount of substance is the mole (NEB 2022, 1m).…
