Title

Mole and formula — counting atoms by weighing them

Href

Icon

Stat Key

Rows

1.
Label

Concept

Text

Atoms are too small to count, so chemists weigh them instead: relative atomic masses (¹²C = 12 exactly, 1 amu = 1.66×10⁻²⁴ g) turn any mass into a particle count via the mole (6.022×10²³ entities). Molecular formula states the actual atom inventory; empirical formula gives the simplest ratio; percentage composition prices each element's share of the mass.

2.
Label

Formula

Kind

formula

Text

n = mass/M = V(STP)/22.4 L = N/Nₐ · Nₐ = 6.022×10²³ mol⁻¹ · %E = (atoms×A_r / M_r)×100 · empirical → molecular: ×(M_r / empirical mass) · SI unit of amount: mole

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

The 22.4 L molar volume holds for ideal gases at STP (0 °C, 1 atm) only. Empirical→molecular scaling needs an independent molar mass (vapour density, freezing-point data). Percentage composition assumes a pure compound of known formula.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Liquid density 0.8792 g/cm³ × 45.6 cm³ = 40.09152 g → 40.1 g to three significant figures (NEB 2023): the answer keeps the FEWEST figures of the inputs (45.6 cm³ has three). Radicals (SO₄²⁻, NO₃⁻) travel as units through reactions — track them whole.

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Mass of 45.6 cm³ liquid, d = 0.8792 g/cm³: m = d×V = 0.8792 × 45.6 = 40.09152 g → 40.1 g (3 sf, limited by 45.6) (NEB 2023, 3 marks). Mole MCQ: SI base unit of amount of substance is the mole — not gram, kilogram or millimole (NEB 2022).

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Relative masses are averages over isotopes — chlorine's 35.5 is not one atom's mass. The mole counts entities, not mass or volume, so '1 mole' of different substances differs in grams. Empirical formula alone never identifies a compound (glucose and formaldehyde share CH₂O).

7.
Label

Derivation

Text

From % composition to formula: divide each % by A_r → mole ratios → divide by the smallest → round to integers (multiply through if .33/.5/.67 persist). Multiply the empirical mass to the measured M_r for the molecular formula — e.g. 40% C, 6.67% H, 53.33% O gives CH₂O, and M_r = 180 reveals C₆H₁₂O₆.

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

Mole triangle: cover what you want — mass/M, ×Nₐ for particles, ×22.4 for gas litres. Percent-to-formula: %→÷A_r→÷smallest→integers. Significant figures follow the weakest input; exact counts (2 atoms) never limit.

9.
Label

Board question

Text

NEB 2023 (3): 0.8792 g/cm³ liquid, 45.6 cm³ — mass to three significant figures. NEB 2022 (1): SI base unit of amount of substance (MCQ: mole). Empirical/molecular formula from % composition repeats yearly.
