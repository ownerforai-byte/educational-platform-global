More ideas

❌ Hund's rule means pair electrons before spreading. ✅ Electrons fill degenerate orbitals singly first with parallel spins. · ❌ The mole is just a number like dozen. ✅ 1 mole = 6.022×10²³ particles (Avogadro constant) — a bridge between atomic and macroscopic scales. · ❌ Molar mass and molecular mass are the same thing. ✅ Molecular mass is in amu; molar mass is in g/mol — numerically equal but different units.
