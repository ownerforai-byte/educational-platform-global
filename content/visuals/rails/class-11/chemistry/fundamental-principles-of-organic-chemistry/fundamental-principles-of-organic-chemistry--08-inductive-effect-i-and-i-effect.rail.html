The inductive effect is the permanent polarisation of σ bonds along a carbon chain, born of an electronegativity difference and fading to nothing within three or four bonds. −I groups (−NO₂, −CN, −COOH, −CHO, the halogens, −OH) pull σ-electrons toward themselves, leaving the chain δ+; +I groups (alkyl) push electrons into the chain.…
