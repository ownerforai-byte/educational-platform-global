The IUPAC system is a reversible code between structure and name. A name is assembled from a word root (meth → hex for C1 → C6), a primary suffix (-ane/-ene/-yne for single/double/triple bonds), functional suffixes with locants (-ol, -al, -one, -oic acid), and alphabetical prefixes with locants for substituents (di-/tri- ignored in alphabetising).…
