Structural isomerism rewires the same atoms five ways. Chain isomerism changes the skeleton (butane/2-methylpropane; the three C₅H₁₂ isomers). Position isomerism slides one group along an unchanged skeleton (propan-1-ol/propan-2-ol). Functional isomerism swaps the group itself, jumping homologous series (ethanol/dimethyl ether; propanal/propanone).…
