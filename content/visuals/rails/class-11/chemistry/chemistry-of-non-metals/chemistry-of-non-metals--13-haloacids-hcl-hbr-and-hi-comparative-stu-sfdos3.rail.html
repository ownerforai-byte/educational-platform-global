Syllabus Topics

  • Hydrogen: chemistry of atomic and nascent hydrogen; isotopes of hydrogen and their uses
  • Application of hydrogen as fuel; heavy water and its applications

Quick concepts

Text

One weak bond explains three trends: as H-X lengthens down the group the bond both releases its proton more easily (acidity up) and snaps homolytically more easily (stability down, reducing power up) — HI is the strongest acid AND the strongest reducer of the three.
