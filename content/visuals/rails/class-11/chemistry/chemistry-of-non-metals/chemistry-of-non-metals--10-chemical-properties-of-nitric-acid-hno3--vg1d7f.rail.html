Syllabus Topics

  • Hydrogen: chemistry of atomic and nascent hydrogen; isotopes of hydrogen and their uses
  • Application of hydrogen as fuel; heavy water and its applications

Nitric acid wears two hats. As a strong monobasic acid it neutralises bases and carbonates like any acid; as a graded oxidising agent it is unmatched.
