Nitrogen dominates the atmosphere and refuses to join it. Its inertness is a four-reason argument: the N≡N triple bond at 941 kJ/mol (bond length 110 pm), the molecule's zero dipole moment (weak grip on reagents), the complete octets inside N₂, and hence a purely kinetic block — N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ is thermodynamically downhill yet needs the Haber process's 450 °C, 200 atm and iron to run at all.…
