1. Topic Introduction — Burning Benzene and Using It Benzene's combustion is the sooty-flame exemplar: 2C₆H₆ + 15O₂ → 12CO₂ + 6H₂O, highly exothermic but incomplete in ordinary flames because the ring is 92.3% carbon. Its uses follow its properties: solvent, precursor to styrene/phenol/cyclohexane, and — increasingly restricted — fuel additive. · ## 2. Theorem Mirror - Combustion equation: 2C₆H₆ + 15O₂ → 12CO₂ + 6H₂O + heat (ΔH ≈ −3268 kJ per 2 mol). - Flame character: luminous, smoky (sooty) — the C : H ratio (1 : 1) leaves carbon unoxidised in the flame's fuel-rich interior; the soot is elemental carbon escaping before full combustion. - Generalisation: aromatic and high-carbon fuels burn smoky; the flame is a composition readout.…
