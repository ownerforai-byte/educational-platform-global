Conditions

❌ Molecules keep moving at the rms speed. ✅ Speeds obey a Maxwell–Boltzmann distribution; the rms value is only an average measure. · ❌ Kinetic theory says molecules never interact. ✅ Interaction is neglected except during collisions — this idealisation is exactly what limits the model.

Problem: rms speed of O₂ at 300 K. Solution: M = 0.032 kg mol⁻¹; v_rms = √(3 × 8.314 × 300/0.032) ≈ 483 m s⁻¹. · Problem: Ratio of rms speeds of H₂ and O₂ at the same temperature. Solution: v ∝ 1/√M → v(H₂)/v(O₂) = √(32/2) = 4; hydrogen molecules are four times faster, hence its escape from the atmosphere.

Derivation

Text

1. Meaning and purpose · The kinetic theory of gases (Clausius, Maxwell, Boltzmann) explains the measurable behaviour of a gas — pressure, volume, temperature and diffusion — in terms of the motion of its molecules. It converts the empirical gas laws into a mechanical model: pressure arises from molecular collisions with the walls, and temperature measures the average molecular kinetic energy.
