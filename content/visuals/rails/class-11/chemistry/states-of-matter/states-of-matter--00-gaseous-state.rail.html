Conditions

Text

❌ Gases have negligible mass. ✅ They have small but definite mass — 22.4 L of air weighs about 29 g at STP. · ❌ Temperature in gas laws may be taken in °C. ✅ Absolute (kelvin) temperature only; 0 °C is 273.15 K.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Air is a mixture (78% N₂, 21% O₂, ~1% Ar/CO₂) that behaves nearly ideally at 1 atm — the everyday ideal gas. · One mole of any gas at STP occupies 22.4 L and, for air, weighs ≈28.97 g (average molar mass).

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Problem: Express 2.5 atm in mm Hg and Pa. Solution: 2.5 × 760 = 1900 mm Hg; 2.5 × 101325 = 253 312 Pa ≈ 2.53 × 10⁵ Pa. · Problem: Which postulate fails when a gas is compressed to 200 atm? Solution: 'molecular volume is negligible' — the molecules' own volume is no longer negligible against the container volume, so the free space shrinks and observed pressure exceeds the ideal prediction (Z > 1).

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

❌ A gas exerts pressure only downward. ✅ Pressure is isotropic — equal in every direction. · ❌ 'Negligible molecular volume' is a minor technicality. ✅ It is one of the two postulates whose failure defines all real-gas deviation (the other is no attraction).

Study notes

Text

• No fixed shape or volume — a gas fills its container completely. · • Very low density compared with liquids/solids (air ≈ 1.3 kg m⁻³ vs water 1000 kg m⁻³); highly compressible; diffuse rapidly. · • Molecules are far apart (intermolecular distances ≈ 10 molecular diameters at STP) with negligible attraction — hence expansion and compressibility.
