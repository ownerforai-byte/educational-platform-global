Derivation

Text

1. Meaning of an ideal gas · An ideal (perfect) gas is a hypothetical gas that obeys the gas laws and PV = nRT exactly under all conditions. Its molecules are point masses with no volume and no intermolecular forces, and collisions are perfectly elastic. No real gas is ideal, but every gas approaches ideal behaviour at low pressure and high temperature.
