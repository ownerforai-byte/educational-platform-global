Label

Conditions

Text

STP means 0 °C and 1 atm (molar volume 22.4 L) — not room temperature; quoting 24 L at "STP" confuses NTP/room conditions. · Boyle's law needs constant temperature, Charles's needs constant pressure: mixing the constraints (or forgetting which variable is held) derails the choice of equation.

Label

Shortcut

Text

"Kelvin before anything". Boyle: multiply, divide (P₁V₁ = P₂V₂). Graham: heavier is slower; square both sides. Over water: subtract aqueous tension. STP = 0 °C, 1 atm, 22.4 L.

6. Common Confusions Celsius must become Kelvin before any gas-law algebra: using 27 °C directly in Charles's law gives nonsense; add 273 first — the single most common numerical error. · Aqueous tension is subtracted, not the other way: the water vapour adds pressure to the wet gas; the dry gas's share is what remains. · Diffusion rate ratios invert with molar mass: the lighter gas is faster — writing r ∝ √M reverses the law.
