Conditions

❌ Boyle's law means PV is always constant. ✅ Only for a fixed mass at constant temperature; PV changes when T or n changes. · ❌ Doubling the Celsius temperature doubles the volume. ✅ Charles' law uses kelvin: 10 °C → 40 °C is a 293 → 313 K change, not a ×4 change.

Solved

Text

Problem: 500 cm³ of a gas at 27 °C and 700 mm Hg — find the volume at STP. Solution: V₂ = P₁V₁T₂/(P₂T₁) = (700 × 500 × 273)/(760 × 300) ≈ 419 cm³. · Problem: Ratio of diffusion rates of H₂ and O₂. Solution: r(H₂)/r(O₂) = √(32/2) = 4, i.e. hydrogen diffuses four times faster.

Derivation

Text

1. Meaning · The gas laws are the experimentally established relations between the pressure (P), volume (V), absolute temperature (T) and amount (n) of a fixed mass of gas. They hold for an ideal gas, i.e. at low pressure and high temperature where molecular volume and intermolecular forces are negligible.
