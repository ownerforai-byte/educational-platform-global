Conditions

Text

❌ Efflorescent, deliquescent and hygroscopic mean the same. ✅ Efflorescent solids lose water to the air, deliquescent solids absorb water and dissolve, hygroscopic solids absorb water and become damp. · ❌ Deliquescence happens in any weather. ✅ It requires air humidity above the deliquescence relative humidity (≈30% for CaCl₂, ≈75% for NaCl).

Problem: 2.86 g of hydrated sodium carbonate loses 1.62 g on strong heating; the anhydrous salt has M = 106 g mol⁻¹. Find n. Solution: anhydrous mass = 1.24 g → 0.0117 mol; water = 1.62/18 = 0.09 mol → n = 0.09/0.0117 ≈ 7.7, i.e. ≈ 8 (illustrating that heating experiments need care, since washing soda is normally the decahydrate). · Problem: Why is CaCl₂ unsuitable for drying ammonia? Solution: CaCl₂ is deliquescent but forms the addition compound CaCl₂·8NH₃, so it would remove ammonia as well; conc. H₂SO₄ or quicklime is used instead.
