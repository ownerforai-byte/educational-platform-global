6. Common Confusions Hygroscopic vs deliquescent — the solution decides: both absorb water; only the deliquescent salt dissolves in what it absorbs. CaO absorbs (stays solid, becomes Ca(OH)₂ by reaction); CaCl₂·6H₂O dissolves. · Efflorescence needs a hydrate: anhydrous solids cannot effloresce — there is no lattice water to lose. · Water of crystallisation is not "wetness": it is stoichiometric, structural water — CuSO₄·5H₂O is a dry, powdery crystal holding five waters per formula unit inside its lattice.
