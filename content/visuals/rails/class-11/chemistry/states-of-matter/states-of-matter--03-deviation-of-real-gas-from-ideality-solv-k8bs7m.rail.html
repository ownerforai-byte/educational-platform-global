6. Common Confusions Deviation has two directions: high pressure inflates volume (Z > 1) while low temperature deflates pressure (Z < 1) — "deviates" does not mean one sign. · a and b are gas-specific constants, not universal: R is universal; a and b must be looked up per gas — quoting R's value for a is a mark-losing slip. · Liquefaction needs attraction, not just pressure: a large a means the gas can be liquefied near room temperature; a tiny a (He) demands extreme cooling first.
