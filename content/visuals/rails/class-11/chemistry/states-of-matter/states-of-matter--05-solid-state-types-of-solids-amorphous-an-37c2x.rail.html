Conditions

Text

❌ Amorphous solids are true solids with a very high viscosity. ✅ They are regarded as pseudo-solids or supercooled liquids because they lack long-range order and flow over long times. · ❌ All solids have sharp melting points. ✅ Amorphous solids soften over a range (glass-transition temperature); only crystalline solids melt sharply.

Special cases

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Problem: Why does glass not show a sharp melting point on heating? Solution: amorphous solids lack a periodic lattice, so bonds break over a range of energies — the material softens gradually between the glass-transition and melting regions instead of melting at one temperature. · Problem: Graphite conducts electricity while diamond does not, though both are carbon. Solution: in graphite each carbon uses three of four valence electrons in σ bonds; the fourth electron is delocalised between layers enabling conduction, whereas in diamond all four electrons are localised in σ bonds.
