Solved

Text

Problem: Why does a wet cloth dry faster on a windy day? Solution: wind removes the vapour layer above the surface, keeping the vapour pressure below saturation so the net evaporation rate stays high; higher temperature and larger exposed area act in the same direction. · Problem: Estimate the boiling point of water at 8000 m where the pressure is about 0.36 atm. Solution: the boiling temperature is reached when vapour pressure equals 0.36 atm ≈ 274 mm Hg; from vapour-pressure tables this corresponds to about 72 °C — hence cooking takes much longer at altitude.

Derivation

1. Meaning of the liquid state · A liquid has a definite volume but no definite shape: molecules are close together (short intermolecular distances) and slide over one another, so a liquid can flow, is practically incompressible and shows short-range order only. Intermolecular forces are strong enough to hold the molecules together but not strong enough to fix them in a lattice, as in a solid.
