6. Common Confusions Lattice vs unit cell: the lattice is the infinite pattern of points; the unit cell is one repeat box of it. Saying "the unit cell is the lattice" loses the mark — one is the map, the other one tile of the map. · Lattice points are positions, not atoms: a lattice point marks where a particle sits; the particle may be an atom, a molecule or a whole ion pair. Do not write "lattice points are atoms". · Shared particles are not counted whole: a corner atom belongs to eight neighbouring cells, so only 1/8 of it lies in any one cell — counting it as 1 gives impossible particle counts.
