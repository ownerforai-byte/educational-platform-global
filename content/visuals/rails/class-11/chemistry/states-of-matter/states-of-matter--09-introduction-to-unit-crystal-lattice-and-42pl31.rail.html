Solved

Text

Problem: Copper (FCC, a = 361 pm, M = 63.5 g mol⁻¹) — find the density. Solution: Z = 4; ρ = ZM/(a³N_A) = (4 × 63.5)/((3.61×10⁻⁸ cm)³ × 6.022×10²³) ≈ 8.97 g cm⁻³, matching the known value for copper. · Problem: Show that the radius of an atom in a BCC lattice is r = √3a/4. Solution: along the body diagonal of length √3a, the atoms touch: √3a = 4r, hence r = √3a/4 ≈ 0.433a.

Derivation

Text

1. Meaning · A crystal lattice is the regular three-dimensional arrangement of points (lattice points) in space at which the constituent particles of a crystal lie. A unit cell is the smallest repeating portion of that lattice which, when repeated in three dimensions, generates the whole crystal — it is the building block or 'brick' of the crystal. Lattice + the atoms/ions/molecules at its points = the crystal structure.
