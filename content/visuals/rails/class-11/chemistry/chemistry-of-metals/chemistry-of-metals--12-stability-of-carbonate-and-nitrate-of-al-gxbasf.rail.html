Thermal stability in Group IIA is the polarisation ladder. The M²⁺ cation bends the big CO₃²⁻/NO₃⁻ electron cloud; polarising power = charge/size, so it falls steadily from Be²⁺ to Ba²⁺. Quick concepts

Text

Polarising power is charge over size, and it is the single dial that tunes the whole group: the smaller the cation, the more it bends the big anion's cloud, and the lower the temperature at which CO2 or NO2+O2 escapes.
