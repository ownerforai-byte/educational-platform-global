Sodium cannot be reduced chemically, so the Downs cell electrolyses molten NaCl + CaCl₂ (42:58) — the eutectic melts at ~600 °C, 200 degrees below sodium's boiling point — between a graphite anode (2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻) and steel cathodes (Na⁺ + e⁻ → Na), with a steel-gauze diaphragm keeping the products apart; the floating sodium is siphoned from a bell and Ca²⁺ stays in the melt.…
