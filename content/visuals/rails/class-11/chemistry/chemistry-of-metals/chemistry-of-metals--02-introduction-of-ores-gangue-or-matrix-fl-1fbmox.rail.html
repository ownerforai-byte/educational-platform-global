Quick concepts

Text

Flux chemistry is just acid-base neutralisation at 1500 C: acidic oxides pair with basic oxides; the salt they form must melt, float and not dissolve in the metal — three physical demands on top of the chemistry.

Key points

Text

Ore = mineral extracted profitably (bauxite yes, clay no). Gangue/matrix = worthless rock (silica, clay). Flux binds gangue into slag: acidic flux SiO2 removes basic gangue; basic flux CaCO3 -> CaO removes acidic gangue, iron furnace: CaCO3 -> CaO + CO2, CaO + SiO2 -> CaSiO3 slag.…
