Down's process — why sodium needs electricity, not carbon

Rows

1.
Label

Concept

Text

Extraction follows reactivity: carbon reduces middle ores (roasted sulphides, calcined carbonates), while Na/Al sit above carbon in the series — only electrolysis (electrometallurgy) rips them free. Down's cell electrolyses fused NaCl (with CaCl₂ to lower the melt point); cathode: Na⁺ + e⁻ → Na, anode: 2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻. Refining (poling, electrorefining) then purifies; flux/slag chemistry floats gangue away.

2.
Label

Formula

Kind

formula

Text

reactivity ladder decides: K Na Ca Mg Al | (C) | Zn Fe | Sn Pb | (H) | Cu Ag Au — above C: electrolysis only · Down's: 2NaCl(fused) → 2Na + Cl₂ (cathode steel, anode graphite) · carbon reduction: MO + C → M + CO (middle metals) · thermite: Fe₂O₃ + 2Al → 2Fe + Al₂O₃

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Down's needs FUSED (molten) NaCl — aqueous solution would discharge H₂ instead (water reduces more easily than Na⁺). Carbon reduction needs the oxide below carbon's line on the Ellingham diagram at furnace T. Roasting (sulphides, in air) vs calcination (carbonates, no air) split by ore type.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Aluminium ignores carbon for the same reason — Hall-Héroult electrolyses alumina in cryolite instead (NEB 2022). Sodium explodes in water (H₂ + heat), forms peroxide with excess O₂, and gives blue solutions in liquid ammonia (solvated electrons). Bleaching powder = CaOCl₂, plaster of Paris = CaSO₄·½H₂O, Epsom = MgSO₄·7H₂O.

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Why not carbon for aluminium? Al sits above carbon in the reactivity series — carbon cannot reduce Al₂O₃ (ΔG positive); only electrolysis works: dissolve alumina in molten cryolite, electrolyse → Al at cathode, CO/CO₂ at the graphite anode (NEB 2022, 3-mark pattern, mirrored by the Down's 5-marker).

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Reactivity-series order is for standard aqueous conditions — high-T Ellingham crossings (e.g. Mg reducing others) break the ladder. Electrolysis is energy-hungry (aluminium's cost is electricity). Bauxite impurities demand Bayer refining before Hall-Héroult even starts.

7.
Label

Derivation

Text

Down's cell logic: melt NaCl → mobile Na⁺/Cl⁻; apply potential above decomposition voltage; cathode reduces Na⁺ (liquid Na floats, collected), anode oxidises Cl⁻ (Cl₂ piped off). CaCl₂/CaF₂ addition lowers mp from 801 °C toward ~600 °C — less heat, less Na vaporisation loss. Sodium's violent water reaction (2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂) proves its series position.

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

'Explain extraction of X' → locate X vs carbon: above → electrolysis (name the cell + electrodes + reactions), middle → roast/calcine + carbon, below → heat alone. Down's 5-marker: cell + why fused + CaCl₂ role + both electrode reactions.

9.
Label

Board question

Text

NEB 2023 (5): Down's process for sodium extraction. NEB 2023 (4): Downs vs MgCl₂-electrolysis comparison. NEB 2022 (3): why carbon fails for aluminium (cryolite electrolysis instead).
