Text

The six trigonometric ratios come from a right triangle: sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse, cos θ = adjacent/hypotenuse and tan θ = sin θ/cos θ, with cosec, sec and cot their reciprocals, and their special values at 0°, 30°, 45°, 60° and 90° are tabulated. Three fundamental identities tie the ratios together: sin^2 θ + cos^2 θ = 1, 1 + tan^2 θ = sec^2 θ and 1 + cot^2 θ = cosec^2 θ.…

Quick concepts

Text

Ratios in a right triangle: sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse, cos θ = adjacent/hypotenuse and tan θ = opposite/adjacent = sin θ/cos θ. · Reciprocal ratios: cosec θ = 1/sin θ, sec θ = 1/cos θ and cot θ = 1/tan θ = cos θ/sin θ. · The identity sin^2 θ + cos^2 θ = 1 and its two rearrangements 1 + tan^2 θ = sec^2 θ and 1 + cot^2 θ = cosec^2 θ. · Compound angles: sin(A ± B) = sin A cos B ± cos A sin B and cos(A ± B) = cos A cos B ∓ sin A sin B, where the two signs are always opposite for cosine.
