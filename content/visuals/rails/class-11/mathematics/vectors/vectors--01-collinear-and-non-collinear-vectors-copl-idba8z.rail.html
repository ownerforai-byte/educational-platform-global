Text

Collinear vectors share a line: b = λa, tested by a × b = 0 (sin θ = 0) or equal component ratios. Coplanar vectors share a plane: tested by [a b c] = a·(b × c) = 0 (zero volume) or the combination form c = λa + μb. The two conditions are dependence in geometric dress — two-vector dependence is parallelism, three-vector dependence is coplanarity — and two vectors are always coplanar, which is why…
