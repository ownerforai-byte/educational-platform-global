Text

The cross product a × b = |a||b|sin θ n̂ returns a vector perpendicular to both inputs (right-hand rule), computed mechanically as the 3×3 determinant with +−+ cofactor signs. It is anticommutative (order flips sign), distributive, and zero exactly for parallel vectors — the mirror of the dot product's perpendicularity criterion.…

Conditions

Text

Treating the cross product as commutative; reversing the order negates the result. · Confusing |a × b| with a · b: the former uses sin θ and is an area, the latter uses cos θ and is a scalar projection.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

The direction of a × b follows the right-hand rule: curl the fingers from a to b, the thumb gives n̂. · A plane through a point p with direction vectors u and v has normal u × v and equation (r − p) · (u × v) = 0.
