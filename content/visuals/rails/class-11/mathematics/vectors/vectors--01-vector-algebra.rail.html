Text

A vector is magnitude with direction; its length is √(a₁² + a₂² + a₃²) and its direction is captured by the unit vector â = a/|a| (NEB 2024: (2î − 3ĵ + 6k̂)/7). Addition obeys the triangle law — head-to-tail — equivalently the parallelogram diagonal, and inherits commutativity, associativity, and inverses from it. Scalar multiplication scales by |λ| and reverses for negative λ.…
