Limitation

Text

The test reads the wedge's own geometry: a ray inside the acute wedge stays below 45 degrees from each arm. · tan theta = |(m2-m1)/(1+m1 m2)|. The acute bisector's half-angle is below 45 degrees, so tan theta < 1 certifies acute, > 1 certifies obtuse - the threshold is the right angle. One tangent comparison replaces angle computation. Dual-channel verification: algebra (the rule) derives, geometry (tan theta) confirms. 1 + m1 m2 = 0 means perpendicular - the formula's failure mode is the answer.
