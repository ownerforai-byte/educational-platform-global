Angle bisectors of pairs of straight lines: the equal-distance locus made algebraic. Normalise each line into a signed distance, equate up to plus/minus, and the two perpendicular bisector equations fall out. The constants-positive convention plus the a1a2 + b1b2 dot-product rule classify acute versus obtuse and select the origin's wedge; the tangent test (compare with 1) certifies the label geometrically.…
