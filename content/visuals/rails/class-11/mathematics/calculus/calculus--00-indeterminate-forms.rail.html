Text

The seven indeterminate forms are races between rates whose outcomes the tools must time: 0/0 and ∞/∞ by factoring, degrees, or L'Hôpital; 0·∞ by flipping a factor; ∞ − ∞ by merging; the power trio by the log bridge. Orders of vanishing are the race schedule, form checks are hypothesis tests, and the flip that simplifies the derivatives is the winning move.…
