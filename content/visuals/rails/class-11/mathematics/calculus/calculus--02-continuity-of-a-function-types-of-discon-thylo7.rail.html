More ideas

Text

A removable discontinuity can be "fixed" by redefining the function at one point. Jump and infinite discontinuities cannot be removed this way. · The function f(x)=sin(1/x) for x=0 and f(0)=0 has an essential discontinuity at x=0 — no matter how you define f(0), the limit does not exist. · A vertical asymptote always indicates an infinite discontinuity, but not all discontinuities involve vertical asymptotes.
