16.
Label

Study notes

Text

Meaning of a limit: limx→a​f(x)=L means that as x gets arbitrarily close to a (from either side, and without ever requiring x=a) the value of f(x) gets arbitrarily close to L. The point x=a may be undefined and the limit still exist — which is why x−2x2−4​, undefined at 2, has limit 4. · The ε-δ definition: for every ε>0 there exists δ>0 such that 0<∣x−a∣<δ implies ∣f(x)−L∣<ε. The condition 0<∣x−a∣ EXCLUDES the point itself, and that exclusion is exactly what makes a limit independent of f(a). · One-sided limits: limx→a−​ approaches from below, limx→a+​ from above. The two-sided limit exists if and only if both exist and are equal. Equal-but-different still means no limit — a jump discontinuity is the standard counter-example.
