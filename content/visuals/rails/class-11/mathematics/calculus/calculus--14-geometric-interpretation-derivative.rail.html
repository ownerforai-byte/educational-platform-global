Text

The derivative's geometric meaning is dynamic: the tangent is the limiting secant, its slope f′(a), the normal its perpendicular partner, and a vertical tangent is the derivative's finite failure. The sign of f′ is a local fact that the Mean Value Theorem promotes to global monotonicity, and the same number reads as a physical rate with units.…
