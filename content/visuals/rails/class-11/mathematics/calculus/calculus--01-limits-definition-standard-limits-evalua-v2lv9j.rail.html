L'Hôpital's rule mechanises the rate race: for a genuine 0/0 or ∞/∞, lim f/g = lim f′/g′ — the ratio of the separate derivatives (never the quotient rule), justified by Cauchy's MVT (f/g = f′(c)/g′(c) for an intermediate c). Discipline is everything: check the form before every pass, differentiate separately, stop when the form resolves ((e^{2x}−1)/sin 3x → 2e^{2x}/3cos 3x → 2/3).…
