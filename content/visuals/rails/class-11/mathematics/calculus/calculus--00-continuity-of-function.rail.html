Text

Continuity is a three-fact contract at each point — limit exists, value exists, they match — and the failure pattern classifies the discontinuity: removable (repairable by re-anchoring), jump (sides disagree), infinite (the approach itself fails), oscillatory. Repair is possible exactly when the limit exists, which is why the audit's order is the diagnosis.…
