6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Statics is dynamics with a = 0 - one law, two subjects. · Statics: equilibrium, sum F = 0. Dynamics: acceleration, sum F = ma - the second law covers both, statics is the a = 0 case. Three laws: inertia (and inertial frames), F = dp/dt = ma, mutual pairs on different bodies. Kinematics: v = u + at, s = ut + at^2/2, v^2 = u^2 + 2as - choose the equation omitting the unneeded quantity. Sign discipline first: declare the positive direction, then the algebra is mechanical.

1. Topic Introduction — Statics and Dynamics: The Two Halves of Mechanics Mechanics splits by one question: is the body accelerating? Statics studies bodies at rest or in uniform motion — forces in balance; dynamics studies accelerated motion — forces causing change. This canonical is the overview that anchors both halves in Newton's three laws. · ## 2.
