Text

A sequence is an ordered list a1, a2, a3, ... following a pattern, and a series is the sum of its terms. In an arithmetic progression the difference d = a_n - a_(n-1) is constant, giving a_n = a + (n-1)d and S_n = n/2[2a + (n-1)d] = n/2(a + l); in a geometric progression the ratio r = a_n/a_(n-1) is constant, giving a_n = a r^(n-1) and S_n = a(r^n - 1)/(r - 1), which reduces to a/(1 - r) only when |r| < 1.…

Special cases

Text

A harmonic progression has no sum formula of its own: take reciprocals, sum the resulting AP, and the sum of the HP is the sum of the reciprocals of those partial sums. · If r = 1 the GP is constant with S_n = na, and the formula carrying (r - 1) in the denominator is undefined.

11.
Label

Quick concepts

Text

Arithmetic progression: the successive differences a_n - a_(n-1) = d are constant, so terms rise by equal jumps. · Geometric progression: the successive ratios a_n/a_(n-1) = r are constant, so each term is a fixed multiple of the previous one. · Harmonic progression: the reciprocals 1/a, 1/(a+d), 1/(a+2d) form an arithmetic progression, so it has no direct sum formula. · Means of two positive numbers a and b: A = (a+b)/2, G = sqrt(ab) and H = 2ab/(a+b), with A >= G >= H.

12.
Label

Key points

Text

A difference signals an AP and a ratio signals a GP, so subtract a(n-1) to get d and divide by a(n-1) to get r. · S_n = n/2(a + l) is often the quickest AP sum, because l is usually given directly in the question. · For a GP with r < 1, write S_n as a(1 - r^n)/(1 - r) so that the bracket stays positive. · Never use S_infinity = a/(1 - r) without first confirming that |r| < 1.

More ideas

Text

An infinite GP converges to a/(1 - r) only when |r| < 1; for r = 1 the terms stay constant and for r > 1 they grow. · The last term l of an AP is l = a + (n-1)d, which lets S_n be written in the short form n/2(a + l). · Sn = a(r^n - 1)/(r-1) (GP)

18.
Label

More ideas

Text

S_infinity = a/(1-r), |r| < 1 (GP) · G = sqrt(ab), H = 2ab/(a+b) · AM, GM and HM are means of two numbers; they are not obtained by averaging the terms of a progression.

19.
Label

More ideas

Text

Negative or zero r still give a valid finite sum formula, but the series diverges to infinity unless |r| < 1. · AM = GM = HM only when every number is equal, so a strict inequality A > G > H is the signal that the numbers are not identical. · Arithmetic mean is always defined, but GM needs non-negative numbers and HM needs non-zero numbers, since the means are taken over reciprocals.

20.
Label

More ideas

Text

The nth term of an AP with first term a and common difference d is a_n = a + (n-1)d, and the sum of n terms is S_n = n/2[2a + (n-1)d] = n/2(a + l). · The nth term of a GP with first term a and common ratio r is a_n = a r^(n-1), and for r != 1 the sum of n terms is S_n = a(r^n - 1)/(r - 1). · An infinite geometric series 1 + r + r^2 + ... sums to S_infinity = a/(1 - r) if and only if |r| < 1.

21.
Label

More ideas

Text

For two positive numbers the geometric mean is G = sqrt(ab) and the harmonic mean is H = 2ab/(a+b), and these satisfy A x H = G^2. · For positive numbers AM >= GM >= HM, with equality in any of the three comparisons only when a = b. · To find two numbers from their AM and GM, set x + y = 2A and xy = G^2, then solve t^2 - 2At + G^2 = 0.
