6. Common Confusions Vertex x-coordinate is −b/2a: not b/2a — the minus sign survives from completing the square. · Holes vs asymptotes: a common factor (x − 2)/(x − 2) leaves a hole at x = 2; an unfactorable zero of q gives an asymptote. · Perpendicular slopes multiply to −1: not "reciprocal" — the reciprocal alone gives parallel-looking errors.
