Formula

Kind

formula

Text

General formula of carbohydrates: Cx​(H2​O)y​ (e.g. glucose C6​H12​O6​). Peptide bonds in a polypeptide of n amino acids = n−1; water lost = n−1. Condensation: monomer–OH + HO–monomer → polymer + H2​O; hydrolysis is the reverse.

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Lipid vs carbohydrate as "polymer": carbohydrates, proteins and nucleic acids are true polymers of repeated monomers; triglycerides are NOT — glycerol and fatty acids are different molecules, not repeated units. NEB loves this trap. · Nucleoside vs nucleotide: nucleoside = sugar + base only; nucleotide = sugar + base + phosphate. Only the nucleotide is the true monomer of nucleic acids.

Quick concepts

Text

Condensation/hydrolysis cycle — the universal build-and-break chemistry of every biomolecule. · Structure determines function — protein shape (sickle-cell), α vs β glycosidic bonds (starch vs cellulose), amphipathic lipid shape (membrane bilayer). · Complementary base pairing — the physical basis of heredity, replication and Chargaff's rule. · Activation energy lowering — the single correct explanation of enzyme catalysis.

Label

Key points

Text

Biomolecules = carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, nucleic acids + minerals, enzymes, water; built from C, H, O, N, P, S. · Monomer → polymer by condensation (water out); polymer → monomer by hydrolysis (water in). · Carbohydrates: Cₓ(H₂O)ᵧ; mono/di/oligo/poly; glucose = universal fuel; starch/glycogen store, cellulose/chitin structure. · α-bonds (starch) digestible; β-bonds (cellulose) not — humans lack cellulase.

Energy density hierarchy — fat (9) > protein = carbohydrate (4) kcal/g, because lipids are more reduced (less oxygen per carbon). · Energy values: carbohydrate 4 kcal/g; protein 4 kcal/g; lipid 9 kcal/g. · Proteins: 20 amino acids, peptide bond −CO−NH−, 4 structural levels, structure determines function.

More ideas

Conjugated protein = protein + prosthetic group (haemoglobin = haem + globin). · Lipids: water-insoluble, organic-solvent-soluble; 9 kcal/g; phospholipids are amphipathic → bilayer membranes. · Saturated fats: single bonds, solid, animal; unsaturated: C=C, liquid oils, plant.

21.
Label

More ideas

Text

Nucleotide = pentose + base + phosphate; DNA: deoxyribose + A,T,G,C, double helix; RNA: ribose + A,U,G,C, single strand. · A=T (2 H-bonds), G≡C (3 H-bonds); Chargaff: %A=%T, %G=%C. · Enzymes: protein catalysts; lower activation energy; apoenzyme + cofactor = holoenzyme; six IUB classes.

22.
Label

More ideas

Text

Water: 70–90% of cell; polar, high specific heat, max density at 4 °C, universal solvent, reactant in hydrolysis/photosynthesis. · Denaturation vs digestion: denaturation (heat, pH) destroys 3-D shape but leaves peptide bonds intact; hydrolysis (digestion) actually breaks peptide bonds into amino acids. · Starch vs glycogen vs cellulose: all are glucose polymers, but starch (plants, α-1,4/1,6), glycogen (animals, more highly branched), cellulose (β-1,4, indigestible). Same monomer, different bonds = different biology.

23.
Label

More ideas

Text

Competitive vs non-competitive inhibition: competitive inhibitors bind the active site and can be overcome by more substrate; non-competitive inhibitors bind an allosteric site and cannot. · Enzyme vs hormone: enzymes are catalysts (not consumed, act locally on substrates); hormones are chemical messengers (consumed after action, transported by blood to distant targets). · DNA "contains protein": DNA is pure nucleic acid; chromosomes contain DNA + histone protein. DNA itself has no protein.
