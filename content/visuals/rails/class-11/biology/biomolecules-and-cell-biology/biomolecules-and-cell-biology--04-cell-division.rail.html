Concept

Text

Cells divide by amitosis, mitosis or meiosis. The cell cycle (G1, S, G2, M) is regulated by checkpoints. Mitosis produces two genetically identical diploid cells for growth, repair and asexual reproduction. Meiosis halves the chromosome number and, through crossing over and independent assortment, generates the genetic diversity needed for sexual reproduction.…

Key points

Mitosis = growth, repair, asexual reproduction; meiosis = gametes + variation. · PMAT is the mnemonic for mitotic phases. · Crossing over (Prophase I) and independent assortment create variation. · S phase doubles DNA; G2 prepares; checkpoints control entry to M.

Study notes

Text

Cell cycle: interphase (G1 growth, S DNA replication, G2 preparation) + M phase (mitosis). · Amitosis (direct division): simple splitting of the nucleus; rare, seen in some fungi and liver cells. · Mitosis: one division producing two genetically identical diploid (2n) daughter cells; phases — prophase, metaphase, anaphase, telophase (PMAT).
