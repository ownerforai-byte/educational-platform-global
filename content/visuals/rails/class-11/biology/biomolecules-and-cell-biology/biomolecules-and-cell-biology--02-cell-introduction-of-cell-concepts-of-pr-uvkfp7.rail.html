Text

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. Cell theory (Schleiden, Schwann, Virchow) unifies all organisms. Cells are grouped into prokaryotes (no true nucleus; bacteria, cyanobacteria) and eukaryotes (true nucleus and membrane-bound organelles; plants, animals). Study notes

Text

Cell theory (Schleiden, Schwann, Virchow): all organisms are made of one or more cells; the cell is the basic structural and functional unit; every cell arises from a pre-existing cell. · Cell = the smallest unit of life; bounded by the plasma membrane; size typically 1-100 µm. · Prokaryotic cell: no membrane-bound nucleus or organelles; 70S ribosomes; e.g. bacteria, cyanobacteria; smaller (1-10 µm).
