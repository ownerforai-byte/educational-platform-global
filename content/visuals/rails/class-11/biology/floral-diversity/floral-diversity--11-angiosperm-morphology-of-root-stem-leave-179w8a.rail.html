Quick concepts

Text

Bud test for stem nature: any flattened/spiny/root-like organ with nodes and buds is a modified stem.

12.
Label

Key points

Text

Roots: tap (dicot) vs fibrous (monocot); modifications — storage, nodulated (Rhizobium), prop (banyan), stilt (maize), pneumatophores (Avicennia). · Stems: nodes/buds always present; underground (tuber, rhizome, corm, bulb), aerial (tendril, thorn, phylloclade), sub-aerial (runner, stolon, offset, sucker). · Leaves: base+petiole+lamina; reticulate (dicot) vs parallel (monocot) venation; simple vs compound (pinnate/palmate); phyllotaxy; modifications — tendril, spine, pitcher. · Inflorescence: racemose (indefinite, acropetal — raceme, spike, umbel, head) vs cymose (definite, basipetal).

Text

Thorn vs prickle: thorn is a modified stem with vascular connection (deep-seated); prickles are superficial outgrowths (rose) removable without tearing. · Fruit vs seed: fruit = fertilised ovary (+ accessories); seed = fertilised ovule. Wheat grain = caryopsis fruit, not a bare seed. · Mango drupe — fleshy mesocarp + stony endocarp around the seed.
