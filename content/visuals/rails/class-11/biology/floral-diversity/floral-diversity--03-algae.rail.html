Concept

Text

Algae are photosynthetic, mostly aquatic organisms that lack true roots, stems, and leaves. They are classified into Chrysophyceae (diatoms, golden algae), Pyrrophyceae (dinoflagellates), and Chlorophyceae (green algae). As primary producers, algae generate about half of the world's oxygen and form the base of aquatic food webs. Their economic importance spans food, industry, and agriculture.…

Conditions

Text

Algae are NOT plants in the strict sense: they lack true tissues, roots, and seeds. They are placed in Protista (or Plantae in some systems) depending on the classification used. · Cyanobacteria (blue-green algae) are prokaryotes, not true algae; they are in the domain Bacteria.
