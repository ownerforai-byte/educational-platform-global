1.
Label

Concept

Text

NEB's plant side is one evolutionary ladder with one shared trick. Ladder: algae → bryophytes (non-vascular; 'amphibians of the plant kingdom') → pteridophytes (first true vascular tissue) → gymnosperms (first seed, naked) → angiosperms (flower + fruit). Trick: every land plant alternates a haploid gametophyte with a diploid sporophyte — and the dominant generation flips from gametophyte (bryophytes) to sporophyte (seed plants) as you climb.

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Classification follows observed characters — cell type, cell wall, mode of nutrition, body organisation and reproduction (Whittaker 1969), while the three-domain system (Woese 1977) rests on 16S rRNA sequencing. Fertilisation in bryophytes and pteridophytes needs a water film because the sperm is flagellated; the seed habit of gymnosperms and angiosperms removes that condition.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Fungi are NOT plants — chitin wall, absorptive heterotrophy, no chloroplast (the standard 'why fungi are not plants' question). Cyanobacteria are not true algae (prokaryotic). Deuteromycetes have no known sexual stage. Algal pigments and stores are the trap: green = chl a + b, starch; brown = a + c + fucoxanthin, laminarin; red = a + d + phycobilins, floridean starch; diatoms wall themselves with SiO₂.

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Walk one plant through the hierarchy: Rhododendron arboreum → Angiospermae (Division) → Dicotyledonae → Ericales → Ericaceae → Rhododendron → arboreum. Fungal spore table the same way: Rhizopus → Phycomycetes (non-septate) · Penicillium → Ascomycetes (ascospores in asci) · Agaricus → Basidiomycetes (basidiospores on basidia) · Aspergillus → Deuteromycetes (imperfect).

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

The systems keep moving: molecular data broke the old two-kingdom scheme and reclassified many deuteromycetes; Protista remains a grab-bag group that is not monophyletic. Alternation descriptions also oversimplify — algae can be haplontic, diplontic or haplo-diplontic, and the label 'lower plant' is convenience, not taxonomy.

7.
Label

Derivation

Text

Derive the ladder from one selection pressure — drying land. Water-dwelling algae need no protection; bryophytes keep flagellated sperm, so they stay small and moist; vascular tissue (xylem + phloem + lignin) lets pteridophytes stand tall but sperm still swims; pollen and the ovule free reproduction from water in gymnosperms; the flower, double fertilisation and fruit make angiosperms the most successful terrestrial group. Each innovation is one problem solved.

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

Four strings carry the unit: kingdoms M-P-F-P-A, domains B-A-E, hierarchy D-K-P-C-O-F-G-S, and 'Bryophyte = dominant gametophyte, seed plant = dominant sporophyte'. Algal stores rhyme — green/starch, brown/laminarin, red/floridean. Asco = sac, Basidio = club, Deutero = imperfect. Moss life cycle sequence: spore → protonema → leafy gametophyte → archegonium/antheridium → zygote → dependent sporophyte.
