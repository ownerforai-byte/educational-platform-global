11.
Label

Quick concepts

Text

The seed habit: integumented megasporangium (ovule) + retained female gametophyte + pollen tube = reproduction freed from external water — the precondition of all seed plants.

12.
Label

Key points

Text

Gymnosperms: naked seeds on megasporophylls, cones not flowers, tracheid-only xylem, no companion cells, wind pollination, pollen-tube fertilisation (siphonogamy), haploid endosperm, polycotyledonous embryo, sporophyte dominant. · Pinus: long + dwarf shoots with needle fascicles; mycorrhizal roots; monoecious; male cones with winged pollen; female cones with two naked ovules per scale; pollen tube delivers non-motile male gamete; winged seeds. · Economic: timber (Cedrus, Pinus), resin/turpentine, paper pulp, chilgoza nuts, Taxol from Taxus, ornamentals (Cycas, Thuja).
