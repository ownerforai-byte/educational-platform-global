Pheretima's reproductive system is complete in both sexes in one body. Conditions

Text

Hermaphrodite does not mean self-fertilising. Pheretima exchanges sperm with another worm. · Fertilisation is not in the oviduct. It is in the cocoon, after both eggs and stored sperm have been shed into it.

Quick concepts

Text

Hermaphrodite with cross-fertilisation: complete male and female systems in one body, but obligate exchange of sperm — reproductive assurance without losing genetic variation.

12.
Label

Key points

Text

Male: testes in sacs (10, 11) → seminal vesicles (9, 12) → vasa deferentia + prostate glands → male pores (18). · Female: ovaries (13) → oviducts → female pore (14); four pairs of spermathecae (6–9) store partner's sperm. · Copulation: ventral apposition, heads opposite; sperm exchanged into spermathecae. Cocoon: clitellar mucus band slides forward, receives ova (14) and sperm; fertilisation in cocoon; direct development; 1–20 young.
