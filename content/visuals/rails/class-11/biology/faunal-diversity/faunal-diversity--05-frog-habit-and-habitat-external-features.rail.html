Rana tigrina is the model amphibian for NEB. It lives near water, spends most of the time on land, and returns to water to breed. External features include moist, glandular, scaleless skin; a tympanum; a nictitating membrane; 4-digit forelimbs; and 5-digit webbed hindlimbs. Rana tigrina (tiger frog / Indian frog): amphibian; ~5–8 cm long; model organism for NEB. · Habit and habitat: lives near water (ponds, streams, wetlands); spends most of the time on land but returns to water for breeding. · External features: moist, glandular, scaleless skin (cutaneous respiration); four limbs (forelimbs 4 digits, hindlimbs 5 digits, webbed); tympanum (eardrum); nictitating membrane (third eyelid); lateral line absent in adults.
