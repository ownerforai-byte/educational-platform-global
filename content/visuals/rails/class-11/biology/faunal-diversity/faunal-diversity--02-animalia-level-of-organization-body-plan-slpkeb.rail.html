3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Acoelomates have NO body cavity; pseudocoelomates have a cavity not fully lined by mesoderm; coelomates have a fully mesoderm-lined cavity. · Cnidaria are radial (adult) but their larvae may be bilateral; echinoderms are radial in adults but bilateral in larvae (secondary radial symmetry).

Quick concepts

Text

The coelom ladder: acoelomate → pseudocoelomate → coelomate tracks increasing body complexity — room for organs, a hydrostatic skeleton and independent gut movement.

12.
Label

Key points

Text

Levels: protoplasmic (Protozoa) → cellular (Porifera) → tissue (Cnidaria) → organ (Platyhelminthes) → organ-system (Annelida onward). · Symmetry: asymmetrical (Porifera), radial (Cnidaria; secondary in adult Echinodermata), bilateral (Platyhelminthes onward). · Germ layers: diploblastic (Cnidaria) vs triploblastic (onward). Body cavity: acoelomate (Platyhelminthes), pseudocoelomate (Aschelminthes), eucoelomate (Annelida onward). · Protostomes (mouth from blastopore; spiral cleavage; schizocoely) vs deuterostomes (anus from blastopore; radial cleavage; enterocoely).
