Rana tigrina is the model amphibian for NEB. It is semi-aquatic, living near water but spending most of the time on land. Its external features — moist, glandular, scaleless skin; a tympanum; a nictitating membrane; 4-digit forelimbs and 5-digit webbed hindlimbs — reflect its amphibious life. The tadpole has a tail and gills; the adult loses the tail and gains lungs.…

Study notes

Text

Rana tigrina (tiger frog / Indian bullfrog): the model amphibian for NEB; ~5–8 cm long; found in ponds, streams, and wet grasslands. · Habit and habitat: semi-aquatic; lives near water but spends most of the day on land; returns to water to breed; buries in moist soil in dry season. · External features: moist, glandular, scaleless skin (mucus + colour glands); head with eyes, tympanum (eardrum), nostrils; distinct neck, trunk; 4-digit forelimbs, 5-digit webbed hindlimbs; nictitating membrane (third eyelid); tail absent in adults (present in tadpole).
