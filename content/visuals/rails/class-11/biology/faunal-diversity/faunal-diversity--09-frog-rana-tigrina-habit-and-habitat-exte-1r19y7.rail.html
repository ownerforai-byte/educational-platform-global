Mouth, pharynx, oesophagus, stomach, duodenum, ileum, rectum, cloaca. Male external signs in season: vocal sac and nuptial pad. Bile emulsifies. Pepsin and pancreatic protease digest protein. Amylase digests starch. Lipase digests fat.

Text

The frog chews with nothing. Maxillary and vomerine teeth only grip. The lower jaw has no teeth. · The tongue is hinged at the front, not at the back. A diagram with the root at the throat is a mammal's tongue.

Board question

Short answer (2m): What is the cloaca? Answer. A common posterior chamber into which the intestine, the urinary ducts and the reproductive ducts all open; it exits to the exterior through the cloacal aperture. Frog, like other amphibians, retains this single exit for faeces, urine and gametes.

Quick concepts

Text

Amphibian duality: every system reflects the double life — lungs plus skin respiration, webbed limbs plus jumping legs, water-bound reproduction.

12.
Label

Key points

Text

Habitat: amphibious, nocturnal, hibernates/aestivates; poikilothermic; cutaneous respiration. · External: head + trunk, no neck; nictitating membrane; tympanum; 4 fingers (male nuptial pad), webbed hind toes; cloaca; male vocal sacs. · Digestive: buccopharyngeal cavity with vomerine/maxillary teeth + bifid protrusible tongue → short oesophagus → stomach (HCl, pepsin) → intestine with liver + pancreas (bile, pancreatic juice) → large intestine → cloaca.
