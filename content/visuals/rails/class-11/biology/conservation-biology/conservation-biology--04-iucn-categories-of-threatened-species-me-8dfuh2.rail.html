The IUCN Red List uses standardized categories (EX, EW, CR, EN, VU, NT, LC, DD, NE) to assess extinction risk. Understanding these categories is essential for prioritizing conservation action and policy.

Critically Endangered (CR): extremely high risk of extinction in the wild; e.g., population < 50 mature individuals or rapid decline. · Endangered (EN): very high risk of extinction in the wild; smaller populations or steeper declines than CR threshold. · Vulnerable (VU): high risk of extinction in the wild; larger populations than EN but still threatened.
