A national park is managed for wildlife and visitors, and people do not live in the core. Chitwan, gazetted in 1973, was Nepal's first. Sagarmatha National Park protects the Everest region and is also a World Heritage site.

Conditions

Text

National park vs conservation area: parks are strictly protected with no consumptive use; conservation areas are community-managed with sustainable use. · Buffer zone vs core zone: the core is strictly protected; the buffer fringe shares benefits with local communities to reduce pressure and conflict.
