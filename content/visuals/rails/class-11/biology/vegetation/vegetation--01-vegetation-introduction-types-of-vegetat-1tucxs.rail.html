Nepal's vegetation stacks six climate zones up its slopes: tropical sal and sissoo forests below 1000 m; subtropical chir pine with Schima and Castanopsis from 1000–2000 m; temperate oak, blue pine, fir, hemlock and rhododendron from 2000–3000 m; subalpine birch and juniper to 4000 m; alpine Kobresia meadows and cushion plants to 5000 m; and the nival snowline beyond, where only lichens persist.…

Key points

Text

Six zones: tropical (<1000 m sal, sissoo), subtropical (1000–2000 m chir pine, Schima), temperate (2000–3000 m oak, blue pine, fir, rhododendron), subalpine (3000–4000 m birch, juniper), alpine (4000–5000 m Kobresia meadows, cushion plants), nival (>5000 m lichens only). · Temperature falls ~6 °C per 1000 m — the zonation driver. · Sal = tropical deciduous dominant; uses: timber, resin, seed oil.
