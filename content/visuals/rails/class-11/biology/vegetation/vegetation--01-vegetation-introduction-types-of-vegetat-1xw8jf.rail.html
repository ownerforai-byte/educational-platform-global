From about 2000 to 3000 m the belt is temperate. Oak and Rhododendron arboreum, the national flower, are the markers. Rhododendron here is a flowering plant. · Toward 4000 m, fir, birch and juniper thin out. The timberline is the last trees. Above it, alpine herbs and grasses continue until the snowline, which is higher. · Forest and other wooded land cover about 45 percent of Nepal. The east is wetter than the west at the same height, so the species lists differ. Community forestry is a management success in the middle hills, not itself a belt.
