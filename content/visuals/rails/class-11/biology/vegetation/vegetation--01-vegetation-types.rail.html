Text

Nepal's vegetation is strongly zoned by altitude and climate. The Terai supports subtropical sal forests and grasslands; the sub-montane and temperate hills have mixed deciduous and broadleaf forests dominated by rhododendron, oak, and chestnut; the sub-alpine zone is coniferous (pine, fir, juniper); the alpine zone has meadows, dwarf shrubs, and lichens; and the nival zone is bare rock and snow.…
