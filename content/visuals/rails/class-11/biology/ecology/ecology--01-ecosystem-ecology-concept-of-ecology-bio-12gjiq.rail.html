Ecology studies life's levels — from a single organism through populations, communities and ecosystems to biomes and the biosphere. Ecosystems combine abiotic factors (light, temperature, soil, topography, chemicals) with biotic ones: producers capture energy, consumers pass it along trophic levels, and decomposers return nutrients.…

Short answer (2m): Distinguish habitat from niche. — Habitat is the physical place a species lives (its address); niche is its functional role — what it eats, when it is active, how it reproduces (its profession). Two species may share a habitat but differ in niche.
