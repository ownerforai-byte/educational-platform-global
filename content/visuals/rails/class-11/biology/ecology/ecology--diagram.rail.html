Energy flow and the ten per cent law as a pyramid

Concept

Text

Green plants fix the Sun's energy and hand it up the food chain, but most of it is spent and lost as heat at every step — which is why a food chain can never be long.

Caption

Upright energy pyramid: each trophic level holds about a tenth of the energy below it.

2.
Label

Formula

Kind

formula

Text

Only about 10 % of the energy at one trophic level reaches the next; GPP − R = NPP; Eₙ = E₁(0.1)ⁿ⁻¹

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Lindeman's ten per cent law is an average for energy transfer between trophic levels, and the energy pyramid it draws is always upright.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

The pyramid of biomass inverts in a pond where phytoplankton turn over fast, and the pyramid of number inverts under one tree hosting thousands of insects.

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Producers 10 000 kJ → primary consumers 1 000 kJ → secondary consumers 100 kJ → tertiary consumers 10 kJ, nine tenths lost at each step.

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Ten per cent is only an average (a transfer can reach twenty) and it applies to energy alone — nutrient cycles are closed loops while energy flow is one-way.

7.
Label

Derivation

Kind

formula

Text

NPP = GPP − R; energy to the next level = NPP × 0.1, applied once per level ⇒ Eₙ = E₁(0.1)ⁿ⁻¹

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

Draw the energy pyramid upright before writing anything; one line of Eₙ = E₁(0.1)ⁿ⁻¹ carries the method mark by itself.

9.
Label

Board question

Text

Explain Lindeman's ten per cent law with a labelled energy pyramid and calculate the energy available at the third trophic level.
