Nitrification vs nitrogen fixation: fixation = N₂ → NH₃ (Rhizobium, cyanobacteria); nitrification = NH₃ → NO₂⁻ → NO₃⁻ (Nitrosomonas, Nitrobacter). · Denitrification vs ammonification: denitrification returns NO₃⁻ to N₂ gas (Pseudomonas); ammonification releases NH₃ from dead matter.

Key points

Text

Carbon cycle: photosynthesis ↔ respiration; fossil-fuel combustion and deforestation raise CO₂ → greenhouse effect; ocean and limestone are sinks. · Nitrogen cycle: fixation (Rhizobium, Azotobacter, Anabaena, lightning, Haber) → nitrification (Nitrosomonas, Nitrobacter) → assimilation → ammonification → denitrification (Pseudomonas). · Succession: directional change → climax. Primary (xerosere: lichen → moss → herb → shrub → forest) vs secondary (faster, soil present). Sere, seral stages, pioneer, climax.
