Study notes

Text

Environmental pollution: the addition of substances or energy to the environment at a rate that exceeds its capacity to dilute, degrade, or absorb them. · Air pollution: SO2 (coal), NOx (vehicles), CO (incomplete combustion), PM2.5/PM10 (particulates), ozone (ground-level), CFCs; sources: industry, vehicles, power plants, biomass burning. · Effects of air pollution: respiratory diseases (asthma, bronchitis), acid rain, smog, global warming, ozone depletion.
