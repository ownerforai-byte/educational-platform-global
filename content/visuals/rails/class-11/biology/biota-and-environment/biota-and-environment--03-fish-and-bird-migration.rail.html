Study notes

Text

[Remember] Migration is the periodic, seasonal, two-way movement of animals between breeding and non-breeding habitats — distinct from one-way dispersal or permanent emigration. · [Remember] Fish migration: anadromous fishes move from sea to fresh water to breed (e.g. Hilsa ilisha ascending Terai rivers in monsoon); catadromous fishes move from fresh water to sea to breed (e.g. eels, Anguilla). · [Understand] Bird migration types: latitudinal or north–south (Siberian ducks wintering at Koshi Tappu and Chitwan), longitudinal, and altitudinal (pheasants and thrushes descending from hills to Terai in winter).
