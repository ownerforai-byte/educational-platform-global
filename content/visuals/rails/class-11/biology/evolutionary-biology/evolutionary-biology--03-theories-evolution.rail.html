Limitation

Text

Natural selection acts on VARIATION in a population; it does not create new traits (mutations create variation). · Genetic drift is a random change in allele frequency (important in small populations); natural selection is NON-random (directed by fitness).

18.
Label

Study notes

Text

Lamarckism (Lamarck, 1809): "use and disuse" of organs + "inheritance of acquired characteristics"; organisms change during life and pass on acquired traits. Mostly rejected (no mechanism for heredity). · Darwinism (Darwin, 1859): natural selection; variation exists in populations; overproduction leads to struggle for existence; organisms better adapted survive and reproduce (survival of the fittest). · Neo-Darwinism (synthetic theory, early 20th c.): integrates Mendelian genetics with natural selection; evolution = change in allele frequencies in a population; mechanisms: mutation, recombination, natural selection, genetic drift, gene flow.
