Text

New World: sideways nostrils + 2.1.3.3 + tail often prehensile. Old World monkey: downward nostrils + 2.1.2.3 + tail not prehensile + ischial callosities. Ape: no tail + long arms. Human: no tail + bipedal skeleton.

Quick concepts

The tail test and the thumb test: tail separates monkey from ape; the opposable thumb and bipedal gait separate ape from man.

12.
Label

Key points

Text

New world (platyrrhine): S. America, flat side nostrils, prehensile tail, less opposable thumb, no cheek pouches — spider, howler, marmoset. · Old world (catarrhine): Africa/Asia, downward nostrils, non-prehensile tail, opposable thumb, cheek pouches, ischial callosities — macaque, langur, baboon. · Apes: tailless, arms > legs, knuckle-walk, 400–500 cc — gibbon (lesser), orangutan, gorilla, chimp (great). · Man: bipedal, legs > arms, ~1400 cc, speech, chin, culture.
