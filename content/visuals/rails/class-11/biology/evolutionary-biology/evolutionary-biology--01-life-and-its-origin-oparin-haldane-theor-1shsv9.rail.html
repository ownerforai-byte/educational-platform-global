Quick concepts

Text

Reducing atmosphere = accumulation: without free O₂ to burn them, organics could build up for millions of years — the precondition of the primordial soup.

12.
Label

Key points

Text

Oparin–Haldane: reducing atmosphere (CH4, NH3, H2, H2O) + UV/lightning → simple organics → primordial soup → macromolecules → coacervates/protobionts → RNA-first self-replication → first anaerobic heterotrophic cells → photosynthesis adds O2. · Miller–Urey 1953: CH4 + NH3 + H2 + H2O vapour + electric discharge, one week → glycine, alanine, aspartic, glutamic acids + organic compounds; supports chemical evolution.
