Concept

Text

Traits travel as discrete factors called alleles; writing the gametes on the two axes of a square and filling it shows how they recombine at fertilisation, and the square is the ratio drawn out.

Caption

Punnett square for Tt × Tt: 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt, read as 3 tall : 1 dwarf.

2.
Label

Formula

Kind

formula

Text

Monohybrid phenotype 3 : 1, genotype 1 : 2 : 1; dihybrid 9 : 3 : 3 : 1; test cross 1 : 1

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Segregation and independent assortment need the genes on different chromosomes (or far apart on one), complete dominance and equally viable gametes.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Incomplete dominance gives 1 : 2 : 1 red : pink : white, codominance shows both alleles at once, and an X-linked allele appears far more often in males.

5.
Label

Solved

Text

Tt × Tt gives TT, Tt, Tt, tt → 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt → 3 tall : 1 dwarf; the test cross tt × TT gives every offspring Tt.

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Linked genes, epistasis, polygenic inheritance and cytoplasmic inheritance all break the neat ratios, so say so whenever a question hints at an exception.

7.
Label

Derivation

Kind

formula

Text

Tt → gametes T and t → 2 × 2 squares → 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt; T dominates, so the phenotype reads 3 : 1

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

Write the gametes on the axes before filling any square, and sanity-check the ratio by its sum: 4 for one gene, 16 for two.

9.
Label

Board question

Text

Work out a monohybrid cross with a Punnett square and state the phenotypic ratio obtained in the F₂ generation.
