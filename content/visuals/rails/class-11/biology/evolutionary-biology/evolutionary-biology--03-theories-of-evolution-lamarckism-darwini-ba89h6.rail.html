11.
Label

Quick concepts

Text

Selection needs three ingredients: variation, heritability, differential success — remove any one and evolution stalls. Selection does not create variation; it filters it.

12.
Label

Key points

Text

Lamarckism: vital forces, new needs, use/disuse, inheritance of acquired characters; giraffe by stretching; disproved by Weismann's germplasm theory (tail-cutting mice). · Darwinism: overproduction → struggle → variation → survival of the fittest → selection and inheritance → descent with modification; example: industrial melanism (Biston betularia), giraffe, antibiotic resistance. · Neo-Darwinism: mutation + recombination + gene flow + selection + drift + isolation → allele-frequency change → speciation.
