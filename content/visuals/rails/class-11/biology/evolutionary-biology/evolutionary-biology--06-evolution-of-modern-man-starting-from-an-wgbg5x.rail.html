Text

Humans are primates in the family Hominidae. They share an ancestor with living apes. They did not descend from a living chimpanzee. Australopithecus afarensis, including the fossil called Lucy, was bipedal with a brain near 400 cubic centimetres.

Evolution of evolvability: each acquisition (hands, fire, language) increased the species' capacity for further change — culture became the main adaptive engine.

12.
Label

Key points

Text

Split ~6–7 mya from a common ape-human ancestor as African forests shrank. · Sequence with drivers: Dryopithecus → Ramapithecus (ground feeding) → Australopithecus (bipedalism, savanna) → H. habilis (tools) → H. erectus (fire, out of Africa) → Neanderthal (burial, cold) → H. sapiens (language, agriculture, global spread). · Four trends: posture, hands, brain/face, culture — with the bipedalism→hands→tools→brain feedback loop.
