Quick concepts

Text

Prokaryote = pre-nucleus: all defining Monera features (size, ribosomes, wall, replication speed) follow from lacking a nuclear envelope and organelles.

12.
Label

Key points

Text

Monera: prokaryotes — no nuclear envelope, 70S ribosomes, peptidoglycan wall, circular DNA + plasmids, binary fission in 20–30 min. · Shapes: coccus (diplo/strepto/staphylo), bacillus, spirillum, vibrio. Gram-positive violet/thick peptidoglycan; Gram-negative pink/thin + LPS. · Nutrition: photoautotrophic (cyanobacteria), chemoautotrophic (Nitrosomonas, Nitrobacter), saprophytic, parasitic, symbiotic (Rhizobium, E. coli). · Growth curve: lag → log → stationary → decline.
