The themes worth an essay Justice versus law — the central theme: the women find the truth the law cannot see, and the play asks whether concealing evidence can be the more just act. The oppression of women — Minnie's life is the case study: childless, voiceless, isolated on a lonely farm, married to a man who silenced her song; the trifles are the archaeology of that oppression.… · ## NEB short answers, solved Q. Trick 1: The title's irony: men dismiss 'trifles' (kitchen things) that actually solve the crime — the women find the evidence the men overlook; this reversal is the core answer. · Trick 2: Evidence of motive: the broken birdcage and the dead canary — Minnie's strangled bird mirrors her strangled spirit; connect symbol to motive.
