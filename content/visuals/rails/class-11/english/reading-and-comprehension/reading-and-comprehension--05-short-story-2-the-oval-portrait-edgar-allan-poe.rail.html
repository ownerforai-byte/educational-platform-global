Quick concepts

Text

Frame narrative: the outer story of the ill narrator contains the inner story of the portrait · First-person narration by the narrator, who observes the portrait and reads the painter's account · The theme of art versus life - art can consume and destroy the life it depicts · Destructive passion: the artist's obsession becomes self-destructive

12.
Label

Key points

Text

Structure: a frame narrative (story within a story), a first-person narrator and an embedded artistic description. · Plot: the ill narrator in an abandoned chateau in the Apennines finds an oval portrait and reads a note about a young painter and his bride. · Themes: art versus life, destructive passion, beauty and death, and the artist's sacrifice. · Gothic elements: the isolated chateau, stormy weather and shadows for setting; a melancholy mood with supernatural undertones; the oval frame as a symbol of life's fragility.
