Text

❌ The T in the Stefan-Boltzmann law can be in Celsius. ✅ The temperature MUST be in Kelvin. At 0°C (273 K), the radiated power is NOT zero — it is σ(273)4≈449 W/m². Using 0°C would incorrectly give zero. · ❌ The Stefan-Boltzmann law applies only to black bodies. ✅ It applies to ALL bodies, with the modification E=eσT4 where e is the emissivity. Real objects with e<1 emit less than a black body at the same temperature. · ❌ A body at thermal equilibrium radiates no energy. ✅ A body at thermal equilibrium radiates energy continuously, but it also ABSORBS exactly the same amount of energy from its surroundings. The NET radiation is zero — not the radiation itself.
