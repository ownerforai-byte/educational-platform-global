Text

The electric field E = F/q₀ is the force per unit positive test charge (N/C); for a point charge E=kq/r2, radially outward for positive q, inward for negative. Fields superpose as vectors: at the midpoint of two equal +2 μC charges 10 cm apart, each field is 7.2×10⁶ N/C but they cancel — net zero (NEB 2020, 3m).…
