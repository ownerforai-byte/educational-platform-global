Conditions

Text

Symmetry Required: Gauss's law is always true but practically useful only for spherical, cylindrical, or planar symmetry where E is constant over the Gaussian surface. · Conductor vs Insulator: For conducting sphere, charge resides on surface → E = 0 inside. For insulating sphere with uniform volume charge, E grows linearly from center.
