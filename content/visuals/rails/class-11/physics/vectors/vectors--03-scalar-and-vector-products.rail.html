Quick concepts

Text

cos for projection, sin for rotation: the dot product measures alignment; the cross product measures perpendicular twist.

12.
Label

Key points

Text

Dot: A⃗·B⃗ = AB cos θ — scalar, commutative, zero at 90°; examples: work, power, flux. · Cross: A⃗×B⃗ = AB sin θ n̂ — perpendicular vector, anticommutes, zero at 0°/180°; examples: torque, angular momentum, magnetic force. · Component dot: A⃗·B⃗ = AₓBₓ + A_yB_y + A_zB_z; cos θ = A⃗·B⃗/(AB).
