Conditions

Text

Temperature vs Heat Distinction: Temperature is an intensive property (average kinetic energy per particle, measured in K). Heat is extensive energy transferred due to temperature difference (measured in J). · Absolute Temperature Requirement: In kinetic theory and gas laws (PV = nRT, <KE> = (3/2)kT), temperature must be in Kelvin (absolute scale).

Text

Temperature is a measure of the average kinetic energy of particles: <KE> = (3/2)kT, where k is Boltzmann's constant. · The Kelvin scale is the SI base unit of thermodynamic temperature and is an absolute scale.

Key points

Text

Temperature scales provide a system for measuring temperature based on fixed reference points and equal divisions. · The Celsius scale has freezing point at 0°C and boiling point at 100°C at standard atmospheric pressure. · The Fahrenheit scale has freezing point at 32°F and boiling point at 212°F at standard atmospheric pressure. · The Kelvin scale is an absolute scale with zero at absolute zero (0 K = -273.15°C).

Universal facts

Text

There is no upper limit to temperature - particle speeds can increase without bound. · The Kelvin scale was originally defined by the triple point of water (273.16 K) and absolute zero (0 K). Since 2019, it is defined by fixing Boltzmann's constant k to exactly 1.380649 × 10⁻²³ J/K.

A temperature scale is a system for measuring temperature based on fixed reference points and equal divisions between them. · The three most commonly used temperature scales are Celsius (°C), Fahrenheit (°F), and Kelvin (K). · The Celsius scale was introduced by Anders Celsius in 1742, with the freezing point of water at 0°C and the boiling point at 100°C at standard atmospheric pressure.
