Text

Thermal equilibrium: no net heat transfer on contact. Zeroth law: A↔C and B↔C in equilibrium implies A↔B in equilibrium.

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Thermal Equilibrium Definition: Two systems are in thermal equilibrium if no net heat flows between them when brought into thermal contact. This means they have the same temperature. Thermal equilibrium is a transitive, symmetric, and reflexive relation. · Zeroth Law Statement: If system A is in thermal equilibrium with system B, and B is in thermal equilibrium with system C, then A is in thermal equilibrium with C. This transitive property allows temperature to be defined as a state variable that can be measured with a thermometer.

15.

Study notes

Text

Two systems are in thermal equilibrium when no net heat flows between them after thermal contact. In ordinary conditions they then have equal temperature, but not necessarily equal internal energy: mass and material also matter. · The zeroth law states that if A is in thermal equilibrium with C and B is in equilibrium with C, then A and B are in equilibrium with each other. This transitive property allows temperature to be measured consistently. · A thermometer is system C. Allow it to reach a stable reading on body A, then place the same calibrated thermometer on B. Equal stable readings imply A and B have equal temperatures.
