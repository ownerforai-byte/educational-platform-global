Criticality: Chain reaction self-sustains when each fission causes exactly one more fission (k = 1). k < 1 subcritical, k > 1 supercritical. · Fusion Temperature: ~10⁸ K needed — much hotter than sun's core (~1.5×10⁷ K) because terrestrial fusion uses D-T (easier than p-p).

NEB 2022 (3m): Distinguish fission and fusion with one example each. — Fission: heavy nucleus splits after neutron capture, e.g. ²³⁵U + n → ¹⁴¹Ba + ⁹²Kr + 3n (~200 MeV). Fusion: light nuclei combine at ~10⁸ K, e.g. ²H + ³H → ⁴He + n + 17.6 MeV. Both release energy as BE/A rises toward iron.
