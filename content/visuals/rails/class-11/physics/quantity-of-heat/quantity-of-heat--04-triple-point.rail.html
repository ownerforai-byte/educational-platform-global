The triple point of a substance is the unique temperature and pressure where solid, liquid and gas coexist in equilibrium — for water 273.16 K at 611.7 Pa. On the P–T phase diagram the fusion, vaporization and sublimation curves meet there; below the triple pressure the liquid phase cannot exist, which is why dry ice sublimes (CO₂'s P_tp = 5.18×10⁵ Pa exceeds 1 atm) and why freeze-drying works.…
