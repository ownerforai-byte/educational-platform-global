Solving numerically: x≈30°, so A≈60°. · Why is the minimum deviation method more accurate than measuring deviation at arbitrary incidence? At minimum deviation, the ray path is symmetric, making the measurement less sensitive to small errors in reading the angles.… · A ray passes through a prism with A=50° and n=1.52. Find the angle of incidence that produces minimum deviation. At minimum deviation: r=A/2=25°. By Snell's law at the first face: sini=nsinr=1.52×sin25°=1.52×0.4226=0.6424. i=arcsin(0.6424)=40.0°.

Prove that for a given prism, the angle of incidence that gives minimum deviation is unique. The deviation function δ(i) is convex (U-shaped) with a single minimum. This can be shown by taking the derivative dδ/di and showing it equals zero at exactly one value of i (where i=e).
