The relative refractive index of medium 2 with respect to medium 1 is n21​=n2​/n1​=v1​/v2​=sini/sinr; reversing the media simply inverts it (n12​=1/n21​), and absolute indices (measured against vacuum) chain through any number of media. A direct consequence is apparent depth: looking vertically into a medium of index n, depths shrink by the factor n — apparent depth = real depth/n.…
