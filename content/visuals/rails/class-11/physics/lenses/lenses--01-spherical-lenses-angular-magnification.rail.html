Sign Convention: Light travels left to right. Distances measured from optical centre. Object distance u is negative (object on left), focal length f positive for convex, negative for concave. · Thin Lens Approximation: Lens formulas assume thin lens (thickness negligible compared to radii and object/image distances).

Board question

Text

Numerical (4m): A convex lens of f = 12 cm forms a real image 3× the object's size. Find u and v. — m = −3 → v = 3u (signs: u = −x, v = 3x). 1/(3x) + 1/x = 1/12 → 4/(3x) = 1/12 → x = 16 cm. Object 16 cm in front, image 48 cm behind.
