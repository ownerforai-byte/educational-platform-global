Study notes

Text

Superficial (area) expansion: When a solid sheet is heated, its area increases. ΔA=A0​βΔT, where β is the coefficient of superficial expansion. · Cubical (volume) expansion: When a solid is heated, its volume increases. ΔV=V0​γΔT, where γ is the coefficient of cubical expansion. · Relation for isotropic solids: α:β:γ=1:2:3. That is, β=2α and γ=3α. This holds for isotropic materials (same properties in all directions).
