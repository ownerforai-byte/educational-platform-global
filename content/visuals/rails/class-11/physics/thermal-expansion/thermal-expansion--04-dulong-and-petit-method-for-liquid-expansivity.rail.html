Text

Apparatus: A U-tube made of glass with two vertical limbs and a horizontal base. One limb is kept in ice (0°C) and the other in steam (100°C) or hot water. The liquid in the tube reaches a steady state with different temperatures in each limb. · Principle: At the horizontal base of the U-tube, the pressure from both limbs must be equal (Pascal's law). The pressure at the base from each limb is P=ρgh, where ρ is the liquid density at that temperature and h is the height of the liquid column. · Equating pressures: ρ0​gh0​=ρ100​gh100​, where h0​ is the height in the cold limb (0°C) and h100​ is the height in the hot limb (100°C). Therefore: h0​h100​​=ρ100​ρ0​​.
