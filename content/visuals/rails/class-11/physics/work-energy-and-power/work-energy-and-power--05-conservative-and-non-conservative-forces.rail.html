3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Gravity vs friction receipts: gravity returns exactly what it took (path-independent); friction keeps everything (path-dependent, dissipative). · Conservative ≠ 'always helpful': gravity can do negative work on the way up — conservativeness is about bookkeeping, not direction.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Friction does NOT have a potential energy — the energy becomes heat, not stored PE. · A conservative force does NOT guarantee energy conservation if non-conservative forces are also present.

Key points

Text

Conservative: path-independent work, zero closed-loop work, PE definable, mechanical energy conserved — gravity, spring, electrostatic. · Non-conservative: path-dependent, dissipative — friction, air resistance, viscosity. · W_conservative = −ΔPE.
