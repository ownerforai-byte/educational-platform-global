Concept

Text

Energy is never created or destroyed — it only changes form. The freely falling body proves it: dropped from height h, a body carries PE = mgh at the top, mgh/2 + mgh/2 halfway down, and KE = ½m(2gh) = mgh just before landing — the total stays mgh at every instant (NEB 2020, 2024). Gravity is the bookkeeper: PE drains into KE at exactly the rate height falls, so v=2gh​ is conservation solved for speed.…

Label

Conditions

Text

Mechanical vs total energy conservation: mechanical (KE+PE) survives only conservative forces; total energy (including heat) always survives. · Energy vs momentum conservation: both hold for isolated systems, but energy is a scalar ledger and momentum a vector one — collisions use them differently.

Text

Mechanical energy conservation requires ALL forces to be conservative — one friction force breaks it. · The reference level for gravitational PE is arbitrary — only differences matter.
