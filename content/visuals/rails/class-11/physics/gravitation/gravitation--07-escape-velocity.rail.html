Energy Conservation Derivation: Escape velocity ve​=2GM/R​ comes from setting total mechanical energy to zero at infinity: 21​mve2​−GMm/R=0. This is the minimum speed to reach infinity with zero residual kinetic energy. · Relation to Orbital Velocity: ve​=2​⋅v0​ where v0​=GM/R​ is the circular orbital velocity at the surface. The factor 2​≈1.414 means escape requires 41% more speed than a low circular orbit.
