Concept

Text

Gravitational potential V=−rGM​ is the work per unit mass to bring a test mass from infinity; potential energy U=−rGMm​ is the negative binding energy of two masses. Near the surface U≈mgh. The field relates to the potential by g=−drdV​, and circular orbits have total energy E=−2rGMm​, confirming their bound nature.

Conditions

Text

Field vs Potential Distinction: Gravitational field g​=−GM/r2r^ is a vector (force per unit mass). Gravitational potential V=−GM/r is a scalar (work per unit mass). The field is the negative gradient of the potential: g​=−∇V=−dV/drr^. · Equipotential Surfaces: Surfaces of constant V. For a point mass, they are concentric spheres. No work is done moving a test mass along an equipotential; the field is always perpendicular to these surfaces.

Limitation

Text

❌ 'Potential and potential energy are the same.' ✅ Potential V is work per unit mass (J/kg); potential energy U=mV (J). V belongs to a point in the field; U belongs to the mass–field system. · ❌ 'Negative energy is unphysical.' ✅ Negative potential energy simply means the system is BOUND — energy must be added to separate it. It is a matter of the reference at infinity.

Study notes

Text

Gravitational potential V: the work done per unit mass in bringing a test mass from infinity to a point, without acceleration. For a point mass M: V=−rGM​. At infinity V=0, and everywhere else V is negative because gravity does positive work as the mass approaches — the field does the work, so potential decreases. · Gravitational potential energy U: the work done against gravity in assembling a system; for two masses separated by r: U=−rGMm​. It is the energy of the whole system, not of one mass alone. U is negative because the system is bound — you must supply GMm/r of energy to separate the masses to infinity. · Why the minus sign matters: potential energy is defined with zero at infinity. Since the force is attractive, energy is released (and PE decreases) as masses come together. A satellite in orbit at radius r has U=−rGMm​ — its total mechanical energy is E=K+U=−2rGMm​, which is negative, confirming a bound orbit.
