Concept

Text

A satellite orbits when gravity supplies the centripetal force: v₀ = √(GM/r) and T = 2π√(r³/GM). Energy is K = GMm/2r, U = −GMm/r, E = −GMm/2r. Orbital speed is mass-independent; higher orbits are slower but longer-period. Weightlessness arises from continuous free fall.

3.

Conditions

Text

Orbital Velocity Derivation: v0​=GM/r​ comes from equating gravitational force to centripetal force: GMm/r2=mv02​/r. This applies to circular orbits around a spherically symmetric mass. The satellite's mass cancels out — all satellites at the same radius have the same speed. · Period Law: T=2πr3/GM​ is Kepler's third law for circular orbits. Squaring gives T2∝r3, independent of satellite mass. This is valid only for bound circular/elliptical orbits — not for escape trajectories (E≥0).
