Concept

Text

Five questions: G statement (2024), escape-velocity derivation plus value (2023), alien-planet g scaling (2022: 8M at 2R doubles g to 19.6), satellite potential energy (2021: 200 kg at 400 km gives minus 1.18x10^9 J). Gravity scales as M/R2 — every numerical is that ratio in disguise.

2.
Label

Formula

Kind

formula

Text

F = GMm/r2, G = 6.67x10^-11 · g = GM/R2 · v_escape = sqrt(2GM/R) = sqrt(2gR) = 11.2 km/s · v_orbit = sqrt(GM/r) · U = -GMm/r · E_total = -GMm/2r · g(h) = g(1-2h/R), g(d) = g(1-d/R)

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Point-mass (or spherical) gravity with centre-to-centre r (meaning satellite r = R + h, never h alone). Escape derivation ignores drag and other bodies. The M/R2 scaling compares surfaces only. Bound orbits carry negative total energy (why the minus sign matters).

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Planet 8M/2R: g scales 8/4 = 2x, so 19.6 m/s2 (NEB 2022). Satellite 200 kg at 400 km: r = 6.8x10^6 m, U = -GMm/r = -1.18x10^9 J (NEB 2021). Escape needs 11.2 km/s regardless of projectile mass.

5.
Label

Solved

Text

NEB 2023 (5): half m v2 = GMm/R gives v = sqrt(2GM/R) = sqrt(2gR) = 11.2 km/s. NEB 2022 (3): g_p = G(8M)/(2R)2 = 2g = 19.6 m/s2. NEB 2021 (3): U = -(6.67x10^-11 x 6x10^24 x 200)/6.8x10^6 = -1.18x10^9 J. NEB 2024 (2): law statement plus G value.

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Mark-losers: altitude used as orbital radius; G value forgotten (6.67x10^-11); positive PE for bound satellites; g-variation formulas applied beyond small h/d. Newton fails extreme fields (Mercury needs Einstein).

7.
Label

Derivation

Text

Escape: kinetic equals binding energy GMm/R. Orbital: gravity supplies centripetal force (v = sqrt(GM/r), T = 2 pi sqrt(r3/GM) — Kepler III). g-variation from g = GM/r2 evaluated at R+h and R-d. Centre of mass vs gravity distinction for extended bodies.

8.
Label

Shortcut

Text

Scaling questions: write the M/R2 ratio, plug multiples. Satellite questions: write r = R + h first line. Escape: sqrt(2gR) shortcut with gR known. Bound means negative total energy.
