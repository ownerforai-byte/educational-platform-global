Conditions

Text

Power Formula Context: P=I2R is used when current is known (series circuits). P=V2/R is used when voltage is known (parallel circuits). Both are derived from P=VI and Ohm's law; using the wrong one gives incorrect results because V and I are coupled through R. · Joule Heating is Irreversible: The power P=I2R dissipated in a resistor represents an irreversible conversion of electrical energy to heat. It cannot be recovered within the circuit. This is distinct from energy stored in inductors/capacitors or converted to mechanical work.
