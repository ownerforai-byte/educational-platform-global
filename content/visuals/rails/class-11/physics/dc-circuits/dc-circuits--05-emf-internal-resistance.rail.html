EMF vs Terminal Voltage: EMF (E) is the open-circuit voltage (no current). Terminal voltage V=E−Ir under discharge; V=E+Ir during charging. The sign of the Ir term depends on current direction. · Series/Parallel Cell Rules: In series: Etotal​=∑Ei​, rtotal​=∑ri​. In parallel (identical cells): Etotal​=E, rtotal​=r/n. These assume no circulating currents between mismatched cells.

Study notes

Text

EMF (mathcalE): the open-circuit voltage of a source. It is the work done per unit charge in moving charge around the complete circuit. · Internal resistance r: the resistance inside the cell due to electrolyte and electrodes. It causes the terminal voltage to drop when current flows. · Terminal voltage: V=mathcalE−Ir. When no current flows (I=0), V=mathcalE. Under load, V<mathcalE.
