Concept

Text

Ohmic conductors — metals at steady temperature, manganin, constantan — give a straight I–V line through the origin: R = V/I constant. Non-ohmic conductors bend the graph: a filament lamp's resistance rises as current heats it; a diode conducts only in forward bias beyond a knee; thermistors' resistance falls with temperature. The graph is the test, and self-heating is the usual cause of curvature.…

Conditions

Text

Ohm's Law Validity: Ohm's law V=IR holds only for ohmic materials at constant temperature and other physical conditions. It is NOT a fundamental law of nature but an empirical observation valid for many conductors. · Dynamic vs Static Resistance: For non-ohmic devices, static resistance R=V/I (at a point) differs from dynamic resistance rd​=dV/dI (slope of V-I curve). The dynamic resistance is what matters for small-signal AC analysis.
