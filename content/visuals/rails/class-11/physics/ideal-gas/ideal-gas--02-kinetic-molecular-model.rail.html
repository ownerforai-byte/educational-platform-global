3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Low Density Required: Model assumes molecular volume ≪ container volume. Valid when gas is dilute (low P, high T). Breaks down near condensation point. · Elastic Collisions Only: Energy conservation in collisions is essential. Inelastic collisions would cause temperature decay — not observed in equilibrium gases.

Limitation

Text

'Ideal' means the assumptions hold: point molecules, elastic collisions, no forces — real gases approximate this at low P, high T. · Pressure is statistical: no single molecule sustains it; 10²³ collisions per second average into a steady push.
