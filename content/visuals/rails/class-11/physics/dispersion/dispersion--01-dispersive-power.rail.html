Conditions

Text

Dispersive power is NOT the same as angular dispersion — dispersive power is dimensionless (a ratio), while angular dispersion has units of angle and depends on the prism angle. · A larger refractive index does not necessarily mean a larger dispersive power — flint glass has both a higher n and a higher ω than crown glass, but the two properties are independent.

Study notes

Text

Dispersion: splitting of white light into its constituent colours because the refractive index of a material depends on wavelength. This occurs because the speed of light in a medium varies with wavelength — violet light (shorter wavelength) slows down more than red light (longer wavelength), so it bends more. · Pure spectrum: a spectrum in which colours do not overlap. Achieved with a narrow slit and a single prism so that each wavelength emerges at a distinct angle. The narrow slit ensures each colour comes from a single point source, eliminating colour blending. · Dispersive power ω: ω=ny​−1nv​−nr​​, where nv​, nr​, ny​ are refractive indices for violet, red, and yellow (sodium D line). Dispersive power is a dimensionless quantity — it is the ratio of angular dispersion to the mean deviation.
