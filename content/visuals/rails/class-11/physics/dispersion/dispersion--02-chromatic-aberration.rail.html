Text

Dispersion Definition: Dispersion is the separation of white light into its constituent colors (spectrum) due to the dependence of the refractive index n on the wavelength λ of light (n(λ)). · Cauchy's Equation: For many transparent materials, the refractive index follows Cauchy's equation n(λ)=A+B/λ2+C/λ4. This explains why blue light (λ smaller) is refracted more than red light (λ larger).

Label

Study notes

Text

Chromatic aberration is colour fringing caused by a lens having different focal lengths for different wavelengths. In ordinary glass, blue/violet light is refracted more strongly than red, so their images do not share one focus. · Longitudinal chromatic aberration is the axial separation Δf=|fv−fr| of two colour foci. Transverse chromatic aberration is a wavelength-dependent lateral image displacement, often visible at the edge of a wide field. · Spherical aberration has a different cause: marginal and paraxial rays of the same colour focus at different points because the surface is spherical. It can be reduced with an aperture stop or aspheric surfaces.
