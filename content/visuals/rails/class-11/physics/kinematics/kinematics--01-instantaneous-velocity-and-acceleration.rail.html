3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Limit Definition Universality: Instantaneous velocity v=dx/dt and acceleration a=dv/dt are defined as limits (Δto0). They apply to any motion — uniform or non-uniform, constant or variable acceleration. The SUVAT equations (v=u+at, etc.) are special cases valid only for constant a. · Graph Duality — Slopes vs Areas: On x-t graph: slope of tangent = v. On v-t graph: slope of tangent = a; area under curve = displacement. On a-t graph: area under curve = change in velocity. Mixing up slope/area is the most common graph error.

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

Speed vs velocity: speed is the scalar path-length rate; velocity is the vector displacement rate — equal only in straight-line motion without reversal. · Zero velocity vs zero acceleration: at the top of a vertical throw v = 0 but a = g downward; in uniform velocity motion a = 0.

Board question

Text

Numerical (3m): A car moving at 10 m/s accelerates uniformly at 2 m/s² for 4 s. Find the final velocity and distance travelled. — v = u + at = 10 + 2×4 = 18 m/s; s = ut + ½at² = 10×4 + ½×2×16 = 40 + 16 = 56 m (NEB 2023).
