For constant acceleration, three equations connect u, v, a, t and s — and the velocity–time graph derives all of them. The slope of the v–t line gives a=(v−u)/t, i.e. v=u+at; the area splits into a rectangle ut and a triangle 21​at2, giving s=ut+21​at2; the total area 2u+v​t rearranges to v2=u2+2as.…

Conditions

Text

Uniform velocity vs uniform acceleration: uniform velocity means a = 0 (v–t graph horizontal); uniform acceleration means the v–t graph is a straight sloped line. · Distance vs displacement in these equations: the equations give displacement; total path length needs separate handling when direction reverses.

Board question

Text

Numerical (3m): A train decelerates uniformly from 30 m/s to rest in 150 m. Find the deceleration and the time taken. — v² = u² + 2as → 0 = 900 − 2a(150) → a = 3 m/s² (deceleration); t = (v−u)/a = 30/3 = 10 s.

Key points

Text

v = u + at (slope of v–t line); s = ut + ½at² (rectangle + triangle area); v² = u² + 2as (average-velocity area). · Choose the equation by which quantity is absent: no t → v² = u² + 2as; no s → v = u + at; no v → s = ut + ½at².
