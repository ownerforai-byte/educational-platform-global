3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Independence of Components: Horizontal motion (constant vx​=ucosheta) and vertical motion (free fall vy​=usinheta−gt) are completely independent. They share only the time variable t. This is the foundational principle — no horizontal force means vx​ is constant. · Trajectory is a Parabola: Eliminating t gives y=xanheta−racgx22u2cos2heta, a downward-opening parabola. This assumes no air resistance, constant g, and flat ground. With drag, the trajectory is not a parabola and range decreases.

Limitation

Angle projection vs horizontal projection: angled launch uses T = 2u sinθ/g; horizontal launch from a height uses t = √(2h/g) — different formulas for different setups. · Maximum range at 45°: true only for level ground (launch and landing at the same height).

Key points

Text

Components: u cos θ constant horizontal; u sin θ vertical under g. · T = 2u sinθ/g; H = u²sin²θ/2g; R = u²sin2θ/g; R max at 45°. · Complementary angles (θ, 90°−θ) give equal ranges — 30° and 60° ratio 1:1. · Horizontal projection: t = √(2h/g), R = ut, v = √(u² + 2gh).
