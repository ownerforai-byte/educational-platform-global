Dark matter (~27% of the universe) supplies the unseen gravity that keeps galaxy rotation curves flat and bends light via lensing — it interacts gravitationally but not electromagnetically. Dark energy (~68%) is the uniform component making the expansion accelerate (1998 supernova surveys). Together the unseen is ~95% of everything.…

1. Topic Introduction — The Invisible 95% Everything we see — stars, gas, dust, us — is only ~5% of the universe. Dark matter holds galaxies together without shining; dark energy pushes the expansion faster; black holes trap light itself; and gravitational waves let us hear spacetime ring. Modern physics is mostly a search for the missing. · ## 2. Fundamental Concepts ### 2.1 Dark matter Galaxy rotation curves refuse to fall off with distance as visible mass allows — the outer stars orbit as fast as the inner ones. An unseen mass component, dark matter, must supply the extra gravity: - ~5× more mass than ordinary (baryonic) matter — about 27% of the universe.… · ## 3. Conceptual Relationships - To Topic 116: dark energy accelerates the expansion Hubble's law measures; dark matter shaped galaxy formation after the Big Bang. - To gravitation (Unit 8): black holes are the extreme limit of Newtonian and Einsteinian gravity. - To Topic 115: dark-matter candidates are new particles beyond the Standard Model.
