Concept

Text

Electric charge is a fundamental, quantized (q=ne), and conserved property of matter. Objects can be charged by triboelectric friction, conduction, or electrostatic induction. Charging by induction polarizes mobile electrons in an insulated conductor; momentary grounding allows electron exchange with Earth, permanently trapping an opposite net charge on the conductor without losing inducing charge.

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Thinking induction creates new charge: Induction does not generate charge from nothing; it redistributes existing mobile valence electrons within the conductor while preserving net charge conservation of the combined system. · Believing grounding always pulls electrons down to Earth: If a positively charged rod is inducing negative charge near it, grounding pulls electrons UP from the Earth into the conductor. If a negative rod is near, electrons flow DOWN into the Earth.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Grounding must be removed BEFORE withdrawing the inducing charged body; otherwise, mobile charges equalize back through ground, leaving the sphere neutral. · Repulsion is the only sure test of electrification because an uncharged body can be attracted by a charged body due to electrostatic polarization.

Electrostatic precipitators: Industrial smoke stacks ionize airborne fly ash and soot particles, which are attracted to grounded collecting plates, purifying factory exhaust by over 99%. · Photocopier (Xerography): An aluminum drum coated with photoconductive selenium is charged by corona wire in the dark; light from the document discharges illuminated areas, leaving an electrostatic latent image that attracts toner powder.

Limitation

Text

Assuming insulators can be charged permanently by induction: Only materials with delocalized mobile charges (conductors) can be charged by induction and grounding. Insulators experience only molecular dielectric polarization.

Key points

Electric charge is quantized in integral multiples of e=1.602×10−19C. · Charge is an isolated invariant scalar conserved across all nuclear and electromagnetic interactions. · Induction charges a conductor with the sign opposite to that of the inducing charged body without contact loss. · Grounding neutralizes electrostatic potential by allowing electron exchange with Earth's infinite charge reservoir.

Universal facts
