Conditions

Text

❌ The mirror formula is 1/f=1/v−1/u. ✅ The correct formula is 1/f=1/u+1/v (with sign convention applied). The lens formula 1/f=1/v−1/u looks similar but has a different sign convention. · ❌ f=R for spherical mirrors. ✅ The focal length is HALF the radius of curvature: f=R/2. The focus is midway between the pole and the center of curvature.

Study notes

Text

Mirror formula. For a spherical mirror, object distance u, image distance v and focal length f satisfy 1/v + 1/u = 1/f. · Focal length. The focal length is half the radius of curvature, f = R/2; it is negative for a concave mirror and positive for a convex mirror under the Cartesian sign convention. · Magnification. Linear magnification m = −v/u = h'/h; a negative value means a real inverted image and a positive value a virtual erect one.
