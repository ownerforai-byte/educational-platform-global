3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Inertial/Non-Inertial Distinction: Centripetal force is a real resultant force in an inertial frame; it is not a 'fictitious' force. Fictitious centrifugal force only appears in the rotating reference frame to maintain Newton's laws. · Directional Constraint: Centripetal force must act strictly perpendicular to the instantaneous velocity for the speed to remain constant. If the net force has a tangential component, the speed changes (non-uniform circular motion).
