Concept

Text

Angular displacement (θ), angular velocity (ω = dθ/dt), and angular acceleration (α = dω/dt) describe rotational motion. For constant angular acceleration, the kinematic equations mirror linear motion. The links v = rω and a_c = rω² connect rotational and translational quantities. rpm is converted to rad/s by multiplying by 2π/60.

Text

Angular velocity: ω = dθ/dt (instantaneous), ω = Δθ/t (average) Angular acceleration: α = dω/dt = d²θ/dt² Kinematic equations (constant α): ω = ω₀ + αt; θ = ω₀t + ½αt²; ω² = ω₀² + 2αθ

3.
Label

Conditions

Text

Axis of Rotation Constraint: Angular quantities (heta, ω, α) are defined with respect to a specific fixed axis. If the axis of rotation changes orientation, the angular velocity becomes a time-dependent vector, and simple scalar kinematics no longer apply. · Kinematic Equations Applicability: The kinematic equations (analogs of v=u+at) for angular motion are strictly valid only for constant angular acceleration (α=extconstant). For time-varying α(t), direct integration of dω/dt is required.

4.
Label

Special cases

Text

Angular velocity points ALONG THE AXIS (right-hand rule), not tangentially — this is the most common confusion. · The kinematic equations for rotation are IDENTICAL in form to linear motion — just swap the variables.

Solved

Text

rpm to rad/s: A wheel rotates at 180 rpm. ω = 180 × 2π/60 = 18.85 rad/s. · Kinematic equation: A wheel starts from rest and accelerates at 3 rad/s². After 4 s: ω = 12 rad/s, θ = 24 rad, revolutions = 24/2π ≈ 3.82 rev.

6.
Label

Limitation

Text

❌ 'Angular displacement and angle are the same.' ✅ Angular displacement is a vector (along the axis), while angle is a scalar. For a full revolution, angular displacement = 2π rad, but the body returns to its starting position. · ❌ 'Angular velocity is always constant in circular motion.' ✅ Only in UNIFORM circular motion is ω constant. In non-uniform circular motion, ω changes and there is angular acceleration.

11.
Label

Quick concepts

Text

Angular velocity (ω = dθ/dt) is the rate of change of angular displacement, with direction along the axis. · The kinematic equations for constant angular acceleration mirror linear motion: ω = ω₀ + αt, θ = ω₀t + ½αt², ω² = ω₀² + 2αθ. · Linear speed and angular speed are linked by v = rω — the basis of circular motion problems. · Centripetal acceleration a_c = v²/r = rω² is directed toward the center of the circular path.

12.
Label

Key points

Text

Angular displacement is measured in radians; 2π rad = 360° = 1 revolution. · Angular velocity is a vector along the axis (right-hand rule), not tangential. · The kinematic equations for rotation mirror linear motion: x→θ, v→ω, a→α. · v = rω links linear and angular speed — the basis of all circular motion problems.

Universal facts

Angular velocity is a vector along the axis of rotation — its direction is given by the right-hand rule. · The kinematic equations for constant angular acceleration are identical in form to those for linear motion.

18.
Label

Study notes

Text

Angular displacement (Δθ): the angle through which a body rotates about a fixed axis. Measured in radians (rad). 1 rad = 57.3°, 2π rad = 360°. Angular displacement is a vector along the axis (right-hand rule): positive for anticlockwise rotation. · Angular velocity (ω): the rate of change of angular displacement: ω=dθ/dt (instantaneous), ω=Δθ/t (average). Unit: rad/s. Direction: along the axis (right-hand rule). For uniform circular motion, ω is constant. · Angular acceleration (α): the rate of change of angular velocity: α=dω/dt=d2θ/dt2. Unit: rad/s2. When α is constant, the kinematic equations mirror linear motion.

More ideas

Text

rpm is converted to rad/s by multiplying by 2π/60 — revolutions = θ/(2π). · Linear-angular links: v = rω; a_t = rα; a_c = v²/r = rω² · rpm conversion: ω (rad/s) = rpm × 2π/60; revolutions = θ/(2π)

20.
Label

More ideas

Text

Reference Frame: Angular velocity is defined in an inertial frame. If observing from a rotating reference frame, non-inertial (fictitious) forces like centrifugal or Coriolis forces appear, which must be accounted for if analyzing dynamics in that frame. · Always convert rpm to rad/s before using formulas: multiply by 2π/60. · ❌ 'The direction of angular velocity is tangential.' ❌ The direction of angular velocity is ALONG THE AXIS of rotation (right-hand rule), not tangential. Tangential velocity is v = rω, but ω itself points along the axis.

Label

More ideas

Text

❌ 'Angular acceleration means the body is speeding up.' ✅ Angular acceleration is the rate of change of angular velocity. If α and ω have the same sign, the body speeds up; if opposite signs, it slows down. · ❌ 'The kinematic equations for rotation are different from linear motion.' ✅ They are IDENTICAL in form: just replace x with θ, v with ω, and a with α. The same problem-solving approach works. · ❌ 'rpm and rad/s are the same.' ✅ 1 rpm = 2π/60 rad/s ≈ 0.105 rad/s. Always convert rpm to rad/s before using formulas.
