Label

Conditions

Text

Energy Conservation (No Non-Conservative Work): Total mechanical energy (K+U) is conserved if only gravity and tension act (tension does zero work). v2=u2−2gh where h=R(1−cosheta) from the lowest point. · String vs. Rod Constraint: - String: Can only pull (tension T≥0). If To0, the string goes slack and the particle follows a parabolic projectile path until the string tightens again. - Rigid Rod: Can push or pull (T can be negative/compressive). The particle completes the circle even if speed at top is zero (but vtop​≥0 is required).
