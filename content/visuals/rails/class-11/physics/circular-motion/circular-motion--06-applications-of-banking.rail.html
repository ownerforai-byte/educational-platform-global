Text

Ideal Banking (No Friction): At the design speed v0​=gRanheta​, the normal reaction alone provides the centripetal force. No friction is required, so the car can negotiate the curve even on a perfectly frictionless surface. · Friction Range (Real Roads): For speeds veqv0​, static friction acts up or down the incline to prevent sliding. The safe speed range without skidding is bounded by vmax​=gRracanheta+μs​1−μs​anheta​ and vmin​=gRracanheta−μs​1+μs​anheta​ (valid only if denominator > 0).
