Gravitational Field Strength

Subtopics
1.
Name

Key points

Points
  • V=−rGM​ is negative, approaching zero at infinity.
  • U=−rGMm​ is the energy of the (bound) system, negative for attraction.
  • Near Earth's surface U=mgh is the appropriate approximation.
  • Potential and field connect: g=−drdV​.
  • Total energy of a circular orbit is negative: E=−2rGMm​.

Variation in g due to Altitude and Depth

Subtopics
1.
Name

Key points

Points
  • Above the surface: g′=g(R+hR​)2 — decreases as (R+h)21​.
  • Below the surface: g′=g(1−Rd​) — decreases linearly to zero at the centre.
  • g is maximum at the surface.
  • Rotation and oblateness also reduce g at the equator.
  • The g–r graph: linear rise inside, 1/r2 fall outside.

  • g at latitude λ due to rotation: g′=g−ω2Rcos2λ
  • g inside a uniform sphere: g′=Rgr​ (r≤R)
