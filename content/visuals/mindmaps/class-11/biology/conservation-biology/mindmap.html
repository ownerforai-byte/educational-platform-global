Biodiversity and Conservation

Subtopics
1.
Name

Key points

Points
  • Biodiversity: genetic, species, ecosystem diversity.
  • Conservation: in-situ (natural habitat) + ex-situ (outside).
  • In-situ: protected areas, community forests, sacred groves.
  • Ex-situ: zoos, botanical gardens, seed banks, gene banks.
  • Threats: habitat loss, overexploitation, pollution, invasive species, climate change, poaching.

  • Ex-situ: zoos + botanical gardens + seed banks + gene banks + cryopreservation (outside habitat)
