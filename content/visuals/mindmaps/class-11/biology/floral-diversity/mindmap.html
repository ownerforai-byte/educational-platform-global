Topic

Three Domains and Classification

Subtopics
1.
Name

Key points

Points
  • Three domains: Bacteria, Archaea, Eukarya (based on 16S rRNA).
  • Five kingdoms: Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, Animalia (Whittaker, 1969).
  • Taxonomic hierarchy: 8 ranks from Domain to Species.
  • Binomial nomenclature: Genus species (italicised, two-part Latin name).
  • Alternation of generations: key feature of all land plants.
2.
Name

Formulas & relations

Points
  • Binomial nomenclature: Genus + specific epithet (e.g., Homo sapiens)
  • Alternation of generations: Sporophyte (2n) → Spores (n) → Gametophyte (n) → Gametes → Zygote (2n)
3.
Name

Statements to quote

Points
  • The three-domain system reorganised all life into three primary lineages based on 16S rRNA sequence analysis.
  • Whittaker's five-kingdom system remains the most widely used classification in school-level biology.
  • All land plants undergo alternation of generations between haploid and diploid phases.

Fungi â€” General Features and Types

Subtopics
1.
Name

Key points

Points
  • Fungi are eukaryotic, heterotrophic, chitin cell wall, no chloroplasts.
  • Four groups: Phycomycetes, Ascomycetes, Basidiomycetes, Deuteromycetes.
  • Body = mycelium (hyphae); reproduction = asexual (spores/budding) + sexual.
  • Ascomycetes → asci; Basidiomycetes → basidia.
  • Economic roles: decomposers, food, medicine, industry; harmful: diseases, crop blights.
2.
Name

Formulas & relations

Points
  • Fungal cell wall: chitin (N-acetylglucosamine polymer)
  • Yeast budding: mother cell → bud → daughter cell (asexual)
  • Ascomycete spore: ascus (sac) containing ascospores (8 typically)
  • Basidiomycete spore: basidium (club) bearing basidiospores (4 typically)

Statements to quote

Points
  • Fungi are the primary decomposers in most ecosystems, recycling organic matter.
  • The chitin cell wall distinguishes fungi from plants (cellulose) and animals (no wall).
  • Ascomycetes and Basidiomycetes are the two most evolutionarily advanced fungal groups.

Topic

Algae

Subtopics
1.
Name

Key points

Points
  • Algae are photosynthetic, mostly aquatic, lack true vascular tissues.
  • Three major groups: Chrysophyceae, Pyrrophyceae, Chlorophyceae.
  • Ecological role: ~50% of Earth's O₂; base of aquatic food chains.
  • Reproduction: asexual (spores, fragmentation) + sexual (isogamy, anisogamy, oogamy).
  • Economic uses: food, industry (agar, alginates), biofertilisers.
2.
Name

Formulas & relations

Points
  • Green algae: Chlorophyll a + b, store starch
  • Brown algae: Chlorophyll a + c + fucoxanthin, store laminarin
  • Red algae: Chlorophyll a + d + phycobilins, store floridean starch
  • Diatom cell wall: SiO₂ (silica)
3.
Name

Statements to quote

Points
  • Algae are the primary producers of most aquatic ecosystems.
  • They contribute approximately 50% of global oxygen via photosynthesis.
  • Diatoms have silica cell walls and form the base of the marine food web.

  • Green algae = starch; Brown = laminarin; Red = floridean starch.
