What is the difference between accuracy and precision? Illustrate with an example.

Marks

Solution

Accuracy = how close a measurement is to the true value; Precision = how close repeated measurements are to each other.

Example: A dartboard â€” if all darts cluster near the bullseye, the throws are both accurate and precise. If they cluster far from the bullseye, they are precise but inaccurate. If they are scattered widely around the bullseye, they are accurate on average but not precise.

Knowledge Note

Accuracy relates to systematic error; precision relates to random error. More measurements improve precision but not accuracy.

1.

State Newton's three laws of motion. Give an example of each.

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

First Law (Inertia): A body at rest stays at rest, and a body in motion continues in a straight line at constant velocity unless acted on by an external force. Example: passengers in a bus lurch forward when brakes are applied.

Second Law: F = ma. Example: a cricket ball accelerates when a bat applies force.

Third Law: For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction. Example: a rocket pushes gas downward and is propelled upward.

Knowledge Note

The three laws hold in inertial frames only; the third implies momentum and force conservation.
