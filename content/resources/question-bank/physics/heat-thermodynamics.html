Solution

If two systems are each in thermal equilibrium with a third system, they are in thermal equilibrium with each other. It is called the 'zeroth' law because it logically precedes the first and second laws, and was recognized after them but is more fundamental â€” hence 'zero'.

What is the difference between heat and temperature? Give their SI units.

Marks

2

Year

2022

Source

NEB Past

Solution

Heat is the energy transferred between systems due to temperature difference. Temperature is a measure of the average kinetic energy of particles.

Heat: SI unit = Joule (J) (energy transfer). Temperature: SI unit = Kelvin (K).

Knowledge Note

Heat is a mode of transfer; temperature is the degree of hotness measured by a thermometer.

1.

State the kinetic theory of gases. Also state its assumptions.

Marks

5

Year

2023

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Kinetic theory: Gas pressure arises from molecular collisions with container walls. The pressure is proportional to the average kinetic energy of molecules.

Assumptions:

  1. Gas consists of many molecules in random motion.
  2. Molecules are point particles with negligible volume.
  3. No intermolecular forces between molecules (except during collisions).
  4. Collisions are perfectly elastic (no KE loss).
  5. Time of collision is negligible compared to time between collisions.
  6. Molecules move with constant velocity between collisions.

2.

What is the law of equipartition of energy? How many degrees of freedom does a monoatomic gas molecule have?

Marks

2

Year

2021

Source

NEB Model

Solution

The law states that energy is equally divided among all available degrees of freedom, each getting (1/2)kT of energy.

A monoatomic gas molecule has 3 translational degrees of freedom (motion along x, y, z). Its molar specific heat at constant volume: Cv = (3/2)R.

Knowledge Note

At thermal equilibrium, a monatomic gas has 3 translational degrees of freedom, a diatomic gas 5.
