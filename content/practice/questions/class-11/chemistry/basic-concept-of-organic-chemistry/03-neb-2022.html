Questions

1.

Define a functional group and a homologous series. State any three characteristics of a homologous series, and write the general formula of the alkanol series with two examples.

Marks

3

Year

2022

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Functional group. An atom or group of atoms present in every member of a family of organic compounds that largely determines its chemical properties, e.g. –OH (alcohols), –COOH (carboxylic acids), –CHO (aldehydes).

Homologous series. A family of organic compounds that differ from one another by one or more –CH₂– units and have similar chemical properties but gradually varying physical properties, e.g. CH₃OH, C₂H₅OH, C₃H₇OH.

Three characteristics.

  1. All members contain the same functional group and behave chemically alike.
  2. Consecutive members differ by a CH₂ unit, i.e. by 14 mass units.
  3. They are represented by a general formula, and physical properties such as boiling point, melting point and density change gradually and regularly with increasing molecular mass.
  4. Physical properties change gradually but chemical properties remain the same.

Alkanols. Functional group –OH joined to a saturated carbon. General formula:

Cn​H2n+2​O

Members are methanol CH3​OH (b.p. 64.7 °C), ethanol C2​H5​OH (78 °C), propan-1-ol C3​H7​OH (97 °C). The boiling points rise regularly by about 20–25 °C per CH₂ unit.

Knowledge Note

A homologous series shares one functional group, has a general formula, and consecutive members differ by a CH₂ unit.
