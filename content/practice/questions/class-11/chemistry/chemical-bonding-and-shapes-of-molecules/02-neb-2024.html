NEB Grade 11 Model Questions / NEB Past Papers 2020-2024

Questions

1.

Write the Lewis dot structure of CO₂. What is its molecular shape?

Marks

3

Year

2022

Source

NEB Model

Solution

C (4 valence e⁻) shares double bonds with each O (6 valence e⁻): O=C=O, every atom octet-complete.

VSEPR: 2 bond pairs, 0 lone pairs on carbon → linear, 180°.

Knowledge Note

Linear symmetry cancels the two C=O dipoles — CO₂ is non-polar despite polar bonds.

2.

Draw the Lewis structures of H₂O and NH₃ and predict their shapes using VSEPR theory.

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

H₂O: O has 2 bond pairs + 2 lone pairs → bent (V-shaped), 104.5°.

NH₃: N has 3 bond pairs + 1 lone pair → trigonal pyramidal, 107°.

Lone pairs repel more strongly than bond pairs, squeezing both angles below the tetrahedral 109.5°.

Knowledge Note

Lone-pair repulsion order lp–lp > lp–bp > bp–bp explains the whole 109.5° → 107° → 104.5° series.

3.

Distinguish between ionic and covalent bonds with one example of each.

Marks

2

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Ionic: complete electron transfer, oppositely charged ions in a lattice — e.g. NaCl. High mp/bp, conducts when molten, brittle.

Covalent: mutual sharing of electron pairs — e.g. H₂, CH₄. Low mp/bp, poor conductors, often gases/liquids.

Knowledge Note

Large electronegativity difference (ΔEN > 1.7) favours ionic; small ΔEN favours covalent — a continuum, not a wall.

4.

State Fajan's rules. How do they explain the covalent character of LiCl vs NaCl?

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Fajan's rules: covalent character grows with (1) small, highly charged cations, (2) large, easily polarised anions.

Li⁺ is far smaller than Na⁺, so it distorts Cl⁻'s electron cloud more — LiCl is markedly more covalent (more soluble in organic solvents, lower melting) than NaCl.

Knowledge Note

Polarising power ∝ charge/(radius)² — the same rule orders BeCl₂ > MgCl₂ > CaCl₂ in covalency.

5.

What is hydrogen bonding? Explain why water has an unusually high boiling point.

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Hydrogen bonding is the strong dipole attraction between H bound to N/O/F and a lone pair on a neighbouring N/O/F.

Each H₂O molecule H-bonds to four neighbours, forming an extensive network. Breaking this network needs extra energy — so water boils at 100°C while similar-mass H₂S (no H-bonding) boils at −60°C.

Knowledge Note

H-bonding also explains ice floating (open tetrahedral network) and proteins' shapes — one concept, many NEB questions.

6.

Why is NH₃ pyramidal while CH₄ is tetrahedral, though both have four electron pairs?

Marks

2

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Both have tetrahedral electron geometry, but shape counts only atom positions.

CH₄ has 4 bond pairs → tetrahedral molecule. NH₃ has 3 bond pairs + 1 lone pair → the lone pair occupies one tetrahedral corner unseen, leaving a pyramid of three H atoms.

Knowledge Note

Electron geometry vs molecular shape: identical pairs, different visible skeletons — the VSEPR distinction NEB tests most.
