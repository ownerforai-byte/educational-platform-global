NEB Grade 11 Model Questions / NEB Past Papers 2020-2024

Questions

1.

What is the modern periodic law? Explain how it improved on Mendeleev's periodic law.

Marks

3

Year

2022

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Modern periodic law: properties of elements are periodic functions of their atomic numbers.

It fixed Mendeleev (atomic-mass) defects: (1) anomalous pairs (Ar/K, Co/Ni) fall in correct order; (2) no unexplained gaps remain — all 118 elements placed; (3) noble gases get a natural group without disturbing the table.

Knowledge Note

Moseley (1913) and X-ray spectra moved the table from mass to atomic number.

2.

What are s-, p-, d-, and f-blocks? Give one element belonging to each.

Marks

2

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Blocks named by the subshell receiving the last electron: s-block — Na; p-block — Cl; d-block (transition metals) — Fe; f-block (inner transition) — U.

s + p = representative elements; d = transition; f = lanthanides/actinides.

Knowledge Note

Helium (1s²) sits with the p-block noble gases by behaviour, not by configuration — the famous exception.

3.

Define ionisation energy. Explain why nitrogen has a higher first ionisation energy than oxygen.

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Ionisation energy is the energy needed to remove the outermost electron from a gaseous atom.

N (1s² 2s² 2p³) has a half-filled p subshell — extra stable. Removing its electron breaks that stability, costing more energy than removing one from O (2p⁴), where repulsion between paired electrons actually helps ejection.

Knowledge Note

Half-filled/full subshells (N, P, Be, Mg, noble gases) always spike above the trend — the standard 'anomaly' question.

4.

Define electronegativity. How does it vary across a period and down a group? Which element is the most electronegative?

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Electronegativity is an atom's pull on shared bond electrons.

It increases left→right (rising nuclear charge) and decreases top→bottom (larger, shielded atoms). Fluorine (4.0) is the maximum.

Knowledge Note

Noble gases have no EN values (no bonds to pull in) — a tidy one-mark follow-up.

5.

How does atomic radius vary across a period and down a group? Explain the trend in each case.

Marks

2

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Across a period it shrinks: added protons pull the same shell tighter.

Down a group it grows: each new principal shell outweighs the extra nuclear charge.

Knowledge Note

Cations < parent atom < anions — size order within one element's ions.

6.

What is diagonal relationship? Give one example with cause.

Marks

2

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Diagonally adjacent elements (e.g. Li and Mg) resemble each other more than their own group members.

Cause: similar charge density (charge/radius) — moving right shrinks size while moving down grows it, roughly cancelling along the diagonal. Both form nitrides, sparingly soluble carbonates, and similar organometallics.

Knowledge Note

Li–Mg, Be–Al, B–Si are the three classic diagonal pairs.
