Solution

For f(x)=x3−2x−5 we have f′(x)=3x2−2, and Newton's formula is xn+1​=xn​−f′(xn​)f(xn​)​

f(2)=8−4−5=−1, f′(2)=3(4)−2=10.

First iteration: x1​=2−10−1​=2+0.1=2.1

Second iteration: f(2.1)=9.261−4.2−5=0.061, f′(2.1)=3(4.41)−2=11.23. x2​=2.1−11.230.061​=2.1−0.005432=2.094568≈2.0946

Third iteration: f(2.0946)≈9.189743−4.1892−5=0.000543, f′(2.0946)=3(4.38735)−2=11.16205. x3​=2.0946−11.162050.000543​=2.0946−0.0000486=2.0945514

Since x2​ and x3​ agree to three decimal places, x≈2.0946​

Knowledge Note

Stop Newton-Raphson only when two successive approximations agree to the required number of decimal places.
