1.

Solve: cos 2θ = cos θ

Marks

4

Year

2022

Source

NEB Past

Solution

cos 2θ = cos θ 2cos²θ - 1 = cos θ 2cos²θ - cos θ - 1 = 0 (2cos θ + 1)(cos θ - 1) = 0

Case 1: cos θ = 1 → θ = 0, 2π Case 2: cos θ = -1/2 → θ = 2π/3, 4π/3

Solutions: θ = 0, 2π/3, 4π/3, 2π

Knowledge Note

cos 2θ = 2cos²θ - 1 = 1 - 2sin²θ = cos²θ - sin²θ
