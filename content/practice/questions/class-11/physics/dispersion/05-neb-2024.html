Questions

1.

What is dispersion of light? Explain its cause with reference to refractive index.

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Dispersion is the splitting of white light into its colours by a prism.

Cause: refractive index depends on wavelength (Cauchy: n = a + b/λ²), so violet (short λ) is slowed and deviated most while red deviates least — the beam fans out into a spectrum.

Knowledge Note

Dispersion needs a wavelength-dependent n; a mirror reflects all colours equally, so spectra never form there.

2.

Define angular dispersion and dispersive power of a prism. Write the formula for dispersive power.

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Angular dispersion (δv − δr) is the angle between the extreme violet and red rays.

Dispersive power ω = (δv − δr)/δy = (nv − nr)/(ny − 1) — the spread per unit mean deviation, a pure number characterising the glass.

Knowledge Note

Flint glass (ω ≈ 0.05) spreads far more than crown (ω ≈ 0.02) — material choice, not prism size, sets ω.

3.

For a glass, nv = 1.53, nr = 1.51, and ny = 1.52. Calculate its dispersive power.

Marks

3

Year

2024

NEB Model

Solution

ω = (nv − nr)/(ny − 1) = (1.53 − 1.51)/(1.52 − 1) = 0.02/0.52 ≈ 0.0385.

Knowledge Note

ω has no units — if your answer carries any, the formula went wrong.

4.

Explain the formation of the primary rainbow.

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Sunlight enters a raindrop (refraction → dispersion), reflects once inside (TIR), and refracts out again. Each colour leaves at its own ~42° cone angle.

Violet bends most (inner edge), red least (outer edge) — the sky wears a 42° circular spectrum centred on the antisolar point.

Knowledge Note

Secondary rainbow: two internal reflections, reversed colours, wider 51° cone — fainter because each reflection loses light.

5.

What is an achromatic combination of prisms? Write its condition.

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Two prisms of different glasses that cancel each other's dispersion while keeping a net deviation — white light stays white.

Condition: equal and opposite angular dispersions, (δv − δr)₁ = (δv − δr)₂, i.e. ω₁δy₁ = ω₂δy₂ with the prisms oppositely oriented.

Knowledge Note

Camera lenses stack crown (converging) and flint (diverging) elements on exactly this condition — achromatic doublets.

6.

Why does red light deviate least and violet most through a prism?

Marks

2

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Deviation grows with refractive index, and n is largest for the shortest wavelength (violet interacts most strongly with the glass's electrons).

So violet is slowed and bent most, red least — VIBGYOR order from most to least deviated.

Knowledge Note

n_red < n_yellow < n_violet is the three-inequality chain behind every dispersion numerical.
