Prompt

State the first law of thermodynamics and give three of its limitations.

Solution

First law: the change in internal energy of a system (ΔU) equals the heat supplied to it (Q) minus the work done by it (W): ΔU=Q−W. Limitations: (1) it says nothing about the direction of natural processes — why heat flows hot to cold and never the reverse; (2) it does not define entropy or disorder; (3) it tracks only energy conservation, not the quality (usefulness) of energy.

Define entropy. What happens to the entropy of the universe in a spontaneous process?

Solution

Entropy (S) is a state function measuring disorder; for a reversible change dS=dQrev​/T. In any spontaneous (irreversible) process the total entropy of the universe — system plus surroundings — increases: ΔSuniv​=ΔSsys​+ΔSsurr​>0. This is the second law; only an ideal reversible process keeps ΔSuniv​=0.
