Questions

1.

Define electric potential at a point. State its SI unit and the relation between electric field and potential.

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Electric potential is the work done per unit positive charge in bringing it from infinity to the point (W/q₀). SI unit: volt (V) = J/C.

Field and potential are linked by E = −dV/dr — the field points down the steepest potential slope.

Knowledge Note

Potential is a scalar (no direction) while field is a vector — potential problems need half the bookkeeping.

2.

Calculate the electric potential at 0.5 m from a point charge of 2 μC in air.

Marks

2

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

V = kq/r = (9 × 10⁹ × 2 × 10⁻⁶)/0.5 = 18,000/0.5 = 36,000 V (36 kV).

Knowledge Note

V ∝ 1/r but E ∝ 1/r² — at large distances the potential outlives the field.

3.

What are equipotential surfaces? Write any three of their properties.

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Surfaces (real or imaginary) over which the potential is constant everywhere.

(1) No work is done moving a charge along one. (2) Field lines always cross them at right angles. (3) Two equipotential surfaces never intersect (that would give one point two potentials).

Knowledge Note

For a point charge they are concentric spheres; for a uniform field, parallel planes.

4.

Define the electron-volt. Convert 1 eV into joules.

Marks

2

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

1 eV is the kinetic energy an electron gains crossing a 1 V potential difference.

1 eV = e × 1 V = (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹)(1) = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J.

Knowledge Note

Atomic energies live in eV, nuclear in MeV — converting to joules first is the safe route in combined problems.

5.

Two charges +3 μC and −2 μC are placed 0.3 m apart in air. Calculate the electric potential energy of the system.

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

U = kq₁q₂/r = (9 × 10⁹)(3 × 10⁻⁶)(−2 × 10⁻⁶)/0.3

U = −54 × 10⁻³/0.3 = −0.18 J.

The negative sign means work must be done to separate them — opposite charges bind the system.

Knowledge Note

Like charges give positive U (work stored pushing them together); unlike charges give negative U (bound).

6.

Show that the electric potential everywhere inside a uniformly charged hollow sphere equals its surface value.

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Inside the shell E = 0 (Gauss's law — no enclosed charge). Since E = −dV/dr, zero field means dV/dr = 0: V is constant throughout the interior.

At the surface r = R it must join the outside value Q/(4πε₀R), so V = Q/(4πε₀R) everywhere inside, right up to the centre.

Knowledge Note

Constant potential ≠ zero potential — the interior sits at the full surface voltage, which is why a hollow conductor is still dangerous to touch.
