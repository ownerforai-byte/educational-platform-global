NEB-Style Questions — Vectors

Class 11PhysicsVectors

Notes

  • Q1. (Derivation) State and prove the parallelogram law of vector addition. Ans: If two vectors P, Q​ are represented by adjacent sides of a parallelogram, the resultant is the diagonal. From the geometry (perpendicular dropped on the extension of P): R=P2+Q2+2PQcosθ​ and tanα=P+QcosθQsinθ​.
  • Q2. (Numerical) Two forces 3 N and 4 N act at a point with an angle of 90° between them. Find the resultant. Ans: R=32+42+2(3)(4)cos90°​=9+16​=5N, direction α=tan−1(4/3)≈53.1°.
  • Q3. (Concept) State the conditions under which two vectors are (i) perpendicular (ii) parallel. Ans: (i) A⋅B=0; (ii) A×B=0.
  • Q4. (Resolution) Resolve F=10N at 30° above the horizontal into its components, cos30°=0.866, sin30°=0.5. Ans: Fx​=10cos30°=8.66N; Fy​=10sin30°=5N.
  • Q5. (Definition) Distinguish between scalar and vector quantities with examples. Ans: Scalar has magnitude only (mass, speed, work, temperature); vector has magnitude and direction (velocity, force, displacement, momentum).
  • Q6. (Dot product) Two vectors A=3i^+4j^​ and B=i^+2j^​. Find the angle between them. Ans: A⋅B=3+8=11; ∣A∣=5, ∣B∣=5​; cosθ=11/(5√5)≈0.984, so θ≈10.3°.
  • Q7. (Cross product) Find i^×j^​ and i^×k^. Ans: i^×j^​=k^; i^×k^=−j^​ (right-hand rule).
