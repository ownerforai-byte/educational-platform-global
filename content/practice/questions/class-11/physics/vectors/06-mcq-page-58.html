Notes

  • Q28. Two equal forces are acting at a point with an angle of 90° between them. If the resultant force is equal to 202​ N, the magnitude of each force is:

    (a) 40N (b) 20N (c) 80N (d) 30N

    Answer: (b) 20N

    Explanation: For two equal forces F at 90°, the resultant is given by R=F2+F2+2F2cos(90°)​=2F2​=F2​. Given R=202​ N, substituting gives F2​=202​, which simplifies directly to F=20N.

  • Q29. Two forces 3N and 2N are at an angle θ such that the resultant is R. The first force is now increased to 6N and the resultant becomes 2R. The value of θ is:

    (a) 30° (b) 60° (c) 90° (d) 120°

    Answer: (d) 120°

    Explanation: From Case 1: R2=32+22+2(3)(2)cos(θ)=13+12cos(θ). From Case 2: (2R)2=62+22+2(6)(2)cos(θ)⇒4R2=40+24cos(θ). Substituting 4(13+12cos(θ))=40+24cos(θ) simplifies to 52+48cos(θ)=40+24cos(θ)⇒24cos(θ)=−12⇒cos(θ)=−1/2, meaning θ=120°.

  • Q30. Two forces F1 and F2 are acting on a body. One force is triple that of the other force and the resultant is equal to the greater force. Then the angle between the two forces is:

    (a) cos−1(1/2) (b) cos−1(−1/2) (c) cos−1(−1/6) (d) cos−1(1/4)

    Answer: (c) cos−1(−1/6)

    Explanation: Let the forces be F and 3F, with the resultant R=3F. Using the law of cosines: (3F)2=F2+(3F)2+2(F)(3F)cos(θ)⇒9F2=F2+9F2+6F2cos(θ). This reduces to 0=F2+6F2cos(θ)⇒6F2cos(θ)=−F2⇒cos(θ)=−1/6, yielding θ=cos−1(−1/6).

  • Q31. Two vectors A and B have equal magnitude. If magnitude of (A+B) is equal to n times the magnitude of (A−B), then the angle between A and B is:

    (a) cos−1((n−1)/(n+1)) (b) cos−1((n2−1)/(n2+1)) (c) sin−1((n−1)/(n+1)) (d) sin−1((n2−1)/(n2+1))

    Answer: (b) cos−1((n2−1)/(n2+1))

    Explanation: Let ∣A∣=∣B∣=x. Then ∣A+B∣2=2x2(1+cos(θ)) and ∣A−B∣2=2x2(1−cos(θ)). Given ∣A+B∣=n∣A−B∣, squaring both sides gives: 2x2(1+cos(θ))=n2⋅2x2(1−cos(θ))⇒1+cos(θ)=n2−n2cos(θ)⇒cos(θ)(n2+1)=n2−1⇒cos(θ)=(n2−1)/(n2+1), which gives θ=cos−1((n2−1)/(n2+1)).

  • Q32. The maximum and minimum resultant of two forces acting at a point are 15N and 5N respectively. If each force is decreased by 2N, the resultant of new forces when acting at a point at an angle of 90° with each other is:

    (a) 146​ N (b) 11N (c) 73​ N (d) 8N

    Answer: (c) 73​ N

    Explanation: Maximum resultant F1​+F2​=15, and Minimum resultant F1​−F2​=5. Solving this linear system gives F1​=10N and F2​=5N. Reducing both by 2N yields new forces F1′​=8N and F2′​=3N. At 90°, the new resultant R=(8)2+(3)2​=64+9​=73​ N.

  • Two equal forces at 90° have resultant F√2, so a resultant of 20√2 N means each force is 20 N. Separately, the maximum and minimum resultants of two forces are their sum and their difference, which is a pair of linear equations for the two unknown forces.

Exercises

1.

Two equal forces at 90° have resultant 20√2 N. Find each force. Then, from a different pair whose maximum resultant is 15 N and minimum is 5 N, find the 90° resultant after each force is reduced by 2 N.

Steps
  • F√2 = 20√2, so F = 20 N.
  • For the second pair, F1 + F2 = 15 and F1 − F2 = 5.
  • F1 = 10 N and F2 = 5 N.
  • After the reduction the forces are 8 N and 3 N.
  • At 90°, R = √(64 + 9) = √73 N.
Answer

Each equal force is 20 N. The reduced pair gives √73 N.

Summary

Equal perpendicular forces add as F√2, so 20√2 N means 20 N each. A pair with maximum 15 N and minimum 5 N is 10 N and 5 N. Reduced by 2 N and set at 90°, they give √73 N.
