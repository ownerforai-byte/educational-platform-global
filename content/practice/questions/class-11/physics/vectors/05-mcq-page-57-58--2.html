  • Q22. If the vectors (2i+3j) and 2k form two sides of a triangle, then area of the triangle is:

    (a) 3​ unit (b) 13​ unit (c) 22​ unit (d) 32​ unit

    Answer: (b) 13​ unit

    Explanation: Area =0.5×∣A×B∣=0.5×∣(2i+3j)×2k∣=0.5×∣−4j+6i∣. Calculating cross-magnitude: 0.5×62+(−4)2​=0.5×52​=0.5×213​=13​.

  • Q23. With respect to a rectangular Cartesian coordinate system, three vectors are expressed... the unit vector along the direction of their sum is:

    (a) (i+j+k)/3​ (b) (i+j+k)/3 (c) (i−j+k)/3​ (d) (i+j−k)/3​

    Answer: (a) (i+j+k)/3​

    Explanation: Summing symmetric vectors leads to a resultant with equivalent component steps along each dimension. Standardized normalization maps this directly to option (a).

  • Q24. Which of the following figures represents the force of 10 N in a direction of 30° east of north?

    (a) Figure A (b) Figure B (c) Figure C (d) Figure D

    Answer: (b) Figure B

    Explanation: A vector heading 'East of North' means the angular separation arc is centered directly on the North axis line and sweeps 30° outward toward the East quadrant.

  • Q25. Three forces acting on a body keep it in equilibrium. If these forces are:

    (a) 7N, 8N, 3N (b) 10N, 8N, 15N (c) 5N, 15N, 5N (d) 2N, 6N, 3N

    Answer: (d) 2N, 6N, 3N

    Explanation: For structural spatial static equilibrium, the vectors must satisfy the triangle inequality theorem: the sum of the magnitudes of any two forces must be greater than or equal to the third. Option (d) fails since 2+3=5<6.

  • Q26. If P+Q=R and ∣P∣=∣Q∣=∣R∣, then the angle between P and Q is:

    (a) 60° (b) 120° (c) 90° (d) 180°

    Answer: (b) 120°

    Explanation: Equal magnitudes forming an equilibrium triad represent a perfect equilateral vector triangle geometry. The exterior angle representing the transition track between consecutive vector headings is 180°−60°=120°.

  • Q27. 20 coplanar forces each equal to 10 N act on a body. Each force makes angle π/10 with preceding force. What is the resultant?

    (a) 1000 N (b) 500 N (c) 250 N (d) Zero

    Answer: (d) Zero

    Explanation: The incremental shift value π/10 converts to 18°. Total angular sweep across all elements tracking sequentially =20×18°=360°. This forms a perfectly closed polygon where vectors natively cancel to Zero.
