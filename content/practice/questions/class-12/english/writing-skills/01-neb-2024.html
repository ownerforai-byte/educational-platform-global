NEB Grade 12 Model Questions / NEB Past Papers 2020-2024

Questions

1.

Write a letter to the editor of a national daily about reckless driving in your city, suggesting measures to control it.

Marks

5

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Format: sender's address → date → receiver's address (The Editor, …) → subject ('Reckless Driving in [City] — A Growing Threat') → salutation (Sir/Madam) → body → closing (Yours faithfully, name).

Body, para 1: the problem — over-speeding, drunk driving and mobile use are killing pedestrians weekly; a recent accident at a main crossing as proof.

Para 2: causes — weak enforcement, easy driving licences, unpunished insurance fraud, no zebra-crossing discipline.

Para 3: suggestions — fixed speed cameras and breathalyser checks at night, strict licence tests, higher fines, public awareness in schools, and better pedestrian infrastructure.

Close: appeal for the paper's support in pressing the authority. Sign 'Yours faithfully'.

Knowledge Note

A letter to the editor is formal: subject line and 'Yours faithfully' (not 'Yours sincerely'), no slang, and solutions rather than only complaints.

2.

Write an essay on 'The Impact of Social Media on Young People' in about 250 words.

Marks

5

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Introduction: social media is now the main street of young lives; how it shapes them decides how the next generation thinks.

Body — advantages: instant connection and learning resources; platforms to publish and organise — students' campaigns and small businesses have grown on it; sharing of study material and cross-border friendships.

Body — harms: shortened attention, comparison-driven anxiety and low self-esteem, sleep loss, cyberbullying, misinformation, and echo chambers that narrow thought; privacy traded for 'likes'.

Balanced argument: the tool is neutral; effects depend on use — an hour of learning or three hours of scrolling. Digital literacy, parental guidance and self-limits decide the outcome.

Conclusion: social media should serve the user's purpose, not consume it; the skill young people need is not abstinence but attention.

Knowledge Note

Use a clear three-part structure with topic sentences; a balanced essay acknowledges both sides before stating a view.

3.

Write a report on your school's annual sports day for the school magazine.

Marks

3

Year

2024

Source

NEB Model

Solution

Headline: Annual Sports Day 2024 Held with Enthusiasm

Opening: date, venue and occasion — the two-day event at the school ground, inaugurated by the principal, with the chief guest, teachers, parents and 1,200 students present.

Events: march past and house-wise parade; track events (100 m, 200 m, relay), long jump, high jump, volleyball and chess; a fun race for alumni and teachers; the new record in the relay.

Highlights: the green house won the overall championship; a differently-abled student's 50 m sprint drew a standing ovation.

Closing: the principal's speech on sportsmanship and discipline, medals distribution and vote of thanks; the event ended with the national anthem.

(signed) — Reporter, Class 12

Knowledge Note

A report is factual and past-tense, with a headline, an opening that answers what/where/when/who, and a neutral tone; avoid first-person emotion.
