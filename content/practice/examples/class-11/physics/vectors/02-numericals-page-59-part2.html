  • Q7. Calculate the value of (a) (2i−j)⋅(3i+k) (b) i⋅(2i−3j+k)

    Answer: 6, 2

    Solution: (a) (2i−j)⋅(3i+0j+k)=(2)(3)+(−1)(0)+(0)(1)=6. (b) (1i+0j+0k)⋅(2i−3j+k)=(1)(2)+0+0=2.

  • Q8. If three force vectors F1​=(2i+4j) N, F2​=(i−3j) N and F3​=(−12i+5j) N satisfy the relation mF1​+nF2​+F3​=0, determine the values of m and n.

    Answer: m=31/10, n=29/5

    Solution: Expand component equations: x-axis: m(2)+n(1)−12=0⇒2m+n=12. y-axis: m(4)+n(−3)+5=0⇒4m−3n=−5. Multiplying the first equation by 3 yields 6m+3n=36. Adding that to the second equation reveals: 10m=31⇒m=31/10. Substitute back: 2(31/10)+n=12⇒31/5+n=12⇒n=12−31/5=29/5.

  • Q9. In fig, the angle between the vector P and the X-axis is 25° and the angle between the vector Q and X-axis is 70°. What is the resultant of P and Q if P=7 units and Q=8 units.

    Answer: 13.86 units, α=24.23°

    Solution: The real relative angular separation between P and Q is θ=70°−25°=45°. Using the law of cosines: R=72+82+2(7)(8)cos(45°)​=49+64+112×0.707​=113+79.19​=192.19​≈13.86 units. Direction relative to vector P: tan(ϕ)=8sin(45°)/(7+8cos(45°))=5.657/(7+5.657)=5.657/12.657=0.447⇒ϕ≈24.09°.

  • Q10. A force vector is given as F1​=(4i−3j) N. Find the vector F2​ of magnitude 10N which is perpendicular to F1​.

    Answer: F2​=(6i+8j) N or (−6i−8j) N

    Solution: Let F2​=xi+yj. Perpendicularity requires 4x−3y=0⇒4x=3y⇒x=0.75y. Magnitude condition requires x2+y2=102=100. Substituting yields (0.75y)2+y2=100⇒0.5625y2+y2=100⇒1.5625y2=100⇒y2=64⇒y=±8. If y=8, x=6. If y=−8, x=−6. Thus, F2​ is either (6i+8j)N or (−6i−8j)N.

  • Q11. If there are two forces F1​=500N due east and F2​=600 N due north, then subtract F2​ from F1​.

    Answer: 780N at 50.19° South of East

    Solution: Vector expression: F1​=500i, F2​=600j. Subtraction R=F1​−F2​=500i−600j. Magnitude =5002+(−600)2​=250000+360000​=610000​≈781N (textbook rounds to 780N). Direction: tan(θ)=∣Ry​∣/Rx​=600/500=1.2⇒θ=tan−1(1.2)≈50.19°. Since Ry​ is negative (south) and Rx​ is positive (east), direction maps to 50.19° South of East.

  • Q12. The vector F is a force of 3 newton making an angle of 60° with the displacement R of magnitude 4m as shown in the figure aside. Find (i) the value of the dot product F⋅R and (ii) the magnitude and direction of the cross product R×F.

    Answer: (i) 6Nm, (ii) 63​ Nm, directed perpendicular to the plane containing F and R

    Solution: (i) Dot Product =F⋅R⋅cos(θ)=3×4×cos(60°)=12×0.5=6 Nm. (ii) Cross Product magnitude =R⋅F⋅sin(θ)=4×3×sin(60°)=12×(3​/2)=63​ Nm. By the right-hand rule, turning from R to F tips the direction directly outwards perpendicular to the page plane.
