1.

Q1(i). Find, from the first principle, the derivative of log(ax + b).

Knowledge Note

First principles for log: [log(ax+ah+b) − log(ax+b)]/h = (1/h)log(1 + ah/(ax+b)) → a/(ax+b) using log(1+u)/u → 1.

Marks

4

Solution

[log(a(x+h)+b) − log(ax+b)]/h = (1/h)log[(ax+b+ah)/(ax+b)] = (1/h)log[1 + ah/(ax+b)]. Put u = ah/(ax+b): the expression is [a/(ax+b)]·[log(1+u)/u] → a/(ax+b)·1 as h→0. Boxed a/(ax+b) (matches the printed key, book p531).

Knowledge Note
