20.

Q8. Same bisectors ⟹ h(a'-b') = h'(a-b).

Knowledge Note

Bisectors coincide ⟺ the bisector equation h(x²-y²) = (a-b)xy is the same pair.

Marks

5

Solution

Bisectors of first: h(x²-y²)-(a-b)xy = 0. Of second: h'(x²-y²)-(a'-b')xy = 0. Same pair ⟺ coefficients proportional: h/h' = (a-b)/(a'-b'). Cross: h(a'-b') = h'(a-b). ∎ (proof-type; no key answer needed).

Source

Sukunda Basic Mathematics Grade XI, Ch.14 Ex 14.1 Q8; book p428 = PDF p455 (vision read)
