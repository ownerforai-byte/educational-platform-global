Q1(a). Angles between x-√3y = a and √3x-y = b.

Knowledge Note

tan θ = (m₁-m₂)/(1+m₁m₂); slopes 1/√3 and √3.

Marks

4

Solution

m₁ = 1/√3, m₂ = √3. tanθ = (√3-1/√3)/(1+1) = (2/√3)/2 = 1/√3. Boxed θ = 30° (matches the printed key, book p398).

10.

Q4. k so that 3x-ky-8 = 0 makes 45° with 3x+5y-17 = 0.

Knowledge Note

tan45° = 1: (3/k+3/5)/(1-9/5k) = ±1 gives k = 12, -3/4.

Marks

4

Solution

m₁ = 3/k, m₂ = -3/5. (3/k+3/5)/(1-9/5k) = ±1: +1 gives 15+3k = 5k-9, k = 12; -1 gives 15+3k = -5k+9, k = -3/4. Boxed k = 12 or -3/4 (matches the printed key).

Source

Sukunda Basic Mathematics Grade XI, Ch.13 Ex 13.2 Q4; book p397 = PDF p424 (vision read)
