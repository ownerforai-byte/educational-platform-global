Knowledge Note

(p∧q)∧~(p∨q): p∧q is T only at (T,T), but p∨q=T there so ~ (p∨q)=F → F in every row.

Marks

1

Solution

p q | p∧q | p∨q | ~(p∨q) | (p∧q)∧~(p∨q) T T | T | T | F | F T F | F | T | F | F F T | F | T | F | F F F | F | F | T | F Last column all F → contradiction. ✅ Proven.
