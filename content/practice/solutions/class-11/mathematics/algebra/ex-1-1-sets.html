Q6(a). If n(U) = 100, n(A) = 75, n(B) = 40 and n(A∪B) = 80, find n(A∩B), n(A−B) and n(overline{A∪B}).

Knowledge Note

Inclusion-exclusion for two sets: n(A∪B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A∩B). Rearranged, the overlap is the sum minus the union. Complement: n(Ā∪B̄) = n(U) − n(A∪B) by De Morgan.

Marks

3

Solution

Step 1 — Overlap: n(A∩B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A∪B) = 75 + 40 − 80 = 35. Step 2 — Only-A: n(A−B) = n(A) − n(A∩B) = 75 − 35 = 40 (remove the overlapped part from A). Step 3 — Neither: n(overline{A∪B}) = n(U) − n(A∪B) = 100 − 80 = 20. ✅ Answers: 35, 40, 20. (Book key 6.a) = 35, 40, 20 ✔)
