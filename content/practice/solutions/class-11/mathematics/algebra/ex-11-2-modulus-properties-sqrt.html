Q5. Prove that z̄/|z|^2 is the multiplicative inverse of z.

Knowledge Note

z·z̄ = |z|², so z·(z̄/|z|²) = 1.

Marks

4

Solution

z·(z̄/|z|²) = (z·z̄)/|z|² = |z|²/|z|² = 1 (for z ≠ 0). So z̄/|z|² is the inverse of z. ∎ (proof-type; no key answer needed).

Q9. If z and w are two complex numbers, prove that |z - w| ≥ |z| - |w|.

Knowledge Note

From Q7 with w → -w: |z-w|² = |z|²+|w|²-2Re(zw̄) ≥ (|z|-|w|)² since Re ≤ |·|.

Marks

5

Solution

|z-w|² = |z|²+|w|²-2Re(zw̄) ≥ |z|²+|w|²-2|zw̄| = |z|²+|w|²-2|z||w| = (|z|-|w|)². Taking square roots: |z-w| ≥ ||z|-|w|| ≥ |z|-|w|. ∎ (proof-type; no key answer needed).
