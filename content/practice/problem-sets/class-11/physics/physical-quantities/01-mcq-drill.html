Notes

  • Q1. Which of these is a fundamental quantity: force, velocity, temperature, work? Answer: Temperature
  • Q2. The dimensional formula of force is: Answer: [M L T⁻²]
  • Q3. The dimensional formula of power is: Answer: [M L² T⁻³]
  • Q4. The number of significant figures in 0.00450 is: Answer: 3 (leading zeros are not significant; the '45' and trailing zero are)
  • Q5. Relative error is defined as: Answer: (mean absolute error) ÷ (mean value)
  • Q6. By the principle of homogeneity, addition is allowed only between: Answer: quantities of the same dimensions
  • Q7. The dimensional formula of the universal gravitational constant G is: Answer: [M⁻¹ L³ T⁻²]
  • Q8. Which quantity is dimensionless: refractive index, force, mass, pressure? Answer: Refractive index
  • Q9. When two quantities have identical dimensions but different meaning, dimensional analysis: Answer: cannot distinguish them (e.g. work and torque)
  • Q10. Order of magnitude of the Sun–Earth distance (1.5 × 10¹¹ m) is: Answer: 10¹¹ m

Confusion

  • A symbol is not a number until the unit is named.
  • A proportional relation is not an equality until the constant is included.
  • A vector answer needs a direction as well as a magnitude.
  • A special case, such as a right angle or a steady temperature, has to be stated before it is used.

Key Points

  • Impulse = F Δt = change of momentum
  • tan of the angle of repose = μ
  • Convert units before substituting.
  • Quote the unit with the result.

Important Statements

  • Impulse is the area under a force-time graph and equals the change of momentum. A steady force for a short time can change the momentum as much as a smaller force acting longer, if the products F Δt match. Action and reaction never cancel on one body, because they act on two bodies.
  • The working relations are Impulse = F Δt = change of momentum and tan of the angle of repose = μ. Name each symbol, convert the data into SI units, and only then substitute.
  • A 2.0 N force acts for 0.40 s on a 0.50 kg body that starts from rest. Find the impulse and the final speed. Impulse = F Δt = 2.0 × 0.40 = 0.80 N s. That equals the change in momentum.

Exercises

1.

A 2.0 N force acts for 0.40 s on a 0.50 kg body that starts from rest. Find the impulse and the final speed.

Steps
  • Impulse = F Δt = 2.0 × 0.40 = 0.80 N s.
  • That equals the change in momentum.
  • 0.80 = m v = 0.50 v.
  • v = 1.6 m/s.
  • The reaction to this force acts on whatever applied the 2.0 N, not on the 0.50 kg body.
Answer

Impulse 0.80 N s, speed 1.6 m/s.

Summary

Impulse equals change of momentum. A 2.0 N force for 0.40 s delivers 0.80 N s and takes a 0.50 kg body from rest to 1.6 m/s. The reaction is on the other body.
