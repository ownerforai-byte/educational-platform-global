  • The magnitude of a vector aî + bĵ + ck̂ is sqrt(a^2 + b^2 + c^2). For A = 5î + 4ĵ, |A| = sqrt(25 + 16) = sqrt(41). For B = 4î + 6ĵ, |B| = sqrt(16 + 36) = sqrt(52).
  • A + B = 9î + 10ĵ, so |A + B| = sqrt(81 + 100) = sqrt(181). A − B = î − 2ĵ, so |A − B| = sqrt(1 + 4) = sqrt(5).
  • The dot product uses components: A·B = 5×4 + 4×6 = 20 + 24 = 44. The angle between A and B then satisfies cosφ = 44 / sqrt(41×52).
  • For a = 2î + 3ĵ + 4k̂ and c = 2î + 3ĵ − 4k̂, the dot product is 4 + 9 − 16 = −3. The negative sign means the angle is greater than 90°.

Confusion

  • A negative component lowers the dot product. It does not get dropped.
  • |A + B| is not |A| + |B| unless the vectors are parallel.
  • The dot product is not a vector and has no î left in it.
  • arccos of a negative number is an obtuse angle, not a negative length.

Key Points

  • Magnitude from the sum of squares.
  • Dot product is the sum of products of components.
  • A negative dot product means an obtuse angle.
  • The angle is arccos of that cosine.

Exercises

1.

Find the angle between a = 2î + 3ĵ + 4k̂ and c = 2î + 3ĵ − 4k̂.

Steps
  • a·c = 2×2 + 3×3 + 4×(−4) = 4 + 9 − 16 = −3.
  • |a| = sqrt(4 + 9 + 16) = sqrt(29).
  • |c| = sqrt(29) as well.
  • cosφ = −3/29 ≈ −0.103.
  • φ = arccos(−0.103) ≈ 96°.
Answer

About 96°, an obtuse angle.

Summary

Magnitudes come from the square root of the sum of squares. The dot product is the sum of the products of matching components, and dividing by the two magnitudes gives the cosine of the angle. A negative dot product means the angle is obtuse.
