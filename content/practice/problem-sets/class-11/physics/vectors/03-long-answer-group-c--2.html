Notes

  • Q1(a). State triangle law of vector addition. Use this law to find out the resultant of two vectors A and B inclined at angle θ.

    Statement: The Triangle Law of Vector Addition states that if two vectors are represented in magnitude and direction by the two sides of a triangle taken in the same order, then their resultant is completely represented in magnitude and direction by the third side of the triangle taken in the opposite order.

  • Mathematical Derivation:

    Let two vectors A and B be represented by sides OP and PQ of a triangle OPQ, inclined at an angle θ to each other. Let R be their resultant represented by the side OQ in the opposite order. To find the magnitude and direction analytically, extend line OP to a point N and draw a perpendicular QN from point Q onto OP extended.

  • Magnitude Proof:

    In right-angled triangle QNP:

    sin(θ)=QN/PQ⇒QN=PQ⋅sin(θ)=B⋅sin(θ)

    cos(θ)=PN/PQ⇒PN=PQ⋅cos(θ)=B⋅cos(θ)

    Now consider the large right-angled triangle QNO:

    OQ2=ON2+QN2

    Since ON=OP+PN:

    OQ2=(OP+PN)2+QN2

    Substitute components:

    R2=(A+B⋅cos(θ))2+(B⋅sin(θ))2

    Expand the algebraic binomial term:

    R2=A2+2AB⋅cos(θ)+B2⋅cos2(θ)+B2⋅sin2(θ)

    Factor out B2 from the trigonometric identities:

    R2=A2+2AB⋅cos(θ)+B2⋅(cos2(θ)+sin2(θ))

    Since cos2(θ)+sin2(θ)=1, we secure the final magnitude formula:

    R=A2+B2+2AB⋅cos(θ)​

  • Direction Proof:

    Let α be the angle made by the resultant vector R with the direction of vector A (along line ON).

    In right-angled triangle QNO:

    tan(α)=QN/ON

    Since ON=OP+PN:

    tan(α)=QN/(OP+PN)

    Substitute our previously resolved scalar values:

    tan(α)=(B⋅sin(θ))/(A+B⋅cos(θ))

    Therefore, the directional orientation angle α is given by:

    α=tan−1(A+B⋅cos(θ)B⋅sin(θ)​)

  • The triangle law puts the tail of the second vector on the head of the first. The resultant runs from the start of the first to the end of the second. Resolving the second vector gives R squared equal to A squared plus B squared plus 2 A B cosθ, and the direction from the first vector follows from the opposite-over-adjacent ratio.
  • The working relations are R = √(A^2 + B^2 + 2 A B cosθ) and tan α = B sinθ / (A + B cosθ). Name each symbol, convert the data into SI units, and only then substitute.

Exercises

1.

A = 3.0 N, B = 4.0 N and θ = 90°. Find R.

Steps
  • cos 90° = 0, so the cross term vanishes.
  • R = √(9 + 16) = √25.
  • R = 5.0 N.
  • tan α = 4/3, so α = 53° from the 3 N force.
  • If θ had been 0°, R would have been 7 N. The angle matters.
Answer

5.0 N at 53° from the 3.0 N force.

Summary

The triangle law gives R = √(A^2 + B^2 + 2AB cosθ). At 90°, 3 N and 4 N give 5 N, at 53° from the 3 N force. Parallel vectors would have given 7 N.
