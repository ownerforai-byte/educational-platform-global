  • For 10 N at 30° above the axis and 5 N at 45° below it, F_x = 10 cos30° + 5 cos45° and F_y = 10 sin30° − 5 sin45°.

Confusion

  • A force below the axis still has a positive x-component if it points to the right.
  • Do not add 10 N and 5 N directly.
  • sin and cos are fixed once θ is measured from the x-axis.
  • The resultant is not the larger component.

Key Points

  • Measure every angle from the +x-axis.
  • Below the axis means a negative y-component.
  • Add components, then use Pythagoras.
  • The angle is arctan(F_y / F_x).

Exercises

1.

Find the resultant of 10 N at 30° above the +x-axis and 5 N at 45° below the +x-axis.

Steps
  • F_x = 10 cos30° + 5 cos45° = 8.66 + 3.54 = 12.2 N.
  • F_y = 10 sin30° − 5 sin45° = 5.00 − 3.54 = 1.46 N.
  • R = sqrt(12.2^2 + 1.46^2) = sqrt(148.8 + 2.13) = sqrt(150.9) = 12.3 N.
  • tanα = 1.46 / 12.2 = 0.120, so α ≈ 6.8°.
  • The resultant is 12.3 N, about 6.8° above the +x-axis.
Answer

12.3 N at about 6.8° above the +x-axis.

Summary

Resolve every force from the same axis, keep the sign of a component that points downward, add the columns, and only then take Pythagoras. The small positive F_y in the 10 N and 5 N example puts the resultant just above the x-axis.
