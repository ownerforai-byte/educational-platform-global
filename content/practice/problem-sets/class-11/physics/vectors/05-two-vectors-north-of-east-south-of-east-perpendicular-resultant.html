Notes

  • If two vectors of equal magnitude 2 m act perpendicular to each other, find the resultant. Let ∣A∣=∣B∣=2m, θ=90A^°: R=A2+B2+2ABcosθ​=22+22+0​=8​=22​m.
  • Direction: tanα=B/A=1⇒α=45A^°, i.e. the resultant lies exactly midway between the two original vectors (e.g. if one points north-of-east and the other south-of-east, the resultant points due east).
  • Two vectors of magnitude 2 m at right angles have resultant sqrt(2^2 + 2^2) = sqrt(8) = 2√2 m, about 2.83 m. The cosine term is zero because the angle is 90°.
  • The direction from either vector is given by tanα = 2/2 = 1, so α = 45°. The resultant is midway between them.
  • Place one vector 45° north of east and the other 45° south of east. They are perpendicular, they have equal magnitude, and east is their bisector.
  • The northward component of the first is 2 sin45°, and the southward component of the second is the same size. Those components cancel.

Confusion

  • 2√2 is not 4. The vectors are not parallel.
  • North of east and south of east are perpendicular only for this 45° choice, not for every pair of those phrases.
  • Cancelling the north and south parts does not cancel the eastward parts.
  • The resultant is not north-east.

Practice

  • Write R = sqrt(A^2 + B^2) at 90° and say what each symbol means.
  • Mark the known values, then substitute one step at a time.
  • Finish with the number, the unit, and the direction when the quantity has one.

Key Points

  • Equal 2 m vectors at 90° give 2√2 m.
  • The resultant bisects the angle.
  • North-of-east and south-of-east can be symmetric about east.
  • The resultant is then due east.

Exercises

1.

Two vectors, each of magnitude 2 m, point 45° north of east and 45° south of east. Find the resultant.

Steps
  • The angle between them is 90°, and the magnitudes are equal.
  • R = sqrt(2^2 + 2^2) = 2√2 m.
  • The northward and southward components are equal and cancel.
  • The eastward components add to 2√2 m.
  • The resultant is 2√2 m due east.
Answer

2√2 m due east.

Summary

Equal vectors at right angles have resultant 2√2 m when each is 2 m, directed along the bisector. For the symmetric north-of-east and south-of-east pair, that bisector is due east because the sideways components cancel.
