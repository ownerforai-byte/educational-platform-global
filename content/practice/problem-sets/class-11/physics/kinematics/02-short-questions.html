Practice Set 2 — Kinematics Short Questions

Notes

  • Q1. Distinguish between distance and displacement. Answer: Distance is the total path length covered (scalar); displacement is the straight-line change of position (vector, direction included). Distance ≥ |displacement|.
  • Q2. Distinguish between speed and velocity. Answer: Speed = distance/time (scalar, always positive); velocity = displacement/time (vector). Average velocity can be zero while average speed is not.
  • Q3. Can a body have zero velocity and non-zero acceleration? Give an example. Answer: Yes. At the highest point of a vertically thrown ball, v = 0 but a = g (downward).
  • Q4. Define relative velocity. Write the relation for two bodies moving along the same straight line. Answer: v_AB = v_A − v_B. Approaching each other: relative speed = v_A + v_B; chasing: = v_A − v_B.
  • Q5. Derive v² = u² + 2as graphically. Answer: From the v–t graph of uniform acceleration, area under the line = s = average velocity × t = ((u+v)/2)t; with v = u + at, eliminating t gives v² = u² + 2as.
  • Q6. State the conditions for a projectile to have maximum range and equal ranges. Answer: Range R = u² sin2θ/g is maximum at θ = 45°. Angles θ and (90° − θ) give the same range.
  • Q7. A stone is thrown vertically up and returns to the thrower's hand. Compare time of ascent and descent. Answer: They are equal, because the upward and downward journeys have equal displacement (and equal speed magnitudes at every height).
