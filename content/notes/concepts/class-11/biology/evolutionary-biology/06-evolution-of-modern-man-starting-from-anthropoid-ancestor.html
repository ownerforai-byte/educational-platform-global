  • [Remember] Humans belong to order Primates, family Hominidae; anthropoid ancestors (dryopithecines) gave rise to hominins — humans did not evolve from living apes but share a common ancestor with them.
  • [Remember] Sequence: Dryopithecus → Australopithecus afarensis ('Lucy', bipedal, ~400 cc brain) → Homo habilis (tool maker, ~650 cc) → Homo erectus (fire, ~900 cc) → Homo sapiens neanderthalensis → Homo sapiens sapiens (~1350 cc).
  • [Understand] Key trends: habitual bipedalism, brain expansion, face flattening with chin, dental reduction, opposable thumb refinement, and prolonged infant dependency with culture and language.
  • [Understand] Evidence: fossils (Africa–Asia–Europe), tool industries (Oldowan, Acheulean), anatomically modern humans appearing ~300,000 years ago in Africa and spreading worldwide.

Confusion

  • Humans did not evolve from living apes; both share an extinct common anthropoid ancestor.
  • Bipedalism came before major brain expansion — Lucy walked upright with a ~400 cc brain.
  • Homo habilis made tools, Homo erectus used fire; do not merge their achievements.
  • Neanderthals were a subspecies of Homo sapiens, not a separate failed genus.

Universal Facts

  • Brain size roughly tripled from Australopithecus (~400 cc) to modern man (~1350 cc).
  • Bipedalism preceded tool culture; tool culture preceded agriculture and writing.
  • Fossil plus tool-industry evidence together make a complete NEB answer.

Examples

  • Australopithecus afarensis 'Lucy': upright walker, ~400 cc brain, no true tools.
  • Homo erectus: controlled fire, Acheulean hand axes, ~900 cc brain, first out-of-Africa spread.
  • Homo sapiens sapiens: ~1350 cc brain, language, art and agriculture.

Important Statements

  • Humans are primates in the family Hominidae. They share an ancestor with living apes. They did not descend from a living chimpanzee.
  • Australopithecus afarensis, including the fossil called Lucy, was bipedal with a brain near 400 cubic centimetres.
  • Homo habilis made stone tools and had a larger brain, near 650 cubic centimetres.

Exercises

1.

Order Australopithecus, Homo habilis and Homo erectus by brain size and name one cultural gain.

Steps
  • Australopithecus had the smallest brain of the three, about 400 cm³, and was already bipedal.
  • Homo habilis comes next, with tools and a brain near 650 cm³.
  • Homo erectus had a brain near 900 cm³ and used fire.
  • The cultural gain can be tools or fire. Farming is far later.
Answer

Australopithecus, then habilis, then erectus. Tools, then fire.
