Exam Short Tricks

  • Focus on definitions and classifications.
  • Use diagrams to understand Ecological Adaptation.
  • Hydrophyte = water + air spaces (aerenchyma) + thin cuticle.
  • Xerophyte = dry + thick cuticle + spines + CAM.
  • Cactus spines = modified leaves (not needles).
  • Adaptation = evolution; Acclimatisation = individual.
