Exam Short Tricks

  • Frog digits: 4 fore + 5 hind (webbed).
  • Amphibian = amphi (both) + bios (life) = land + water.
  • Tympanum = ear; Nictitating membrane = third eyelid.
  • Tadpole = tail + gills; Adult = no tail + lungs.
  • Frog reproduction = EXTERNAL + amplexus + water.
  • Male = no copulatory organ; Female = oviducts with jelly coat.
  • Tadpole = gills + tail; Adult = lungs + no tail.
  • Bio-indicator = sensitive skin = early warning of pollution.
  • Focus on definitions and classifications.
  • Use diagrams to understand Protista and Protozoa.
