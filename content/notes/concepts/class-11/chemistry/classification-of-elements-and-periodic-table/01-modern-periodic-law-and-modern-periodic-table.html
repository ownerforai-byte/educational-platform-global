Notes

  • 1. Historical Evolution of Periodic Classification

  • • Dobereiner's Law of Triads (1829): Grouped elements into triads where atomic mass of middle element was roughly arithmetic mean of first and third (e.g., Li 7, Na 23, K 39; Ca 40, Sr 88, Ba 137; Cl 35.5, Br 80, I 127). Failed because only a few triads could be identified.
  • • Newlands' Law of Octaves (1865): When elements are arranged in order of increasing atomic mass, every eighth element shows similar physical and chemical properties like musical notes (Sa, Re, Ga, Ma...). Failed after Calcium (valid only up to Ca, Z=20) and could not accommodate noble gases discovered later.
  • • Lothar Meyer's Curve (1869): Plotted atomic volume against atomic weight. Alkali metals occupied sharp peaks; alkaline earth metals occupied descending curves; halogens occupied ascending slopes; transition metals occupied valleys.
  • • Mendeleev's Periodic Law (1869): 'The physical and chemical properties of elements are periodic functions of their atomic masses.' Mendeleev left gaps for undiscovered elements (Eka-Boron = Scandium, Eka-Aluminium = Gallium, Eka-Silicon = Germanium). Anomalies: Inverted pairs (Ar 39.9 before K 39.1; Co 58.9 before Ni 58.7; Te 127.6 before I 126.9), no fixed position for Hydrogen, isotopes could not be accommodated.

  • 2. Moseley's Experiment and Modern Periodic Law (1913)

  • • English physicist Henry Moseley bombarded various metal anticathodes with high-speed cathode rays (electrons) and studied characteristic X-ray emission spectra.
  • • Moseley's Law: The square root of the frequency (√ν) of the characteristic X-ray spectral line is directly proportional to the atomic number (Z) of the target element:
  • ν​=a(Z−b)
  • where ν = frequency of K_α X-ray line, Z = atomic number (nuclear charge), a and b are constants characteristic of the spectral line series (screening constant b ≈ 1 for K-series).
  • • Fundamental Conclusion: Plot of √ν vs Z yields a straight line, whereas plot of √ν vs atomic mass (A) does not. This conclusively proved that atomic number (Z), representing the net positive nuclear charge (number of protons), is the fundamental property governing the chemical identity of elements, NOT atomic mass.
  • • Statement of Modern Periodic Law: 'The physical and chemical properties of elements are periodic functions of their atomic numbers.'

  • 3. Resolution of Mendeleev's Anomalies by Modern Periodic Table

  • • Anomalous Pairs Resolved: Argon (Z=18) naturally precedes Potassium (Z=19); Cobalt (Z=27) naturally precedes Nickel (Z=28); Tellurium (Z=52) naturally precedes Iodine (Z=53) purely by increasing atomic numbers, regardless of atomic weights.
  • • Position of Isotopes: Since isotopes share identical atomic numbers (same Z, different mass number A), they occupy one single position in the periodic table without conflict.
  • • Scientific Division into Blocks: Reflects filling of electron shells according to quantum mechanics (Aufbau principle, Pauli exclusion, Hund's rule).

  • 4. Structural Layout of the Long Form (Bohr's Form) Periodic Table

  • • Periods (7 Horizontal Rows): Corresponds to principal quantum number n of outermost valence shell.
    • Period 1 (n=1): 2 elements (1s²)
    • Period 2 (n=2): 8 elements (2s² 2p⁶)
    • Period 3 (n=3): 8 elements (3s² 3p⁶)
    • Period 4 (n=4): 18 elements (4s² 3d¹⁰ 4p⁶)
    • Period 5 (n=5): 18 elements (5s² 4d¹⁰ 5p⁶)
    • Period 6 (n=6): 32 elements (6s² 4f¹⁴ 5d¹⁰ 6p⁶, including Lanthanides ₅₈Ce–₇₁Lu)
    • Period 7 (n=7): 32 elements (7s² 5f¹⁴ 6d¹⁰ 7p⁶, including Actinides ₉₀Th–₁₀₃Lr)
  • • Groups (18 Vertical Columns): Elements having identical outer electronic configurations and exhibiting similar chemical properties.
  • • IUPAC Nomenclature for Elements with Z > 100: Uses numerical roots for digits: 0 = nil, 1 = un, 2 = bi, 3 = tri, 4 = quad, 5 = pent, 6 = hex, 7 = sept, 8 = oct, 9 = enn. Suffix '-ium'. Example: 104 = Unnilquadium (Unq), 118 = Ununoctium (Uuo / Oganesson).

Confusion

  • ❌ Modern Periodic Law is based on atomic mass like Mendeleev's law. ✅ Modern Periodic Law is strictly based on atomic number (Z), discovered by Moseley via X-ray spectroscopy.
  • ❌ Isotopes need separate positions in the modern periodic table. ✅ Isotopes possess identical atomic numbers, so they naturally share the exact same slot in the modern table.
  • ❌ Cobalt was placed before Nickel by Mendeleev as an error. ✅ Mendeleev placed Co before Ni based on chemical properties despite Co being heavier (58.93 vs 58.69); modern table confirms Co (Z=27) correctly precedes Ni (Z=28).
  • ❌ Moseley's graph plotted frequency ν against atomic weight. ✅ Moseley plotted the square root of frequency (√ν) against atomic number (Z), obtaining a perfect straight line: √ν = a(Z - b).
  • ❌ Period number corresponds to total number of electrons. ✅ Period number corresponds directly to the principal quantum number (n) of the outermost valence electron shell.

Universal Facts

  • Henry Moseley established atomic number as the genuine identity of chemical elements in 1913, shortly before dying in World War I at age 27.
  • The 7 periods of the modern table correspond exactly to principal energy levels n = 1 to 7.
  • Periodicity in chemical properties arises from the periodic recurrence of similar valence shell electronic configurations.
