  • 2. Fundamental Concepts (NEB 2023, 2022, 2021, 2024)

    2.1 Atoms, molecules, ions and radicals

    • Atom: the smallest particle of an element that takes part in a chemical reaction (e.g. Fe, He).
    • Molecule: the smallest particle of an element or compound that exists independently (H₂, O₂, CO₂, H₂O).
    • Ion: an atom or group carrying charge — cation (+, e.g. Na⁺) or anion (−, e.g. Cl⁻).
    • Radical: an atom or group of atoms behaving as a single unit in reactions, carrying charge (inorganic radicals: SO₄²⁻, NO₃⁻, NH₄⁺) or an unpaired electron (free radicals: Cl·).

    2.2 Relative atomic mass and the amu

    • Relative atomic mass (A_r): mass of an atom compared with 1/12 the mass of one carbon-12 atom — dimensionless (H = 1, C = 12, O = 16).
    • Atomic mass unit (amu): 1 amu = 1/12 mass of one C-12 atom = 1.66×10⁻²⁷ kg.
    • Relative molecular mass (M_r): sum of the A_r of all atoms in the molecule (H₂O = 18, CO₂ = 44, H₂SO₄ = 98).

    2.3 Measurement basics (NEB 2023, 2022, 2021)

    • SI base units (NEB 2022, 1m): metre, kilogram, second, ampere, kelvin, mole (amount of substance), candela.
    • Significant figures (NEB 2023, 3m): in a product, the number of significant figures equals the least precise factor.
    • Mean and absolute error (NEB 2021, 2m): mean = (x₁+x₂)/2; absolute error = (x_max − x_min)/2; relative error = Δx/mean × 100%.

    2.4 Molecular formula vs empirical formula — similarities and differences

    Similarities: both express which elements are present and in what whole-number ratio; both are derived from percentage composition; both obey valency rules. Differences:

    Empirical formulaMolecular formula
    ShowsSimplest whole-number ratioActual number of atoms per molecule
    ValueOne per compound (ratio)n × empirical formula
    Example (glucose)CH₂OC₆H₁₂O₆
    Determined from% composition% composition + molar mass

    2.5 Worked numericals

    Q (2m, 2024): Calculate the number of atoms in 5.6 g of iron (Fe = 56). Moles = 5.6/56 = 0.1 mol → atoms = 0.1 × 6.022×10²³ = 6.022×10²² atoms.

    Q (3m, 2023): The density of a liquid is 0.8792 g/cm³. Calculate the mass of 45.6 cm³, to three significant figures. m = ρV = 0.8792 × 45.6 = 40.09152 g → 40.1 g (3 s.f., limited by the 45.6).

    Q (2m, 2021): Two readings of a wire's length are 10.4 cm and 10.6 cm. Find the mean and absolute error. Mean = 10.5 cm; absolute error = (10.6 − 10.4)/2 = 0.1 cm (relative error ≈ 0.95%).

Summary

The atom is the smallest reacting particle; the molecule the smallest independently existing one; ions carry charge; radicals act as units (SO₄²⁻) or carry unpaired electrons (Cl·). The amu = 1/12 of carbon-12 = 1.66×10⁻²⁷ kg, and relative atomic mass compares atoms to that standard. SI's base unit of amount of substance is the mole (NEB 2022, 1m). Measurement demands significant figures (product keeps the least precise count — NEB 2023: 0.8792 × 45.6 → 40.1 g) and error analysis (mean 10.5 cm, absolute error 0.1 cm — NEB 2021). The empirical formula (simplest ratio, CH₂O) differs from the molecular formula (actual atoms, C₆H₁₂O₆ = 6 × CH₂O) in scale, not in kind.
