Notes

  • The standard limits that unlock everything else: limx→0​xsinx​=1, limx→0​xtanx​=1, and limx→0​x21−cosx​=21​. The second follows from the first and the third from the half-angle identity, so only one needs proving.
  • All three assume RADIANS. In degrees each is scaled: limxsinx​=180π​ and limx21−cosx​=360π2​. A calculator left in degree mode produces exactly the wrong value, which is the most practical trap in the topic.
  • Rational functions and polynomials substitute directly: since polynomials are continuous everywhere and rational functions are continuous away from their zeros, limx→a​Q(x)P(x)​=Q(a)P(a)​ whenever Q(a)=0.
  • Exponential and logarithmic limits are continuous functions: limx→a​ef(x)=ef(a) and limx→a​lnf(x)=lnf(a) for f(a)>0. The domain condition on the logarithm is what excludes f(a)≤0.
  • The 1∞ family via logarithms: for y=(1+x1​)x, take logs to get limxln(1+x1​), a 0/0 form, and L'Hôpital then gives \lim\frac{\frac{1}{x}cdot rac{1}{1+1/x}}{1}=1, so the limit is e.
  • Composition of continuous functions settles most limits: if f is continuous at a and g is continuous at f(a), then limx→a​g(f(x))=g(f(a)). This is why almost no work is needed for expressions built from polynomials, roots, exponentials and logarithms with well-behaved arguments.

Confusion

  • Using the standard limits with x in degrees, which changes every value by a factor involving π.
  • Applying limlnf=ln(limf) when the limit of f is negative, where the logarithm is not defined at all.
  • Forgetting that the 1∞ case needs logarithms and cannot be handled by L'Hôpital directly.

Universal Facts

  • The limit defining e is exactly the 1∞ case, which is why e governs continuous compounding rather than discrete interest steps.
  • In physics, limt→0​θsinθ​=1 is why the small-angle approximation sinθ≈θ is valid for pendulum and projectile work — and it only holds in radians.

Summary

Three standard limits — the sine, tangent and cosine forms — unlock most of the topic, and all assume radians, which is the trap that changes every numerical answer. Rational functions and compositions of continuous functions settle themselves by direct substitution. The remaining family, limits of the form (1+1/x)x, is handled by taking logarithms and reducing to a 0/0 quotient, which is where the definition of e itself comes from.
