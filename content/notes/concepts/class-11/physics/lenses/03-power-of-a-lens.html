Notes

  • Lens power measures convergence or divergence: P=1/f when f is in metres. Its SI-derived unit is the dioptre, 1 D=1 m⁻¹. A converging lens has positive power and a diverging lens negative power in air.
  • Worked example: f=+25 cm=+0.25 m gives P=+4 D. A diverging lens with f=−50 cm=−0.50 m has P=−2 D.
  • Thin lenses in contact have additive powers. A +4 D lens and −1.5 D lens make Peq=+2.5 D, so feq=1/2.5=0.40 m.
  • Power is not magnification: focal length determines power, while image size and orientation also depend on object distance through the lens equation.
  • For separated lenses the equivalent power includes separation; the simple algebraic sum applies only when thin lenses are in contact or separation is negligible.
  • The lens-maker formula uses the relative index. In water the factor (μ_glass/μ_water − 1) is smaller than (μ_glass − 1) in air, so the same glass lens has a longer focal length and a smaller power. A glass lens does not keep its air power when it is submerged.

Specific Conditions

  • Lens Power Definition: Power P=1/f (in meters) measured in diopters (D). A converging lens has P>0, a diverging lens has P<0. Power is additive for thin lenses in contact: Ptotal​=∑Pi​.
  • Combination of Thin Lenses: For thin lenses in contact, the total focal length is given by 1/ftotal​=∑1/fi​, or equivalently Ptotal​=∑Pi​. This assumes negligible separation between lenses. For separated lenses, use the equivalent focal length formula including the separation d.
  • Power vs Magnification: Lens power P=1/f relates to focal length, not directly to magnification. Magnification m=v/u=f/(f−u) depends on object distance. A high-power lens (short f) gives larger magnification for a given object distance, but m is not simply proportional to P.

Exercises

1.

A glass lens has P = 5.0 D in air, from (μ − 1) = 0.50. In water the relative factor becomes 0.17 instead of 0.50. Estimate the power in water.

Steps
  • Power is proportional to (μ_relative − 1) for the same shape.
  • The factor falls from 0.50 to 0.17.
  • The ratio is 0.17/0.50 = 0.34.
  • P_water ≈ 5.0 × 0.34 = 1.7 D.
  • The focal length becomes longer.
Answer

About 1.7 D in water.
