Notes

  • 1. Topic Introduction — The Ledger That Never Fails

    Energy changes form — kinetic, potential, heat, sound, light, chemical — but the total of an isolated system never changes. The freely falling body is the standard proof: mgh drains into ½mv² at exactly the rate height is lost (NEB 2020, 2024).

  • 2. Fundamental Concepts

    Law of conservation of energy: energy can neither be created nor destroyed; it only changes from one form to another — the total energy of an isolated system is constant.

    2.1 Verification for a freely falling body (NEB 2020, 2024 — 3m)

    Take a body of mass m dropped from height h (air resistance neglected, ground as the zero of PE):

    PositionPEKETotal
    Top (v = 0, height h)mgh0mgh
    Midway fallen (height h/2)mgh/221​m(gh​)2=mgh/2mgh
    Just before ground (height 0)021​mv2 with v2=2gh → mghmgh

    At every point PE+KE=mgh: the total mechanical energy is conserved; PE drains into KE at exactly the rate height is lost. ∎

    2.2 Energy transformations beyond mechanics

    TransformationExample
    Chemical → kineticMuscles, engines
    Electrical → light + heatBulbs
    Kinetic → heatBrakes, friction
    Nuclear → electricalReactors, and the Sun's output
    Potential → kinetic → electricalHydropower (Nepal's grid)

    When friction acts, mechanical energy (PE+KE) is not conserved — it degrades to heat — but total energy still is. This distinction separates conservative from non-conservative forces (next topic).

  • 3. Worked Numericals

    Q1: A 2 kg ball is dropped from 45 m. Find its KE and speed at 20 m above the ground (g = 10). PE lost = mgΔh = 2 × 10 × 25 = 500 J = KE gained. v=2KE/m​=500​=22.4 m/s.

    Q2: A pendulum bob swings; at the lowest point its speed is 3 m/s. Find the height it rises to (g = 10). KE at bottom = PE at top: 21​v2=gh→h=v2/2g=9/20=0.45 m.

  • 4. Conceptual Relationships

    • To Topic 18: conservation is the WET with conservative forces only — work done by gravity reappears as PE loss.
    • To Topic 20: conservative forces permit PE bookkeeping; friction breaks mechanical conservation but not total-energy conservation.
    • To Topic 9 (free fall): the verification table is free fall's kinematics re-read energetically.
    • To hydropower (Nepal context): falling water's PE → KE → turbine → electricity — the national grid runs on this topic.

    5.3 Common examination mistakes

    1. Forgetting the mid-flight row that shows PE + KE constant throughout (not just at the endpoints).
    2. Claiming mechanical energy is conserved with air resistance present — only total energy is.
    3. Mixing sign conventions for PE's zero level mid-solution.
    4. Omitting the statement of the law before the verification.
    5. Writing v2=2gh without deriving or citing it.

Confusion

  • Mechanical vs total energy conservation: mechanical (KE+PE) survives only conservative forces; total energy (including heat) always survives.
  • Energy vs momentum conservation: both hold for isolated systems, but energy is a scalar ledger and momentum a vector one — collisions use them differently.

Key Points

  • Law: energy changes form; total of an isolated system is constant.
  • Free-fall verification: top PE = mgh; mid PE+KE = mgh; ground KE = mgh — total constant throughout.
  • Friction degrades mechanical energy to heat, but total energy still balances.

Summary

Energy is never created or destroyed — it only changes form. The freely falling body proves it: dropped from height h, a body carries PE = mgh at the top, mgh/2 + mgh/2 halfway down, and KE = ½m(2gh) = mgh just before landing — the total stays mgh at every instant (NEB 2020, 2024). Gravity is the bookkeeper: PE drains into KE at exactly the rate height falls, so v=2gh​ is conservation solved for speed. Friction degrades mechanical energy to heat — the pendulum eventually stops — but total energy, including heat, still balances. Nepal's hydropower is this law at national scale: falling water's potential energy becomes the grid's electricity.

Special Notes

  • Mechanical energy conservation requires ALL forces to be conservative — one friction force breaks it.
  • The reference level for gravitational PE is arbitrary — only differences matter.
  • In a spring-mass system, energy oscillates between kinetic and potential: ½mv² + ½kx² = constant.
  • The work-energy theorem is more general than mechanical energy conservation — it works with friction.
  • NEB frequently asks: 'Find the speed at the bottom' — use mgh = ½mv² for smooth surfaces.

Grammar

Notes
  • Conservation (noun): the act of preserving something. Conserved (adjective): kept constant.
  • Energy (noun): capacity to do work. Uncountable in physics context.
  • Potential energy (noun phrase): stored energy due to position.
Important Statements
  • In physics, 'conservation of energy' is a fundamental principle, not just a suggestion.
  • The word 'conserved' means 'remains constant over time', not 'saved' in the everyday sense.
