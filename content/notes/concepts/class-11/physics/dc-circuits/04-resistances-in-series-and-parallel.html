Notes

  • 1. Topic Introduction — The Two Ways to Wire Anything

    Every network, however tangled, reduces to series and parallel blocks. Series adds resistances; parallel adds conductances — and NEB 2021 and 2024 both tested the parallel rule with numericals.

  • 2. Fundamental Concepts (NEB 2021, 2024)

    2.1 Series

    Resistors end-to-end carry the same current; voltages add: Rs​=R1​+R2​+R3​+⋯ — larger than the largest; used to divide voltage or limit current.

    2.2 Parallel

    Resistors across the same two nodes share the same voltage; currents add: Rp​1​=R1​1​+R2​1​+R3​1​+⋯ — smaller than the smallest; used to share current. Two resistors: Rp​=R1​+R2​R1​R2​​.

    2.3 Worked numericals

    Q (4m, 2021): Three resistors of 2 Ω, 3 Ω and 6 Ω are connected in parallel across a 12 V battery of negligible internal resistance. Find the total resistance and the total current drawn.

    • R1​=21​+31​+61​=63+2+1​=1 → R = 1 Ω
    • I=RV​=112​= 12 A
    • Check by branches: I₁ = 12/2 = 6 A, I₂ = 12/3 = 4 A, I₃ = 12/6 = 2 A → total 12 A ✓

    Q (3m, 2024): Resistors of 6 Ω and 3 Ω are connected in parallel across a 12 V battery. Find the current through each resistor and the total current.

    • Parallel branches share the full voltage: I₁ = 12/6 = 2 A; I₂ = 12/3 = 4 A
    • Total I = 2 + 4 = 6 A (check: R_eq = (6×3)/(6+3) = 2 Ω, 12/2 = 6 A ✓)

    2.4 Wheatstone bridge (context)

    Four resistors in a diamond: the bridge is balanced when no current flows through the central galvanometer — the two branch points sit at equal potential. Balance condition: QP​=SR​ — an unknown resistance follows from three known ones.

  • 3. Conceptual Relationships

    • To Topic 99: the rules operate on R = ρl/A values.
    • To Topic 102: a potential divider is a series pair tapping voltage.
    • To capacitors (Topic 95): the combination rules are exactly swapped.

Practice Questions

Formulas

    • R_s = R₁ + R₂ + R₃
    • 1/R_p = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃
    • Two parallel: R₁R₂/(R₁+R₂)
    • Wheatstone balance: P/Q = R/S

Key Points

  • Series: R_s = ΣRᵢ. Parallel: 1/R_p = Σ1/Rᵢ.

Summary

Series resistors share one current and add: R_s = R₁ + R₂ + ⋯ — larger than the largest. Parallel resistors share one voltage and add reciprocals: 1/R_p = Σ1/Rᵢ — smaller than the smallest. NEB 2021's trio (2, 3, 6 Ω across 12 V) collapses to 1 Ω drawing 12 A, verified branch by branch (6 + 4 + 2 A) — 4m. NEB 2024's pair (6 Ω, 3 Ω across 12 V) draws 2 A and 4 A with 6 A total (3m). The Wheatstone bridge — four resistors in a diamond — balances when P/Q = R/S and no current flows through the galvanometer (NEB 2024, 3m).
