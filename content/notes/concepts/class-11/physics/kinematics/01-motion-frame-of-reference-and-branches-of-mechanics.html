Motion, Frames of Reference & Branches of Mechanics

Class 11PhysicsKinematics

Notes

  • A frame of reference is the body, or the set of axes, from which positions and times are measured. The same motion can look different in two frames. A passenger at rest in a train is moving in the platform frame.
  • An inertial frame is one in which Newton's first law holds. A frame fixed to the Earth is close enough for the problems in this course. A frame that accelerates, such as a braking bus, is non-inertial, and a pseudo-force is then added if you still want to use F = ma inside it.
  • Mechanics divides into kinematics, dynamics and statics. Kinematics describes motion with displacement, velocity and acceleration, without asking for the force. Dynamics relates the motion to forces. Statics is dynamics with zero acceleration.
  • Relative velocity of A with respect to B is v_A − v_B. The subtraction is a vector subtraction. If the bodies move on one line, opposite directions get opposite signs.
  • Displacement is the change of position and is a vector. Distance is the length of the path and is a scalar. Speed is distance over time. Velocity is displacement over time.
  • A rest statement is incomplete until the frame is named. Rest in one frame can be motion in another. The laws used to predict the motion are written in an inertial frame.

Confusion

  • Rest and motion are not properties of a body alone. They depend on the frame.
  • Speed and velocity are not interchangeable when the path bends or reverses.
  • Statics is not a different kind of force. It is the zero-acceleration case.
  • Subtracting speeds that point in different directions as if they were scalars gives the wrong relative velocity.

Exercises

1.

Car A moves east at 20 m/s and car B moves east at 12 m/s. Find the velocity of A relative to B.

Steps
  • Take east as positive.
  • v_A = +20 m/s and v_B = +12 m/s.
  • v of A relative to B is v_A − v_B.
  • That is 20 − 12 = +8 m/s.
  • The positive sign means 8 m/s eastward, as seen from car B.
Answer

8 m/s east.
