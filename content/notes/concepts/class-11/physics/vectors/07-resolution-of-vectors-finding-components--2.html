Resolution of Vectors — Finding Components

Class 11PhysicsVectors

Notes

  • When several vectors lie in one plane, resolve each one into x and y components, then add the components separately. Components along the same line add as ordinary signed numbers.
  • R_x is the sum of every F cosθ, with a minus sign if that component points along the negative axis. R_y is the sum of every F sinθ, with the same care about signs.
  • The resultant magnitude is sqrt(R_x^2 + R_y^2). The angle with the +x-axis satisfies tanα = |R_y / R_x|, and the quadrant follows the signs of R_x and R_y.
  • This component method is the working form of the polygon law. Drawing the polygon is the picture. Adding components is the calculation.
  • A vector along the negative x-axis has F_x = −F and F_y = 0. A vector straight down has F_x = 0 and F_y = −F. Leaving these signs out is the usual arithmetic error.
  • If the axes are rotated, the components change but the resultant vector does not. Choose axes that set as many angles as possible to 0° or 90°.

Confusion

  • You cannot add the magnitudes of vectors that point in different directions.
  • tanα needs the quadrant. A calculator's arctan may sit in the wrong one.
  • Summing F cosθ and then also adding the original F double-counts.
  • Opposite components cancel before the square root is taken.

Exercises

1.

Two forces act at a point: 8.0 N along +x, and 6.0 N along +y. Find the resultant.

Steps
  • R_x = 8.0 N and R_y = 6.0 N.
  • R = sqrt(8.0^2 + 6.0^2) = sqrt(64 + 36) = sqrt(100) = 10 N.
  • tanα = R_y / R_x = 6/8 = 0.75.
  • α = arctan(0.75) ≈ 37°.
  • Both components are positive, so the resultant is 10 N at about 37° above the +x-axis.
Answer

10 N at about 37° above the +x-axis.
