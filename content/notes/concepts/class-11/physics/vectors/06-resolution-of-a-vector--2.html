Resolution of a Vector

Class 11PhysicsVectors

Notes

  • Resolution replaces one vector by two perpendicular vectors that add up to it. Along axes at right angles, F_x = F cosθ and F_y = F sinθ, where θ is measured from the x-axis.
  • The original vector and its two components are not three forces acting at once. The components are a replacement. Using both the original vector and its components double-counts the force.
  • A component is zero when the vector is perpendicular to that axis, because cos 90° = 0 or sin 0° = 0. A component is negative when it points along the negative axis.
  • The magnitude can be rebuilt as F = sqrt(F_x^2 + F_y^2). The direction satisfies tanθ = F_y / F_x, with the quadrant taken from the signs of the components.
  • Perpendicular components are independent. A force along x does not change the y-equation. That is why a projectile's horizontal and vertical motions are solved separately.
  • Any pair of perpendicular directions may be chosen. Axes along a slope and perpendicular to it are often easier than horizontal and vertical axes.

Examples

  • Resolution replaces one vector by two perpendicular vectors that add up to it. Along axes at right angles, F_x = F cosθ and F_y = F sinθ, where θ is measured from the x-axis.

Confusion

  • Do not add F and F cosθ as if they were separate extra forces.
  • sin and cos swap if θ is measured from the y-axis. State the axis.
  • A negative component is real. It is not dropped.
  • tanθ alone does not choose the quadrant.

Exercises

1.

A force of 10 N acts at 30° above the +x-axis. Find its components. Take cos 30° = 0.866 and sin 30° = 0.500.

Steps
  • θ is from the +x-axis, so F_x = F cosθ and F_y = F sinθ.
  • F_x = 10 × 0.866 = 8.66 N.
  • F_y = 10 × 0.500 = 5.00 N.
  • Both components are positive, so they point along +x and +y.
  • Check: sqrt(8.66^2 + 5^2) = sqrt(75 + 25) = sqrt(100) = 10 N.
Answer

8.66 N along +x and 5.00 N along +y.
