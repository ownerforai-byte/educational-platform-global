  • Two equal forces of 10 N each have a resultant of 10 N. Find the angle between them. [Solution: R=2Pcos(θ/2), so 10=2(10)cos(θ/2), giving cos(θ/2)=1/2, so θ/2=60° and θ=120°.]

  • Three forces of 3 N, 4 N, and 5 N act along the sides of a right triangle taken in order. Find their resultant. [Solution: Since 32+42=52, these forces form a closed triangle. By the triangle law, the resultant is R=0. The system is in equilibrium.]
