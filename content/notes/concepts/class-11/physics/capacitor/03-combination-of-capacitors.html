Notes

  • 1. Topic Introduction — Wiring Capacitors Together

    Capacitors in series divide the voltage; in parallel they share the charge. Their combination rules run opposite to resistors', and NEB 2024 tested both in one 3-mark numerical.

  • 2. Fundamental Concepts (NEB 2024)

    2.1 Series combination

    Capacitors end to end carry the same charge Q (induced plate by plate); voltages add: V=V1​+V2​=C1​Q​+C2​Q​⇒Cs​1​=C1​1​+C2​1​ — the equivalent capacitance is smaller than the smallest; series withstands higher total voltage.

    2.2 Parallel combination

    Capacitors wired across the same two points share the same voltage V; charges add: Q=Q1​+Q2​⇒Cp​=C1​+C2​ — the equivalent capacitance is larger than the largest; parallel stores more charge at the same voltage.

    2.3 Comparison with resistors

    CombinationCapacitorsResistors
    Series1/C addsR adds
    ParallelC adds1/R adds

    2.4 Worked numerical

    Q (3m, 2024): Capacitors of 2 μF and 3 μF are connected (i) in series and (ii) in parallel. Find the equivalent capacitance in each case.

    • Series: C1​=21​+31​=65​ → C = 1.2 μF (smaller than either).
    • Parallel: C = 2 + 3 = 5 μF.

    Q (NEB-style): Three capacitors 2, 3 and 6 μF in series. Find C_s. Cs​1​=21​+31​+61​=1 → C_s = 1 μF.

  • 3. Conceptual Relationships

    • To Topic 93/94: each capacitor still obeys Q = CV individually.
    • To Topic 96: energy storage sums (parallel) or must be computed per capacitor (series).
    • To resistors (Unit 25): the rules swap — a favourite conceptual MCQ.

Mcs

1.

Three capacitors of capacitance 3 μF each are connected in series. The equivalent capacitance of the combination is:

Options
  • 9 μF
  • 1 μF
  • 3 μF
  • 0.33 μF
Answer

B

Explanation

In series, 1/C_s = 1/3 + 1/3 + 1/3 = 3/3 = 1 μF⁻¹, giving C_s = 1 μF.

2.

When two charged capacitors at different potentials are connected together by a conducting wire, which quantity is conserved?

Options
  • Total electrostatic energy only
  • Total electric charge only
  • Both total charge and total energy
  • Neither charge nor energy
Answer

B

Explanation

Electric charge is strictly conserved by the law of conservation of charge. Electrostatic potential energy decreases due to dissipation as heat during current flow.

3.

The ratio of equivalent capacitance in parallel to that in series for n identical capacitors is:

Options
  • n
  • 1/n
  • n²
  • 1/n²
Answer

C

Explanation

C_p = nC and C_s = C/n. Therefore C_p / C_s = nC / (C/n) = n².

Summary

Capacitors combine in two ways. Series (end-to-end): each carries the same induced charge Q while voltages add, giving Cs​1​=C1​1​+C2​1​ — equivalent capacitance smaller than the smallest, but withstanding higher total voltage. Parallel (same two nodes): each sees the same V while charges add, giving Cp​=C1​+C2​ — larger than the largest, storing more charge. NEB 2024's pair (2 μF, 3 μF) gives 1.2 μF in series and 5 μF in parallel (3m). The rules are the exact inverse of resistor combinations — the classic exam contrast.
