Notes

  • 1. Topic Introduction — Building Capacitance from First Principles

    Two parallel plates, area A, gap d: Gauss's law gives the field, V = Ed gives the voltage, and dividing Q by V delivers C = ε₀A/d. NEB 2023 and 2024 both asked this as a 5-mark derivation — the most repeated derivation in the electricity section.

  • 2. Fundamental Concepts (NEB 2023, 2024)

    2.1 Definition of capacitance (5m, part 1)

    Capacitance C = Q/V is the charge stored per unit potential difference; SI unit farad (F) = coulomb per volt.

    2.2 Derivation of C = ε₀A/d (5m, part 2)

    Two parallel plates, area A, separation d, carrying ±Q:

    1. Field between the plates (from Gauss's law, treating the plates as a large sheet pair):

    E=ε0​σ​=Aε0​Q​

    1. Potential difference (uniform field):

    V=Ed=Aε0​Qd​

    1. Capacitance:

    C=VQ​=Qd/Aε0​Q​=dε0​A​​

    1. With a dielectric of relative permittivity εᵣ filling the gap:

    C=dεr​ε0​A​

    2.3 Design lessons

    • C grows with area A — stack plates for more.
    • C grows as the separation shrinks — thinner dielectric, bigger capacitance.
    • C multiplies by εᵣ with a dielectric — mica, paper, ceramic between plates.

    2.4 Worked numericals

    Q (3m, 2024): A parallel plate air capacitor has plates of area 0.04 m² separated by 2 mm. Calculate its capacitance. (ε₀ = 8.85×10⁻¹² F/m) C=dε0​A​=2×10−38.85×10−12×0.04​=1.77×10−10 F≈177 pF

    Q (NEB-style): The 177 pF capacitor above is filled with mica (εᵣ = 6). Find the new capacitance. C = εᵣ × 177 pF ≈ 1062 pF ≈ 1.06 nF.

  • 3. Conceptual Relationships

    • To Topic 88/89: E = σ/ε₀ comes straight from Gauss's law.
    • To Topic 93: the derivation gives meaning to C = Q/V.
    • To Topic 97: inserting a dielectric multiplies C by εᵣ via polarisation.

Practice Questions

  • Numerical (3m): A 200 pF air capacitor has plates 1 mm apart. Find the area. — A = Cd/ε₀ = 200×10⁻¹² × 10⁻³/8.85×10⁻¹² ≈ 0.0226 m² ≈ 226 cm².

Formulas

    • E = σ/ε₀ = Q/Aε₀
    • V = Ed = Qd/Aε₀
    • C = ε₀A/d; C = εᵣε₀A/d

Key Points

  • C = ε₀A/d; with dielectric C = εᵣε₀A/d.

Summary

A parallel plate capacitor (area A, gap d, charge ±Q) derives its capacitance in three steps: Gauss's law gives the uniform field E=σ/ε0​=Q/Aε0​; the potential difference is V=Ed=Qd/Aε0​; and C=Q/V collapses to C=ε0​A/d​ — multiplied by εᵣ when a dielectric fills the gap (NEB 2023, 2024, 5m each). NEB 2024's numerical (A = 0.04 m², d = 2 mm) gives C = 1.77×10⁻¹⁰ F ≈ 177 pF (3m). Bigger plates, smaller gaps and better dielectrics all raise capacitance — the design rules behind every capacitor.

Special Notes

  • When a conducting slab of thickness t (t < d) is inserted, κ → ∞, so C = ε₀A / (d - t). If t = d, the plates short-circuit.
  • If multiple dielectric slabs of thicknesses t₁, t₂ with constants κ₁, κ₂ fill the gap, C = ε₀A / (t₁/κ₁ + t₂/κ₂).

Important Statements

  • "The capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor is ε₀A/d, increased εᵣ-fold by a dielectric filling."

Important Notes

  • Verified PYQs: definition + derivation (2023, 2024 — 5m each); numerical 2024 3m.

Exam Short Tricks

  • "C = 8.85×10⁻¹² × A/d — keep ε₀'s exponent straight."

Exam Notes

  • Write all four derivation steps; state the dielectric extension for full marks.

Mcs

1.

The capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor does NOT depend on:

Options
  • area of the plates
  • medium between plates
  • distance between plates
  • metal material of the plates
Answer

D

Explanation

Capacitance depends strictly on geometry (area, separation) and dielectric medium (permittivity), not on the metal composition of the conducting plates.

2.

If a copper sheet of thickness t = d/2 is placed between plates of separation d, the new capacitance is:

Options
  • halved
  • doubled
  • unchanged
  • zero
Answer

B

Explanation

For a metal (conductor), κ → ∞. Effective distance becomes d' = d - t = d - d/2 = d/2. Thus C' = ε₀A / (d/2) = 2C₀.

3.

The attractive force between the plates of an isolated charged parallel plate capacitor:

Options
  • increases as separation d increases
  • decreases as separation d increases
  • is independent of separation d
  • is inversely proportional to d²
Answer

C

Explanation

For an isolated capacitor, charge Q is constant. Force F = Q² / (2ε₀A), which has no dependence on separation distance d.

Important Concepts

  • Three-step derivation: E → V → C — memorise the chain, not just the result.

Important Tasks

  • Derive C = ε₀A/d from Gauss's Law and V = Ed.
  • Derive C = ε₀A / [d - t(1 - 1/κ)] for a slab of thickness t.
  • Calculate changes in Q, V, E, and U when a dielectric is inserted with battery connected vs disconnected.
