Dimensions & Dimensional Formula

Class 11PhysicsPhysical Quantities

Notes

  • The dimension of a quantity records which base quantities it contains and to what power. In mechanics the symbols are M for mass, L for length and T for time. The dimensional formula of speed is [L T^-1].
  • Force is mass times acceleration, and acceleration is length over time squared, so [F] = [M L T^-2]. Work and energy are force times distance, so [W] = [M L^2 T^-2]. Power is energy over time, so [P] = [M L^2 T^-3].
  • An equation can be right only if every term has the same dimensions. This is a necessary check, not a proof that the equation is the correct physical law. A missing numerical factor such as 1/2 is invisible to dimensions.
  • Angles in radians, strains, and coefficients that are pure ratios have dimension 1. They may be arguments of a sine or an exponential. A dimensioned quantity may not sit alone inside a sine.
  • The dimensional formula is written in square brackets. [v] = [M^0 L T^-1] says mass does not appear. Leaving out a zero power is allowed when the quantity is absent.
  • Changing the unit, from metres to centimetres, does not change the dimension. Dimensions and units are related, but they are not the same statement.

Confusion

  • Matching dimensions does not fix a factor of 2.
  • A degree Celsius interval is a temperature difference. A formula that needs kelvin must be given kelvin.
  • sin(x) is legal only when x is dimensionless.
  • Energy and torque share [M L^2 T^-2], so dimensions cannot tell them apart.

Exercises

1.

Show that the formula v^2 = u^2 + 2as is dimensionally homogeneous.

Steps
  • [v^2] = [L T^-1]^2 = [L^2 T^-2].
  • [u^2] has the same dimensions.
  • [a] = [L T^-2] and [s] = [L], so [2as] = [L T^-2][L] = [L^2 T^-2].
  • The factor 2 is a pure number and adds no dimension.
  • Every term is [L^2 T^-2], so the equation is homogeneous.
Answer

Each term has dimensions [L^2 T^-2].
