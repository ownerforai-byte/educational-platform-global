Errors in Measurement, Scientific Notation & Order of Magnitude

Class 11PhysicsPhysical Quantities

Notes

  • An error is the difference between a measured value and the true value. A systematic error repeats in the same direction, as when a metre scale has a worn zero end. A random error scatters readings both ways and is reduced by averaging.
  • The best estimate from n readings is their arithmetic mean. The absolute error of one reading is its difference from the mean. The mean absolute error is the average of those differences, taken as positive.
  • The result is written as mean ± mean absolute error. The relative error is that absolute error divided by the mean. The percentage error is the relative error times 100.
  • When quantities are multiplied or divided, relative errors add. If z = xy, then Δz/z = Δx/x + Δy/y, using the absolute values of the relative errors. For a power, z = x^n, the relative error is multiplied by n.
  • Scientific notation writes a number as a coefficient between 1 and 10 times a power of ten. The order of magnitude is that power of ten after the coefficient is used to decide the nearest power. A coefficient of 3 or more often rounds the order upward in the rough classroom rule of 'greater than about sqrt(10)' .
  • A gross error is a mistake, such as misreading a scale. It is not treated by the random-error formula. The reading is rejected and taken again.

Confusion

  • Averaging does not remove a systematic zero error.
  • Relative errors add for a product. Absolute errors do not simply add as the final answer without that rule.
  • The order of magnitude is a power of ten, not the significant-figure count.
  • A percentage error is not the absolute error. It is the absolute error as a percent of the value.

Exercises

1.

The side of a square is 10.0 cm ± 0.1 cm. Find the percentage error in the area.

Steps
  • Relative error in the side is 0.1 / 10.0 = 0.01.
  • Area depends on the square of the side, so the relative error doubles.
  • Relative error in the area is 0.02.
  • Percentage error is 0.02 × 100 = 2%.
  • The absolute error in the area is not required for this question.
Answer

2%.
