Notes

  • White light contains a range of wavelengths. In ordinary glass, refractive index is greater for violet than red, so violet bends more through a prism: this wavelength-dependent separation is dispersion.
  • For a thin prism of apex angle A and small deviations, a colour with refractive index n has deviation δ=(n−1)A. Therefore the red–violet angular spread is Δδ=(nv−nr)A.
  • Dispersive power compares colour spread with mean yellow deviation: ω=(δv−δr)/δy=(nv−nr)/(ny−1). It is dimensionless and describes relative dispersion of the material, not the prism angle.
  • Worked example: nv=1.532, nr=1.514 and ny=1.523 give ω=0.018/0.523≈0.0344. A higher ω means more red–violet separation for the same mean deviation.
  • The thin-prism ratio follows by dividing Δδ=(nv−nr)A by δy=(ny−1)A; A cancels. In a different surrounding medium, use refractive indices relative to that medium.
  • Dispersive power compares the spread of colours with the mean deviation. A flint glass can have a larger dispersive power than crown glass even when a particular prism of flint deviates the mean ray less, because the ratio, not the deviation alone, is ω.

Confusion

  • Do not confuse dispersive power with total deviation; total deviation depends on apex angle.
  • Use small-angle thin-prism relations only within their approximation.
  • A symbol is not a number until the unit is named.
  • A proportional relation is not an equality until the constant is included.
  • A vector answer needs a direction as well as a magnitude.
  • A special case, such as a right angle or a steady temperature, has to be stated before it is used.

Summary

A prism separates colours because refractive index varies with wavelength. For a thin prism, ω=(nv−nr)/(ny−1) measures relative colour spread.

Specific Conditions

  • Dispersion Definition: Dispersion is the separation of white light into its constituent colors (spectrum) due to the dependence of the refractive index n on the wavelength λ of light (n(λ)).
  • Cauchy's Equation: For many transparent materials, the refractive index follows Cauchy's equation n(λ)=A+B/λ2+C/λ4. This explains why blue light (λ smaller) is refracted more than red light (λ larger).
  • Prism Validity: The formula for dispersive power ω=(nv​−nr​)/(ny​−1) assumes small angle of deviation and that the prism is in air. For non-thin prisms or different mediums, angular dispersion depends on the geometry.

Exercises

1.

A thin prism has μ_v = 1.52, μ_r = 1.50 and mean μ = 1.51. Find the dispersive power.

Steps
  • ω = (μ_v − μ_r) / (μ − 1).
  • The numerator is 0.020.
  • The denominator is 0.51.
  • ω = 0.020 / 0.51 = 0.039.
  • Dispersive power has no unit.
Answer

0.039.
