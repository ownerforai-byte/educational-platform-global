  • 1. Topic Introduction — The Periodic Table of Matter Itself

    Atoms are not elementary: protons and neutrons are built from quarks, and a whole zoo of particles — baryons, mesons, leptons, neutrinos — populates the Standard Model. Every particle has an antiparticle; matter is the survivor of an almost perfect matter–antimatter cancellation.

  • 2. Fundamental Concepts

    2.1 Particles and antiparticles

    Every particle has an antiparticle with the same mass but opposite charge (and opposite quantum numbers): electron–positron, proton–antiproton, neutron–antineutron, photon (its own antiparticle). Particle–antiparticle pairs can be created from energy (≥ 2mc²) and annihilate into photons.

    2.2 The quark model

    • Quarks come in six "flavours": up (u, +⅔e), down (d, −⅓e), strange, charm, bottom, top. No free quark is ever observed — confinement.
    • Baryons (protons, neutrons) = 3 quarks: proton = uud (+e), neutron = udd (0).
    • Mesons = quark–antiquark pairs (e.g. pions) — unstable, mediating the nuclear force.
    • Leptons are truly elementary, feeling no strong force: electron, muon, tau, and their neutrinos (ν) — nearly massless, chargeless, passing through matter almost untouched (~10¹⁴ solar neutrinos cross you each second).

    2.3 The families

    FamilyMembersCompositionExamples

    Mesonsquark + antiquarkCompositepion, kaon
    Leptonselementary—e, μ, τ, ν_e, ν_μ, ν_τ

    Baryons and mesons together are hadrons — particles feeling the strong force.

    2.4 Worked reasoning

    Q (NEB-style): Show that a proton's charge follows from its quark content. Proton = uud: (+⅔e) + (+⅔e) + (−⅓e) = +e ✓. Neutron = udd: (+⅔e) + (−⅓e) + (−⅓e) = 0 ✓.

    Q (NEB-style): Why are neutrinos so hard to detect? They carry no charge and almost no mass, and feel neither the strong nor the electromagnetic force — only the weak force. A neutrino can traverse light-years of lead with little chance of interaction.

Summary

Every particle has an antiparticle — same mass, opposite charge — created in pairs from energy (≥ 2mc²) and annihilating into photons. Matter's building blocks: quarks (six flavours; u +⅔e, d −⅓e), which never appear free (confinement). Baryons = 3 quarks (proton uud = +e; neutron udd = 0); mesons = quark–antiquark pairs (pions); both are hadrons feeling the strong force. Leptons — electron, muon, tau and their neutrinos — are elementary, and neutrinos are neutral, nearly massless, and so weakly interacting that 10¹⁴ pass through you each second unnoticed.
