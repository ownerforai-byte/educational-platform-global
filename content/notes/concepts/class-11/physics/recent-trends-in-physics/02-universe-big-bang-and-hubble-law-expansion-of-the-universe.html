Summary

The Big Bang theory describes the universe originating from an extremely hot, dense state ~13.8 billion years ago and expanding ever since. Hubble's Law v = H₀d states that galaxies recede at speeds proportional to their distance, providing direct evidence for cosmic expansion. The Hubble constant H₀ ≈ 70 km/s/Mpc quantifies the current expansion rate.

Notes

  • Big Bang Theory: The universe began as a singularity of infinite density and temperature approximately 13.8 × 10⁹ years ago. Space itself expanded, cooling the universe and allowing formation of subatomic particles, atoms, stars, and galaxies over time.
  • Cosmic Timeline: t = 0: Singularity. t < 10⁻⁴³ s: Planck epoch (quantum gravity). t ~ 10⁻³⁶ s: Inflationary epoch (exponential expansion). t ~ 3 min: Nucleosynthesis (H, He, Li formed). t ~ 380,000 yr: Recombination (CMB released). t ~ 400 Myr: First stars. t ~ 9 Gyr: Solar system forms.
  • Evidence for Big Bang: (1) Hubble's Law — galaxies receding proportionally to distance. (2) Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) — 2.725 K blackbody radiation, uniform to 1 part in 10⁵. (3) Abundance of light elements — H:He ratio ~3:1 matches nucleosynthesis predictions. (4) Large-scale structure — galaxy distribution matches gravitational growth from initial fluctuations.
  • Hubble's Law: v = H₀d, where v is recession velocity, d is proper distance, and H₀ is Hubble constant. Discovered by Edwin Hubble in 1929 using Cepheid variable stars as standard candles. Current best estimate: H₀ ≈ 67–74 km/s/Mpc (tension between CMB and local measurements).
  • Interpretation of Redshift: Cosmological redshift z = Δλ/λ is NOT Doppler shift from motion through space. It results from stretching of photon wavelengths as space expands during transit. For small z: v ≈ cz; for large z: relativistic cosmology required.
  • Hubble Time and Age: t_H = 1/H₀ ≈ 14.4 Gyr gives rough age estimate assuming constant expansion. Actual age differs due to deceleration (matter domination) then acceleration (dark energy). Current age: 13.8 Gyr from ΛCDM model fitting.
  • Expansion of Space vs Motion Through Space: Galaxies are not flying apart through pre-existing space. Space itself is stretching, carrying galaxies with it. No center exists — every observer sees others receding. Analogy: dots on inflating balloon surface.
  • Dark Energy and Accelerated Expansion: Type Ia supernovae observations (1998) revealed expansion is accelerating, not slowing. Attributed to dark energy (Λ in Einstein's equations), constituting ~68% of total energy density. Fate: eternal accelerated expansion ('Big Freeze').
  • Limitations and Open Questions: Singularity problem (quantum gravity needed). Horizon problem (solved by inflation). Flatness problem (solved by inflation). Nature of dark matter (~27%) and dark energy (~68%). Multiverse speculation from eternal inflation.

Specific Conditions

  • Homogeneity and Isotropy: Cosmological principle assumes universe looks same everywhere (homogeneous) and in all directions (isotropic) on large scales (>100 Mpc). Validated by CMB uniformity and galaxy surveys.
  • General Relativity Framework: Expansion described by Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric derived from Einstein's field equations. Newtonian mechanics insufficient for cosmological scales.
  • Redshift-Distance Relation Linear Only Locally: v = H₀d holds for z < 0.1. Higher redshifts require full relativistic treatment with scale factor a(t) and cosmological parameters Ω_m, Ω_Λ.
  • Hubble Constant Tension: Local distance ladder (Cepheids + SNe Ia) gives H₀ ≈ 73 km/s/Mpc; CMB (Planck satellite) gives H₀ ≈ 67.4 km/s/Mpc. Discrepancy unresolved — possible new physics or systematic errors.

Key Points

  • Universe originated ~13.8 Gyr ago from hot, dense state (Big Bang).
  • Hubble's Law v = H₀d proves cosmic expansion.
  • CMB at 2.725 K is relic radiation from recombination epoch.
  • Redshift measures expansion of space, not Doppler motion.
  • Dark energy drives accelerated expansion discovered in 1998.
  • H₀ tension between local and CMB measurements remains unsolved.

Important Statements

  • Statement 1: Hubble's Law provides observational evidence that the universe is expanding uniformly.
  • Statement 2: Cosmic microwave background radiation confirms the hot Big Bang origin of the universe.
  • Statement 3: Cosmological redshift arises from expansion of space, not relative motion through space.
  • Statement 4: The universe has no center or edge — expansion occurs everywhere simultaneously.
  • Statement 5: Dark energy constitutes ~68% of cosmic energy density and causes accelerated expansion.

Important Notes

  • 1 Mpc = 3.26 million light-years = 3.086 × 10²² m.
  • CMB was accidentally discovered by Penzias and Wilson in 1965 (Nobel Prize 1978).
  • Georges Lemaître first proposed expanding universe and 'primeval atom' hypothesis in 1927, before Hubble's observations.
  • NEB asks to explain Hubble's Law, interpret redshift, and list evidence supporting Big Bang theory.

Exam Short Tricks

  • v = H₀d: linear relation between speed and distance.
  • Higher z → farther away → older lookback time.
  • CMB = afterglow of Big Bang at 2.725 K.
  • Three pillars: Expansion + CMB + Light element abundance.
  • Accelerating expansion → dark energy dominates today.

Examples

  • Galaxy at d = 100 Mpc: v = 70 × 100 = 7000 km/s. Redshift z ≈ v/c = 7000/300000 ≈ 0.023.
  • Hubble time: t_H = 1/(70 km/s/Mpc) = 1/(2.27×10⁻¹⁸ s⁻¹) ≈ 4.4×10¹⁷ s ≈ 14.0 Gyr.
  • CMB peak wavelength: λ_max = 2.9 mm·K / 2.725 K ≈ 1.06 mm (microwave region).
  • Lookback time for z = 1: Light emitted when universe was half its current size (~7.7 Gyr ago).

Universal Facts

  • Edwin Hubble published his law in 1929; Georges Lemaître derived it theoretically in 1927 from general relativity.
  • The observable universe has radius ~46.5 billion light-years despite age 13.8 Gyr due to expansion during light travel.
  • Olbers' paradox (why night sky is dark) resolved by finite age and expansion redshifting distant starlight.
  • Future telescopes (Euclid, Roman) aim to resolve H₀ tension and constrain dark energy equation of state w.

Confusion

  • Thinking Big Bang was explosion IN space: It was expansion OF space itself — no pre-existing void.
  • Believing galaxies move THROUGH space: Comoving galaxies are stationary; space stretches between them.
  • Assuming universe expands into something: There is no 'outside' — spacetime is self-contained.
  • Confusing Hubble flow with peculiar motion: Local gravitational interactions cause deviations from pure Hubble flow.
  • Thinking CMB comes from stars: It predates star formation by hundreds of millions of years.
