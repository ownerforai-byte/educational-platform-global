  • A 0.5 kg ball is attached to a 1 m string and swings in a conical pendulum at θ=45∘. Find the period, tension, and speed. Solution: Tperiod​=2πlcosθ/g​=2π1×0.707/9.8​=2π0.0721​=2π×0.269=1.69 s. T=mg/cosθ=0.5×9.8/0.707=6.93 N. r=lsinθ=0.707 m. v=grtanθ​=9.8×0.707×1​=2.63 m/s.
  • A conical pendulum has a period of 2 s and a string length of 1 m. Find the angle θ and the radius of the circle. Solution: Tperiod​=2πlcosθ/g​, so 2=2πcosθ/9.8​. (2/(2π))2=cosθ/9.8. cosθ=9.8/(π2)=0.993. θ=6.8∘. r=1×sin6.8∘=0.119 m.
  • A 0.2 kg mass on a 0.5 m string rotates in a horizontal circle at 3 rad/s. Find the angle, tension, and height. Solution: ω=g/(lcosθ)​, so cosθ=g/(lω2)=9.8/(0.5×9)=2.18. Since this exceeds 1, the pendulum cannot maintain this angular velocity — the required angle would exceed 90 degrees. The maximum ω=g/l​=9.8/0.5​=4.43 rad/s.
  • Two conical pendulums: Pendulum A has length 0.8 m at θ=30∘, Pendulum B has length 0.5 m at θ=45∘. Which has the shorter period? Solution: hA​=0.8cos30∘=0.693 m, hB​=0.5cos45∘=0.354 m. TA​=2π0.693/9.8​=1.67 s, TB​=2π0.354/9.8​=1.19 s. Pendulum B has the shorter period.
  • A 100 g ball swings as a conical pendulum with a period of 1.5 s and string length 0.6 m. Find the angle, tension, and speed. Solution: Tperiod​=2πlcosθ/g​, so cosθ=gT2/(4π2l)=9.8×2.25/(4π2×0.6)=22.05/23.69=0.931. θ=21.4∘. T=mg/cosθ=0.1×9.8/0.931=1.05 N. r=0.6sin21.4∘=0.217 m. v=2πr/T=2π×0.217/1.5=0.91 m/s.
