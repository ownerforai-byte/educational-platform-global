Notice, Advertisement & Formal Announcements

Notes

  • What a notice is: a short, formal, public announcement — one paragraph, boxed, displayed on a board or website. It must be readable in under 15 seconds. Every wasted word costs marks.
  • Fixed format (5 parts, in order): 1) the word NOTICE at top-centre; 2) date of issue (top-left or top-right); 3) HEADING announcing the event in one line; 4) BODY — what, when, where, who, (whom to contact); 5) signature block: name, designation, issuing body, in the bottom-left corner.
  • The 4 W + 1 H checklist: WHAT is happening, WHEN (date + time), WHERE (venue), WHO is issuing/is eligible, HOW to participate/register. Miss any of these and the notice fails its purpose.
  • Language rules: present or future tense only ('is organised', 'will be held'); no first-person pronouns (I/we/my) — the notice speaks for the institution; no signatures of individuals inside the body; keep it under 50 words where possible.
  • Voice: passive voice dominates notices because the actor is the institution — 'A one-day workshop WILL BE CONDUCTED by the English Club.'
  • Registration/contact lines: end the body with action — 'Interested students may register with the undersigned by 15 March.' The word 'undersigned' replaces a personal signature.
  • Common exam tasks: notice for a lost item, a meeting, a tour, a competition, a change of schedule, an appeal for blood donation. Structure never changes — only the heading and the 4W+1H content.
  • Marks distribution (typical): format ~2, content (all 4W+1H) ~2, expression (concise, formal, no errors) ~1. Format errors are the cheapest marks lost — box your notice.

Confusion

  • ❌ Writing a notice like a letter (Dear Sir, yours faithfully). ✅ A notice has NO salutation and NO closing courtesy — it ends with name/designation only.
  • ❌ 'Our school is organising...' ✅ Notices avoid first person: 'The school is organising...' — the issuer is the institution, not the writer.
  • ❌ Past tense: 'A workshop was conducted...' ✅ Announcements look forward: 'will be conducted/is being organised'.
  • ❌ Long descriptive paragraphs. ✅ A notice is compressed information — usually 40-60 words, one block.
  • ❌ Forgetting the box/heading. ✅ The word NOTICE and the heading are structural marks — examiners award format marks for them.

Examples

  • LOST AND FOUND notice: 'A leather wallet containing Rs. 2,000 was lost near the library on 5 March. The finder is requested to deposit it with the undersigned. Ravi Sharma, Class 11'B'.
  • MEETING notice: 'The Student Council will hold its monthly meeting on 10 March at 1 p.m. in the conference hall. All members must attend. Anisha Karki, Secretary.'
  • TOUR notice: 'The Geography Club is organising a three-day field trip to Chitwan from 20-22 April. Fee: Rs. 4,000. Interested students of Classes 11-12 may register with the undersigned by 10 April.'

Key Points

  • Format: NOTICE → date → heading → 4W+1H body → name/designation.
  • Present/future tense, passive voice, no personal pronouns.
  • Under 50-60 words; every word must carry information.
  • End with a clear action line (register/contact/deposit).

Summary

Notice writing is compressed formal announcement: a fixed 5-part format, the 4W+1H information checklist, future-tense passive language, institutional voice, and an action line — all in under 60 words.

Practice Questions

  • Draft a notice for the school's annual sports meet (date, venue, registration deadline).
  • Draft a notice announcing the loss of a school library book and requesting its return.
  • Identify the missing 4W+1H element in the sample notice shown in class.
