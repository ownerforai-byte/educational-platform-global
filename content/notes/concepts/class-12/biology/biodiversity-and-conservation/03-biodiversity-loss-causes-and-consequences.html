    Fill order per concept: definition → structure → process → significance (§3.2). Step-by-step numbered points, Pioneer style.

  • Species are disappearing at 100–1000× the natural background rate. The driving causes are summarised as the four major causes: habitat loss and fragmentation, over-exploitation, alien species invasions, and co-extinctions. The consequences are not just sad stories — losing species erodes ecosystem services, narrows the genetic bank for food and medicine, and destabilises the systems that produce our food and water.

  • Worked example — the island cat. On a small oceanic island, one pair of introduced cats can exterminate a flightless bird species within decades: the bird evolved without mammal predators and nests on the ground. Cause 3 (alien invasion) acting on a small, isolated population. The lesson: island and freshwater ecosystems are the most fragile, and biosecurity (checking boats, cargo, pets) is conservation's cheapest tool.
