  • Heredity is chemistry. DNA was found in 1869 (Miescher), shown to be the transforming principle in 1944 (Avery), confirmed by the Hershey–Chase blender experiment in 1952, and its double helix solved by Watson and Crick in 1953 using Chargaff's base rules and Franklin's X-ray photo. From that structure follow everything: semi-conservative replication, the central dogma DNA → RNA → protein, the genetic code, and gene regulation (the lac operon as the model).

  • Worked example — reading the code. A template DNA strand reads 3'-TAC GGA TTA-5'. The mRNA is 5'-AUG CCU AAU-3' (U replaces T; transcription starts at the promoter, not at AUG by chance). Reading in frame: AUG = methionine (start), CCU = proline, AAU = asparagine — peptide Met-Pro-Asn. Note the logic: complement the template, then translate the mRNA, never the DNA directly.
