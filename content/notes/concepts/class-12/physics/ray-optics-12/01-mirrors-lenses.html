Notes

  • In the Cartesian convention the incident-light direction is positive, so a real object has a negative object distance. The mirror formula is then 1/v + 1/u = 1/f. The thin-lens formula is 1/v − 1/u = 1/f. The two formulas are not interchangeable.
  • For a concave mirror the focal length is negative in that convention. A real image has a negative v, on the same side as the object. A convex mirror has positive f and always gives a virtual, diminished image for a real object.
  • A convex lens has positive f. A real image on the far side of the lens has positive v. Use the lens formula there, not the mirror formula.
  • Mirror magnification is −v/u, and lens magnification is v/u. A negative value means the image is inverted. The size is the absolute value times the object size. For the mirror numbers u = −30 cm and v = −15 cm, m = −v/u = −0.50, an inverted image half as tall.
  • Power of a lens is 1/f with f in metres, in dioptres. A shorter focal length is a stronger lens. Two thin lenses in contact add their powers.
  • Rays are the check. A ray through the centre of a lens goes straight. A ray parallel to the axis passes through the focus. The formula and the ray diagram must agree on real or virtual.

Confusion

  • ❌ 'Convex lenses always produce real images.' ✅ They produce a virtual image if the object is placed between the lens and its focal point (e.g., in a magnifying glass).
  • ❌ 'Concave mirrors always form smaller images.' ✅ They form magnified, upright virtual images if the object is placed closer than the focal point.
  • ❌ 'The magnification formula is m = v/u.' ✅ For lenses, m = v/u; but for mirrors, m = -v/u. Sign conventions are crucial!
  • Object distance for a real object is negative in the Cartesian convention.
  • A concave mirror's f is negative there.
  • A positive lens is converging.
  • Magnification's sign is inversion, and its size uses the modulus.

Exercises

1.

A concave mirror has f = −10 cm. An object is at u = −30 cm. Find v with the mirror formula and say whether the image is real.

Steps
  • Use 1/v + 1/u = 1/f.
  • 1/v = 1/f − 1/u.
  • 1/f − 1/u = −1/10 − 1/(−30) = −1/10 + 1/30.
  • −3/30 + 1/30 = −2/30 = −1/15.
  • v = −15 cm. Negative v means a real image, 15 cm in front of the mirror.
Answer

v = −15 cm, a real image.
