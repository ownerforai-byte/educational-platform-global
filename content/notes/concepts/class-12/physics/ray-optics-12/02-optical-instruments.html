Notes

  • A simple microscope is one convex lens, a magnifying glass. The object sits inside the focal length, and the eye sees a virtual, upright, enlarged image.
  • If the image is at the near point, the magnification is about 1 + D/f. If the eye is relaxed and the image is at infinity, the object is at the focus and the magnification is D/f.
  • D is 25 cm for a normal eye. A shorter focal length is a stronger magnifier, with the limit that the lens must still be well made.
  • An astronomical refracting telescope in normal adjustment has the final image at infinity. The objective and eyepiece are separated by f_o + f_e, and the magnifying power is f_o/f_e.
  • The image is inverted. A terrestrial telescope adds an erecting lens, which lengthens the tube. That extra lens is not part of the magnifying-power formula.
  • This treatment is the single-lens magnifier and the telescope length. The compound microscope's two-lens product was the other instrument page, so the numbers here are different.

Confusion

  • ❌ 'Magnification of a microscope is just product of objective and eyepiece magnifications.' ✅ Magnification depends on the final image position (e.g., at near point vs. infinity) and the tube length.
  • ❌ 'Telescopes always make objects look bigger.' ✅ They make objects look closer and larger in angular size, but their absolute size (linear magnification) is tiny.
  • ❌ 'A microscope is just a lens that makes things big.' ✅ It is a system to increase the angular magnification of tiny objects placed near the focal point of the objective lens.
  • 1 + D/f is the near-point magnifier. D/f is the relaxed magnifier.
  • The simple microscope is one lens.
  • An erecting lens does not appear in f_o/f_e.
  • The final image at infinity is virtual, not a real image on a screen.

Exercises

1.

A telescope has f_o = 100 cm and f_e = 5.0 cm, set for a relaxed eye. Find the magnifying power and the tube length.

Steps
  • m = f_o / f_e = 100 / 5.0 = 20.
  • Tube length = f_o + f_e = 105 cm.
  • The final image is at infinity.
  • The image is inverted.
  • This is not the compound-microscope product of two ratios.
Answer

Power 20, tube length 105 cm.
