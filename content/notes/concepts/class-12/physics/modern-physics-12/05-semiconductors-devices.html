Notes

  • Silicon and germanium are semiconductors. At room temperature a few bonds break, giving a free electron and a hole. The conductivity lies between a metal and an insulator and rises with temperature.
  • Doping with a group-15 atom adds an extra electron. That is n-type, and electrons are the majority carriers. Doping with a group-13 atom leaves a hole. That is p-type, and holes are the majority carriers.
  • A p-n junction has a depletion layer. In forward bias the p-side is made positive, the barrier falls, and a large current flows. In reverse bias the barrier rises and only a tiny leakage current flows.
  • The diode is a one-way device. A rectifier uses that one-way behaviour to turn alternating voltage into a unidirectional voltage.
  • A bipolar transistor has two junctions, emitter, base and collector. A small base current controls a larger collector current. That is the current gain.
  • An LED is a diode that emits light when forward biased, as electrons and holes recombine. A solar cell is a junction that produces an emf when light creates pairs.

Confusion

  • ❌ Assuming intrinsic semiconductors conduct well at room temperature ✅ Intrinsic semiconductors have very few free charge carriers; doping is required to significantly increase conductivity.
  • ❌ Confusing P-type with positive charge ✅ P-type semiconductors are electrically neutral; they have "holes" as majority carriers, not a net positive charge.
  • ❌ Misinterpreting the depletion layer as an insulator ✅ The depletion layer is a region depleted of free charge carriers, acting as a barrier, not an absolute insulator.
  • n-type is not negatively charged overall. It is still neutral.
  • Forward bias is p positive, not p negative.
  • Holes are missing electrons, not positive protons moving.
  • Temperature helps a semiconductor conduct. It usually hurts a metal.

Exercises

1.

A silicon diode is connected with the p-side to the positive terminal of a battery and the n-side to the negative terminal. Say which bias this is, and whether a large current flows.

Steps
  • The p-side is positive.
  • That is forward bias.
  • The depletion barrier decreases.
  • A large current flows once the forward voltage is enough for silicon, about 0.7 V.
  • Swapping the battery would be reverse bias, with only leakage current.
Answer

Forward bias, and a large current flows.
