Notes

  • Two thin lenses in contact behave as one lens whose power is the sum of the powers. A 4 D convex lens and a 2 D convex lens in contact make 6 D. The equivalent focal length is 1/P with P in dioptres and the length in metres.
  • A convex lens and a concave lens in contact subtract. If the convex power is larger, the combination still converges, but more weakly. Spectacle corrections are built this way.
  • If the lenses are separated, you do not add the powers. Find the image in the first lens, then use that image as the object for the second, measuring the object distance from the second lens.
  • A lens in front of a mirror is the same idea. Light passes the lens, reflects from the mirror, and passes the lens again. Each pass is a separate calculation, and the sign convention stays the same.
  • A real image from the first element can fall to the right of the second element. For the second element that object is virtual, and its object distance is positive in the Cartesian convention. The sign is the part that needs a sketch.
  • The equivalent of two separated lenses depends on the gap. Pulling them apart changes the combination even though each lens is unchanged.

Universal Facts

  • Lens maker: 1/f = (μ − 1)(1/R₁ − 1/R₂). Power P = 1/f (dioptre, f in metres); lenses add powers in contact.
  • Refraction at a plane surface (Snell): n₁ sinθ₁ = n₂ sinθ₂. Total internal reflection when θ > θ_c, sinθ_c = n₂/n₁ (n₂<n₁).
  • Prism minimum deviation: n = sin((A + D_min)/2)/sin(A/2). Achromatic doublet pairs crown (low Abbe) + flint (high Abbe).

Key Points

  • A positive and a negative power can cancel.
  • A mirror and a lens are handled one surface at a time.
  • The first image is the object for the next element.

Summary

Thin lenses in contact add their powers, so a 4 D lens and a 2 D lens make 6 D. Separated lenses are treated one at a time, and the image from the first is the object for the second. A concave lens contributes a negative power.

Exercises

1.

A 4.0 D lens and a 2.0 D lens are in contact. Find the equivalent power and focal length.

Steps
  • The lenses touch, so the powers add.
  • P = 4.0 + 2.0 = 6.0 D.
  • f = 1/P = 1/6.0 m.
  • f = 0.167 m = 16.7 cm.
  • The combination converges, more strongly than either lens alone.
Answer

6.0 D, and f = 16.7 cm.
