Notes

  • Faraday found that an emf appears in a coil while the magnetic flux through it is changing. A bar magnet at rest in the coil, however strong, induces nothing.
  • The flux through one turn is B times the perpendicular area. For N turns the flux linkage is N times that flux. The induced emf equals the negative of the rate of change of flux linkage.
  • The flux can be changed by moving the magnet, by switching the field, by changing the area of the loop, or by rotating the coil. A generator uses the rotation.
  • A faster change produces a larger emf. Doubling N doubles the emf for the same change of flux per turn. That is why a search coil has many turns.
  • If the circuit is closed, the emf drives an induced current. If the circuit is open, the emf is still there and can be read by a voltmeter, but no current flows.
  • The direction of the induced current is fixed by Lenz's law so that it opposes the change of flux. Faraday's law supplies the size. Lenz's law supplies the direction.

Universal Facts

  • Force on moving charge F = q(v×B); on a wire F = BIL sinθ.
  • Faraday: EMF ε = −dΦ/dt; Lenz's law gives the sign (induced current opposes the change in flux).
  • Self-inductance L = NΦ/I; inductance stores energy U = ½LI². Mutual inductance M couples two coils.

Key Points

  • • Faraday's laws of electromagnetic induction
  • A changing flux induces an emf.
  • More turns mean a larger emf.
  • A steady flux induces nothing.
  • The minus sign is Lenz's law.

Summary

An emf is induced only while flux linkage is changing, and its magnitude is N times the rate of change of flux. More turns or a faster change mean a larger emf. A steady flux induces nothing, and Lenz's law fixes the direction.

Exercises

1.

A coil of 100 turns has its flux increased by 0.020 Wb in 0.50 s. Find the magnitude of the average induced emf.

Steps
  • Average emf = N ΔΦ / Δt.
  • N ΔΦ = 100 × 0.020 = 2.0 Wb-turns.
  • Divide by 0.50 s.
  • The magnitude is 4.0 V.
  • The direction opposes the increase of flux.
Answer

4.0 V.
