Magnetic Force on Moving Charges

Class 12PhysicsMagnetism

Notes

  • A charge moving in a magnetic field feels F = q v B sinθ, where θ is the angle between the velocity and the field. The force is zero if the charge is at rest or if it moves parallel to the field.
  • The direction is perpendicular to both v and B. For a positive charge the right-hand rule gives it. A negative charge feels the opposite force.
  • Because the force is always perpendicular to the velocity, it does no work and does not change the speed. It changes only the direction.
  • In a uniform field, motion perpendicular to the field is a circle of radius m v /(q B). Motion with a component along the field is a helix.
  • Combined electric and magnetic forces are the Lorentz force, qE + q v B in vector form. A velocity selector sets the two forces opposite and equal so that qE = q v B.
  • The tesla is the field that exerts 1 N on a charge of 1 C moving at 1 m/s perpendicular to the field.

Confusion

  • ❌ 'A magnetic force can do work on a charged particle.' ✅ Magnetic force is always perpendicular to velocity, so it does zero work; it changes direction, not speed.
  • ❌ 'A stationary charge experiences magnetic force.' ✅ The particle must be moving (v ≠ 0) and not parallel to B; a stationary charge feels no magnetic force.
  • ❌ 'The force direction is given by the right-hand rule for negative charges too.' ✅ For negative charges (electrons), reverse the right-hand rule direction (or use left hand).
  • A stationary charge feels electric force but not magnetic force.
  • The magnetic force does not speed the charge up.
  • sinθ is not optional when the velocity is not perpendicular.
  • Negative charges reverse the rule.

Exercises

1.

A charge of 2.0 mC moves at 10 m/s perpendicular to a 0.50 T field. Find the force.

Steps
  • q = 2.0 × 10^−3 C, v = 10, B = 0.50, θ = 90° so sinθ = 1.
  • F = q v B = 2.0 × 10^−3 × 10 × 0.50.
  • F = 0.020 × 0.50 = 0.010 N.
  • The force is perpendicular to the velocity, so the speed does not change.
  • A negative charge of the same size would feel 0.010 N the other way.
Answer

0.010 N, perpendicular to the velocity.
