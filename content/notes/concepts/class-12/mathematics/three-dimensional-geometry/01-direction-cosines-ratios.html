Confusion

  • ❌ 'Direction cosines are arbitrary' ✅ Direction cosines l, m, n are fixed values for a given vector: l = cos α, m = cos β, n = cos γ.
  • ❌ 'Sum of direction ratios is always 1' ✅ The sum of the squares of direction cosines (l² + m² + n²) equals 1; direction ratios do not have this constraint.
  • ❌ 'Direction ratios and cosines are the same' ✅ Direction ratios (a, b, c) are proportional to direction cosines (l, m, n), not equal.
