> This policy applies to **any paid feature** of the App (premium access,
> added AI credits, or subscriptions). All details are filled in (refund window:
> 7 days; support: ravikisan1814@gmail.com). If you use Google Play Billing,
> keep it consistent with your Play Billing setup before submission. If the app

## 1. Free features (no purchase required)

- Browsing notes, mind maps, labs, PYQs, quizzes, and progress tracking
- A limited **daily credit pool** (default: a small number of AI actions per
  day, resetting at midnight) is available to all signed-in users at no cost.

## 2. Paid features (if enabled)

- **Premium / Pro access** – unlocks unlimited or higher AI usage and any
  premium-gated features.
- **Extra AI credits** – one-time purchases that top up your credit pool.
- Pricing, billing cycle (one-time vs. subscription), and what each includes
  will be clearly shown **before** you confirm a purchase.

## 3. Billing (Google Play Billing, where used)

- Purchases are processed by **Google Play** and billed to your Google account.
- Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each cycle **unless you
  cancel before the renewal date**. You manage renewals in the Play Store under
  *Subscriptions*.
- You will be notified of any price change at least **3 days** before it takes
  effect.
- Prices are in the currency of your store region and may vary by country.

## 4. Refund policy

- **Google Play** handles refunds for in-app purchases and subscriptions. To
  request a refund, use *Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Order history*
  or contact Google.
- **In-app / service-credit refunds (extra AI credits):**
  - AI actions that **fail on our side** are refunded automatically to your pool
    (the platform does not charge you for a failed request).
  - Unused credit or premium purchased **within `7` days** of purchase can be
    requested for a refund by emailing **ravikisan1814@gmail.com** from your
    account address; we will review and process or explain why it cannot be
    refunded.
- Refunds of subscription periods already used are at Google's discretion per
  Play policy.

## 5. Cancellations

- **Subscriptions:** cancel anytime via Play Store; you keep access until the
  paid period ends.
- **Credit tops:** credits are non-refundable once consumed by successful AI
  actions; unused credits may be refundable within the window in §4.

## 6. Support

For billing issues: **ravikisan1814@gmail.com** (include your account email, order
ID, and a short description).
