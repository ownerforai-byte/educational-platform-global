Cookie & Session Policy

App: Ravikisan's Platform · Last updated: 14 October 2026 · Contact: ravikisan1814@gmail.com

What we use

We use functionally essential cookies only, to keep you signed in and protect your account. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers.

Cookies set

CookiePurposeTypeLifetime
sb-access-tokenShort-lived session (keeps you logged in)Essential · httpOnly · Secure in production · SameSite=Lax~1 hour, auto-refreshed
sb-refresh-tokenLong-lived token to stay signed inEssential · httpOnly · Secure · SameSite=Lax30 days, rotated on refresh
  • Both are httpOnly (not readable by page scripts).
  • In production both are flagged Secure (HTTPS only).
  • SameSite=Lax limits cross-site sending (reduces CSRF risk).

Browser / PWA local storage

If you install the App as a PWA or use it in a browser, we may use local storage for non-essential, non-personal UI preferences (e.g. theme). It does not contain your password or personal data.

Controlling / removing

  • We do not set tracking cookies to opt out of.
  • Blocking the two session cookies means you cannot stay signed in, and authenticated features (progress, bookmarks, AI history) become unavailable.
  • Use Sign out to clear both session cookies.

Protection & changes

Sessions are protected in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (Supabase). We never read or log full passwords. We may update this policy; changes are marked with a new date.
