Geography · Physical Essential GK

Ecological Belts, Peaks & River Basins

Nepal covers 147,516 km² across Mountain (15%), Hill (68%), and Terai (17%). Highest point: Sagarmatha (8,848.86 m). Lowest point: Kechana Kawal, Jhapa (58 m).

• Koshi River: Largest volume (7 tributaries: Sunkoshi, Tamor, Dudhkoshi, Arun, Tamakoshi, Indrawati, Likhu).
• Karnali River: Longest river inside Nepal (507 km). Deepest gorge: Kali Gandaki (Dana).
• Major Lakes: Tilicho (4,919 m), Rara (largest, 10.8 km²), Shey Phoksundo (deepest, 145 m).
Geography · Administrative Federal Nepal

7 Provinces & Local Governments

Under Constitution 2072: 7 Provinces, 77 administrative districts, and 753 Local Units (6 Metros, 11 Sub-Metros, 276 Municipalities, 460 Rural Municipalities).

• Largest Province by Area: Karnali (27,984 km²). Smallest: Madhesh (9,661 km²).
• Largest District by Area: Dolpa (7,889 km²). Smallest: Bhaktapur (119 km²).
Constitution · Fundamental Rights Part 3

31 Fundamental Rights (Articles 16–46)

Every citizen is guaranteed 31 fundamental rights under Part 3 of the Constitution of Nepal (promulgated Ashwin 3, 2072).

• Art. 16: Right to live with dignity. Art. 17: Right to freedom.
• Art. 18: Right to equality. Art. 27: Right to information.
• Art. 46: Right to constitutional remedies (Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Quo Warranto, Certiorari, Prohibition).
History · Milestones Timeline

Dynasties, Unification & Treaties

Gopal dynasty (first king Bhuktaman), Kirat (29 kings, Yalambar), Lichhavi golden age (Mandev, Anshuverma), and Modern Unification.

• Treaty of Sugauli: Signed March 3, 1816 ending Anglo-Nepal War.
• Kot Massacre: Ashwin 2, 1903 BS (rise of Jung Bahadur Rana).
• Declaration of Republic: Jestha 15, 2065 BS by first Constituent Assembly.

Question 1 · Constitution of Nepal 2 Marks

Under the Constitution of Nepal, which Article guarantees the "Right to Information" (सूचनाको हक)?

Explanation:

Article 27 guarantees every citizen the right to demand and receive information on any matter of public interest. (Article 25 is Right to Property; Article 30 is Clean Environment).

Question 2 · Geography of Nepal 2 Marks

Which of the following is the longest river wholly flowing inside the territory of Nepal?

Explanation:

Karnali is the longest river inside Nepal (507 km). Koshi has the largest water discharge volume, and Narayani has the deepest gorge (Kali Gandaki).

Nepal Public Service Commission Examination Regulations

Syllabus structure for Section Officer (शाखा अधिकृत), NaSu, and Kharidar.

Section Officer (4 Papers Structure)

  • First Paper (100 Marks): General Knowledge (GK - 60 Marks) & General Mental Ability (IQ - 40 Marks). 100 MCQs, 1 hr 30 mins. Negative mark: 20%.
  • Second Paper (100 Marks): Governance System (शासन प्रणाली), Constitution, Federalism, Public Administration & Development.
  • Third Paper (100 Marks): Contemporary Issues (समसामयिक मामिला) - Economic, Social, Environmental & International Affairs.
  • Fourth Paper (100 Marks): Service Specific (Administration / Foreign Service / Audit / Judiciary).
