Question 1 · Mathematics 1 Mark

The value of ∫ [ (sin x - cos x) / (sin x + cos x) ] dx is:

Explanation:

Let u = sin x + cos x. Then du = (cos x - sin x) dx = -(sin x - cos x) dx. Thus ∫ -du/u = -ln|u| + C = -ln|sin x + cos x| + C.

Question 2 · Physics 2 Marks

A solid cylinder and a hollow sphere of equal mass and radius roll down an incline without slipping. Which reaches the bottom first?

Explanation:

Acceleration a = (g sin θ) / (1 + k²/r²). Solid cylinder has k²/r² = 1/2 = 0.5. Hollow sphere has k²/r² = 2/3 ≈ 0.67. Smaller k²/r² gives larger acceleration, so the solid cylinder arrives first.

Key Examination Regulations

  • Negative Marking: 10% penalty for every incorrect choice (i.e. -0.1 on 1-mark questions, -0.2 on 2-mark questions). Unanswered questions suffer no penalty.
  • Eligibility Criteria: Minimum 45% or Grade C in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics in +2 Science or A-Levels.
  • Constituent Campuses: Pulchowk Campus (Central), Thapathali Campus (Kathmandu), Paschimanchal Campus (WRC Pokhara), Purwanchal Campus (ERC Dharan).
