✅ Clarity notes — Biomolecules, Polymers & Practical

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Reducing vs Non-reducing sugar vs free C=O, Tollens+ — test: locked glycosidic, Tollens− (Sucrose? non-reducing)
  • Addition vs Condensation polymer vs no by-product (Teflon) — test: −H2O/NH3 lost (Nylon) (By-product? condensation)
  • Thermoplastic vs Thermosetting vs remouldable — test: cross-linked, set once (Heats soft? plastic)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Sucrose = glucose+fructose, NON-reducing trap
  • ⛔ Nylon-6,6: hexamethylene + adipic (6,6)
  • ⛔ Protein test: biuret violet; primary structure = sequence
