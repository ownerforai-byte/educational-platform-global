✅ Clarity notes — p-Block, d-Block & Coordination

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Oxidation state vs Coordination number vs charge on metal — test: ligand donor atoms (Counting charge or neighbours?)
  • Linkage vs Ionisation isomerism vs same inside, different donor atom — test: ion swaps in/out (Formula same? check brackets)
  • High vs Low spin vs Δ0<P (weak field) — test: Δ0>P (strong field) (Strong ligand (CN−,CO)? low spin)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ KMnO4: acidic MnO4−→Mn²⁺ (5e−); neutral→MnO2
  • ⛔ Cr2O7²⁻ orange→green Cr³⁺ on reduction
  • ⛔ en/dien count 2/3 toward CN; bidentate ×2 sites
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 SPECTRO: CO>CN>NO2>en>NH3>H2O>F — strong→low spin
  • 💡 CFT-P: Pairing costs P, splitting pays Δ
  • 💡 MNO4-5E: acidic permanganate takes 5 electrons
