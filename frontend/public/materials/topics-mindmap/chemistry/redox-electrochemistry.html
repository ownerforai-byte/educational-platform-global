✅ Clarity notes — Redox & Electrochemistry

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Oxidation vs Reduction vs loss of e− (ON ↑) — test: gain of e− (ON ↓) (ON up? oxidised (reducing agent))
  • Galvanic vs Electrolytic vs spontaneous, anode(−) — test: needs power, anode(+) (Who pays? chemical vs battery)
  • Ecell vs E°cell vs real (Nernst, Q) — test: standard (Q=1) (Concentrations 1M? E°)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Balance O with H2O, H with H+, charge with e−
  • ⛔ Nernst: E=E°−0.0591/n·logQ at 25°C
  • ⛔ Λm RISES on dilution (weak: steeply)
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 OIL-RIG: Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain
  • 💡 AN-OX: ANode OXidation, CAT-RED
  • 💡 NERNST-59: 0.0591/n per decade
