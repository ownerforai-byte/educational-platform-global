✅ Clarity notes — Mole Concept & Stoichiometry

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Molarity vs Molality vs n/V (T-dependent) — test: n/kg solvent (T-free) (Heating? molality stable)
  • Empirical vs Molecular formula vs simplest ratio — test: ×n actual (Molar mass given? multiply)
  • Limiting vs Excess reagent vs runs out first — test: left over (Divide n by coeff, smallest wins)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ % questions: convert %→g→mol→ratio, assume 100 g
  • ⛔ Dalton partial p=χ·Ptotal; Graham rate∝1/√M
  • ⛔ n=m/M for molecules; N/NA for atoms — don't mix
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 MVM: Molarity-Volume-Moles triangle
  • 💡 LIMIT: 'Least Is Miserably Important Target'
  • 💡 PV-NRT: 'Pressure Volume — Never Run Thirsty'
