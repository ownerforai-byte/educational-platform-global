✅ Clarity notes — Alcohols, Carbonyls & Amines

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Aldol vs Cannizzaro vs α-H present — test: no α-H + conc. OH− (α-H? yes→aldol)
  • Basicity aq vs gas vs aq: 2°>1°>3° — test: gas: 3°>2°>1° (In water? solvation flips order)
  • Tollens vs Fehling vs Ag mirror (aldehyde) — test: red Cu2O (aliphatic aldehyde) (Aromatic aldehyde? Fehling fails)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Lucas: 3° instant turbidity, 1° heat needed
  • ⛔ Iodoform: CH3CO− or CH3CH(OH)− only
  • ⛔ Diazotisation at 0–5°C, then Sandmeyer/Gattermann
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 LUCAS-321: 3-fast, 2-slow, 1-sleeps
  • 💡 TOLL-SILVER: aldehyde → silver mirror
  • 💡 DIAZO-05: keep at 0–5°C
