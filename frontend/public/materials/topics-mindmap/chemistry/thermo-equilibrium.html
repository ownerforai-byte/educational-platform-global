✅ Clarity notes — Thermodynamics & Equilibrium

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Kc vs Q vs at equilibrium — test: right now (Q<K? go forward)
  • ΔG vs ΔG° vs any point — test: standard state; −RTlnK (At eq? ΔG=0, ΔG° fixed)
  • Buffer vs Salt hydrolysis vs resists pH change — test: pH from Ka/Kb of ions (Strong-strong salt? neutral)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Kp=Kc(RT)^Δng — Δng counts GASES only
  • ⛔ Le Chatelier: pressure shifts to fewer gas moles
  • ⛔ Henderson: pH=pKa+log[S]/[A] — salt over acid
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 LE-CHAT: 'Less Exerts, Change Halts At Target'
  • 💡 K-Q-GO: Q chases K to make ΔG zero
  • 💡 HENDY: Salt-on-top (log S/A)

📋 Formula sheet list — Thermodynamics & Equilibrium (same as diagram, selectable)

  1. Enthalpy: ΔH=ΔU+ΔngRT ● Hess's law additivity ● Enthalpy H = heat at const P
  2. Entropy & Gibbs: ΔS=q_rev/T ● ΔG=ΔH−TΔS ● Entropy S = disorder J/K
  3. Spontaneity: ΔG<0 spontaneous ● ΔG°=−RT lnK ● Spontaneous = ΔG<0 feasible
  4. Chemical Eq.: Kc, Kp=Kc(RT)^Δng ● Le Chatelier shifts ● Kc vs Kp = Kp=Kc(RT)^Δng
  5. Ionic Eq.: Ka·Kb=Kw ● pH=−log[H⁺] ● Kw = 1e−14 at 25°C
  6. Buffers & Salts: Henderson: pH=pKa+log[S]/[A] ● Ksp solubility ● Henderson = pH=pKa+log(S/A)
