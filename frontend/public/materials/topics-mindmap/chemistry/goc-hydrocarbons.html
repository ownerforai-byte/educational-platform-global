✅ Clarity notes — GOC & Hydrocarbons

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Inductive vs Resonance vs σ distance-fades — test: π delocalised (Through p-orbital? resonance)
  • +M vs −M groups vs donate (−OH,−NH2) — test: withdraw (−NO2,−CN) (Speeds E+ attack? +M)
  • Markovnikov vs Anti-Markovnikov vs H to more-H carbon — test: peroxide only HBr (Peroxide present? flip for HBr)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Carbocation 3°>2°>1° stability; rearrangement possible
  • ⛔ Hückel 4n+2: benzene, tropylium pass; COT fails
  • ⛔ Ozonolysis: = becomes C=O on each side
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 Huckel-42: 4n+2 aromatic
  • 💡 MARKOV: 'Majority Hugs Majority' (H joins H-rich)
  • 💡 HYPER-A: α-H count = stability points
