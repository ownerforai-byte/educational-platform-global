✅ Clarity notes — Chemical Kinetics

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Rate vs Rate constant vs varies with conc — test: fixed at T (Doubling conc? rate changes, k doesn't)
  • Order vs Molecularity vs expt sum of powers — test: theory steps (Fractions/zero? must be order)
  • Threshold vs Activation energy vs energy to react — test: barrier above reactants (Ea = threshold − avg energy)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Units of k reveal order: 1st s⁻¹, 2nd M⁻¹s⁻¹
  • ⛔ t½=0.693/k ONLY first order
  • ⛔ Catalyst lowers Ea, NEVER changes ΔH/K
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 K-UNITS: s⁻¹ first, M⁻¹s⁻¹ second
  • 💡 HALF-693: first-order half 0.693/k
  • 💡 ARRHY: k=A·e^(−Ea/RT) — heat Accelerates
