✅ Clarity notes — Chemical Bonding

Mix-ups fixed:
  • σ vs π bond vs axial, stronger — test: lateral, weaker (Rotation allowed? σ)
  • Hybridisation vs Geometry vs central-atom mixing — test: incl. lone pairs (VSEPR) (Lone pairs? change shape not hybridisation)
  • Bond order vs Bond energy vs (Nb−Na)/2 — test: higher BO = shorter+stronger (O2 BO=2 paramagnetic proof of MOT)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Fajan: small cation + big anion = covalent
  • ⛔ H-bond needs H–F/O/N + lone pair on other
  • ⛔ Formal charge: minimise; negative on most EN atom
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 SIG-PI: 'Single Is Gold, Pair Is Plastic'
  • 💡 VSEPR-AXE: count X+E, look up shape
  • 💡 MOT-BO: minus Antibonding over 2
