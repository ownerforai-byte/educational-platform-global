✅ Clarity notes — Reproduction in Plants & Humans

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Pollination vs Fertilisation vs transfer — test: fusion (double in angiosperms) (Zygote formed? fertilisation)
  • Sperm vs Ovum contribution vs 4 motile, tiny — test: 1 + cytoplasm (Who gives mitochondria? mother)
  • FSH vs LH surge vs follicle growth — test: ovulation trigger (Mid-cycle peak? LH)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Double fertilisation: syngamy + triple fusion
  • ⛔ Menstrual: proliferative estrogen, secretory progesterone
  • ⛔ Zygote→morula→blastula→gastrula order
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 DOUBLE-FERT: 'Two Fusions, One Flower'
  • 💡 LH-SURGE: LH Launches the egg
  • 💡 28-DAY: 14 ovulation, 28 repeat

🥊 Topic word duels — quick list (same as diagram above)

  • T1 Asexual: ✔ Fission (parent splits in two) ⚔ ✘ budding (outgrowth breaks off) — test: split or sprout?
  • T2 Flower & Pollination: ✔ Self-pollination (same flower) ⚔ ✘ cross-pollination (another flower) — test: self or other?
  • T3 Seed & Fruit: ✔ Albuminous (endosperm kept) ⚔ ✘ exalbuminous (endosperm eaten) — test: kept or eaten?
  • T4 Male System: ✔ Sperm (tiny + motile) ⚔ ✘ ovum (big + still) — test: small-fast or big-still?
  • T5 Female System: ✔ Follicular (estrogen grows lining) ⚔ ✘ luteal (progesterone keeps it) — test: grow or keep?
  • T6 Pregnancy & Health: ✔ Contraception (pauses fertility) ⚔ ✘ sterilisation (permanent cut) — test: pause or stop?
