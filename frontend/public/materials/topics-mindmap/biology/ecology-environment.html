✅ Clarity notes — Ecology & Environment

Mix-ups fixed:
  • J vs S curve vs boom-bust, no K — test: logistic, K capped (Levels off? S)
  • Mutualism vs Commensalism vs both gain (+/+) — test: one gains, other neutral (+/0) (Who gains? count the pluses — lichen vs barnacle)
  • Primary vs Secondary succession vs bare rock (slow) — test: disturbed area (faster) (Soil present? secondary)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ 10% law: only ~10% transfers per level
  • ⛔ Inverted biomass pyramid: ocean (phytoplankton light)
  • ⛔ Minamata Hg; Itai-itai Cd; Montreal→CFC
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 10-PC: ten Percent Climb
  • 💡 J-BOOM S-FLAT: J jumps, S settles
  • 💡 MERCURY-MINA: Minamata = Mercury
