✅ Clarity notes — Plant Physiology

Mix-ups fixed:
  • C3 vs C4 vs CAM vs RuBisCO, photorespiration — test: Kranz/no-resp; night CO2 (Kranz? C4. Night stomata? CAM)
  • Xylem vs Phloem vs up, water (transpiration) — test: both ways, food (pressure flow) (Direction? up-only xylem)
  • PSII vs PSI vs P680 splits water — test: P700 makes NADPH (O2 released? PSII)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Calvin: 6 turns → 1 glucose (12 NADPH, 18 ATP)
  • ⛔ Glycolysis in cytoplasm, NOT mitochondria
  • ⛔ ABA closes stomata (stress); auxin apical dominance
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 CALVIN-6: six turns one sugar
  • 💡 XY-UP: Xylem Yanks Up
  • 💡 ABA-STOP: ABA Stops Transpiration, Opens? No—closes
