✅ Clarity notes — Genetics: Heredity & Molecular Basis

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Dominant vs Recessive vs shows in F1 — test: hidden in F1, 3:1 (F1 uniform? dominant)
  • Genotype vs Phenotype vs alleles (Tt) — test: trait (tall) (Letters or looks?)
  • DNA vs RNA vs deoxy, double, T — test: ribo, single, U (U present? RNA)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Dihybrid 9:3:3:1; test cross with recessive
  • ⛔ Leading continuous, lagging Okazaki fragments
  • ⛔ lac operon ON when lactose present (inducer)
