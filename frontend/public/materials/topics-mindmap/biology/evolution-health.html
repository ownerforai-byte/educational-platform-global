✅ Clarity notes — Evolution & Human Health

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Homologous vs Analogous vs same origin, diff use — test: same use, diff origin (Same bones? homologous)
  • Active vs Passive immunity vs self-made, slow — test: ready-made, fast (Mother's IgA? passive)
  • Benign vs Malignant vs encapsulated, stays — test: metastasis, spreads (Spreads? malignant)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Hardy-Weinberg: p+q=1; disturb = evolution
  • ⛔ Widal = typhoid; malaria = Plasmodium (female Anopheles)
  • ⛔ HIV attacks CD4 T-helper
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 HW-1: p²+2pq+q²=1
  • 💡 WIDAL-TY: Widal Typhoid
  • 💡 CD4-DOWN: HIV hits helpers
