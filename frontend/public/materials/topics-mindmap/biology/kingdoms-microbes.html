✅ Clarity notes — Kingdoms, Microbes & Structural Organisation

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Gram+ vs Gram− vs thick, purple — test: thin, pink (Purple? Gram+)
  • Bryophyta vs Pteridophyta vs no vascular — test: vascular, no seeds (Vessels? pteridophyta)
  • Lytic vs Lysogenic vs bursts now — test: hides as prophage (Cell bursts? lytic)
