✅ Clarity notes — Cell Structure & Biomolecules

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Prokaryote vs Eukaryote vs no nucleus/organelles — test: true nucleus + organelles (Membrane organelles? eukaryote)
  • SER vs RER vs smooth, lipid — test: rough, protein (ribosomes) (Dots (ribosomes)? RER)
  • Mitosis vs Meiosis vs 1 division, same number — test: 2 divisions, halved (Gametes? meiosis)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Mitochondria: inner folds = cristae, own DNA
  • ⛔ Chromatin: hetero dark silent, eu light active
  • ⛔ Km LOW = affinity HIGH (Michaelis)
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 POWER-MITO: Mitochondria = Powerhouse
  • 💡 RER-PRO: Rough→Protein, Smooth→Steroid
  • 💡 CELL-2D: 2 Divisions halve (meiosis)
