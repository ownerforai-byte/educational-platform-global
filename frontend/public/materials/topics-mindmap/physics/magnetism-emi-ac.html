✅ Clarity notes — Magnetism, EMI & AC

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Electric vs Magnetic force vs qE any charge — test: qv×B only moving ⊥ (Charge at rest in B? no magnetic force)
  • Lenz sign ε=−dΦ/dt vs minus = opposes — test: magnitude Faraday (Direction? Lenz; size? Faraday)
  • XL vs XC vs ωL rises with f — test: 1/ωC falls with f (High freq blocked? inductor)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Right-hand rules: fingers v, curl to B, thumb F — order matters
  • ⛔ Resonance f0: XL=XC, current MAX (series)
  • ⛔ Transformer: DC does NOTHING — AC only
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 F-BIL: F=BILsinθ
  • 💡 LENZ: 'Lazy Eel Never Zaps' — opposes change
  • 💡 X-RIDE: XL Xcites with f, XC Calms with f
