✅ Clarity notes — Units, Vectors & Kinematics

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Scalar vs Vector vs magnitude only — test: magnitude+direction (Direction matters? speed 5m/s vs velocity 5m/s North)
  • Dot vs Cross product vs ABcosθ scalar (work) — test: ABsinθ vector (torque) (Result scalar or vector?)
  • Distance vs Displacement vs path length ≥0 — test: straight-line change (can be 0) (Round trip? distance>0, displacement=0)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Always check dimensions [M L T] before trusting a formula
  • ⛔ Projectile: max range at 45°, max height at 90° — don't swap R and H formulas
  • ⛔ Vernier: L.C.=1MSD−1VSD; zero error subtract with sign
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 S-V-A: 'Silly Vet Acts' — Speed scalar, Velocity vector, Acceleration vector
  • 💡 Trig ASTC: All Silver Tea Cups (Q1 all +ve)
  • 💡 Units ladder: mm→cm→m→km, divide/multiply by 10/100/1000
