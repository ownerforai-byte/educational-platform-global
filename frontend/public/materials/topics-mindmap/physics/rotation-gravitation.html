✅ Clarity notes — Rotation & Gravitation

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Mass vs Weight vs constant kg — test: mg, varies with g (On Moon? mass same, weight ÷6)
  • g(h) vs g(depth) vs g(1−2h/R) — test: g(1−d/R) (Height doubles the drop vs depth)
  • Orbital vs Escape velocity vs √(GM/r) — test: √(2GM/R)=√2×orbital (Escape = √2 × surface orbital)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Moment of inertia depends on AXIS — same shape, different I
  • ⛔ Kepler T²∝r³: compare ratios, don't plug G unnecessarily
  • ⛔ Satellite E=−GMm/2r is negative (bound); energy needed = +GMm/2r
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 MIRACLE: MI Rod-centre ML²/12, Disc ½MR²
  • 💡 KPLR: Kepler Cubes the Radius
  • 💡 g-HD: Height Double-drop, Depth single
