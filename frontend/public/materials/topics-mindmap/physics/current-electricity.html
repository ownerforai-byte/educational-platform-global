✅ Clarity notes — Current Electricity

Mix-ups fixed:
  • EMF vs Terminal V vs open-circuit E — test: loaded V=E−Ir (Current flowing? V<E)
  • Series vs Parallel cells vs V adds, same I — test: I adds, same V (Need more volts or more hours?)
  • Ammeter vs Voltmeter vs series, low R — test: parallel, high R (Break the wire? ammeter)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Short circuit: R→0, I=E/r max — burns
  • ⛔ Potentiometer null point: NO current, E∝l
  • ⛔ Heating P=V²/R only for fixed-V; use I²R for fixed-I
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 KIRCH: Junction In=Out, Loop sum=0
  • 💡 EMF-IR: Every Measurement Falls with Internal R
  • 💡 SHUNT: Small resistance Helps Ammeter
