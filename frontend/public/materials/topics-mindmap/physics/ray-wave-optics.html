✅ Clarity notes — Ray & Wave Optics

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Interference vs Diffraction vs 2 slits, equal fringes — test: 1 slit, wide central max (Equal bright bands? interference)
  • Real vs Virtual image vs screen-catchable — test: mirror-only (Can project? real)
  • Total internal reflection vs denser→rarer + i>C — test: otherwise refracts (Going OUT of glass? maybe TIR)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Mirror sign: distances in FRONT negative (−u); keep one convention
  • ⛔ TIR needs BOTH conditions, not just angle
  • ⛔ Brewster μ=tan ip gives fully polarised reflected ray
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 MIR-R2: Mirror R=2F
  • 💡 TIR-C: sinC=1/μ — Critical inside denser
  • 💡 YDSE: Bright d·sinθ=nλ, fringe β=λD/d
