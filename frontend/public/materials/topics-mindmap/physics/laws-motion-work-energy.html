Laws of Motion & Work, Energy, Power

1. Newton's Laws

  • F=ma, F₁₂=−F₂₁
  • Inertia, momentum p=mv

2. Friction

  • f_s ≤ μ_s N
  • f_k = μ_k N

3. Circular Dynamics

  • a_c = v²/r
  • Banking: tanθ=v²/rg

4. Work & Energy

  • W = F·s cosθ
  • KE = ½mv²

5. Conservation

  • U_g=mgh, U_s=½kx²
  • E = KE+U = const

6. Collisions

  • Elastic: KE kept
  • e = (v₂−v₁)/(u₁−u₂)

✅ Clarity notes — Laws of Motion & Work, Energy, Power

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Static vs Kinetic friction vs self-adjusts ≤μsN — test: fixed μkN (Moving? No→fs=applied; Yes→fk)
  • Work vs Energy vs Power vs transfer F·s — test: stored KE/PE; rate P=W/t (Asked in Joules or Watts?)
  • Elastic vs Inelastic collision vs KE conserved — test: KE lost (e<1) (e=1 elastic; e=0 perfectly inelastic)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Normal N≠mg on incline/vertical acceleration — resolve first
  • ⛔ Work sign: cosθ — 90° force does ZERO work
  • ⛔ e formula: (v2−v1)/(u1−u2), order matters
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 FMA: Force Makes Acceleration
  • 💡 WEP: 'Work Ends Peacefully' — Work→Energy→Power
  • 💡 COLLIDE: e=1 sticks? No — e=0 sticks
