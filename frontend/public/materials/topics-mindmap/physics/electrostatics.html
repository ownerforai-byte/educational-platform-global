✅ Clarity notes — Electrostatics

Mix-ups fixed:
  • E vs V vs vector N/C — test: scalar volt; E=−dV/dr (Vector arrows? E. Contours? V)
  • Series vs Parallel capacitors vs Q same, 1/C add — test: V same, C add (Same plate charge? series)
  • Conductor vs Dielectric inside vs E=0 inside — test: E=E0/K reduced (K=1? conductor-like zero)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Zero V ≠ zero E (midpoint of like charges)
  • ⛔ Dipole far field ∝1/r³, NOT 1/r²
  • ⛔ Energy ½CV² — the ½ is the #1 forgotten factor
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 E-V-DR: E minus dV/dR
  • 💡 CAP-S: Series Same-charge, Parallel same-V
  • 💡 GAUSS: flux = charge IN ÷ ε₀
