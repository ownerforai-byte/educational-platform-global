✅ Clarity notes — Modern Physics & Semiconductors

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Photon energy vs Intensity vs hν per photon — test: photons/sec = brightness (Colour vs brightness?)
  • StP vs Photoelectric vs Compton vs emission by heat/light/scatter — test: threshold differs (What kicks the electron out?)
  • NAND/NOR universality vs any gate from NAND — test: any from NOR (Only one gate type allowed?)

  • ⛔ Stopping potential ×e = Kmax — convert eV↔J with 1.6e−19
