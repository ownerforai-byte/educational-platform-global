✅ Clarity notes — Solids, Fluids & Thermodynamics

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Heat vs Temperature vs Internal energy vs in transit Q — test: avg KE T; stored U (Moving? heat. Stored? U)
  • Cp vs Cv vs at const P (Cp) — test: at const V (Cv); Cp−Cv=R (Gas expands? use Cp)
  • Isothermal vs Adiabatic vs T const, ΔU=0 — test: Q=0, PV^γ const (Slow+conducting vs fast+insulated)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Bernoulli: fast flow = LOW pressure (lift, kinked pipe)
  • ⛔ Stokes valid only for small, slow spheres
  • ⛔ η=1−T2/T1 needs KELVIN, never °C
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 Y-STRESS: Young = Stress/Strain
  • 💡 BERNOULLI: 'Big pipes, lazy pressure'
  • 💡 Thermo FIRST: ΔQ=ΔU+ΔW (sign: work BY gas +ve)
