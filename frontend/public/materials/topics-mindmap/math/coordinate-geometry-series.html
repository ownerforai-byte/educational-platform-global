📋 Formula sheet list — Coordinate Geometry & Series (same as diagram, selectable)

  1. Straight Lines: y=mx+c ● Distance |ax₁+by₁+c|/√(a²+b²) ● m=tanθ = slope angle
  2. Circles: x²+y²+2gx+2fy+c=0 ● Tangent T=0 ● Centre-radius = (x−h)²+(y−k)²=r²
  3. Parabola: y²=4ax, focus (a,0) ● Latus rectum 4a ● y²=4ax focus = (a,0) + directrix
  4. Ellipse/Hyperbola: e<1 ellipse, e>1 hyperbola ● Asymptotes x²/a²−y²/b²=0 ● e<1 ellipse = two foci sum const
  5. AP/GP/HP: aₙ=a+(n−1)d ● S_∞=a/(1−r) ● an=a+(n−1)d = AP term
  6. Binomial & Induction: (1+x)ⁿ expansion ● nCr Pascal ● ⁿCr Pascal = triangle numbers

  5. Parametric at²,2at = para-metr-i-cata-t → beside + measure + “i” + “cata” + “t” (means: t traces all)

  7. e>1 hyperbola = e-hyper-bola → “e” + over + “bola” (means: difference const)
