✅ Clarity notes — Vectors & 3D Geometry

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Dot vs Cross vs scalar ab·cosθ — test: vector, area, ⊥ (Need number or direction?)
  • Collinear vs Coplanar vs b=λa — test: scalar triple=0 (One line or one plane?)
  • Line vs Plane form vs r=a+λb — test: r·n=d (λ or normal?)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ |a×b| = area of parallelogram (½ for triangle)
  • ⛔ Shortest distance skew lines: |(a2−a1)·(b1×b2)|/|b1×b2|
  • ⛔ Plane intercepts: x/a+y/b+z/c=1
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 DOT-COS: dot loves cos
  • 💡 CROSS-SIN: cross loves sin
  • 💡 TRIPLE-VOL: box = volume
