✅ Clarity notes — Limits, Continuity & Differentiability

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Continuity vs Differentiability vs no break (LHL=RHL=f) — test: no corner + continuous (Sharp corner? cont. but NOT diff.)
  • 0/0 vs ∞/∞ vs try factorise/cancel — test: try divide by highest power (Form? both → L'Hôpital allowed)
  • LHD vs RHD vs left slope — test: right slope (Equal? differentiable)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ lim sinx/x=1 needs x in RADIANS
  • ⛔ L'Hôpital only 0/0 or ∞/∞ — check first
  • ⛔ Differentiable ⇒ continuous; converse FALSE
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 SINX-1: sinx/x→1 (radians!)
  • 💡 DIFF-CONT: Diff needs Cont
  • 💡 HOPITAL-00: only 0/0 or ∞/∞
