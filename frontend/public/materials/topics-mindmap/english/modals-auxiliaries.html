Modals & Auxiliaries

1. Ability & Permission

  • can/could; be able to
  • may for permission

2. Obligation & Advice

  • must = speaker's order
  • have to = outside rule; should = advice

3. Possibility & Deduction

  • may/might/could = maybe
  • must = sure; can't = impossible

4. Requests & Offers

  • Could/would you…? polite
  • Shall I…? / I'll… offers

5. Semi-modals

  • need to, have to, used to
  • dare/need as modal in negatives

6. Past Habits

  • used to + V1 (stopped now)
  • would + V1 (repeated past)

✅ Clarity notes — Modals & Auxiliaries

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Must vs Have to vs speaker's order — test: outside rule (Whose rule?)
  • May vs Might vs permission / 50-50 — test: weaker chance (How sure?)
  • Used to vs Would vs state or habit (stopped) — test: repeated action only (State verb?)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ mustn't = forbidden; don't have to = free choice — opposites!
  • ⛔ could = past ability OR polite now — check time words
  • ⛔ Modals never take -s or to (cans ✗, to must ✗)
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 MUST-ME, HAVE-LAW
  • 💡 MIGHT-LIGHT: weaker chance
  • 💡 NO-S-TO: modals reject -s and to
