✅ Clarity notes — Writing Skills

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Report vs Notice vs detailed past event — test: short boxed alert (Length?)
  • Formal vs Informal letter vs subject + polite + full block — test: chatty, first name (To whom?)
  • Summary vs Essay vs shrink given text — test: grow own ideas (Given or own?)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Notice without date/signature loses marks
  • ⛔ Letter: subject line + clear demand sentence is mandatory
  • ⛔ Précis in own words — lifted lines = penalty
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 IBC: Intro-Body-Conclusion
  • 💡 NOTICE-5: what/when/where/contact/sign
  • 💡 TAGS-CHECK: tense-article-grammar-spelling
