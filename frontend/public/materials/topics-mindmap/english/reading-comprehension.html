Reading Comprehension

1. Skimming & Scanning

  • Skim headings + first lines for gist
  • Scan for names, dates, numbers

2. Main Idea & Inference

  • Main idea = what every para proves
  • Inference = stated + known, never wild

3. Vocabulary in Context

  • Guess from neighbours + tone
  • Use prefix/root + sentence role

4. Fact vs Opinion

  • Fact is checkable (dates, data)
  • Opinion: feels, best, should, must

5. Summary Writing

  • One-third length, own words
  • Keep order; drop examples and lists

6. QA Strategies

  • Read questions before passage
  • Answer in own words, same tense

✅ Clarity notes — Reading Comprehension

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Skimming vs Scanning vs fast gist of whole — test: find one specific fact (Need what — gist or fact?)
  • Fact vs Opinion vs checkable data — test: feels / best / should (Can it be proved?)
  • Summary vs Retelling vs one-third, own words — test: full-length retell (Shorter?)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Copying passage lines scores zero in summary — always own words
  • ⛔ Inference must still point to a line — no wild guesses
  • ⛔ Match the question's tense and person in your answer
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 SKIM-SCAN: Skim Sky (whole), Scan Sand (grains)
  • 💡 FBI: Fact-Before-Inference, always
  • 💡 Q-FIRST: read Questions before passage
