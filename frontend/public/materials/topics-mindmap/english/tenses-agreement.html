Tenses & Subject-Verb Agreement

1. Present Forms

  • s/es with he/she/it (goes)
  • do/does in questions and negatives

2. Past Forms

  • V2: regular +ed; go→went
  • did + V1 in ? and − (did went ✗)

3. Future Forms

  • will + V1 for promises
  • be going to = fixed plan

4. Perfect vs Continuous

  • have/has/had + V3 = done
  • be + V-ing = ongoing

5. Time Markers

  • since + point (2019, Monday)
  • for + period (3 years); already/yet + perfect

6. Agreement Rules

  • each/every/one + singular verb
  • neither/nor → verb matches nearer subject

✅ Clarity notes — Tenses & Subject-Verb Agreement

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Since vs For vs point: 2019, Monday — test: period: 3 years (Point or period?)
  • Perfect vs Continuous vs finished (have done) — test: ongoing (is doing) (Done or still on?)
  • Each vs They vs each/every = singular — test: they = plural (Which subject?)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ did + V2 (did went) is always wrong — did + V1
  • ⛔ for/since swap is the No.1 tense trap — point vs period
  • ⛔ Neither/nor: verb follows the NEARER subject only
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 SINCE-POINT, FOR-PERIOD
  • 💡 DID-DO: did forces V1
  • 💡 NEAR-RULE: nor looks near
