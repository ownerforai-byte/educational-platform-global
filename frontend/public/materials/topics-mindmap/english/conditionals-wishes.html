✅ Clarity notes — Conditionals & Wishes

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Second vs Third vs dream now (V2/would) — test: regret past (had V3) (Now or past?)
  • Unless vs If-not vs unless already negative — test: if not needs not (Double negative?)
  • Wish-past vs Wish-perfect vs present wish (V2) — test: past regret (had V3) (Which time?)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Unless + not = double negative, never (unless you don't ✗)
  • ⛔ Third conditional needs had + V3 in BOTH halves correctly placed
  • ⛔ wish + would is for complaints (I wish you would stop)
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 WERE-DREAM, HAD-REGRET
  • 💡 UNLESS-ALONE: no not after
  • 💡 F-V-W: First-Vill, Second-Vould, Third-Vould have

  • 🔤 unless — HERE: if-not (already −ve) | ELSEWHERE: unleash? (test: grammar or release?)
