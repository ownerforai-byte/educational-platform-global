Articles, Prepositions & Determiners

1. A/An/The Rules

  • a/an = first mention
  • the = known/second mention

2. Zero Article

  • Meals, games, languages: no article
  • Plural general truths: no the

3. Prepositions of Time

  • at + clock; on + day; in + longer
  • by = deadline; till = up to

4. Place & Direction

  • at = point; in = inside; on = surface
  • into/to/towards = movement

5. Fixed Phrases

  • good at, afraid of, married to
  • Learn as chunks, never translate

6. Common Error Pairs

  • discuss ∅ (no about)
  • enter ∅, reach ∅ (no to)

✅ Clarity notes — Articles, Prepositions & Determiners

Mix-ups fixed:
  • In vs On vs At vs at point, on day — test: in month/year (Time size?)
  • Since vs For vs point vs period — test: same as tenses unit (Point?)
  • Beside vs Besides vs next to — test: in addition (Extra?)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ discuss/enter/reach take NO preposition (discuss about ✗)
  • ⛔ married to, good at — fixed, memorise
  • ⛔ the with superlatives and rivers (the best, the Koshi)
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 AT-ON-IN: clock-day-long
  • 💡 DISCUSS-Ø: verbs that refuse about
  • 💡 THE-BEST: superlatives take the
