✅ Clarity notes — Reported Speech

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Said vs Told vs say stands alone — test: tell needs an object (Object?)
  • Backshift vs No-shift vs normal past → shift back — test: universal truth stays (Always true?)
  • Question vs Command vs asked + if/wh clause — test: told + to + V1 (Order or ask?)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ Universal truths never backshift (The earth moves)
  • ⛔ Reported questions lose ? and inversion (he asked where I was)
  • ⛔ said to + person = told + person
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 SAY-SOLO, TELL-TEAM (needs object)
  • 💡 TRUTH-STAYS: facts don't shift
  • 💡 IF-WH: questions take if/wh
