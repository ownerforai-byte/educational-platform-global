✅ Clarity notes — Vocabulary Building

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Their vs There vs belongs (their book) — test: place (there it is) (Belong or place?)
  • Your vs You're vs your pen (belongs) — test: you're = you are (Expand test?)
  • Affect vs Effect vs affect = verb (influences) — test: effect = noun (result) (Verb or noun?)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ its (belongs) vs it's (it is) — apostrophe = missing letter
  • ⛔ Collocations can't be translated — memorise chunks
  • ⛔ Phrasal verb meaning ≠ verb + particle meanings
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 THEY'RE-3: place/belong/they-are
  • 💡 AFFECT-ACT, EFFECT-END (noun)
  • 💡 CHUNK-IT: learn word friends
