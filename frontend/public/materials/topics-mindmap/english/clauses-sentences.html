Clauses & Sentence Types

1. Noun Clauses

  • Does noun's job (what…)
  • that/what/whether starters

2. Adjective Clauses

  • Describes noun (who/which/that)
  • who = people, which = things

3. Adverb Clauses

  • Tells when/why/how (because…)
  • because/since/if/when openers

4. Simple/Compound/Complex

  • Simple = 1 clause
  • Compound = FANBOYS; complex = sub-clause

5. Fragments & Run-ons

  • Fragment = unfinished piece
  • Run-on = 2 sentences glued; fix with . / ; / and

6. Punctuation

  • Comma after opener; no comma before that
  • Semicolon joins close equals

✅ Clarity notes — Clauses & Sentence Types

Mix-ups fixed:
  • Who vs Which vs That vs people — test: things (Person?)
  • Fragment vs Run-on vs too short (piece) — test: too long (glued) (Break or join?)
  • Compound vs Complex vs FANBOYS equals — test: sub-clause bossed (Equal or bossed?)
Exam traps:
  • ⛔ which with commas = extra info (removable); that = essential
  • ⛔ Run-on fix needs . or ; or FANBOYS — comma alone = splice error
  • ⛔ No comma before that-clauses
Memory hooks:
  • 💡 WHO-HUMAN, WHICH-THING
  • 💡 FRAG-PIECE, RUN-GLUE
  • 💡 FANBOYS: for and nor but or yet so
