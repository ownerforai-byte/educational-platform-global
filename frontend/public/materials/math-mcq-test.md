## 4. Boundary & Limit Cards
- **Cramer's rule boundary:** applies ONLY when det A ≠ 0 and the system is square (same equations as unknowns).
- **Euler / De Moivre boundary:** angles must be in radians when differentiating e^(iθ)-form; in degrees add π/180 factor.
- **Real-variable vs complex:** for z₁,₂ complex, “z₁>z₂” is meaningless; you may compare |z₁| and |z₂| only.
- **Mean Value Theorem boundary:** f must be continuous on [a,b] AND differentiable on (a,b); otherwise no guarantee of c.
- **Integration boundary:** ∫(1/x)dx = ln|x|+C, NOT ln x (the |.| matters on x<0).
