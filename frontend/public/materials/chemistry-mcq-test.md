## 4. Boundary & Limit Cards
- **Ideal gas boundary:** holds at **low P, high T**. Deviations increase at **high P, low T** (finite volume + intermolecular attraction).
- **Bohr model boundary:** works ONLY for hydrogen-like (one-electron) species; fails for multi-electron atoms, fine structure, Zeeman & Stark effects.
- **Rydberg boundary:** the series label depends on the LOWER level (n₁); emission lines only.
- **Kinetic theory boundary:** assumes point particles with elastic collisions. Real gases deviate: high P → volume term; low T → attraction term.
- **Triple point boundary:** each pure substance has ONE triple point; CO₂'s is above 1 atm so dry ice sublimes at room pressure.
