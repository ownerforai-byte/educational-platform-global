Dimensions of derived quantities

Q1.Write the dimensional formula of force. [1]

F = ma = [M][L T⁻²]. So [F] = M L T⁻².

[M L T⁻²]

Q2.Write the dimensional formula of work. [1]

W = Fs = [M L T⁻²][L]. So [W] = M L² T⁻².

[M L² T⁻²]

Q3.Write the dimensional formula of pressure. [1]

p = F/A = [M L T⁻²]/[L²]. So [p] = M L⁻¹ T⁻².

[M L⁻¹ T⁻²]

Q4.Write the dimensional formula of Planck's constant. [1]

E = hν ⇒ h = E/ν = [M L² T⁻²]/[T⁻¹]. So [h] = M L² T⁻¹.

[M L² T⁻¹]

Dimensional homogeneity

Q5.Check the equation v = u + at dimensionally. [2]

[v] = [u] = L T⁻¹. [at] = [L T⁻²][T] = L T⁻¹ — all three terms agree.

Dimensionally correct

Dimensional homogeneity

Q6.Check the equation s = ut + ½at² dimensionally. [2]

[ut] = [L T⁻¹][T] = L. [at²] = [L T⁻²][T²] = L — every term is a length.

Dimensionally correct

Dimensional homogeneity

Q7.Show that kinetic energy and gravitational potential energy have the same dimensions. [2]

½mv² = [M][L T⁻¹]² = M L² T⁻². mgh = [M][L T⁻²][L] = M L² T⁻² — the two agree.

Both are [M L² T⁻²]

Deriving formulae

Q8.Derive the form of the period T of a simple pendulum from dimensions, given T ∝ lᵃ gᵇ. [3]

[T] = [L]ᵃ[L T⁻²]ᵇ gives L: a + b = 0 and T: −2b = 1. So b = −½, a = ½ and T = k√(l/g).

T = k √(l/g)

Deriving formulae

Q9.The frequency n of a stretched string depends on its length l, tension F and mass per unit length m. Derive n = k lᵃ Fᵇ mᶜ. [3]

[T⁻¹] = [L]ᵃ[M L T⁻²]ᵇ[M L⁻¹]ᶜ. M: b + c = 0, L: a + b − c = 0, T: −2b = −1 ⇒ b = ½, c = −½, a = −1.

n = k (1/l) √(F/m)

Limitations of dimensional analysis

Q10.Why can dimensional analysis not give the factor ½ in s = ut + ½at²? [3]

The method fixes only the powers of the base quantities. Any dimensionless constant, including ½, is invisible to dimensions.

Dimensions fix powers, never pure numbers

Q11.Write the dimensional formula of the gravitational constant G. [2]

F = Gm₁m₂/r² ⇒ G = Fr²/(m₁m₂). = [M L T⁻²][L²]/[M²] = M⁻¹ L³ T⁻².

[M⁻¹ L³ T⁻²]

Limitations of dimensional analysis

Q12.In (P + a/V²)(V − b) = RT, find the dimensions of a and b. [3]

b is added to a volume, so [b] = L³. a/V² has the dimensions of pressure ⇒ [a] = [p][V²] = [M L⁻¹ T⁻²][L⁶].

[a] = M L⁵ T⁻², [b] = L³
