Linear momentum and impulse

Q1.Find the momentum of a 2 kg body moving at 3 m/s. [1]

p = mv = 2(3). = 6 kg m s⁻¹.

6 kg m/s

Linear momentum and impulse

Q2.Find the impulse of a 10 N force acting for 0.5 s. [1]

J = FΔt = 10(0.5). = 5 N s.

5 N s

Linear momentum and impulse

Q3.A 0.2 kg ball hits a wall at 10 m/s and rebounds at 10 m/s. Find the impulse. [2]

Taking the incoming direction as positive, Δp = m(v − u) = 0.2(−10 − 10). Magnitude = 4 N s, directed away from the wall.

4 N s away from the wall

Linear momentum and impulse

Q4.Find the force needed to stop a 1000 kg car from 20 m/s in 5 s. [2]

a = (0 − 20)/5 = −4 m/s². F = ma = 1000(−4) = −4000 N, i.e. 4000 N opposite to the motion.

4000 N

Newton's laws applied

Q5.State Newton's second law in terms of momentum. [1]

The rate of change of momentum of a body is proportional to the applied force and takes place in the direction of the force: F = dp/dt.

F = dp/dt

Newton's laws applied

Q6.A 5 kg body rests on a smooth horizontal surface. Find its acceleration under a 20 N horizontal force. [2]

a = F/m = 20/5. = 4 m/s².

4 m/s²

Newton's laws applied

Q7.A lift of mass 100 kg accelerates upward at 2 m/s². Find the tension in its cable (g = 10 m/s²). [3]

T − mg = ma ⇒ T = m(g + a) = 100(12). = 1200 N.

1200 N

Newton's laws applied

Q8.Find the tension when the same lift accelerates downward at 2 m/s². [2]

mg − T = ma ⇒ T = m(g − a) = 100(8). = 800 N.

800 N

Conservation of linear momentum

Q9.A 2 kg body moving at 5 m/s sticks to a 3 kg body at rest. Find their common velocity. [2]

Momentum before = 2(5) = 10 kg m/s, after = 5v. So v = 2 m/s.

2 m/s

Conservation of linear momentum

Q10.A 3 kg body at 4 m/s collides elastically with a 1 kg body at rest. Find both final velocities. [3]

v₁ = ((m₁ − m₂)/(m₁ + m₂))u = (2/4)(4) = 2 m/s. v₂ = (2m₁/(m₁ + m₂))u = (6/4)(4) = 6 m/s.

2 m/s and 6 m/s

Recoil and collisions

Q11.A 4 kg gun fires a 0.02 kg bullet at 400 m/s. Find the recoil speed of the gun. [2]

Momentum is conserved starting from rest: 0.02(400) = 4v. v = 8/4 = 2 m/s backward.

2 m/s

Recoil and collisions

Q12.A 0.05 kg bullet at 400 m/s embeds in a 1.95 kg block at rest. Find the common speed and the kinetic energy lost. [3]

Momentum: 0.05(400) = 2v ⇒ v = 10 m/s. KE before = ½(0.05)(160000) = 4000 J; after = ½(2)(100) = 100 J, so 3900 J is lost.

10 m/s, losing 3900 J
