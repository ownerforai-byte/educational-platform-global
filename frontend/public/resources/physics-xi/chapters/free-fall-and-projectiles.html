Freely falling bodies

Q1.A ball is dropped from rest. Find its speed after 3 s (g = 10 m/s²). [1]

v = u + gt = 0 + 10(3). = 30 m/s.

30 m/s

Freely falling bodies

Q2.Find the height it has fallen in those 3 s. [2]

h = ½gt² = ½(10)(9). = 45 m.

45 m

Freely falling bodies

Q3.A body is thrown vertically upward at 20 m/s. Find the time to reach the top. [2]

At the top v = 0: 0 = 20 − 10t. t = 2 s.

2 s

Freely falling bodies

Q4.Find the greatest height reached in that throw. [1]

H = u²/2g = 400/20. = 20 m.

20 m

Freely falling bodies

Q5.Find the total time of flight for the same upward throw. [2]

Ascent 2 s and descent 2 s, since the motion is symmetric about the top.

4 s

Time of flight

Q6.A projectile is fired at 20 m/s at 30° to the horizontal. Find its time of flight (g = 10 m/s²). [2]

T = 2u sin θ/g = 2(20)(0.5)/10. = 2 s.

2 s

Time of flight

Q7.Find the maximum height of that projectile. [1]

H = u²sin²θ/2g = 400(0.25)/20. = 5 m.

5 m

Range and maximum height

Q8.Find its horizontal range. [2]

R = u²sin 2θ/g = 400 sin 60°/10. = 400(0.866)/10 = 34.6 m.

34.6 m

Range and maximum height

Q9.At what angle should a projectile be fired at 20 m/s for the maximum range, and what is that range? [3]

Range is greatest at 45°: R = u²/g = 400/10. = 40 m.

45°, giving 40 m

Range and maximum height

Q10.For what angle of projection is the maximum height equal to the horizontal range? [3]

H = R ⇒ u²sin²θ/2g = u²(2 sin θ cos θ)/g. Cancelling gives tan θ = 4, so θ = 76°.

θ = tan⁻¹4 ≈ 76°

Relative velocity in two dimensions

Q11.A boat heads across a river at 4 m/s while the current flows at 3 m/s. Find its speed over the ground. [2]

The two velocities are perpendicular: v = √(16 + 9). = 5 m/s.

5 m/s

Relative velocity in two dimensions

Q12.An aircraft flies north at 120 km/h in a wind blowing east at 50 km/h. Find its speed relative to the ground. [2]

v = √(120² + 50²) = √(14400 + 2500) = √16900. = 130 km/h, directed east of north.

130 km/h
