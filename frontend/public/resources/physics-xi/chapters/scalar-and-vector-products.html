Dot product and its applications

Q1.Find A·B for A = 2i + 3j and B = i − j. [1]

A·B = (2)(1) + (3)(−1) + (0)(0). = −1.

A·B = −1

Cross product and its applications

Q2.Evaluate i × j. [1]

The cross product of the unit vectors follows the cyclic order i → j → k. So i × j = k.

k

Angle between two vectors

Q3.State the angle between i and k. [1]

The coordinate axes are mutually perpendicular. So the angle is 90° and i·k = 0.

90°

Q4.What does A·B = 0 mean for non-zero vectors? [1]

Q9.Find the area of the triangle whose two sides from a vertex are A = i + j and B = j + k. [3]
