Instantaneous velocity and acceleration

Q1.A car moves so that x = 3t² metres. Find its velocity at t = 2 s. [1]

v = dx/dt = 6t. At t = 2, v = 12 m/s.

12 m/s

Instantaneous velocity and acceleration

Q2.For x = 3t², find the acceleration. [1]

a = dv/dt = d(6t)/dt. = 6 m/s², a constant.

6 m/s²

Instantaneous velocity and acceleration

Q3.Distinguish average velocity from instantaneous velocity. [2]

Average velocity is the total displacement divided by the total time, Δx/Δt. Instantaneous velocity is its limit as Δt → 0, i.e. dx/dt.

Δx/Δt against dx/dt

Instantaneous velocity and acceleration

Q4.A body moves 10 m east and then 10 m west in 5 s. Find its average velocity. [2]

Displacement = 10 − 10 = 0 m, while the distance travelled is 20 m. Average velocity = 0/5.

0 m/s (but the average speed is 4 m/s)

Graphical treatment of motion

Q5.What do the slope and the area of a velocity–time graph give? [2]

Slope = dv/dt = acceleration, and area under the graph = ∫v dt = displacement.

Slope → acceleration, area → displacement

Graphical treatment of motion

Q6.A velocity–time graph rises from 0 to 20 m/s in 4 s, stays at 20 m/s for 6 s and falls to 0 in 2 s. Find the distance covered. [3]

Area = ½(4)(20) + 20(6) + ½(2)(20). = 40 + 120 + 20 = 180 m.

180 m

Graphical treatment of motion

Q7.What does the area under an acceleration–time graph represent? [2]

a = dv/dt, so ∫a dt = Δv. The area gives the change in velocity, not the distance.

The change in velocity

Equations of motion

Q8.A body starts from rest with a = 3 m/s². Find its speed after 4 s. [1]

v = u + at = 0 + 3(4). = 12 m/s.

12 m/s

Equations of motion

Q9.A body has u = 5 m/s and a = 2 m/s². Find the distance in 3 s. [2]

s = ut + ½at² = 5(3) + ½(2)(9). = 15 + 9 = 24 m.

24 m

Equations of motion

Q10.A car slows from 20 m/s to rest in 50 m. Find its deceleration. [2]

v² = u² + 2as ⇒ 0 = 400 + 100a. a = −4 m/s², so the retardation is 4 m/s².

4 m/s²

Relative velocity

Q11.Two cars travel in the same direction at 10 m/s and 15 m/s. Find their relative velocity. [2]

Relative velocity = 15 − 10. = 5 m/s.

5 m/s

Relative velocity

Q12.Rain falls vertically at 10 m/s while a man walks horizontally at 10 m/s. In what direction does the rain appear to him? [3]

The apparent velocity is the rain velocity minus his own: (0, −10) − (10, 0). tan θ = 10/10 = 1 ⇒ 45° to the vertical, speed √200 ≈ 14.1 m/s.

45° from the vertical, 14.1 m/s
