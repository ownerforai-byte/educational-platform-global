Rectangular components

Q8.Forces of 4 N east, 3 N north and 4 N west act on a particle. Find the resultant. [3]

The east and west forces cancel, leaving only the north component. So R = 3 N north.

3 N due north

Adding vectors by components

Q9.Find A + B for A = i + j and B = i − j, and state its direction. [3]

The j components cancel: A + B = 2i. A vector along +x has no angle with the x-axis.

2i, along the x-axis (0°)

Q11.Resolve a 50 N force acting at 37° to the horizontal, taking cos 37° = 0.8. [3]

Angle of the resultant

Q12.Can three vectors of magnitudes 3, 4 and 8 units give a zero resultant? [2]

A zero resultant needs the largest to be no greater than the sum of the others. Here 8 > 3 + 4, so no triangle can close.

No — 8 > 3 + 4
