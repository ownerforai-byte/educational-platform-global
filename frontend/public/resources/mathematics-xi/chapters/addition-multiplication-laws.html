Q22.Why does the addition law subtract P(A ∩ B)? [2]

To count the overlap only once

Addition law Match

Q23.Match mutually exclusive and independent events with (a) P(A ∩ B) = P(A)P(B), (b) P(A ∩ B) = 0. [2]

One forbids co-occurrence, the other merely makes it predictable.

Mutually exclusive → (b), independent → (a)
