Retardation Two stages long answer

L1.A car accelerates uniformly from 10 m/s to 30 m/s in 8 s, then brakes to rest in a further 40 m. (a) Find the acceleration in the first stage and the distance covered. (b) Find the retardation in the second stage. (c) Find the total time taken. [6]

  1. H1. Use v = u + at for the acceleration, then s = ut + ½at² for the distance.
  2. H2. For braking, v = 0 and v² = u² + 2as with u = 30, s = 40.
  3. H3. The braking time follows from v = u + at with the new acceleration.

Given: stage 1 u = 10, v = 30, t = 8; stage 2 u = 30, v = 0, s = 40 Required: a₁, s₁, a₂ (retardation), total time 1. a₁ = (30 − 10)/8 = 2.5 m/s² 2. s₁ = 10(8) + ½(2.5)(64) = 80 + 80 = 160 m 3. stage 2: 0 = 900 + 2a₂(40) ⇒ a₂ = −900/80 = −11.25 m/s² 4. retardation 11.25 m/s² 5. braking time: 0 = 30 − 11.25t ⇒ t = 30/11.25 = 2.67 s 6. total time = 8 + 2.67 ≈ 10.67 s over 200 m

(a) a₁ = 2.5 m/s², s₁ = 160 m (b) retardation 11.25 m/s² (c) total time ≈ 10.7 s.

Marking split — a₁ 1 + s₁ 1.5 + retardation 1.5 + braking time 1 + total 1 = 6
Common pitfall — Using the first stage's acceleration in the second stage, or treating braking distance as if the car still sped up.
