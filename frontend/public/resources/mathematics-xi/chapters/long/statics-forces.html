Resultant of two forces Resultant and equilibrant long answer

L1.Two forces of 8 N and 6 N act at right angles at a point. (a) Find the magnitude and direction of the resultant. (b) Find the force that keeps the system in equilibrium. (c) Resolve the 8 N force along and perpendicular to the 6 N force. [6]

  1. H1. For perpendicular forces R = √(P² + Q²).
  2. H2. The direction is tan α = Q/P measured from the larger force.
  3. H3. The equilibrant equals the resultant in magnitude but opposite in direction.

Given: P = 8 N, Q = 6 N, angle 90° Required: resultant, its direction, the equilibrant, and a resolution 1. R = √(64 + 36) = √100 = 10 N 2. tan α = 6/8 = 0.75 ⇒ α = 36.87° from the 8 N force 3. the resultant bisects nothing here — it is 36.87° from 8 N and 53.13° from 6 N 4. equilibrant: 10 N at 180° + 36.87° = 216.87° from the 8 N force 5. resolution along the 6 N force: 8cos90° = 0; perpendicular: 8sin90° = 8 N 6. so the 8 N force has no component along the 6 N force at all

(a) R = 10 N at 36.87° to the 8 N force (b) the equilibrant is 10 N opposite to the resultant (c) 0 along and 8 N perpendicular to the 6 N force.

Marking split — magnitude 1.5 + direction 1.5 + equilibrant 1.5 + resolution 1.5 = 6
Common pitfall — Giving the equilibrant the same direction as the resultant — equilibrium requires the opposite sense.
