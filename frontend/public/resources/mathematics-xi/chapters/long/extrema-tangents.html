Concavity and absolute extrema Maxima and minima long answer

L1.For f(x) = x³ − 6x² + 9x + 1 on the closed interval [0, 4], (a) find the stationary points and classify them, (b) find the absolute maximum and minimum on [0, 4]. [6]

  1. H1. f′(x) = 3x² − 12x + 9; set it to zero and factor.
  2. H2. Classify with the sign of f′ or the second derivative.
  3. H3. On a closed interval always compare the stationary values with the endpoint values.

Given: f(x) = x³ − 6x² + 9x + 1, x ∈ [0, 4] Required: stationary points, their type, and the absolute extremes 1. f′(x) = 3x² − 12x + 9 = 3(x − 1)(x − 3) ⇒ x = 1, 3 2. f″(x) = 6x − 12: f″(1) = −6 < 0 ⇒ local max; f″(3) = 6 > 0 ⇒ local min 3. f(1) = 1 − 6 + 9 + 1 = 5; f(3) = 27 − 54 + 27 + 1 = 1 4. endpoints: f(0) = 1, f(4) = 64 − 96 + 36 + 1 = 5 5. values: 1, 5, 1, 5 ⇒ max 5, min 1 6. both extremes are attained twice — at a stationary point and at an endpoint

local max 5 at x = 1, local min 1 at x = 3; absolute maximum 5 (at x = 1 and x = 4), absolute minimum 1 (at x = 0 and x = 3).

Marking split — derivative and roots 1.5 + classification 1.5 + values 1 + endpoints 1 + conclusion 1 = 6
Common pitfall — Ignoring the endpoints. On a closed interval the largest or smallest value can occur at an end point.
