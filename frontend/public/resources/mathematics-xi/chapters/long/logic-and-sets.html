Cardinality Venn diagram long answer

L1.In a survey of 120 students, 65 read Nepali, 55 read English and 30 read both. (a) Write the exclusive regions of the Venn diagram. (b) Find how many read at least one language and how many read neither. (c) Verify the result with the inclusion–exclusion formula. [6]

  1. H1. Start from the overlap: 30 read both, so subtract 30 from each single count.
  2. H2. "At least one" is the union — the sum of the three regions inside the circles.
  3. H3. "Neither" is the whole class minus the union.

Given: n(U) = 120, n(N) = 65, n(E) = 55, n(N ∩ E) = 30 Required: exclusive regions, n(N ∪ E), n((N ∪ E)′) 1. only Nepali = 65 − 30 = 35 2. only English = 55 − 30 = 25 3. both = 30 4. n(N ∪ E) = 35 + 30 + 25 = 90 5. n((N ∪ E)′) = 120 − 90 = 30 6. check: n(N) + n(E) − n(N ∩ E) = 65 + 55 − 30 = 90 ✓

90 students read at least one language; 30 read neither.

Marking split — regions 2 + 3 (b) + 1 + 1 (formula check) = 6
Common pitfall — Counting the 30 in both circles twice — the union is 65 + 55 − 30, never 65 + 55.
