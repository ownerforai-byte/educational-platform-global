L1.The points A(1, −2, 3) and B(3, 4, −5) are given. (a) Find the point that divides AB externally in the ratio 2 : 3. (b) Find the midpoint of AB and check your external point against it. [6]

  1. H1. External division uses (mx₂ − nx₁)/(m − n) with m = 2, n = 3.
  2. H2. Apply the same formula to each coordinate separately.
  3. H3. The midpoint uses the ordinary averages of the coordinates.

Given: A(1, −2, 3), B(3, 4, −5), ratio 2 : 3 external Required: the external point and the midpoint 1. x = (2·3 − 3·1)/(2 − 3) = (6 − 3)/(−1) = −3 2. y = (2·4 − 3(−2))/(−1) = (8 + 6)/(−1) = −14 3. z = (2(−5) − 3·3)/(−1) = (−10 − 9)/(−1) = 19 4. external point P(−3, −14, 19) 5. midpoint M = ((1 + 3)/2, (−2 + 4)/2, (3 − 5)/2) = (2, 1, −1) 6. P, M, B all lie on AB with M between A and B; the external point lies beyond A

P(−3, −14, 19) divides AB externally in 2 : 3; the midpoint of AB is M(2, 1, −1).

Marking split — each coordinate 1.5 + point 1 + midpoint 1.5 = 6
Common pitfall — Adding instead of subtracting in external division — external means the difference of the products.
