Classical probability Dice long answer

L1.Two fair dice are thrown together. Find (a) P(sum = 9), (b) P(both numbers even), (c) P(sum ≥ 10 | sum is even). [6]

  1. H1. The sample space has 36 equally likely ordered pairs.
  2. H2. List the four pairs giving 9: (3,6), (4,5), (5,4), (6,3).
  3. H3. For the conditional part, restrict the sample space to even sums first.

Given: two fair dice, S with n(S) = 36 Required: two simple probabilities and one conditional probability 1. sum 9: (3,6), (4,5), (5,4), (6,3) ⇒ 4 outcomes ⇒ P = 4/36 = 1/9 2. both even: 3 × 3 = 9 outcomes ⇒ P = 9/36 = 1/4 3. even sums: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 with counts 1, 3, 5, 5, 3, 1 ⇒ 18 outcomes 4. of these, sum ≥ 10 means 10 or 12 ⇒ 3 + 1 = 4 outcomes 5. P = 4/18 = 2/9 6. conditional probability always divides by the reduced sample space, never by 36

(a) 1/9 (b) 1/4 (c) 2/9.

Marking split — each of (a) and (b) 1.5 + reduced space 1.5 + (c) 1.5 = 6
Common pitfall — Dividing the conditional count by 36. The condition shrinks the sample space to the 18 even-sum pairs.
