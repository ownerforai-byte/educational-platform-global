Range and mean deviation Dispersion long answer

L1.For the data 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 find (a) the mean and standard deviation, (b) the coefficient of variation, (c) state, with a reason, whether the distribution is symmetric. [6]

  1. H1. The data are equally spaced, so the mean is the middle value.
  2. H2. Use σ² = Σ(x − x̄)²/n with the deviations −6, −4, …, 6.
  3. H3. CV = (σ/x̄) × 100; symmetry follows from mean = median and balanced deviations.

Given: 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 (n = 7) Required: mean, SD, CV and the shape of the distribution 1. x̄ = (4 + 6 + 8 + 10 + 12 + 14 + 16)/7 = 70/7 = 10 2. deviations: −6, −4, −2, 0, 2, 4, 6; squares: 36, 16, 4, 0, 4, 16, 36 3. Σ(x − x̄)² = 112 ⇒ σ² = 112/7 = 16 ⇒ σ = 4 4. CV = (4/10) × 100 = 40% 5. median = 10 = mean, and the deviations cancel in pairs 6. so the distribution is symmetric — zero skewness

x̄ = 10, σ = 4, CV = 40%; the distribution is symmetric because mean = median and the deviations pair off.

Marking split — mean 1 + variance 2.5 + SD 1 + CV 1 + symmetry 0.5 = 6
Common pitfall — Dividing by n − 1 in an NEB item that asks for the population standard deviation given the whole data set.
