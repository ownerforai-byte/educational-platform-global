L1.For a = 2i − j + 3k and b = i + 2j − k, find (a) a·b and a×b, (b) the angle between a and b, (c) the area of the parallelogram with adjacent sides a and b. [6]

  1. H1. Dot product: multiply matching components and add.
  2. H2. Cross product: expand the 3×3 determinant with i, j, k in the first row.
  3. H3. cosθ = (a·b)/(|a||b|); the parallelogram area is |a×b|.

Given: a = (2, −1, 3), b = (1, 2, −1) Required: dot and cross products, the angle, the parallelogram area 1. a·b = 2(1) + (−1)(2) + 3(−1) = 2 − 2 − 3 = −3 2. a×b = i[(−1)(−1) − 3(2)] − j[2(−1) − 3(1)] + k[2(2) − (−1)(1)] 3. a×b = i(1 − 6) − j(−2 − 3) + k(4 + 1) = −5i + 5j + 5k 4. |a| = √14, |b| = √6, |a×b| = 5√3 5. cosθ = −3/√84 = −3/(2√21) ⇒ θ ≈ 109.1° 6. area = |a×b| = 5√3 ≈ 8.66 square units

a·b = −3, a×b = −5i + 5j + 5k; θ ≈ 109.1°; parallelogram area 5√3 ≈ 8.66.

Marking split — dot 1 + cross 2 + angle 2 + area 1 = 6
Common pitfall — Losing the sign of the j-component: the determinant gives −j(...), so the middle term flips.
