Roots and coefficients Roots and coefficients long answer

L1.If α and β are the roots of 3x² − 6x + 4 = 0, then (a) form the equation whose roots are 1/α and 1/β, (b) find the value of α² + β², (c) state the nature of the roots. [6]

  1. H1. Read off α + β = 2 and αβ = 4/3 from the coefficients.
  2. H2. For the new roots, build the new sum and product from the old ones, then clear fractions.
  3. H3. α² + β² = (α + β)² − 2αβ; nature comes from the discriminant.

Given: 3x² − 6x + 4 = 0 with roots α, β Required: equation with roots 1/α, 1/β; α² + β²; nature of the roots 1. α + β = 6/3 = 2, αβ = 4/3 2. new sum = 1/α + 1/β = (α + β)/αβ = 2/(4/3) = 3/2 3. new product = 1/(αβ) = 3/4 4. equation: x² − (3/2)x + 3/4 = 0 ⇒ 4x² − 6x + 3 = 0 5. α² + β² = (α + β)² − 2αβ = 4 − 8/3 = 4/3 6. Δ = 36 − 4(3)(4) = −12 < 0 ⇒ two complex conjugate roots

(a) 4x² − 6x + 3 = 0 (b) α² + β² = 4/3 (c) Δ = −12 < 0, so the roots are complex conjugates.

Marking split — new equation 2 + α² + β² 2 + nature 2 = 6
Common pitfall — Applying the quadratic formula to the new roots instead of using the symmetric sums — slower and error-prone.
