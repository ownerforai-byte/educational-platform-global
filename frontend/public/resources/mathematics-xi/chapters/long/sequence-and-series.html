Geometric progression Geometric progression long answer

L1.Three consecutive terms of a geometric progression have sum 26 and product 216. (a) Find the three terms, taking the common ratio to be less than 1. (b) Find the sum to infinity of the progression. [6]

  1. H1. Symmetry pays: write the terms as a/r, a, ar so the product is simply a³.
  2. H2. a³ = 216 gives a = 6; the sum then gives a quadratic in r.
  3. H3. Divide by the larger root and use S∞ = a/(1 − r) for |r| < 1.

Given: a/r + a + ar = 26, (a/r)(a)(ar) = 216 Required: the three terms with r < 1, then S∞ 1. product: a³ = 216 ⇒ a = 6 2. sum: 6(1/r + 1 + r) = 26 ⇒ 6r² − 20r + 6 = 0 ⇒ 3r² − 10r + 3 = 0 3. (3r − 1)(r − 3) = 0 ⇒ r = 1/3 or r = 3 4. r < 1 ⇒ r = 1/3 ⇒ terms 18, 6, 2 (sum 26 ✓, product 216 ✓) 5. first term a₁ = 18, so S∞ = a₁/(1 − r) = 18/(2/3) = 27

the terms are 18, 6, 2 and the sum to infinity is 27.

Marking split — a from the product 1.5 + quadratic 2 + correct root and terms 1.5 + S∞ 1 = 6
Common pitfall — Using a, ar, ar² instead of a/r, a, ar — the product then needs an extra division and the algebra doubles.
