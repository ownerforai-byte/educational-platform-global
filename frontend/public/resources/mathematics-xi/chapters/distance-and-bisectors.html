Distance between parallel lines Spot the error

Q20.A student finds the distance between 3x + 4y = 2 and 6x + 8y = 9 as |9 − 2|/5. Correct it. [2]

The coefficients must be made equal first: divide the second by 2 to get 3x + 4y = 4.5

Distance = |4.5 − 2|/5 = 0.5

The perpendicularity is a general property, not special to this pair.

Both true, and the reason explains the assertion

Q22.Why is the perpendicular distance used rather than a horizontal one? [2]

The perpendicular is the shortest route to the line.

Because the shortest distance from a point to a line is measured along the perpendicular
