Inverse identities

Q7.Prove sin⁻¹x + cos⁻¹x = π/2 for −1 ≤ x ≤ 1. [4]

Let sin⁻¹x = θ; then cos(π/2 − θ) = sin θ = x

Hence cos⁻¹x = π/2 − θ ⇒ sum = π/2 ✔

Inverse identities

Q8.Prove tan⁻¹x + tan⁻¹(1/x) = π/2 for x > 0. [4]

Both angles are acute and their tangents are reciprocal

tan A·tan B = 1 with A + B = π/2 ✔
