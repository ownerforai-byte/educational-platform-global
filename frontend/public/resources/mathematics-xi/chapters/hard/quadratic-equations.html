Roots and coefficients Symmetric functions hard

H1.If α and β are the roots of 2x² − 5x + 3 = 0, find α² + β². [4]

  1. H1. Read off α + β = −b/a and αβ = c/a.
  2. H2. Write α² + β² as (α + β)² − 2αβ.
  3. H3. Substitute the two known symmetric sums.

α + β = 5/2, αβ = 3/2 α² + β² = (5/2)² − 2(3/2) = 25/4 − 3

α² + β² = 13/4

Forming equations Forming equations hard

H2.Form the quadratic whose roots are α + 1 and β + 1, where α, β are the roots of 2x² − 5x + 3 = 0. [5]

  1. H1. New sum = (α + β) + 2.
  2. H2. New product = αβ + (α + β) + 1.
  3. H3. Use x² − (sum)x + (product) = 0 and clear fractions.

sum = 5/2 + 2 = 9/2 product = 3/2 + 5/2 + 1 = 5 x² − (9/2)x + 5 = 0

2x² − 9x + 10 = 0

Nature of roots Nature of roots hard

H3.Find k so that 2x² + kx + 2 = 0 has equal roots. [3]

  1. H1. Equal roots means the discriminant is zero.
  2. H2. Δ = b² − 4ac = k² − 16.
  3. H3. Solve k² = 16, keeping both signs.

k² − 4(2)(2) = 0 k² = 16

k = ±4

Nature of roots Complex roots hard

H4.Solve x² − 4x + 13 = 0 over the complex numbers. [4]

  1. H1. Compute the discriminant: 16 − 52.
  2. H2. A negative discriminant gives an imaginary square root.
  3. H3. √(−36) = 6i, then apply the quadratic formula.

Δ = 16 − 52 = −36 = (6i)² x = (4 ± 6i)/2

x = 2 ± 3i

Roots and coefficients Root relations hard

H5.One root of x² + px + 8 = 0 is double the other. Find p. [4]

  1. H1. Let the roots be α and 2α.
  2. H2. Product: 2α² = 8 gives α² = 4.
  3. H3. Sum: 3α = −p fixes p once α is known.

2α² = 8 ⇒ α = ±2 3α = −p ⇒ p = −3α taking α = 2: roots 2 and 4, p = −6

p = −6 (roots 2 and 4)

Applications Extremum hard

H6.Find the maximum value of 3 − 4x − 2x². [4]

  1. H1. Factor −2 out of the quadratic part.
  2. H2. Complete the square inside the bracket.
  3. H3. A negative square term is largest when it is zero.

3 − 4x − 2x² = 3 − 2(x² + 2x) = 3 − 2[(x + 1)² − 1] = 5 − 2(x + 1)²

maximum value 5, attained at x = −1
