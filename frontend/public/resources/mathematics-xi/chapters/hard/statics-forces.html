Resultant of two forces Resultant hard

H1.Two forces of 6 N and 8 N act at 60°. Find the magnitude of the resultant. [4]

  1. H1. Use R = √(P² + Q² + 2PQcosθ).
  2. H2. P² + Q² = 36 + 64 = 100 and 2PQcos60° = 2·48·0.5.
  3. H3. Add under the root.

R = √(100 + 48) = √148

R = 2√37 ≈ 12.17 N

Applied problems Equilibrium hard

H2.A 100 N weight hangs from two strings making 30° and 60° with the vertical. Find the tensions. [5]

  1. H1. The strings are perpendicular, so Lami's theorem applies with the angles between them.
  2. H2. Opposite the first tension the angle is 120°; opposite the weight it is 90°.
  3. H3. T₁/sin120° = T₂/sin150° = 100/sin90°.

T₁ = 100 sin120° = 100(√3/2), T₂ = 100 sin150° = 100(1/2) check: T₁cos30° + T₂cos60° = 75 + 25 = 100

T₁ = 50√3 ≈ 86.6 N, T₂ = 50 N

Resultant of two forces Resultant hard

H3.Find the resultant of two equal 10 N forces acting at 120°. [4]

  1. H1. Use the cosine rule form with P = Q = 10.
  2. H2. 2PQcos120° = 200(−1/2) = −100.
  3. H3. R = √(100 + 100 − 100).

R = √100 = 10

R = 10 N, bisecting the 120° angle

Equilibrium Equilibrium hard

H4.Forces of 3 N, 4 N and 5 N keep a particle in equilibrium. Find the angle between the 3 N and 4 N forces. [5]

  1. H1. The resultant of the 3 N and 4 N forces must be 5 N, opposite the third force.
  2. H2. Use R² = P² + Q² + 2PQcosθ with R = 5.
  3. H3. 25 = 25 + 24cosθ.

25 = 9 + 16 + 2(3)(4)cosθ ⇒ cosθ = 0

θ = 90°

Resolution of a force Resolution hard

H5.Resolve a 20 N force acting at 30° above the horizontal into horizontal and vertical components. [3]

  1. H1. The horizontal component uses cosine.
  2. H2. The vertical component uses sine.
  3. H3. cos30° = √3/2 and sin30° = 1/2.

Fx = 20cos30° = 10√3, Fy = 20sin30° = 10

horizontal 10√3 ≈ 17.32 N, vertical 10 N

Applied problems Applied hard

H6.A 50 N block rests on a smooth plane inclined at 30°. Find the component of its weight along the plane and normal to it. [4]

  1. H1. The weight makes 30° with the normal to the plane.
  2. H2. Along the plane: W sinθ.
  3. H3. Normal component: W cosθ.

along = 50 sin30° = 25, normal = 50 cos30° = 25√3

25 N down the plane, 25√3 ≈ 43.3 N perpendicular to it
