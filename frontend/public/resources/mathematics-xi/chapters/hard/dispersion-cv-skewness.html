Variance and standard deviation Variance hard

H1.Find the variance and standard deviation of 2, 4, 6, 8, 10. [5]

  1. H1. The mean is 30/5 = 6.
  2. H2. Square the deviations from the mean: 16, 4, 0, 4, 16.
  3. H3. Variance is the mean of those squares; take the square root for the SD.

Σ(x − x̄)² = 16 + 4 + 0 + 4 + 16 = 40 σ² = 40/5 = 8, σ = √8

variance = 8, SD = 2√2 ≈ 2.83

Coefficient of variation Coefficient of variation hard

H2.Series A has mean 60 and SD 6; series B has mean 75 and SD 9. Which is more consistent? [4]

  1. H1. Consistency is measured by relative spread, not absolute spread.
  2. H2. Compute CV = 100σ/x̄ for each series.
  3. H3. A = 10%, B = 12%; smaller CV means more consistent.

CV(A) = 100(6)/60 = 10% CV(B) = 100(9)/75 = 12%

series A is more consistent

Coefficient of variation Coefficient of variation hard

H3.A series has mean 40 and coefficient of variation 15%. Find its standard deviation. [3]

  1. H1. CV = 100σ/x̄.
  2. H2. So 15 = 100σ/40.
  3. H3. Solve for σ.

15 = 100σ/40 ⇒ σ = 15·40/100

σ = 6

Skewness Skewness hard

H4.For a distribution the mean is 45, the mode 40 and the SD 5. Find Karl Pearson's coefficient of skewness. [3]

  1. H1. Use (mean − mode)/SD.
  2. H2. The numerator is 45 − 40 = 5.
  3. H3. Divide by the SD.

Sk = (45 − 40)/5 = 5/5

coefficient of skewness = 1 (positively skewed)

Variance and standard deviation Change of scale hard

H5.Every observation of a series is multiplied by 3. How do the variance and SD change? [4]

  1. H1. Multiplying data by a constant multiplies deviations by that constant.
  2. H2. Variance involves squared deviations, so it picks up the square.
  3. H3. Hence SD is multiplied by 3 and variance by 9.

y = 3x ⇒ y − ȳ = 3(x − x̄) σy² = 9σx², σy = 3σx

SD becomes 3 times larger, variance 9 times larger

Range and mean deviation Mean deviation hard

H6.Find the mean deviation about the mean for 3, 5, 7, 9, 11. [4]

  1. H1. Mean = 35/5 = 7.
  2. H2. Take absolute deviations: 4, 2, 0, 2, 4.
  3. H3. MD = (sum of absolute deviations)/n.

Σ|x − x̄| = 4 + 2 + 0 + 2 + 4 = 12 MD = 12/5

mean deviation = 2.4
