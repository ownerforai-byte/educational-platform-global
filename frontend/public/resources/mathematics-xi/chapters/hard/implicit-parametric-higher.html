Implicit differentiation Implicit hard

H1.Find dy/dx if x² + y² + xy = 3. [4]

  1. H1. Differentiate every term with respect to x, remembering y is a function of x.
  2. H2. The xy term needs the product rule: y + x y′.
  3. H3. Collect all y′ terms on one side.

2x + 2y y′ + y + x y′ = 0 y′(2y + x) = −(2x + y)

dy/dx = −(2x + y)/(2y + x)

Parametric differentiation Parametric hard

H2.For x = a(θ − sin θ), y = a(1 − cos θ) find dy/dx. [5]

  1. H1. Compute dx/dθ and dy/dθ first.
  2. H2. Then dy/dx = (dy/dθ)/(dx/dθ) = sinθ/(1 − cosθ).
  3. H3. Use the half-angle identities to simplify.

dx/dθ = a(1 − cosθ), dy/dθ = a sinθ sinθ/(1 − cosθ) = 2sin(θ/2)cos(θ/2) / 2sin²(θ/2)

dy/dx = cot(θ/2)

Second derivatives Second derivative hard

H3.If y = e^{2x} sin 3x, find y″. [5]

  1. H1. First derivative by the product rule, with e^{2x} factored out.
  2. H2. Differentiate again, applying the product rule to each of the two terms.
  3. H3. Collect the sin 3x and cos 3x coefficients.

y′ = e^{2x}(2sin3x + 3cos3x) y″ = 2e^{2x}(2sin3x + 3cos3x) + e^{2x}(6cos3x − 9sin3x) = e^{2x}(12cos3x − 5sin3x)

y″ = e^{2x}(12 cos 3x − 5 sin 3x)

Parametric differentiation Parametric hard

H4.For x = t + 1/t and y = t − 1/t find dy/dx. [4]

  1. H1. Differentiate both with respect to t.
  2. H2. dx/dt = 1 − 1/t² and dy/dt = 1 + 1/t².
  3. H3. Divide and multiply through by t².

(1 + 1/t²)/(1 − 1/t²) = (t² + 1)/(t² − 1)

dy/dx = (t² + 1)/(t² − 1)

Second derivatives Second derivative hard

H5.For x² + y² = 25 find d²y/dx² at the point (3, 4). [5]

  1. H1. From the first derivative, dy/dx = −x/y.
  2. H2. Differentiate again using the quotient rule: d²y/dx² = −(y − x y′)/y².
  3. H3. Substitute x = 3, y = 4 and y′ = −3/4.

y′ = −3/4 y″ = −(4 − 3(−3/4))/16 = −(4 + 9/4)/16

d²y/dx² = −25/64

Motion and rates Motion hard

H6.A particle moves with s = 2t³ − 9t² + 12t. Find the times when it is at rest and its acceleration at those times. [5]

  1. H1. At rest means v = ds/dt = 0.
  2. H2. v = 6t² − 18t + 12 = 6(t − 1)(t − 2).
  3. H3. Acceleration is a = dv/dt = 12t − 18; evaluate at each root.

v = 6(t − 1)(t − 2) = 0 ⇒ t = 1, 2 a = 12t − 18 ⇒ a(1) = −6, a(2) = 6

at rest at t = 1 s and t = 2 s; a = −6 and a = 6 units/s²
