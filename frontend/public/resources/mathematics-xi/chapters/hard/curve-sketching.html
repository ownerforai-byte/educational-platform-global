Standard curves Parabola hard

H1.Sketch y = x² − 4x + 3, stating the vertex, the intercepts and the direction of opening. [4]

  1. H1. Complete the square for the x-terms.
  2. H2. Factor to find the x-intercepts.
  3. H3. A positive coefficient of x² opens the parabola upward.

y = (x − 2)² − 1 ⇒ vertex (2, −1) y = (x − 1)(x − 3) ⇒ x = 1, 3 y(0) = 3

upward parabola, vertex (2, −1), x-intercepts 1 and 3, y-intercept 3

Standard curves Cubic sketch hard

H2.Sketch y = (x − 1)(x + 2)(x − 3) using a sign chart. [4]

  1. H1. The roots −2, 1, 3 cut the axis into four intervals.
  2. H2. Test one convenient value in each interval to fix the sign.
  3. H3. A positive cubic falls on the far left and rises on the far right.

x < −2: − −2 < x < 1: + 1 < x < 3: − x > 3: + y(0) = (−1)(2)(−3) = 6

cubic crossing at −2 (rising through), 1 (falling through), 3 (rising through)

Standard curves Asymptotes hard

H3.Find the asymptotes of y = (2x + 1)/(x − 2). [3]

  1. H1. A vertical asymptote appears where the denominator vanishes and the numerator does not.
  2. H2. Divide numerator and denominator by x to study x → ±∞.
  3. H3. The horizontal asymptote is the ratio of the leading coefficients.

x − 2 = 0 ⇒ x = 2 vertical y = (2 + 1/x)/(1 − 2/x) → 2 as x → ±∞

x = 2 (vertical) and y = 2 (horizontal)

Transformations Transformations hard

H4.Sketch y = |x − 1| + 2 and state its minimum value. [3]

  1. H1. Start from the V-shaped graph of y = |x|.
  2. H2. Shift it 1 unit right and 2 units up.
  3. H3. The lowest point of a V is its corner.

corner when x − 1 = 0 ⇒ x = 1, y = 2 slope −1 for x < 1, +1 for x > 1

V-shape with minimum value 2 at x = 1

Standard curves Circle hard

H5.For x² + y² − 6x + 4y − 12 = 0 find the centre and the radius. [4]

  1. H1. Group the x-terms and the y-terms together.
  2. H2. Complete the square in each group (add 9 and 4 to both sides).
  3. H3. Compare with (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r².

(x² − 6x + 9) + (y² + 4y + 4) = 12 + 9 + 4 (x − 3)² + (y + 2)² = 25

centre (3, −2), radius 5

Standard curves Parabola hard

H6.For the parabola y² = 8x state the vertex, focus and directrix. [3]

  1. H1. Compare with the standard form y² = 4ax.
  2. H2. 4a = 8 gives a; a is the focal distance from the vertex.
  3. H3. The focus is (a, 0) and the directrix is x = −a.

4a = 8 ⇒ a = 2 focus (2, 0), directrix x = −2

vertex (0, 0), focus (2, 0), directrix x = −2
