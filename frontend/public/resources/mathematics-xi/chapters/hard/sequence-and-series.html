Sums to n and to infinity Series summation hard

H1.Find the sum of n terms of the series 3 + 33 + 333 + … [5]

  1. H1. Write each term as 3(1 + 10 + … + 10^{k−1}) = (10^k − 1)/3.
  2. H2. Split into two sums: a geometric sum of 10^k and n copies of 1.
  3. H3. Add the geometric progression 10 + 10² + … + 10ⁿ.

S = Σ(10^k − 1)/3 = (1/3)[10(10ⁿ − 1)/9 − n] = (10^{n+1} − 10 − 9n)/27

Sₙ = (10^{n+1} − 9n − 10)/27

Geometric progression Geometric progression hard

H2.In a GP the third term is 4 and the fifth term is 16. Find a and r and the sum of eight terms. [5]

  1. H1. Write the terms as ar² = 4 and ar⁴ = 16.
  2. H2. Divide the second equation by the first to isolate r².
  3. H3. With r known, recover a, then use Sₙ = a(rⁿ − 1)/(r − 1).

ar² = 4, ar⁴ = 16 ⇒ r² = 4 ⇒ r = 2 a·4 = 4 ⇒ a = 1 S₈ = (2⁸ − 1)/(2 − 1) = 255

a = 1, r = 2, S₈ = 255

Sums to n and to infinity Telescoping hard

H3.Sum the series 1/(1·2) + 1/(2·3) + … + 1/(n(n+1)). [4]

  1. H1. Split each term by partial fractions.
  2. H2. 1/(k(k+1)) = 1/k − 1/(k+1).
  3. H3. Write the sum out and watch the adjacent terms cancel.

S = (1 − 1/2) + (1/2 − 1/3) + … + (1/n − 1/(n+1)) = 1 − 1/(n+1)

Sₙ = n/(n+1)

Means Means hard

H4.For the numbers 4 and 9, write the AM, GM and HM and verify GM² = AM · HM. [4]

  1. H1. AM = (a + b)/2, GM = √(ab), HM = 2ab/(a + b).
  2. H2. Compute each value for a = 4, b = 9.
  3. H3. Multiply AM by HM and compare with GM squared.

AM = 13/2 = 6.5 GM = √36 = 6 HM = 72/13 ≈ 5.538 AM·HM = 6.5 × 72/13 = 36 = GM²

AM = 6.5, GM = 6, HM = 72/13, and G² = A·H holds

Arithmetic progression Arithmetic progression hard

H5.How many terms of the series 1 + 3 + 5 + … are needed to reach the sum 400? [4]

  1. H1. The nth term is 2n − 1, so this is an AP with a = 1, d = 2.
  2. H2. The sum of the first n odd numbers is n².
  3. H3. Solve n² = 400 and keep the positive integer root.

Sₙ = n² = 400 ⇒ n = 20 last term = 2(20) − 1 = 39

20 terms (1 + 3 + … + 39 = 400)

Sums to n and to infinity Infinite GP hard

H6.Express the recurring decimal 0.444… as a fraction using a geometric series. [4]

  1. H1. Write 0.444… = 4/10 + 4/100 + 4/1000 + ….
  2. H2. This is an infinite GP with a = 0.4 and r = 0.1.
  3. H3. Use S∞ = a/(1 − r).

S∞ = 0.4/(1 − 0.1) = 0.4/0.9

0.4̄ = 4/9
