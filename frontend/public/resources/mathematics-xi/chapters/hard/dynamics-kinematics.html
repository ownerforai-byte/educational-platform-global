The three equations Three equations hard

H1.A body starts with u = 5 m/s and moves with a = 3 m/s² for 4 s. Find its final velocity and distance. [4]

  1. H1. v = u + at.
  2. H2. s = ut + ½at².
  3. H3. 20 + ½(3)(16).

v = 5 + 12, s = 20 + 24

v = 17 m/s, s = 44 m

Retardation Retardation hard

H2.A car at 20 m/s brakes to rest in 50 m. Find its retardation. [4]

  1. H1. Use v² = u² + 2as with v = 0.
  2. H2. 0 = 400 + 100a.
  3. H3. Solve for a; the negative sign is the retardation.

a = −400/100

a = −4 m/s², i.e. a retardation of 4 m/s²

Free fall Free fall hard

H3.A ball is thrown vertically upward at 20 m/s (g = 10). Find the greatest height and the total time of flight. [4]

  1. H1. At the top v = 0, so 0 = u² − 2gh.
  2. H2. h = u²/2g.
  3. H3. The time up equals the time down, each u/g.

h = 400/20 = 20 m t_up = 20/10 = 2 s

greatest height 20 m, total time 4 s

Free fall Free fall hard

H4.A stone falls freely from rest through 45 m. Find the time taken and the final velocity (g = 10). [4]

  1. H1. s = ½gt² with u = 0.
  2. H2. 45 = 5t².
  3. H3. Then v = gt.

t = √9 = 3 s, v = 10(3)

t = 3 s, v = 30 m/s

Multi-stage motion Multi-stage hard

H5.A cyclist accelerates from rest at 2 m/s² for 5 s, then travels at constant speed for 3 s. Find the total distance. [5]

  1. H1. Stage 1: s₁ = ½at², v = at.
  2. H2. Stage 2: constant speed, so s₂ = v × 3.
  3. H3. Add the two distances.

s₁ = ½(2)(25) = 25 m, v = 10 m/s s₂ = 10(3) = 30 m

total distance 55 m

H6.A body falls from rest through 80 m. Find the time of fall and the distance covered in the fourth second. [5]

  1. H1. 80 = 5t² gives the total time.
  2. H2. Distance in the first 4 s is 80 m; in the first 3 s it is 45 m.
  3. H3. Subtract to get the distance during the fourth second.

t = √16 = 4 s s₄ − s₃ = 80 − 45

time 4 s, distance in the fourth second = 35 m (v = 40 m/s at impact)
