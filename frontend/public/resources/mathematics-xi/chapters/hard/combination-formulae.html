Product-to-sum Product-to-sum hard

H1.Prove that sin(A + B)sin(A − B) = sin²A − sin²B. [4]

  1. H1. Convert the product into a difference of cosines.
  2. H2. sin(A+B)sin(A−B) = ½[cos 2B − cos 2A].
  3. H3. Replace each cosine with 1 − 2sin² of the half angle.

= ½[(1 − 2sin²B) − (1 − 2sin²A)] = ½[2sin²A − 2sin²B]

= sin²A − sin²B — identity proved

Addition and subtraction Addition formula hard

H2.If tan A = 1/2 and tan B = 1/3 with A and B acute, find tan(A + B) and hence A + B. [4]

  1. H1. Use tan(A + B) = (tanA + tanB)/(1 − tanA tanB).
  2. H2. The numerator is 5/6 and the denominator 1 − 1/6 = 5/6.
  3. H3. The tangent is 1, and the sum lies between 0 and π because both angles are acute.

tan(A+B) = (1/2 + 1/3)/(1 − 1/6) = (5/6)/(5/6) = 1

A + B = π/4

Product-to-sum Conditional identity hard

H3.Prove that cos²A + cos²(A + 120°) + cos²(A − 120°) = 3/2. [5]

  1. H1. Replace each square using cos²θ = (1 + cos 2θ)/2.
  2. H2. The three 1s give 3/2; what remains is ½ times a sum of three cosines.
  3. H3. cos(2A+240°) + cos(2A−240°) = 2cos2A·cos240° = −cos2A.

= 3/2 + ½[cos2A + cos(2A+240°) + cos(2A−240°)] = 3/2 + ½[cos2A − cos2A]

= 3/2 — identity proved

Addition and subtraction Compound angle hard

H4.If sin α = 3/5 and cos β = 5/13 with α, β acute, find sin(α + β). [4]

  1. H1. Recover the missing ratios from right triangles: cos α = 4/5, sin β = 12/13.
  2. H2. sin(α + β) = sinα cosβ + cosα sinβ.
  3. H3. Substitute and put over 65.

= (3/5)(5/13) + (4/5)(12/13) = 15/65 + 48/65

sin(α + β) = 63/65

Double and half angles Double angle hard

H5.Prove that (1 + sin 2A)/cos 2A = (1 + tan A)/(1 − tan A). [5]

  1. H1. Write 1 + sin2A as (sinA + cosA)².
  2. H2. Factor cos2A = (cosA − sinA)(cosA + sinA).
  3. H3. Cancel the common factor and divide top and bottom by cosA.

(sinA + cosA)²/[(cosA − sinA)(cosA + sinA)] = (cosA + sinA)/(cosA − sinA) = (1 + tanA)/(1 − tanA)

identity proved

Addition and subtraction Exact value hard

H6.Find the exact value of tan 15°. [4]

  1. H1. Write 15° as 45° − 30°.
  2. H2. Apply tan(A − B) with tan45° = 1 and tan30° = 1/√3.
  3. H3. Rationalise (√3 − 1)/(√3 + 1) by multiplying by the conjugate.

tan15° = (1 − 1/√3)/(1 + 1/√3) = (√3 − 1)/(√3 + 1) = (√3 − 1)²/2 = (4 − 2√3)/2

tan 15° = 2 − √3
