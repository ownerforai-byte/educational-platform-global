Collinearity

Q1.Show A(1, 2, 3), B(3, 6, 9), C(5, 10, 15) are collinear. [4]

AB = (2, 4, 6), AC = (4, 8, 12) = 2·AB

The vectors are parallel, so the points are collinear

Coplanarity by triple product

Q2.Are (1, 2, 3), (2, 4, 6), (3, 6, 9) coplanar? [2]

All three are multiples of (1, 2, 3)

Yes — they are parallel, hence coplanar

Collinearity

Q3.Find λ if (1, 2, 3) and (2, 4, λ) are parallel. [2]

Each component must scale by the same factor 2

λ = 6

Coplanarity by triple product

Q4.Evaluate [i j k]. [2]

i·(j × k) = i·i

1

Coplanarity by triple product

Q5.Show that (1, 2, 3), (1, 1, 1), (2, 3, 4) are coplanar. [5]

b × c = (1, −2, 1) a·(b × c) = 1 − 4 + 3 = 0

Triple product 0 ⇒ coplanar

Coplanarity by triple product

Q6.Find λ if (1, 2, 3), (2, 3, 4), (3, 4, λ) are coplanar. [6]

b × c = (3λ − 16, 12 − 2λ, −1) a·(b × c) = −λ + 5 = 0

λ = 5

Volume applications

Q7.Find the volume of the parallelepiped with edges i, j, k. [2]

Volume = |[i j k]|

1 cubic unit

Perpendicular and unit vectors

Q8.Find a unit vector perpendicular to both i + j and j + k. [4]

(1, 1, 0) × (0, 1, 1) = (1, −1, 1), magnitude √3

(i − j + k)/√3

Perpendicular and unit vectors

Q9.If a·b = 0, |a| = 2 and |b| = 3, find |a + b|. [4]

|a + b|² = 4 + 0 + 9

Perpendicular and unit vectors

Q10.If |a + b| = |a − b|, show that a ⊥ b. [4]

Squaring: |a|² + 2a·b + |b|² = |a|² − 2a·b + |b|² ⇒ 4a·b = 0

a·b = 0 ⇒ a ⊥ b ✔

Collinearity

Q11.Find λ so that |λ(1, 2, 2)| = 3. [4]

|λ|·√9 = 3 ⇒ 3|λ| = 3

λ = ±1

Coplanarity by triple product

Q12.Show that (1, 0, 0), (0, 1, 0), (1, 1, 0) are coplanar. [2]

The third is the sum of the first two

Triple product 0 ⇒ coplanar

Coplanarity by triple product Spot the error

Q20.A student writes: since [a b c] = 0, the vectors are independent. Correct it. [2]

Zero volume means they lie in one plane.

A zero triple product means they are coplanar, hence dependent

Collinearity Assertion–reason

Q21.Assertion: (1, 2, 3), (2, 4, 6), (3, 6, 9) are collinear. Reason: each displacement vector is a multiple of the first. [2]

All three lie on one line through the origin.

Both true, and the reason proves collinearity

Perpendicular and unit vectors Explain

Q22.Why does a zero triple product force the three vectors into one plane? [2]

The triple product is the volume they would enclose.

Zero volume means they span no solid, so they must be coplanar

Volume applications Match

Q23.Match [a b c] = 0 and |a × b| with (a) area of the parallelogram, (b) coplanar vectors. [2]

One is a volume condition, the other an area.

[a b c] = 0 → (b), |a × b| → (a)
