Variance and standard deviation Explain

Q22.Why is the variance difficult to read directly? [2]

It is measured in squared units of the data.

Because its units are squared, the standard deviation is used to return to the original units

Range and mean deviation Match

Q23.Match range and mean deviation with (a) average of |x − x̄|, (b) largest − smallest. [2]

One uses only the extremes, the other all values.

Range → (b), mean deviation → (a)

Coefficient of variation Case study

Q24.A series has mean 50 and SD 5. Find the CV and the variance. [3]

CV = 100(5)/50; variance = 5²

CV = 10%, variance = 25
