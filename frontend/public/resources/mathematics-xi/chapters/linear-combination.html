Q3.Find x, y if x(1, 2) + y(2, 1) = (5, 4). [4]

x + 2y = 5, 2x + y = 4 ⇒ subtract: x = 1, y = 2

Q5.Are (1, 0, 0), (0, 1, 0), (0, 0, 1) independent? [2]

Q6.Are i + j and i − j independent? [4]

Yes

Q7.Show (1, 1, 0), (1, 0, 1), (0, 1, 1) are independent. [5]

det = 1(0 − 1) − 1(1 − 0) + 0 = −2 ≠ 0

Independent ✔

Q8.Are (1, 2, 3), (2, 4, 6), (1, 1, 1) independent? [2]

The second is twice the first

No — dependent

Basis in the plane and in space

Q9.Do (1, 2) and (3, 6) span the plane? [4]

No

Vector proofs

Q10.Prove the midpoint theorem with vectors: if M is the midpoint of AB and O is any origin, then OM = (OA + OB)/2. [4]

OM = OA + AM = OA + ½AB = OA + ½(OB − OA)

= (OA + OB)/2 ✔

Vector proofs

Q11.Show that the diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other. [5]

Midpoint of AC is (a + c)/2 and of BD is (b + d)/2; with c = b + d − a both agree

The same midpoint ⇒ bisection ✔

Basis in the plane and in space

Q12.What is the minimum number of independent vectors needed to span space? [2]

Three non-coplanar directions are required.

Three — a basis of ℝ³
