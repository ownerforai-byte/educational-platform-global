Q8.A train with u = 15 m/s and a = 1.5 m/s² travels 8 s. Find the distance. [4]

Q9.u = 5 m/s, a = 3 m/s², s = 20 m. Find v. [4]

v = √145 ≈ 12.04 m/s

Q10.A body falls from rest for 2 s (g = 10). Find the distance. [2]

Q11.Find the distance covered in the 3rd second of free fall (g = 10). [4]

Multi-stage motion

Q12.A car accelerates from rest at 2 m/s² for 10 s, then moves uniformly for 5 s. Find the total distance. [5]

Stage 1: v = 20, s = 100 Stage 2: s = 20 × 5 = 100

200 m

Retardation Spot the error

Q20.A student says a car slowing from 20 m/s to rest in 4 s has a = 5 m/s². Correct the statement. [2]

The velocity is decreasing, so a is negative.

a = −5 m/s², i.e. a retardation of 5 m/s²

Free fall Explain

Q22.Why does a heavier body not fall faster in a vacuum? [2]

Gravity gives every body the same acceleration there.

Because acceleration is independent of mass, so both fall together

Multi-stage motion Match

Q23.Match v = u + at and s = ut + ½at² with (a) displacement–time relation, (b) velocity–time relation. [2]

One gives position, the other speed.

v = u + at → (b), s = ut + ½at² → (a)

Q24.With u = 8 m/s, a = 2 m/s², t = 4 s find v and s. [4]

v = 8 + 8; s = 32 + ½(2)(16)

v = 16 m/s, s = 48 m
