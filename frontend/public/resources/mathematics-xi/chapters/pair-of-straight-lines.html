Angle between a pair

Q6.Find the angle between the pair x² − 4xy + y² = 0. [5]

tanθ = 2√(h² − ab)/(a + b) = 2√(4 − 1)/2 = √3

60°

Q7.Find the angle between 6x² + 5xy − 6y² = 0. [4]

a + b = 6 − 6 = 0 ⇒ the lines are perpendicular

90°
