Increasing and decreasing Assertion–reason

Q21.Assertion: f(x) = x² is decreasing on (−∞, 0). Reason: f′(x) = 2x is negative there. [2]

Concavity and absolute extrema Explain

Q22.Why must the endpoints be checked for absolute extrema? [2]

The greatest value on a closed interval need not be at a stationary point.

Because the maximum or minimum can occur at an end of the interval

Concavity and absolute extrema Match

Q23.Match f″ > 0 and f″ < 0 with (a) concave down, (b) concave up. [2]

The sign of the second derivative gives concavity.

f″ > 0 → (b), f″ < 0 → (a)

Greatest 2, least −2
