3.2 · Pair of Straight Lines — two-mark conceptual answers

Topics Homogeneous pairs · Angle between a pair · Condition for a real pair · Slopes and special cases

Each question is the two-mark kind NEB asks at the start of the paper; the model answer is one or two precise sentences.

Homogeneous pairs 2 marks

C1. What does ax² + 2hxy + by² = 0 represent? [2]

Ans. A pair of straight lines through the origin. Being homogeneous and of degree two, it factors into two linear equations with zero constant terms.

Angle between a pair 2 marks

C2. When are the two lines of ax² + 2hxy + by² = 0 perpendicular? [2]

Ans. When a + b = 0, i.e. the sum of the coefficients of x² and y² vanishes.

Angle between a pair 2 marks

C3. When do the two lines coincide? [2]

Ans. When h² = ab, because then the quadratic in the slope has equal roots and both lines have the same slope.

Condition for a real pair 2 marks

C4. Why does the pair of lines pass through the origin? [2]

Ans. Putting x = 0 gives by² = 0, so y = 0; the point (0, 0) satisfies both factors.

Condition for a real pair 2 marks

C5. What is the angle between the lines ax² + 2hxy + by² = 0? [2]

Ans. tan θ = 2√(h² − ab)/(a + b), which comes from the difference of the two slopes.

Slopes and special cases 2 marks

C6. What is the sum of the slopes of the pair ax² + 2hxy + by² = 0? [2]

Ans. The slopes satisfy bm² + 2hm + a = 0, so their sum is −2h/b.
