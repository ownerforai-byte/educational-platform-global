Q2.Find the general solution of cosθ = 1/2.[2]

θ = 2nπ ± π/3

θ = 2nπ ± π/3, n ∈ ℤ

Q3.Find the general solution of tanθ = −1.[2]

θ = nπ + tan⁻¹(−1)

θ = nπ − π/4, n ∈ ℤ

sin θ = k

Q4.Find the general solution of sinθ = 0.[2]

sinθ vanishes at every multiple of π

θ = nπ, n ∈ ℤ

cos θ = k

Q5.Find the general solution of cosθ = −1.[2]

cos θ = −1 only at odd multiples of π

θ = (2n + 1)π, n ∈ ℤ

Q7.Solve 2cos²θ − 3cosθ + 1 = 0.[5]

(2cosθ − 1)(cosθ − 1) = 0 ⇒ cosθ = 1/2 or 1

θ = 2nπ ± π/3 or θ = 2nπ

tan θ = k

Q8.Solve tanθ = cotθ.[4]

tan²θ = 1 ⇒ tanθ = ±1

θ = nπ ± π/4, n ∈ ℤ

Q9.Solve sinθ + cosθ = 0.[4]

Q11.Solve cos2θ = cosθ.[5]

2θ = 2nπ ± θ ⇒ θ = 2nπ or 3θ = 2nπ

θ = 2nπ or θ = 2nπ/3, n ∈ ℤ

Q12.How many solutions does sin3θ = 0 have in [0, π]?[4]
