Mandatory Nepal Engineering Council (NEC) Registration

Under the Nepal Engineering Council Act, passing the NEC Licensure Examination is required to legally practice as an 'A' grade registered engineer in Nepal. The exam consists of 100 objective questions covering discipline fundamentals and engineering ethics.

Question 1 · NEC Act & Ethics 1 Mark

According to the Nepal Engineering Council (NEC) Code of Ethics, what is the primary obligation of a registered professional engineer?

Explanation:

Under both national (NEC Act 2055) and international (IEEE/ASCE/FIDIC) engineering codes of ethics, the engineer's fundamental duty is holding paramount public safety, health, and welfare.

Question 2 · Structural / Material Mechanics 1 Mark

For a simply supported beam of span L carrying a uniformly distributed load w across the entire span, what is the maximum bending moment?

Explanation:

Reactions R_A = R_B = wL/2. Taking moment at x = L/2 gives M_max = (wL/2)(L/2) - w(L/2)(L/4) = wL²/4 - wL²/8 = wL²/8.

Nepal Engineering Council (NEC) Regulations

Official licensure examination syllabus, pass criteria, and legal provisions.

NEC Licensure Exam Blueprint

  • Total Marks: 100 Marks (100 Multiple Choice Questions).
  • Duration: 2 Hours (Computer Based Examination).
  • Pass Mark: 50% (50 marks).
  • Negative Marking: None (unlike IOE or Loksewa, there is NO negative marking in NEC licensure).
  • Core Section (Common): Engineering Ethics, Project Planning, Procurement Act, Environmental Impact (EIA/IEE).

Legal Scope of Practice

Section 11 of the NEC Act 2055 prohibits any person from practicing engineering professions, signing blueprints, or stamping government permits without a valid NEC registration certificate.

• NEC General Registration: Passed BE/B.Tech from recognized university + passed licensure exam.
• NEC Professional Registration: 5+ years of certified field practice + professional interview.
