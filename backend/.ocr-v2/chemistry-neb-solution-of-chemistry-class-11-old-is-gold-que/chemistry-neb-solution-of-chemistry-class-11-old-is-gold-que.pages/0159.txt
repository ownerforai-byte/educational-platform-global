Electron Affinity
The amount of energy which is released when an electron is added to an isolated gaseous atom
in its ground state in order to change into the negatively charged ion is called electron affinity.
The factor which influences the magnitude of electron affinity is nuclear charge. The value of
electron affinity increases with increase in nuclear charge.
