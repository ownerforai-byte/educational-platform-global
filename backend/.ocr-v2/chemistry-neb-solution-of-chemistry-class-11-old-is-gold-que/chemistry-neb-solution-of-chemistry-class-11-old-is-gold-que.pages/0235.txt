In the presence of sunlight, pure nitric acid (HN03) decomposes slowly into nitrogen dioxide (N02),
water (H20) and gaseous oxygen (02). Due to this, acid turns into yellow or brown (depending on
how much is present). To reduce the rate of that decomposition when in storage, it's stored m dark
bottles.
