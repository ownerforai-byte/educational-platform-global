Why is a mercury droplet spherical?
Mercury metal is found in liquid state. In mercury, cohesive force is greater than adhesive force,
due to this the surface of liquid tends to contract to the smallest possible area for a given
volume of liquid wrhich is called surface tension. Hence, due to the very high surface tension
mercury droplets are spherical.
