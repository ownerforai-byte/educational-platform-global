Depending upon the nature of groups attached to the carbon chain, there are two types of

Positive inductive effect or + I effect : If the group attached to carbon atom tn the carbon chain

inductive effect is known as positive inductive effect. Examples: -CH3, -COO¯, -CH2R,-CR3 etc.
