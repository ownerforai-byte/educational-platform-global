What is meant by Green house effect? Name any one gas contributing to the Green house
effect.
The atmosphere that surrounds the earth acts like the glass or plastic roof and wall of a green
house. When solar energy reaches the earth passing through the atmosphere surrounding the
earth, the COD water vapour, etc. present in the atmosphere absorb most of the solar energy
and gets heated and excited. The excess energy present in them is converted into heat.
consequently, the earth is warmed up resulting with the increase of earth's temperature.
However, when earth cools, the energy is re-emitted from the earth's surface in the form of
infrared radiation and some is absorbed by atmospheric carbon dioxide and water vapours but
can't pass through them and once again radiated back to the surface of the earth. In this way,
additional heat is kept in the lower atmosphere resulting in the warming of the earth which is
called green house effect.
