39• How many molecules are contained in 0.35 mole of N2?
Solution
1 mole of N2 molecule = 6.022 x 1023 N2 molecules
0.35 mole of N2 molecule = 6.022 x 1023 x 0.35
= 2.17 x 1023 N2 molecules
Hence, 2.17 x 1023 N2 molecules are contained in 0.35 mol.
