Both oxidation and reduction involves in the same chemical reaction. The species which loses
the electron in the reaction is said to be oxidized and the species which gains the electron is said
to be reduced. In a chemical reaction, oxidation process is always followed by reduction and
Vice-versa. Both process take place side by side i.e. occur simultaneously which is known as
oxidation reduction reaction or Redox reaction.
