23• Why is the ionisation energy of lithium greater than sodium?
The atomic size of Lithium is smaller than that of Sodium. We know that smaller the size of
atom stronger the force of attraction between nucleus and outermost electron and greater the
ionization energy. So, Lithium has greater ionization energy than Sodium.
24• What was the basis of the classification of elements in Mendeleev's periodic
[21
table?
In 1869 a Russian Scientist Dmitri Mendeleev brought a broad concept of a systematic
classification and arrangement of elements. Mendeleev's periodic law states that "The physical
and chemical properties of all are the periodic function of their atomic masses. i.e. all
the elements are arranged in the order of increasing atomic mass and the table based on this law
is known as Mendeleev's periodic table.
