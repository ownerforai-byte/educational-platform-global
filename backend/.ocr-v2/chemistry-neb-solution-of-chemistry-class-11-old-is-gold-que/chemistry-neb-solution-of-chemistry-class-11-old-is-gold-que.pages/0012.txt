During carbon reduction process, infusible (earthy, rocky) impurities are converted to fusibl
mass by adding third chemical substances. This third chemical is called flux. Th
fusible mass formed is called slag. This forms a molten layer on surfaces which can b
easily removed.
The flux are of two types:
Acidic flux: Si02 and Na2B407.10H20
Basic flux: Cao, MgO, CaC03, MgC03
The choice of flux depends upon the nature of impurities. If impurities are basic, Acidic flu
should be added or vice-versa.
