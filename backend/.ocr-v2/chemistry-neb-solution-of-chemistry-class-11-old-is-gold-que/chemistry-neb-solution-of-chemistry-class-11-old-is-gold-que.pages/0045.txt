7• HOW many molecules are contained in 0.35 mole of N2?
;olution
1 mole of N2 contains 6.022 x 1023 molecules
0.35 mole of N2 contains 0.35 x 6.022 x 1023 molecules = 2.10 x 1023 molecules.
Hence, 2.10 x 1023 molecules of N2 are contained in 0.35 mole of N2.
