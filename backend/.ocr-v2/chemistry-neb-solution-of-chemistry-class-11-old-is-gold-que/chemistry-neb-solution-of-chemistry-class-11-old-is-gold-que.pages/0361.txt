066 a.No.2 Which terminology is used to grade the quality of fuel? Define the terminology.
The octane number is used to grade the quality of a fuel. Higher the value of octane number,
better the quality of fuel. Generally all the fuel that have octane number 80 or above are graded
as good fuel. Octane number is defined as the percentage of iso-octane by volume in the given
mixture of iso-octane and n-heptane which has thc same antiknocking properties as the fuel
under examination.
