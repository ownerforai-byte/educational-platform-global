Why is ozone layer formed at stratosphere? Give an importance of ozone layer.
2-
The stratosphere is the second layer of the atmosphere as we go upward. The stratosphere
stretches between 15 and 50 km up from the Earth's surface. Ozone is produced naturally the
stratosphere when highly energetic solar radiation breaks down molecules of oxygen (02) and
causes the two oxygen atoms to split apart in a process called photolysis. If a freed atom
collides with another (02), it joins up, forming ozone (03). Ozone layer is relatively abundant m
the stratosphere, this layer absorbs energy from incoming ultraviolet radiation from the Sun.
