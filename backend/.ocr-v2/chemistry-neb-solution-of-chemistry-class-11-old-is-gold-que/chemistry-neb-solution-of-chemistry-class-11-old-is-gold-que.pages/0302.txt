below their melting point is called roasting. During roasting (i) sulphide ores are generally
converted into oxides (ii) volatile impurities are removed (iii) free S, As and Sb are removed as

Reduction by carbon (Smelting): The oxides of less electropositive metals like P b, Zn,FeSn,Cu
etc are reduced by strongly heating them with coal or coke. Reduction of oxide with carbon at
high temperature is known as smelting. Here, the calcinated or roasted ore is mixed with coal or
coke and flux and heated in a reverberatory furnace or blast furnace in the presence of air.
