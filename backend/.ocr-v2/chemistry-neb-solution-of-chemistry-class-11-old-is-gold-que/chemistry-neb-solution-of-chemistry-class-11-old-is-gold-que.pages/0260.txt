Allotropy: The two or more forms of the same element having same chemical propernes but
different physical properties are allopropes and the phenomenon is called Allotropy.
Experiment shows that both diamond and graphite contain carbon only:
1 g of diamond is taken in combustion tube and heated in the presence of excess of air.
carbondioxide gas is evolved which is passed through the aqueous solution of KOH. The KOH
solution is kept in different bulbs which are kept in series. The carbondioxide gas evolved
absorbed by KOH solution. The weight of C02 gas is taken.
The same experiment is done in 1 g of graphite and again weight of C02 gas is taken.
Experimentally, it is found that the weight of carbondioxide gas produced by diamond is
to the weight of carbondioxide produced by graphite. This result shows both diamond and
graphite contains same weight of carbon only.
