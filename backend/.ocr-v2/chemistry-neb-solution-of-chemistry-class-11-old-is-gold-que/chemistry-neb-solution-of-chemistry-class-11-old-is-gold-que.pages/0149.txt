48• How would you explain metallic bond in light of electron-sea model?
Atoms of metal in a solid piece of metal are bonded strongly together by means of very strong
bonds called metallic bond. In a metal atom there are more hybrid orbitals than number of
valence electrons. Hence the valence electrons become mobile. They can move from one orbital
to another and from one atom to another in a solid piece of metal. Thus atoms become
positively charged by losing their valence electrons. These positive ions are held in the three
dimensional space in a definite pattern in the sea of mobile electrons. Thus, this model is knowrn
as electron sea model.
