32- Give the characteristics of Alkaline earth metals.
[51
Follovving are the characteristics of alkaline earth metals:
a. The elements of group IIA of modern periodic table are alkaline earth metals.
i. They are divalent elements and are highly electropositive elements.
ii- They have oxidation state +2.
iii. They are malleable, ductile and good conductor of heat and electricity.
iv- They generally form ionic compounds.
v. They react with hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, halogens to form hydride, oxide, nitride, and
halides
vi. They act as reducing agent.
