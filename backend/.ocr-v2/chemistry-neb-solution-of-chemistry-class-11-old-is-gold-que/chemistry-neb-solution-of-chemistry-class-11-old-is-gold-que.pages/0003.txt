3. Why does boiling point of liquid remain constant even though the heating is continued?
The boiling point of liquid remains constant even though the heating is continued. The heat
supplied is consumed to make the liquid molecules a part form the attractive force which hold
the liquid molecule. Thus, the heat energy goes to compensate the loss of energy due to the
escaping of molecules. As a result, there is no change in kinetic energy of the liquid molecules.
This cause temperature of boiling liquid constant even through the heating is continued.
