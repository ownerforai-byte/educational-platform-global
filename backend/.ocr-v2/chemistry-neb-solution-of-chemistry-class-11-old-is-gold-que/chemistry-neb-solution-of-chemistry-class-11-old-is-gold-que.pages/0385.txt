25. Write notes on Acidic nature of ethyne
The polarity of the bond between H atom and triple bonded carbon atom can be explained on
the basis of hybridization. In acetylene the carbon atoms are sp-hybridization and they possess
50% s-character and 50% p-character. So, the sp-hybridized carbon atom is more electronegative
than hydrogen and the bonding pair of electrons are displaced towards the carbon atom and
bearing partial negative charge and hydrogen atom bears partial positive charge. Due to this
polarity generated, H- atoms are easily donated as their proton indicating the acidic property of
acetylene.
