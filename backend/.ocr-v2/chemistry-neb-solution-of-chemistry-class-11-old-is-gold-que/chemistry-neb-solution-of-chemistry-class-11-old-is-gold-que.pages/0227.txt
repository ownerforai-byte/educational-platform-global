Working plant for the manufacture of Ammonia by Haber's process:
Production of Nzand gas: N2 gas is obtained by fractional distillation of air and H2 gas is obtained
by fractional liquefaction of water gas (CO + H2)
Compressor: The mixture of N2 and H2 gas is passed Into the purifying chamber to a pressure of 200-
900 atmospheric pressure. The purifying chamber contains soda-lime water which absorbs C02 and
moisture.
Catalyst chamber: This chamber is packed with finely divided Fe and little Mo as a promoter. The
mixture of gas are passed through catalyst chamber at about 450-5000C.
Condenser: The ga.s which is coming out from catalytic chamber contains NH3 along with N2 and H2.
The mixture of gas is passed through a condenser where NH3 gas is condensed to liquid NH3 which
is collected in a receiver.
