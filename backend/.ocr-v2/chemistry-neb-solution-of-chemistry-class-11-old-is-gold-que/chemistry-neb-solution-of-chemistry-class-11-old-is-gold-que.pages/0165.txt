40• State Modern Periodic law. What are the advantages of modem periodic table?
b Modern Periodic Law: In 1912, Mosley developed a periodic law modifying Mendeleev's
periodic law. It states that the physical and chemical properties of the elements are the periodic
function of their atomic numbers.
Advantages of Modern periodic table are:
i. Position of isotopes: The classification is based on the atomic numbers, the position of isotopes
is fully justified.
ii. Explanation of atomic properties: This periodic table has become successful to define the
atomic properties like atomic radius, ionization of energy, electronegativity etc.
iii. Division of the elements into s, p, d and f blocks: The division of the elements into s, p, d, f
blocks according to their electronic configuration has made the study simpler.
iv. Beauty of periodic law: The arrangement of the elements according to the atomic number is
more scientific way to arrangrng the elements.
v. Separation of sub-groups: This table has given separate positions for sub-group A and sub-
group B.

Ionization potential or energy: Electron of an atom can be removed by supplying energy. This
energy is known as ionization energy or ionization potential or ionization enthalpy. Thus it is
defined as "the minimum energy* ',.vhich is required to remove the most loosely bounded
electron (valence electron) from an isolated gaseous atom in its ground state". It is denoted by
I.E. or I.P. and expressed in electron volt (eV).

Atomic Size: I.E. decrease with increase in atomic size because the larger the distance from the
nucleus, the lesser will be the attraction between the nucleus and the valence electrons.
Nuclear Charge: The greater the number of protons, the greater the attraction of the electrons to
the nucleus and the harder it is to remove the electrons. The number of protons in the nucleus is
known as the nuclear charge. So, the greater the value of nuclear charge, more difficult will it
be to remove an electron.
