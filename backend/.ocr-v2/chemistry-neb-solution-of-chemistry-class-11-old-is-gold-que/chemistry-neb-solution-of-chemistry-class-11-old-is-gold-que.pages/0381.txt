Fig: Lnboratory method of preparation of ethyne
Procedure: Few grams of calcium carbide is taken in a conical flask containing a layer of sand.
The flask is fitted with a dropping funnel. Fewr mL of water is dropped from the dropping
funnel over calcium carbide. Ethyne gas is liberated, which is passed through acidified solution
of copper sulphate, which removes the impurifies like H2S,PH3,NH3 etc. the pure ethyne gas is
collected in gas jar by the downward displacement of water.
