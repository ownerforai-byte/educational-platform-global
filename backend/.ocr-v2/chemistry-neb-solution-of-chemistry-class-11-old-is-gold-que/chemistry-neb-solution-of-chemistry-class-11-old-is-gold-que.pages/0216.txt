Isotopes of hydrogen: Hydrogen has three isotope. They are (i) Protium (ii)
(iii)Tritium
i. Protium: It contains one proton, one electron and no neutron. It is also called
ordinary hydrogen atom. Naturally occurring hydrogen about 99.9%

Its mass no. is 1.
ii- Deuterium: It contains one proton, one electron and one neutron. It is also

iii. Tritium: It contains one proton, one electron and two neutrons. It is also called
radioactive hydrogen. Naturally occurring hydrogen contains about 0.001 % and
denoted by T3. mass no. is 3.
