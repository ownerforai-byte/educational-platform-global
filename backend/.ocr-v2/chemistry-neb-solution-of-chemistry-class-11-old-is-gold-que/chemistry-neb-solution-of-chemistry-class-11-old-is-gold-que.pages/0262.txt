Coke: It is balck solid mass which is obtained by the destructive distillation of coal. It is used as

Charcoal: It is black soft form of carbon. The main varieties of charcoal are as:
Lamp black: It is obtained by burning oil in the absence of air or limited supply of air.
Sugar charcoal: It is obtained by heating sugar in the absence of air. It is used as black Pigment
Wood charcoal: It is obtained by destructive distillation of wood in the absence of air.
Animal charcoal: It is obtained by destructive distillation of animal bones.
