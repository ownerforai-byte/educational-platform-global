Preparation of nitric acid from ammonia:
The industrial preparation of nitric acid is known as the Ostwald process. The Osbvald process
involves the conversion of ammonia into nitric acid in various stages:
Firstly, ammonia is heated with oxygen in the presence of a platinum catalyst to form nitric
oxide and water.
4NH3(g) + 502(g) -...-.........-..-......+ 4NO(g) + 6H20(g)
Secondly, nitric oxide reacts with oxygen to form nitrogen dioxide. This gas is then readily
absorbed by the water to produce nitric acid. A portion of nitrogen dioxide is reduced back to
nitric oxide.
2NO(g) + 02(g) -........-.............+ 2N02(g)
3N02(g) + H20(1) 2HN03(aq) + NO(g)
The NO is recycled, and the acid is concentrated to the required strength by a process called
distillation.
