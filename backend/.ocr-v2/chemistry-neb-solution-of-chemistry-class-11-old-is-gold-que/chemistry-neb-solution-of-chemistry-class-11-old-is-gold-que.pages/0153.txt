67• Why are metals malleable and ductile?
In metal the positive ions of metals are surrounded by sea of mobile electrons and the force of
attraction occur between metal ion and mobile electrons which is placed uniform in all
directions. The metal ions are held together by strong force in crystal lattice. Due to this one
layer of metal can slide over other when they are stretched and hammered. That is why metals
are malleable and ductile.
68. Why is solid sodium chloride a non conductor of electricity?
Solid sodium chloride is an ionic compound and ions present in sodium chloride is strongly
held together by an electrostatic force of attraction. They are strongly fixed in crystal lattice.
Hence, there are no mobile electrons or free electrons in solid state of sodium chloride. So,
sodium chloride is non-conductor of electricity.

70• Why does water have such a relatively high boiling point?
Stronger the hydrogen bond, greater the intermolecular attraction and larger heat energy is
required to separate the molecule before they boil. So the compound having hydrogen bond
possess high boiling point. That is why water have a relatively high boiling point.
