Periodic Classification of Elements
position of hydrogen: Position of hydrogen in this periodic table is controversial because
a.
hydrogen behaves like alkali metals as well as like halogens. Thus it can be placed with alkali
metals as well as with halogens.
b. position of isotopes: The atoms of same element having same atomic number but different in
mass number are called isotopes. Example- hydrogen will need three positions because there
are three isotope of hydrogen but it is not in periodic table.
c. Fault of the periodic law: Certain elements like Ar & K, Ni & Co and Te & I do not obey this
law. Argon (Ar) having atomic mass 40, is placed before Potassium (K) having atomic mass 39.
Cobalt(Co) having atomic mass 59, is placed before Nickel(Ni) having atomic mass 58.6 and
Tellurium(Te) having atomic mass 127.6, is placed before Iodine(l) having atgmic mass 127.
d. Separation of identical elements and grouping of dissimilar elements: Elements having
similar properties are kept in different group while some dissimilar elements are placed in the
same group. Example: Cu and Hg resemble in their properties but they are separated. Alkali
metals like Li, Na and K were grouped with coinage metals Cu, Ag and Au.
e. Position of lanthanides and actinides: Mendeleev's periodic table has not given a separate
place for the lanthanides and actinides series from the main body of the periodic table.
