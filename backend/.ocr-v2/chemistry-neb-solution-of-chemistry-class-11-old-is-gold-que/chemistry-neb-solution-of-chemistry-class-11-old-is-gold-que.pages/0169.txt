51. Periodic properties of elements.
Periodic properties:
Various atomic properties like ionization potential, electron affinity, atomic size etc. are directly
related to electronic configuration but properties like boiling point, melting point, density etc.
are indirectly related to the E.C. These properties of an element are called periodic properties.
The variation of these properties along with a group and period are described below.
Ionization potential or energy: Electron of an atom can be removed by supplying energy. This
energy is known as ionization energy or ionization potential or ionization enthalpy. Thus it is
defined as "the minimum energy which is required to remove the most loosely bounded
electron (valence electron) from an isolated gaseous atom in its ground state". It is denoted by
I.E. or I.P. and expressed in electron volt (eV).

Ionization Energy depends upon the following factors
1. Atomic Size: I.E. decrease with increase in atomic size because the larger the distance from the
nucleus, the lesser will be the attraction between the nucleus and the valence electrons.
2. Nuclear Charge: The greater the number of protons, the greater the attraction of the electrons to
the nucleus and the harder it is to remove the electrons. The number of protons in the nucleus is
known as the nuclear charge. So the greater the value of nuclear charge, the more difficult will
it be to remove an electron.
3. Screening effect or shielding effect: The outermost electrons are screened / shielded from the
nucleus by inner electrons. This phenomenon is called shielding effect or screening effect. The
greater the number of inner electrons, the minimum the value of ionization potential because as
a result of screening effect, the electrons are less effectively attracted towards the nucleus.
Therefore, it is easier to reruove the loosely bound electron.
4• Electronic configuration: The more stable the electronic configuration, the greater will be the
ionization potential. Elements having half-filled and full-filled orbitals are more stable or have
extra stability because it is difficult to remove electron from their shells.
