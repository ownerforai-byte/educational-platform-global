Application Of Le•Chatelier's principle i
chemical equilibrium.
In 1884, a French chemist Le-Chatelier studied the effect of concentration, pressure
temperature on the state of equilibrium. It states that "If a system in equilibrium is subjected
a change in concentration, pressure or temperature then the equilibrium shifts in such a ways
as to nullify the effect of the change." That is in an equilibrium reaction:
1. On increasing the concentration, the equilibrium shift to the direction where the increasa
concentration is consumed. On doing so, the effect of increased concentration will b
minimized.
2. On increasing the pressure, the equilibrium shifts to the direction where volume
decreased. As we know that when the pressure is increased the volume will decreased' I
the equilibrium shifts to the side where volume is increased then the effect of increasa
pressure will be minimized.
3. On increasing the temperature, the equilibrium shifts to the direction where heat
absorbed. As we know that the increased temperature raises the heat. If the equilibriUt
shifts to the side where heat is absorbed then the effect of increased temperature will
minimized.
