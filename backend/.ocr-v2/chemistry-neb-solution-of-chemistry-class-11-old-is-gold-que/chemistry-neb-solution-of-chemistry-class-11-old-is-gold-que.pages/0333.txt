Detection of nitrogen by Lassaigne test:
presence of nitrogen is detected in an organic compound by Lassaigne's method. In this method
the organic compound is fused with a piece of sodium in a fusion tube. After complete fusion,
the red hot fusion tube is dipped in distilled '.vater contained in a mortar and the whole mass is
grinded with a pestle. The hot solution is filtered and the filtrate v•.'hich is called 'sodium
extract' is used for the detection of nitrogen.
The reactions occurs during the process of fusion:
If the organic compound contains nitrogen, sodium cyanide is formed which remains dissolved
in the sodium extract.
Detection of nitrogen:
About 2 ml of sodium extract solution is taken in clean and dry test tube. About 1 ml of freshly
prepared ferrous sulphate solution is added to it. The solution is heated and 2-3 drops of Ferric
chloride solution is added. The solution is acidified by adding dil. HCl. The solution becomes
bluish green. This indicates the presence of nitrogen in given organic compound.
