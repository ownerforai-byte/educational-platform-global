28. rule
"The addition of halo acid or other unsymmetrical reagent to unsymmetrical alkene occurs in
such a way that the positive part of reagent goes to the carbon atom having greater number of
hydrogen atom and negative part of reagent goes to the carbon atom having lesser number of
carbon atom." Example:

I-bromopropane
The major product is 2-bromopropane.
Mechanism: It involves the formation of carbocation and carboanion. The carbocation and
carboanion are formed by the displacement of bonding pair of electrons toward more
electronegative carbon atom. The positive part of reagent i.e. H + of HBr adds to the negative
part i.e. carboanion and negative part of reagent i.e. Br— of HBr adds to the positive part i.e.
carbocation.
