a. What is the difference between accurate and precise measurement?
Accurate measurement means capable of providing a correct reading or measurement. In physical
science, it means correct. A measurement is accurate if it correctly reflects the size of the thing being
measured.
Precise measurement means exact, as in performance, execution, or amount. In physical science it
means repeatable, reliable, getting the same measurement each time.

d. Does the acceleration ofa body moving in a circular path remain constant?
No, the acceleration of a body moving in a circular path does not remain constant. Although, the
magnitude of acceleration of the body moving in a circular path remains constant, its direction
changes with time. Being acceleration a vector quantity, it has both magnitude and direction. Due to
change in direction, the acceleration changes with time in a circular path but not remain constant.
e. Sand is thrown on the roads covered with snow. Why?
Sand is thrown on the roads covered with snow because it increases friction. When the sand is
thrown in• the icy road, it increases the coefficient of friction between the tyres of the vehicles and the
road which prevents the sliding of vehicles.
