PROCESS
Take a glass tube and bend it at right angle. Take a clean round bottom
flask. To this flask put the mixture of ammonium chloride and slaked
lime in 1:2 ratio by weight. Add few drops of water to make the mixture
into paste. Fit the apparatus as shown in figure and make it airtight. Then
heat the mixture and collect the ammonia gas by downward
displacement of air. Study properties by performing following
experiments.
