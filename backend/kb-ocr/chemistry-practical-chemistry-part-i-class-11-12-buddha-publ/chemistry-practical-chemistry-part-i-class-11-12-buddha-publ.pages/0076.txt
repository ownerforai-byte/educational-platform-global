Practical Chemistry
a) Cutting a glass tube: When you need to cut a glass tube then
follow the steps given below.
i) Put the glass tube on the edge of your working table and
hold it with your left hand.
ii) Give a mark at the point of the glass tube where you Want
to cut. Take a triangular file in the right hand and make a
single deep scratch on the mark by drawing the file towards
yourself.
iii) Do not move the file back and forth. If one operation is not
sufficient to make a deep scratch, you can draw the file
again in the same way at the same place.
iv) Place the thumbs on either side of the scratch with the
scratch facing outwards.
v) Break the tube by pulling gently towards yourself.
vi) The broken tube has sharp edge. Make this sharp edge
smooth by heating the edges in the oxidising flame (non-
luminous flame) and allow it to cool on the asbestos sheet.
