Fitting the thistle funnel and delivery tube
d)
After making the suitable delivery tube and hole in cork, thistle
funnel and delivery tube are fitted. Fitting is done in following
steps.
i) Wet the ends of the thistle funnel and delivery tube with a
few drops of water or glycerine.
ii) Insert the tubes through the holes of the cork gently with a
rotary motion. While doing so, hold the tube near to the
hole of the cork. This prevents from breaking of tube. Rotate
the cork slightly instead of rotating the tube to avoid
breakage.
iii) At last, fit the cork having the tube into the neck of the flask'
