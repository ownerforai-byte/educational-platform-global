THEORY
Volatile acid can be prepared by the action of less volatile acid with the
salt of corresponding more volatile acid. The solution of hydrogen
chloride gas i.e. hydrochloric acid is a volatile acid. Therefore, hydrogen
chloride gas is prepared by heating its salt, sodium chloride, with less
volatile concentrated sulphuric acid. The gas is collected by upward
displacement of air since it is water soluble and heavier than air.
