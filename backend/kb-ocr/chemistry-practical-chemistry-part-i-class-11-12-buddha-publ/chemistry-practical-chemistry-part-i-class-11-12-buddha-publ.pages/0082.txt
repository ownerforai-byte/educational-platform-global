Note: Instead of dilute hydrochloric acid, sulphuric acid and nitric acid cannot
be used. On using sulphuric acid, insoluble calcium sulphate is formed whicll
covers the marble pieces and keeps the marble pieces out of contact of sulphuric
acid. In case of nitric acid, along with C02 other gas is also evolved since
HN03 is an oxidising agent.
Powdered CaC03 also cannot be used in the preparation of C02 gas because
the reaction is too fast.
