THEORY
The presence of soluble substances in the wood-ash can be known by
dissolution of the wood-ash in water followed by filtration and
evaporation. Filtration can be defined as the method of separafing the
insoluble component and solution of the soluble component of the mixture
by means of a porous medium like filter paper, glass wool etc. The
insoluble component is obtained on the filter paper as residue whereas the
solution of the soluble component iS collected below the funnel as filtrate.
