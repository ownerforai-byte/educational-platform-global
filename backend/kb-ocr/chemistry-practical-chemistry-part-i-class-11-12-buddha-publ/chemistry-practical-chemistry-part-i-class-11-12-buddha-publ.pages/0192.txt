Object; TO DETERMINE THE RELATIVE SURFACE TENSION OF
UNKNOWN LIQUID BY DROP COUNT METHOD
APPARATUS REQUIRED
3. Beaker
1. Stalagmometer 2. Pyknometer
5. Clamp and stand
4. Rubber tube
CHEMICALS REQUIRED
1. Distilled water 2. Given liquid
THEORY
Molecules of surface liquid are only subjected to attractive force of
molecules below these surface molecules so surface molecules are under
tension. Surface tension can be defined as force per unit length acting
perpendicular to the tangential line of the force. The spherical shape and
the rise of liquid in capillary tube etc. are effect of surface tension. It
depends upon temperature and density of liquid.
The relative surface tension of liquid can be determined in lab by
drop count method. This method is based on counting number of drops
of same volume of given liquid with respect to water using tube
stalagmometer.
The formula used to calculate the relative surface tension of liquid is
—IL nw dl
nl x dw
Where,
YI = surface tension of given liquid
yw = surface tension of water
nl = number of drops of liquid
nw = number of drops of water
dl = density of liquid
dw = density of water
densities can be determined by measuring mass with help Of
pyknometer.
