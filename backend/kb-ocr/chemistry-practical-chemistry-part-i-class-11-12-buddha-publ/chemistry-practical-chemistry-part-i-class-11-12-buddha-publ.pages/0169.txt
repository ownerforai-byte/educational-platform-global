THEORY
Nitric acid acts as an acidic agent. Therefore, it reacts with bases and
liberates carbon dioxide gas from carbonates. It also acts as an oxidising
agent and oxidises many of the elements and compounds being itself
reduced to nitric oxide, nitrogen peroxide etc.
