Redox Titration
The titration in which an oxidising agent and a reducing agent are made
to react each other is called redox titration. Here, the redox reaction takes
place completely at the end point. For example: titration of KMn04
solution (oxidising agent) vs. oxalic acid solution (reducing agent).
