THEORY
Pure crystals of copper sulphate can be obtained by crystallisation of
saturated solution of impure Bazzar copper sulphate. The solution which
can dissolve no more solute at a given temperature is called saturated
solution at that temperature. The process by which a saturated solution at
a given temperature is heated to a temperature, called crystallisation
point and then allowed to cool to obtain a crystalline substance is called
crystallisation.
