THEORY
From the solution of the mixture of copper sulphate and common salt,
copper hydroxide is precipitated out by the addition of excess of sodium
hydroxide solution. Copper hydroxide is decomposed into black oxide of
copper (i.e., cupric oxide) under the action of heat. The black oxide of
copper is treated with dilute sulphuric acid. Thus obtained copper
sulphate solution is subjected to crystallization to obtain blue vibiol
crystals.

oxide of copper with distilled water several times. The residue-washed
water is treated with AgN03 solution and BaC12 solution separately.
Presence of chloride and sulphate ions is indicated by formation of a
