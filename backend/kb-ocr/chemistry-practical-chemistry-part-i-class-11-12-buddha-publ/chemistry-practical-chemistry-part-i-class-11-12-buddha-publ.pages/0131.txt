PROCEDURE
Wash the whole apparatus with water.
Clean the burette with distilled water. Rinse the burette with the
given KMn04 solution.
Clamp the burette to the stand. Fill the burette with the KMn04
solution upto the zero mark. The upper meniscus of the solution
should coincide the mark of the burette.
Clean the pipette with distilled water. Rinse it with standard oxalic
acid solution.
Pipette out 10 ml of the acid solution into a conical flask. Add a test
tubeful of bench H2S04 and heat the flask to about 60-700C.
Titrate the hot oxalic acid solution by adding KMn04 solution from
the burette dropwisely swirling the flask continuously.
Continue adding KMn04 soluüon till a light pink colour is seen
permanently.
