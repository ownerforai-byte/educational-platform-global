THEORY
The titration between bench alkali and bench acid or bench alkali and
dilute acid does not give correct titre value. This is because a small
volume of the bench acid or bench alkali is consumed by a large volume
of the dilute acid. A very small volumetric error, therefore, gives a
different resulting strength. Hence, correct titre value is obtained when
the dilute solutions are titrated. For this reason, double titration is
performed. At first, bench alkali is titrated against dilute acid to have
only one rough reading. Then the approximate strength of the bench
alkali is determined.
The bench alkali is diluted to the required extent such that the
diluted alkali and acid neutralize more or less in equal volumes. Then
second titration is done to standardize this diluted alkali solution. By
knowing the strength of the diluted alkali, the strength of the bench alkali
