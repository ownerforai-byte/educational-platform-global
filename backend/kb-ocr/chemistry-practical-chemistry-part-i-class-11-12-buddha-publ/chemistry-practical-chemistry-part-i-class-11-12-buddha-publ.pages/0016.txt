How to write in your Practical Note Book
The work which is done in the practical class should be written in the
practical note book. The note book carries certain marks. The rules to be
followed to write in the practical note book are as follows:
1.
Write out clearly the title of the experiment in block letters.
2.
At the heading of each experiment, write clearly the date and serial
number of the experiment.
3.
Begin each experiment on a fresh page.
4.
Do not paste papers on any page for additional writings. Do not tear
any page.
5.
Do not leave blank pages on you note book.
6.
Always keep your note book fair and clean.
7.
Submit your note book regularly for examination by the teachers.
8.
Draw the labelled diagram on the left hand plain side of the note
book. Do not use ink for drawing diagrams.
9.
Write the name of the experiment (in block letters), page number and
date at the contents. The contents should also be made signed.
10.
Write the name of the apparatus, name of the chemicals and
observations.
11.
Write the result and precautions at the last of the experiment.
12.
Begin each new experiment on the right hand side of fresh page.
13.
Mention any reactions concerned of the experiment.
14.
Write the theory (principle) of the experiment in the present passive
tense. Make the theory as short as possible.
15.
Write the process (procedure) of the experiment in the past passive
tense. Process can be written pointwise or in paragraphs.
Format
EXPERIMENT NO.:
Date,
NAME OF THE EXPERIMENT
(Write in capital letters)
APPARATUS REQUIRED
