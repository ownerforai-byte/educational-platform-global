PROCEDURE
1. Use a beaker as voltmeter and establish electric circuit by making
copper plate as anode and iron nail as cathode.
2. Take dil. CuS04 solution as electrolyte.
3. Connect circuit with battery.
4. Observe cathode and anode after sometime of experiment.
RESULT
Iron nail was coated with copper by the process of electroplating.
PRECAUTIONS
1. Two electrode should be sufficiently far apart.
2. Dilute solution should be used.
