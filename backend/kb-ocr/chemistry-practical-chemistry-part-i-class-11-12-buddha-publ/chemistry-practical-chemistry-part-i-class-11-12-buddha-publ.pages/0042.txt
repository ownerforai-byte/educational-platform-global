THEORY
Pure water can be obtained from its impure sample by distillation
method. The conversion of a liquid substance into its vapour by heating
followed by conversion of the vapour into the liquid form by cooling is
called distillation. In other words, distillation is defined as the process of
evaporation by the application of heat followed by cooling. The pure
liquid obtained in the receiver after condensation is called distillate.
