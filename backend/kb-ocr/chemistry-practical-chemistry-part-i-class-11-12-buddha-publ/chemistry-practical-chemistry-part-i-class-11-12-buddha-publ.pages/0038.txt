THEORY
Barium sulphate (BaS04) can be obtained in solid state by treating barium
chloride solution with excess of sulphuric acid solution. This is
precipitation reaction that can be defined as the double decomposition
reaction in which an insoluble solid is formed by the action of two
substances in solutions. The insoluble solid formed is called precipitate.
Here, BaS04 is formed as a white precipitate which can be obtained in
pure state after several washings.
