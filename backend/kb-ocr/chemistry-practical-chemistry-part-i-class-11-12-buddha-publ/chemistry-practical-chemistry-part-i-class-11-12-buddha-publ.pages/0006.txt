Foreword
It is the matter of great pleasure for having a book "Practical Chemistry" which, I
believe, should be a useful textbook of chemistry for the practical courses. It
'J
appears that this book has been written in accordance with the HSEB syllabus of
Grade XI and XII. The book is expected to be equally useful for the students of
Proficiency Certificate Level.
The authors have done their best in presenting the subject matter in a lucid,
interesting and precise manner. This book enables the to understand the
subject matter more clearly, because each experiment described in the book
includes well explained theory and procedure as well as clear diagram,
observations and precautions. The "Suggested Works" provided at the bottom of
the each of the experiment is expected to inspire the learners to perform similar
kinds of new experiments.
It is my belief that this book will be received by teaching and learning
communities of our country. Finally, I congratulate the authors for producing a
fine book.
Professor Mohan Bikram Gewali
