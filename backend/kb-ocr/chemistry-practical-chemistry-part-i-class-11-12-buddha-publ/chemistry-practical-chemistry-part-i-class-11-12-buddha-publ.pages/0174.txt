THEORY
Many of the substances are found to lose their water of crystallization or
to be decomposed evolving the gases like C02, N02 etc. while heating.
Due to this, weight of the substances is lost. Then the percentage loss in
weight is calculated as:
Weight of water or gas lost
% loss in weight =
Weight of hydrated salt
The number of moles of water of crystallization in a mole of the
hydrated salt can be determined as:
Weight of water in gram that
combines with 1 mole of anhydrous salt
Number of moles of water =
Molecular weight of water
The water of crystallization is responsible for crystalline property Of
all hydrated salts.
