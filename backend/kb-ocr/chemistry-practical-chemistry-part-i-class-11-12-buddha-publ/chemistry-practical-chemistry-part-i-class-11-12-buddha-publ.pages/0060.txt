Note: Addition ofNH4Cl suppresses ionization of aqueous (NH4)2 C03 due to
common ion effect. For this reason, ionic product of the metal carbonate
decreases and solubility product becomes greater than its ionic product. That's
why MgC03 does not get precipitated out. But, solubility product of CaC03 is
very low and still the decreased ionic product value can exceed its solubility
product value. Hence, CaC03 gets precipitated out.
