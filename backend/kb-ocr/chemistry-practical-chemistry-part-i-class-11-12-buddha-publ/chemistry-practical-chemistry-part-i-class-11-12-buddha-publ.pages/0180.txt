RESULT
Black oxide of copper was prepared by the action of copper sulphate
solution with dilute sodium hydroxide followed by heating. Red oxide
was prepared by the reduction of thus formed black oxide. Important
properties of both the oxides were studied.
PRECAIÄIONS
1. All the glasswares should be handled with care.
2. All the copper sulphate should be converted into cupric hydroxide to
get the maximum amount of black and red oxides.
