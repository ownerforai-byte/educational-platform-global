Separation of Mixtures
RESULT
White vitriol crystals were obtained from the mixture of zinc carbonate
and magnesium carbonate by different chemical actions followed by
filtration and crystallization.
PRECAUTIONS
The precipitation of zinc hydroxide should be complete.
1.
The crystals should not be dried over the Bunsen flame.
2.
The solution should not be overheated during crystallization.
3.
During crystallization, the evaporated solution should be sårred
4.
with a glass rod to avoid crust formation.
All the glasswares should be handled with care.
5.
Note:
1. Repeated crystallization can be done to get the pure crystals.
2. A long cooling gives better crystals. Care should be taken that the mother
liquor does not get cooled to contaminate the crystals.
SUGGESTED WORKS
1. To prepare white vitriol crystals from the mixture of ZnS04 and
NH4C1.
2. To prepare white vitriol crystals from the mixture of ZnC03 and
NaCl.
