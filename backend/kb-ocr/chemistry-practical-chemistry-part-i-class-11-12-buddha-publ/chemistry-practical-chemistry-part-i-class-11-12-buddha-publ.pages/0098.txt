PROCESS
Put about 15 gm of sodium chloride in a dry and clean round bottom
flask. Fit up the apparatus as shown in figure. Check whether the
apparatus is airtight or not. Make it airtight if it is not. Pour concentrated
sulphuric acid into the flask through the thistle funnel to cover the end of
the funnel. Heat the flask gently. Remove the burner if there is frothing
due to vigorous reaction. Collect the gas in the gas jar by Upward
displacement of air and perform the following experiments to study the
properties of the gas.
