Separation of Mixtures
RESULT
precipitate of pure and dry BaS04 is obtained by treating BaC12 solution
with H2S04 solution followed by washing and drying.
PRECAUTIONS
1. The precipitation should be complete.
2. During washing by decantation, there should not be any loss of the
precipitate.
3. All the glasswares should be handled with care.
4. While drying the ppt over burner, care should be taken so that the
filter paper does not burn.
5. The precipitant should never be added in a very large excess.
Note: The ppt can be obtained in the crystalline state. In order to coagulate the
ppt, boiling is done.
