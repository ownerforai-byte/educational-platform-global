THEORY
Equivalent weight of an element is defined as the number of parts by
weight of which displaces or combines with 1.008 parts by weight of
hydrogen or 8 parts by weight of hydrogen or 35.5 parts by weight of
chlorine.
The equivalents weight of the metals which can replace hydrogen
from aqueous mineral acids (e.g. Zn, Mg, Fe) can be determined by
hydrogen displacement method.
MgS04 + H2 t
Mg + H2S04
In other words, equivalent weight of the metals having lower
reduction potential than that of hydrogen can be found by this method.
In this method, a given piece of metal is treated with dilute H2S04 or
dilutes HCl. Then the weight of H2 gas evolved is determined. Finally,
weight of metal is calculated by using the relation:
