Preface
It gives us immense pleasure to present this book "Practical Chemistry" of the
Buddha's Practical Series. This book is applicable to the students of Grade XI &
as well as those of Proficiency Certificate Level of Science. This book has
mainly been designated according to the syllabus of Higher Secondary Education
Board (HSEB), Nepal. It endeavours to provide a complete material as a text book
of the practical courses. This book has basically been prepared as per the teaching
and laboratory experiences of the authors. The problems plus the errors that are
being faced frequently by the teachers and the students in many of the
experiments have been tried out to be eliminated. Valuable suggestions from the
persons related to the academic field in chemistry have been included in this
book as far as possible.
Dealing with the experiments in a comprehensive and lucid manner is the
chief feature of this book. The theory and the procedure are explained more
clearly in presence of the self-explanatory diagrams. All the essential reactions
and precautions to be taken concerned to the experiment are mentioned. The
experiments are also believed to be helpful for the course content of the theory
paper. In addition, each experiment is accompanied with "Suggested Works"
which are similar to the concerned experiment.
