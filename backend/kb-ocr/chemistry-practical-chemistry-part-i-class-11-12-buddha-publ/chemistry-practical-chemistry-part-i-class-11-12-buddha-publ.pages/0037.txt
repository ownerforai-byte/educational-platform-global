PRECAUTIONS
1. The solution should not be overheated while evaporating it.
2. The crystals should not be dried over the Bunsen burner.
3. All the glasswares should be handled with care.
4. Mother liquor should be rejected carefully so that there will be no
loss of crystals.
Note: It is very difficult to obtain the absolutely pure crystals of the
ingredients by fractional crystallization method.
