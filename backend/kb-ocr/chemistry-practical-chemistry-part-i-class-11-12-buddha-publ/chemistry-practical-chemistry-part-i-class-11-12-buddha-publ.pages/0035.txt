THEORY
Fractional crystallisation is the method employed to separate the
components of a mixture of two or more soluble solids having different
solubility. When a solution containing dissolved crystalline solids with
varying solubility is gradually crystallised, the solid with lower solubility
(here, potassium nitrate) is crystallised out first. Another solid (here,
cupric nitrate) remains in the mother liquor which is crystallised out
later.
Crystallisation can be defined as the process of getång the crystals of
pure solid when a saturated solution of the solid is evaporated to a
temperature, called crystallisation point and then cooled.
