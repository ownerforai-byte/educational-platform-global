Object: TO DETERMINE THE EQUIVALENT WEIGHT OF COPPER

CHEMICALS REQUIRED

THEORY
Equivalent weight of an element is defined as number of parts by weight
of which replaces or combines with 1.008 parts by weight of hydrogen or
8 parts by weight of oxygen or 35.5 parts by weight of chlorine. The
equivalent weight of copper can be determined by oxide formation
method. Copper can not replace H2 gas from dilute mineral acids and it
can form oxide with air. Being very less active metal, it cannot be
completely converted into its oxide easily by simple heating with oxygen.
Therefore, it is converted into its oxide by indirect method. For this
purpose a known weight of copper is treated with conc. HN03 f01101ved
by heating strongly.
Cu + 4HN03 Cu (N03)2 + 2H20 + 2N02
2Cu (N03)2 2CuO + 4N02 + 02
The weight of thus formed black oxide is found. The weight Of
oxygen combined with given weight of copper to form the black oxide is
calculated. Then equivalent weight of copper is given by,
Weight of copper
Equivalent weight = weight of oxygen
Since valency of copper in Cuo is 2, atomic weight of copper is given
by,
Atomic weight = Equivalent weight x Valency
Atomic weight = Equivalent weight x 2
