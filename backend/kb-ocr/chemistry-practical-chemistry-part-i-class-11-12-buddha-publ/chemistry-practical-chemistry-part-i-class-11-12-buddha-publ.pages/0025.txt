CHAPTER 2
Separation of Mixtures
Introduction
The mixing of two or more than two substances results in the impure
substance. Impure substances are also known as mixtures. Mixtures are
of homogeneous or heterogeneous types. There are various techniques
employed to separate the components of a mixture. The technique
depends on the nature of the components.

THEORY
The soluble and insoluble components of a mixture can be separated by
dissolving the mixture in water followed by filtration. Sand and common
salt are separated from their mixture by dissolving the mixture in water
followed by filtration.
