D. Miscellaneous properties of H2S04
l.
The dissolution of H2S04 in water is exothermic. While
preparing the solution of H2S04, we always have to add conc.
H2S04 to water and not water to conc. H2S04.. If water is added
to conc. H2S04, a large amount of heat liberated evaporates
water and the test tube will be burst.
