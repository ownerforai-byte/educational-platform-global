PROCESS
Take a clean porcelain crucible with lid. Weigh out the crucible with lid.
Weigh out the clean copper wire (0.2—0.3 gm) in this crucible. Place the
crucible on the table and add conc. HN03 carefully with the help of a
glass tube. Brown fumes are evolved. The lid of the crucible can be kept
upside down to prevent the loss of copper nitrate solution. While adding
fresh conc. HN03, rise the lid a little above the crucible. When all the
copper dissolves in HN03, wash the blue liquid adhering on the lid very
carefully into the crucible with a little distilled water.
Heat the crucible to dryness and strongly till the whole mass
becomes black. Allow the crucible to cool by placing it over the asbestos
sheet and weigh out it. Repeat the heating, cooling and weighing process
tant weight is observed.
