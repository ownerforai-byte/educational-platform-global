PROCEDURE
1.
Take a stalagmometer and a pykometer, wash with chromic acid
followed by water and dry them.
2.
Take the weight of empty and dry pykometer. Fill it with given
liquid and water in turn and note down weight again.
3.
Ringe the stalagmometer with liquid and clamp it verücally in stand.
4.
Take given liquid in a beaker and suck the liquid in the
stalagmometer upon upper mark of tube with help of rubber tube.
5.
Count the number of complete drops as the liquid passes from upper
mark to lower mark and record it.
6.
Repeat this process three times and carry out same process for
distilled.
