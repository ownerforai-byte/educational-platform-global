Separation of Mixtures
EXPERIMENT NO. 10
object: TO SEPARATE WATER AND CHLOROFORM FROM THEIR
MIXTURE
APPARATUS REQUIRED
1. Separating funnel with stopper
2. Beakers
3. Measuring cylinder
CHEMICALS REQUIRED
Chloroform
THEORY
The mixture of two immiscible liquids like chloroform and water can be
separated by using the device like separating funnel. When the mixture is
taken into this device, chloroform (an organic liquid) forms the bottom
layer and water forms the upper layer. The two •layers are separated by
controlled opening of the tap of the separating funnel.
Figure 2.14: Separating funnel
PROCEDURE
Take the mixture of chloroform and water (in nearly 1:5 ratio by
volume) in a separating funnel. Close the funnel with the stopper.
Shake the mixture for about a minute. Hold the funnel vertically to
allow the components of the mixture to settle.
The liquids form two distinct layers. Remove the stopper and slowly
3.
open the tap to run down the bottom layer (chloroform) into a beaker.
