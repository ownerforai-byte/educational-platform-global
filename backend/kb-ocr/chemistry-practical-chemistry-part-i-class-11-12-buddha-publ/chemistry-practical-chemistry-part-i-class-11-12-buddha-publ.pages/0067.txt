53
Separation of Mixtures
Note:
1. Big crystals can be obtained when the solution is allowed to stand for a
long time. Rapid cooling does not give good crystals. On the other hand,
a long cooling ntay contaminate the crystals due to cooling of the mother
liquor.
2. Repeated crystallization can be done to get the pure crystals.
SUGGESTED WORKS
1. To prepare barium chloride crystals from the mixture of barium
chloride and common salt.
2. To prepare copper sulphate crystals from the mixture of black oxide
of copper and charcoal.
(Hints: Dissolve the mixture in water, leave for some time, charcoal floats on
the surface, remove it, dissolve cupric oxide in dil. H2S04 and carry out
crystallisation).
