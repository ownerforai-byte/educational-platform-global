Cut a Whatman filter paper 5 inches square. Draw a line with pencil
about 3 cm above from any one edge of the paper.
Put a spot of ink at the middle of the pencil line with a help of a fine
capillary tube.
Dry the paper in air. Introduce another drop of the ink on the same
spot and again dry the paper in air.
Roll the paper up into the shape of a cylinder. Fix the two ends of the
cylindrical paper with the clips.
Place the cylindrical paper in a beaker (500 ml capacity) containing
Water and methanol such that the pencil line is about 2 cm above the
upper level of the solvent.
Without disturbing, leave the paper for about half an hour. (At the
mean time, draw the observation table and study the rest of the
procedure well).
