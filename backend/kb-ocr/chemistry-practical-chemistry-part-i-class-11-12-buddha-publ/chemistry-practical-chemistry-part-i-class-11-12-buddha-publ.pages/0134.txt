THEORY
The titration between bench acid and bench alkali or bench acid and
dilute alkali does not give correct titre value. This is because a small
volume of the bench acid or bench alkali is consumed by a large volume
of the dilute alkali. A very small volumetric error, therefore, gives a
different resulting strength. Hence, correct titre value is obtained when
the dilute solutions are titrated. For this reason, double titraüon is
performed. At first, bench acid is titrated against dilute alkali to have
only one rough reading. Then the approximate strength of the bench acid
is determined.
The bench acid is diluted to the required extent such that the diluted
acid and alkali neutralize more or less in equal volumes. Then second
titration is done to standardize this diluted acid solution. By knowing the
strength of the diluted acid, the strength of the bench acid can be
calculated. The unknown strength is determined using the normality
equation,
