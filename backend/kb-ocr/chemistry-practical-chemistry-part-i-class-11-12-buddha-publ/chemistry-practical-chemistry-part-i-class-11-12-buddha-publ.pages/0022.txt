The burner burns with a mixture of fuel gas and air. Depending
upon the quantity of air mixed, two types of flame can be seen.
1. With air holes closed: If the air holes are kept closed and the
burner is lighted, it gives a yellow and smoky flame, called the
luminous flame (or, reducing flame).
2. With air holes opened: If the air holes are slowly opened,
smoky flame gradually disappears and non-luminous (or
oxidising) flame is produced. This is the normal flame of the
burner. This flame is used for heating purposes. This flame
consists of two parts: inner blue part and outer hottest part.
