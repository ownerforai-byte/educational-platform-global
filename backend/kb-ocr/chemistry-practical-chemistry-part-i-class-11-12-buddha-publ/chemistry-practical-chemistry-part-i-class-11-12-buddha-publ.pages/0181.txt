EXPERIMENT NO. 8
04
Object: TO STUDY DIFFERENT REDOX REACTIONS
APPARATUS REQUIRED
1. Test tubes
2. Test.tube stand and test tube holder
CHEMICALS REQUIRED
1. Potassium permanganate solution
2. Potassium dichromate solution
3. Sulphuric acid
4. Oxalic acid solution
5. Ethyl alcohol
6. Zinc pieces
7. Ferric chloride solution
8. Ferrous sulphate
THEORY
The reactions in which oxidation and reduction go hand in hand is called
redox reaction. The reaction in which loss of electron occurs is called
oxidation and the reaction in which reduction occurs is called reduction.
The electrons lost by one species should be gained by another species.
Therefore, oxidation and reduction always go hand in hand.
The chemical substance whose oxidation state increases is called
reductant or reducing agent. It reduces other substances being itself
oxidised. The chemical substance whose oxidation state decreases is
called oxidant or oxidising agent. It oxidises other species being itself
reduced. Many redox reactions are known to occur as a result of some
visual changes.
PROCESS
Take a number of clean test tubes in a test tube stand.
Take few ml. of diluted potassium permanganate solution in a test
tube. Add few drops of dilute sulphuric acid to it and shake. Add
few ml. of oxalic acid solution. Warm the solution. The pink colour
gets discharged.
Take few ml. of potassium dichromate solution in another test tube.
Add few drops of concentrated sulphuric acid to it and shake. Add
few ml. of ethyl alcohol. Warm the solution. The orange colour
changes to bluish green.
