EXPERIMENT NO. 5
Object: TO FIND THE APPROXIMATE pH OF SOME SOLUTIONs
USING UNIVERSAL INDICATOR AND pH PAPER
APPARATUS REQUIRED
1. Test tubes
2. Measuring cylinder
3. Test tube stand
CHEMICALS REQUIRED
1. Universal indicator solution/ pH paper
2. Vegetable and fruit juices
3. Antacid solution
4. Distilled water and tap water
THEORY
A universal indicator or a pH paper contains a mixture of several acid-
base indicators and hence can show several colour changes over a wide
range of pH values. Hence it can be conveniently used to determine the
approximate pH of any solution.
pH of a solution can be defined as the negative logarithm of
hydrogen ion or hydronium ion concentration.
pH = - log[H+]
or, = log [H30+1
PROCESS
A.
Using universal indicator
1, Take 10ml of different juices or samples in different test tubes
with the help of a measuring cylinder.
2. Add 3 drops of universal indicator into each and shake well.
3. Note the colour developed in each sample and compare with the
colour chart given on the universal indicator bottle.
4. Note down the approximate pH of each sample.
