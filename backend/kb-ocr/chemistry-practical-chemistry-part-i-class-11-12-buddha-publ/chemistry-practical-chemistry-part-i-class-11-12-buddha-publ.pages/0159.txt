THEORY

heat released when 1 gm equivalent of the acid (or the base) is
neutralized by 1 gm equivalent of a base (or an acid), both being in dilute
SOIutions. The enthalpy of neutralization of a strong acid with a strong
base is almost constant and is nearly equal to —13.7 K cals or — 56 KJ. This
is because the dilute solutions of sfrong acids and strong bases dissociate
Completely and the amount of heat released is always same.
