Normality Factor
It is the numerical value by which the actual strength (in terms of
normality) of a solution differs from expected sfrength. In the case,
when exact weight of the substance seems to be difficult to weigh due to
different reasons, then the weight of the substance slightly higher or
lower than the required weight can be taken. In such a case, the
normality factor should be introduced. The expected strength is
multiplied by this factor to get the true strength.
