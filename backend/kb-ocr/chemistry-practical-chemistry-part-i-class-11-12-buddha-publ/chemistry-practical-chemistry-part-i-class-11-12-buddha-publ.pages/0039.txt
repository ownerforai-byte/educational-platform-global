4.

5.
If a white ppt appears, add few more drops of the precipitant to the

6.
Heat the solution to boiling, allow it to stand to settle down the ppt
and check the completeness of precipitation as in number (4).
If the fresh ppt appears in the test tube, the precipitation is
incomplete. Add few more drops of dilute H2S04. The completion of
precipitation reaction should be checked again.
