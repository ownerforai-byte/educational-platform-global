Conical flask
It is also called titration flask. Generally, a 250 ml conical flask is used as
a titration flask in laboratory. Titration has to be done in this flask. Before
using, the flask should be thoroughly washed with distilled water. It
should never be rinsed otherwise the volume of the solution by which it's
rinsed will not be accurate.
During titration, the conicai flask should be shaken continuously
with clockwise rotation while putting the solution from the burette
dropwisely. The solution should not splash. Hold the neck of the conical
flask with your right hand and stop cock of the burette with your left
hand as shown in figure below. As soon as the end point is noticed, close
the stop cock immediately.
