First Aid in Laboratory
Minor injuries and accidents may occur in chemist-xy laboratory.
Therefore, each who works in the chemistry laboratory should have some
idea on first aid in the laboratory. A first aid kit should be kept ready in
the laboratory. The kit normally contains the following materials and
chemical substances.
Tincture of iodine, boric acid, dettol, sodium bicarbonate solution
(concentrated and diluted), glycerine, fireproof blanket, ammonia
solution, ammonium carbonate solution, bandage, cotton, rectified spirit,
towel or handkerchief, lime water, castor oil, handiplast, forceps etc.
