THEORY
Equivalent weight of an element can be defined as number of parts by
weight of which combines with or replaces 1.008 parts by weight of
hydrogen or 8 parts by weight of oxygen or 35.5 parts by weight of
chlorine. The equivalent weights of those metals which can replace
hydrogen from aqueous mineral acids can be determined by hydrogen
displacement method. In other words, equivalent weights of the metals
having lower reduction potentials than those of hydrogen can be
determined by this method. Therefore, equivalent weight of the metals
like Zn, Mg, Fe, Al etc. can be determined by this method.
