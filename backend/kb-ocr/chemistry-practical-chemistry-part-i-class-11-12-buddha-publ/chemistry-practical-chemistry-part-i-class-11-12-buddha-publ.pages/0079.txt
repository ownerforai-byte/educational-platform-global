Preparation of Gases
Pour some water through the thistle funnel into the Woulfs
i)
bottle or round bottom flask such that the lower end of the
funnel is just under water. Raising the flask, blow in
through the other end of the delivery tube. Water will rise
in the funnel to a certain height. Quickly close the end of the
delivery tube with your finger. If the water does not fall
down, the apparatus is air tight.
Close the end of the delivery tube and pour a little amount
ii)
of water from a beaker slowly into the thistle funnel. If the
apparatus is air tight, water will not fall down into the flask.
iii) If the apparatus is not airtight, make it airtight.
iv) If the apparatus is airtight, then blow out the water inside
the flask. The corks, thistle funnel and delivery tube should
not be opened once the apparatus is made airtight.
