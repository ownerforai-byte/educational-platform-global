THEORY
A known weight of the metal is treated with excess of acid which has
already been standardized. The volume of excess of acid (acid unused by
metal) is calculated by titrating the acid against the standard alkali. by
subtracting this volume from the volume of the acid taken, the volume of
the acid consumed by the metal can be calculated. The equivalent weight
of the metal can be calculated by knowing the volume of the acid used by
the metal.
At the equivalence point:
1 gm equivalent of acid neutralizes 1 gm equivalent of metal.
i.e., 1000 ml of N-acid = 1 gm equivalent of metal
Hence, the equivalent weight of the metal will be the weight of the
metal that has exactly combined with 1000 ml of N-acid.
The unknown normality can be determined using the normality
equation
VI Sl = V2 S2
