Terms Expressing Strength
The strength of a solution can be expressed in terms of any of the
following terms:
1. Normality (N)
It represents the number of gram equivalent of solute dissolved in per litre of its
solution.
If one gram equivalent of solute is dissolved per litre of its solution,
then the solution is called normal solution (1 N).

Normality = volume of solution in litre
