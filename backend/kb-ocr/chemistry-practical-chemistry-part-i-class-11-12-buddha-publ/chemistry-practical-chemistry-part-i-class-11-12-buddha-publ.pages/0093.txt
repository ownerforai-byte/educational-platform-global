THEORY
Most of the metallic sulphides are decomposed by dilute mineral acids
and produce H2S gas. In laboratory, it is prepared by the action dilute
HCl or dilute H2S04 on iron sulphide. The gas is collected by upward
displacement of air since it's water soluble and slightly heavier than air.
