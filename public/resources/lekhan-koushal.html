Established academic fact

Effective writing aligns purpose, audience, genre and evidence. A clear claim supported by relevant details and an organized structure helps readers follow the reasoning; revision improves meaning as well as correctness.

Conditions, assumptions & limits

Register and conventions depend on the task: an application, essay, report and personal narrative have different audiences and structures. Do not treat one template as universal; check prompt constraints, intended readers and the prescribed Nepali conventions.

Open research question — Meaning across language, performance and translation

A poem, story or performance can support multiple interpretations when its words, form and context leave meaningful ambiguity. For Nepali texts, distinguish the original wording from a translation, and do not apply an English stress pattern or an unverified metre label as though it were universal.
