Principles of biotechnology

#Concept
1### 1. What is Biotechnology?
2Biotechnology is the use of living organisms or their products to make or modify products for practical use. Modern biotechnology involves genetic engineering.
3### 2. Core Techniques
4- **Recombinant DNA (rDNA) Technology**: Combining DNA from different sources
5- **PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction)**: Amplifying DNA segments
6- **Gel Electrophoresis**: Separating DNA fragments by size
7- **Gene Cloning**: Making copies of genes
8### 3. Tools of Recombinant DNA Technology
9- **Restriction Enzymes (Molecular Scissors)**: Cut DNA at specific sequences (palindromes)
10- **Examples**: EcoRI (cuts at GAATTC), HindIII (cuts at AAGCTT)
11- **Sticky ends**: Overhanging single-stranded ends after cutting
12- **Blunt ends**: No overhang after cutting
13- **Vectors (Molecular Vehicles)**: Carry foreign DNA into host
14- **Examples**: Plasmids, bacteriophages, cosmids, Ti plasmid
15- **Competent Host**: Modified to accept foreign DNA (CaCl2 treatment for bacteria)
16### 4. Steps in rDNA Technology
171. Isolation of genetic material (DNA extraction)
182. Cutting DNA with restriction enzymes
193. Separation by gel electrophoresis
204. Insertion of DNA into vector
215. Transformation into host cell
226. Expression of desired gene
23### 5. PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction)
24- Amplifies specific DNA segment millions of times
25- **Steps**: Denaturation (94°C), Annealing (50-65°C), Extension (72°C)
26- **Enzyme**: Taq polymerase (heat-stable from Thermus aquaticus)
27- **Components**: Template DNA, primers, dNTPs, Taq polymerase
28- **Applications**: Forensics, disease diagnosis, genetic research
29### 6. Gel Electrophoresis
30- Separates DNA fragments by size using electric field
31- DNA is negatively charged, moves toward positive electrode
32- Smaller fragments move faster through agarose gel
33- Visualized with ethidium bromide under UV light
34### 7. Entrance Exam Focus
35Identify restriction enzymes; Describe PCR steps; Explain vector requirements.

Key Points

Point
DNA ligase seals the sugar-phosphate backbone after complementary sticky ends pair, forming the recombinant plasmid.
EcoRI cuts 5'-GAATTC-3' to leave 5' AATT overhangs, and HindIII cuts 5'-AAGCTT-3' to leave 5' AGCT overhangs.
The rDNA steps are: isolation of DNA, cutting with restriction enzymes, separation by gel electrophoresis, insertion into a vector, transformation of the host, and expression of the gene.
PCR components are template DNA, forward and reverse primers, the four dNTPs, Taq polymerase and a buffer containing Mg2+ ions.
Bands are visualised after staining with ethidium bromide under UV light; smaller fragments travel furthest from the wells.
Gene cloning in a vector produces many identical copies, that is clones, of a single DNA fragment.

Common Confusions

❌ / ✅
x Any enzyme can cut DNA. x Only specific restriction enzymes recognize specific sequences.
x PCR makes infinite copies. x Each cycle doubles DNA - limited by reagents.
x DNA moves to positive electrode because it's positive. x DNA is NEGATIVE, moves to POSITIVE.

Summary: Biotechnology is the use of living organisms or their products to make or modify products for practical use, and modern biotechnology is built on recombinant DNA (rDNA) technology. The essential tools are restriction enzymes, which cut DNA at specific palindromic recognition sequences such as EcoRI at GAATTC to give sticky or blunt ends, and vectors such as plasmids, bacteriophages and cosmids, which carry foreign DNA into a competent host. rDNA technology proceeds through six steps from isolation of DNA to expression of the gene, while PCR amplifies a chosen DNA segment using thermostable Taq polymerase and gel electrophoresis separates fragments according to size. These techniques together underpin every product of modern biotechnology.
