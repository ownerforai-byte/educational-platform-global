Probability: independent cases, mathematical and empirica…

#Concept
1**Fact.** P(E) = favourable/total. Additive law: P(A∪B) = P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B). If mutually exclusive, P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B).
2**Fact.** Multiplicative: P(A∩B) = P(A)P(B|A); independence: P(A∩B) = P(A)P(B).
3**Fact.** Complement: P(A') = 1 − P(A). For discrete: ΣP = 1; expected value E(X) = Σxᵢpᵢ.

Academic Extension: Evidence, Conditions & Open Questions

Topic bridge — Statistics And Probability. Use this extension to connect the topic’s core material with deeper academic inquiry: state a claim, name the conditions under which it holds, and distinguish established knowledge from an open research or interpretive question.

Established academic fact

A statistic calculated from a sample varies from sample to sample. A confidence interval is produced by a method whose long-run coverage depends on its assumptions; a single interval is not a probability statement about a fixed parameter.

Conditions, assumptions & limits

Check how observations were sampled, whether they are independent, what distributional assumptions are used, and how missing data or outliers were handled. A p-value is not the probability that a hypothesis is true.

Open research question — From association to causation

A correlation in sampled data does not by itself establish that one variable causes another. Researchers continue to develop and test causal-inference methods that state assumptions about confounding, selection, measurement and study design explicitly.
